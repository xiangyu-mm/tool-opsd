The most likely diagnosis is **enterovirus meningitis (acute viral/aseptic meningitis)**.

Supporting features include:

- Acute fever, headache, photophobia, and neck discomfort
- Fine erythematous rash, commonly associated with enteroviral infection
- CSF lymphocytic pleocytosis
- Mildly elevated protein
- Normal CSF glucose

Bacterial meningitis would usually cause neutrophil predominance, higher protein, and low glucose. Tuberculous, cryptococcal, and other fungal meningitides are generally more subacute and have lower CSF glucose. Viral encephalitis would typically include altered mental status, seizures, or focal neurologic findings.
