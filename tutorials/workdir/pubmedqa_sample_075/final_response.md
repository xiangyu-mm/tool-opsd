Yes. In streptozotocin-induced type 1 diabetes mellitus, rats showed increased hippocampal kynurenic acid (KYNA):

- In the primary study (PMID:25443746), hippocampal KYNA measured five weeks after diabetes induction was **220% of control** in untreated diabetic rats—approximately a **120% increase**.
- Insulin-treated diabetic rats still had elevated KYNA at **170% of control**—approximately a **70% increase**.
- The increase was region-selective; KYNA was not increased in the frontal cortex or striatum.
- A follow-up study (PMID:31737685) independently confirmed increased hippocampal KYNA in untreated and insulin-treated diabetic rats.

Thus, the evidence supports that experimental type 1 diabetes increases hippocampal KYNA content in rats.
