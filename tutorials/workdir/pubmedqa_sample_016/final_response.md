A 2014 cross-sectional speckle-tracking study in 86 diabetic hypertensive patients found that participants with impaired global longitudinal strain (GLS ≤18.5) had higher plasma osteoprotegerin (OPG) than those with better GLS (P<0.001). OPG independently predicted impaired GLS in the reported analysis (P=0.001); OPG >6.45 pmol/L identified participants with GLS ≤18.5.

This supports an association between OPG and subclinical LV systolic dysfunction in this population, but the observational study does not establish that OPG causes the dysfunction. **PMID: 25442456.**
