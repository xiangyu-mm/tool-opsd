Yes. The study **PMID:25424625** directly evaluated the anti-IGF-1R monoclonal antibody **LMAb1** in acquired trastuzumab-resistant **SKOV3-T** ovarian cancer cells.

LMAb1 inhibited several carcinogenicity-related phenotypes, including:

- Cell growth/proliferation
- Migration
- Colony formation
- Xenograft tumor growth in vivo

The combination of LMAb1 and trastuzumab produced the strongest effects, including smaller tumors and longer survival in mice. The proposed mechanism involved blocking IGF-1R signaling through the MAPK/AKT pathways. The available evidence does not specifically establish apoptosis as the primary endpoint, but it does directly demonstrate inhibition of malignant and tumorigenic activity.
