Yes. A 2016 study found that reduced **Drosophila glob1** expression caused oogenesis abnormalities associated with improperly formed F-actin structures. Somatic and germline clone analyses supported a cell-autonomous role for glob1 in maintaining F-actin cytoskeletal integrity in both somatic and germ-cell derivatives (PMID: 27503269, *Developmental Dynamics*).

The evidence specifically supports a requirement for **F-actin-based** cytoskeletal integrity during oogenesis; it does not establish a general role for every cytoskeletal component.

FINAL DECISION: YES
