**Yes, the study suggests short-term atorvastatin can reduce arterial stiffness**, but the finding is not definitive for the specific middle-aged subgroup.

A 2017 study of 37 women with SLE gave atorvastatin 20 mg/day for 8 weeks and measured carotid–femoral PWV. In participants with pathological baseline stiffness, PWV fell from **8.43 ± 1.45 to 7.42 ± 1.06 m/s** (**P=0.002**); the reduction was reported to persist six months after treatment withdrawal. In the middle-aged subgroup, PWV decreased by about **0.40 m/s** (**7.16 ± 1.23 to 6.76 ± 0.82 m/s; P=0.05**), a borderline result.

**Caveat:** This was a small, within-patient study without a parallel placebo or untreated control, so it suggests improvement but cannot establish that atorvastatin caused it. Evidence: PMID **27510602** (search finding source: `call_JJs5oCdekV2NzLEO8SM3OBdv`).
