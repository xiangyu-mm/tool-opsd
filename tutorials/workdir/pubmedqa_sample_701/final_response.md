Yes. A study reported a BALB/cHeNhg-derived **Rb1 promoter deletion** with about **1.5-fold lower Rb1 expression** in reporter and in-vivo assays. In irradiated mice, reduced Rb1 dosage was associated with significantly increased osteosarcoma incidence, supporting a contribution to susceptibility (PMID: 25092376).

The evidence supports a susceptibility effect in this mouse model; the retrieved abstract did not provide group sizes or exact incidence and statistical values, and it does not establish that the promoter variant alone is sufficient to cause osteosarcoma.

FINAL DECISION: YES
