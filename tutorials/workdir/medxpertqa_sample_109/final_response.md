The presentation is classic for **ureteral nephrolithiasis**: acute flank-to-groin pain, nausea/vomiting, urinary frequency, and marked hematuria without leukocyte esterase or nitrites. In pregnancy, **renal/bladder ultrasound** is the first-line imaging modality because it avoids ionizing radiation. Doppler assessment may help evaluate hydronephrosis and ureteral jets.  

MRI urography is reserved for an equivocal or nondiagnostic ultrasound; CT and radiographic studies are not routine initial tests in pregnancy.
