**Yes—candidate genotype associations have been reported.** In a cohort study, **ENPP1 rs858339 TT** was reported as protective against TMD, while **AT** was associated with greater TMD risk. **ESR1 rs1643821 AA** was reported as a risk factor for worsening dysfunction after orthognathic surgery (PMID: 27519661).

A follow-up study of 174 patients associated ENPP1 rs858339 with TMD diagnoses in craniofacial-asymmetry subgroups and reported associations between other ENPP1/ESR1 variants and asymmetry characteristics. TMD symptoms often improved after treatment, but that does not establish that genotype predicts who will improve (PMID: 29103441).

These are observational associations, not proof that the variants cause TMD or reliably predict an individual patient’s surgical outcome. The abstracts do not provide enough information to establish clinical predictive value.
