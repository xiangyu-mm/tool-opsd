**Yes.** A randomized crossover study in healthy premenopausal women found that a single application of 1% testosterone solution to the axilla produced faster and greater serum testosterone increases than application to the inner arm. Baseline-corrected, AUC-based bioavailability was approximately **twofold higher** from the axilla (PMID: 25073836).
