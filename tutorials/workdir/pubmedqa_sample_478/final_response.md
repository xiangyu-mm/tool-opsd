The available evidence supports **yes**, specifically in rheumatoid arthritis.

A pooled analysis of 1,068 participants from the GO-BEFORE and GO-FORWARD golimumab trials (PMID:25091439) found that higher BMI independently predicted:

- Less radiographic progression on X-ray at 52 and 104 weeks, using the van der Heijde–Sharp score.
- Less MRI erosion-score progression over 2 years.
- Less baseline MRI bone edema after adjustment for disease-severity measures, including MRI synovitis.

Thus, the direction of association was consistent across X-ray and MRI over 1–2 years. However, the retrieved evidence did not include adjusted odds ratios or confidence intervals, and the conclusion is based primarily on one directly relevant study rather than a broad replicated literature.
