The phase II study (PMID: 10735904) evaluated intravenous vinorelbine 30 mg/m² every 2 weeks in 36 patients with advanced AIDS-related Kaposi sarcoma whose disease had progressed after at least one prior systemic chemotherapy regimen.

**Efficacy**
- 35 patients were assessable.
- Complete response: **9%**
- Partial response: **34%**
- Overall objective response rate: **43%** (95% CI, 26%–61%)
- Median response duration: **176 days**
- Median progression-free survival: **151 days**
- Median survival: **216 days**
- Responses occurred even in some patients previously resistant to other vinca alkaloids.

**Safety**
- Vinorelbine was reported as generally well tolerated.
- Toxicities, including neurologic toxicity, were described as mild and reversible.
- **Neutropenia** was the principal dose-limiting toxicity.

**Interpretation**
These results indicate clinically meaningful antitumor activity and acceptable short-term tolerability in heavily pretreated patients. However, the study was small and single-arm, without a comparator group, so it cannot establish superiority over other treatments or fully characterize uncommon or long-term risks. The conclusion applies primarily to the studied population and regimen.
