**Yes—in the rat model described.** In the study *“Prenatal zinc reduces stress response in adult rat offspring exposed to lipopolysaccharide during gestation”* (PMID: 25445220), pregnant rats received LPS on gestational day 9.5, followed one hour later by zinc sulfate or saline. After a two-hour restraint challenge as adults, female offspring in the prenatal-zinc group had **lower serum corticosterone** and **reduced striatal norepinephrine turnover** than offspring exposed to LPS followed by saline.

This supports reduced acute stress response in that experimental model. It is evidence from one animal study, not proof of a general effect across other models or in humans.
