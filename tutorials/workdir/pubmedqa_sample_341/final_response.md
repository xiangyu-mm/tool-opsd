Yes. The primary study (2016, PMID: 27514378) found that LPS increased SBD-1 mRNA in cultured ovine oviduct epithelial cells in a concentration- and time-dependent manner.

Evidence for p38 MAPK mediation included:

- LPS induced phosphorylation of p38 MAPK.
- The p38 inhibitors SB203580 and SB202190 reduced LPS-induced SBD-1 expression.
- TLR4 neutralization reduced both p38 phosphorylation and SBD-1 induction.
- JNK inhibition had little effect, while ERK inhibition produced only a minor effect, supporting p38 as the principal pathway.

Thus, LPS induces SBD-1 expression through a TLR4–p38 MAPK contribution in this cell model. The evidence specifically demonstrates increased SBD-1 mRNA in vitro, not necessarily increased protein or an in-vivo response.

FINAL DECISION: YES
