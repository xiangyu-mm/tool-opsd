The MRCP findings of diffuse multifocal intrahepatic and extrahepatic duct beading are characteristic of **primary sclerosing cholangitis (PSC)**. PSC is strongly associated with inflammatory bowel disease, which may be asymptomatic or clinically occult.

Therefore, the next diagnostic test is **ileocolonoscopy with biopsies** to evaluate for associated ulcerative colitis or Crohn disease.

- **ERCP/PTC:** Not routinely needed after diagnostic MRCP; reserved for therapeutic intervention or evaluation of a dominant/indeterminate stricture.
- **Liver biopsy:** Reserved for suspected small-duct PSC or autoimmune hepatitis overlap.
- **Antimitochondrial antibodies:** Used primarily when evaluating suspected primary biliary cholangitis, which does not cause multifocal biliary beading.
- **Serum IgG4:** May help evaluate IgG4-related sclerosing cholangitis but is not the most appropriate next test in this classic PSC presentation.
