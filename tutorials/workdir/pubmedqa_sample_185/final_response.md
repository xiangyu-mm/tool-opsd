**Yes.** A primary study in pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma cells reported that PRDX1 interacts with active p38 MAPK, and PRDX1–phosphorylated-p38 complexes localize at the leading edges of migrating cells. Suppressing PRDX1 reduced active p38 at cell protrusions and inhibited protrusion formation and Matrigel invasion. The p38 inhibitor SB203580 also reduced protrusions and invasion.

This supports PRDX1 promoting pancreatic cancer cell invasion in association with p38 MAPK activity. A qualification: the reported findings support the pathway link, but the abstract does not describe a p38 rescue experiment demonstrating that p38 mediates all of PRDX1’s effect.

**Source:** Taniuchi K, et al. *Peroxiredoxin 1 promotes pancreatic cancer cell invasion by modulating p38 MAPK activity.* *Pancreas.* 2015;44(2):331–340. PMID: 25426613. DOI: 10.1097/MPA.0000000000000270.
