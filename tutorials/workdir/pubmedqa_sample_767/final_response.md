Yes. Human clinical evidence supports rapid control of gastric acid hypersecretion with intravenous pantoprazole in Zollinger–Ellison syndrome:

- In a 21-patient study (PMID: 10734021), an initial **80-mg IV dose infused over 15 minutes** controlled acid output in **all patients within 1 hour**; mean onset was **41 minutes**. Acid output fell from a baseline mean of **40.2 mEq/h** to approximately **4.0 mEq/h** in follow-up assessments. [Source: `call_sTCpDF2OYl9Gnm5XTCzlKVbC`]
- Acid suppression was measurable within approximately **15 minutes**, with normalization by **60 minutes** in another report involving 21 patients; control lasted a mean of **10.9 hours** after infusion. [Source: `call_97ECtJfQnQGWqEVN9lNaYeUj`]
- Divided doses of **80–120 mg every 8–12 hours** generally maintained control, although 4 of 21 patients required dose escalation.

The evidence is based mainly on a small, nonrandomized clinical cohort, but it directly supports rapid and clinically effective suppression.

FINAL DECISION: YES
