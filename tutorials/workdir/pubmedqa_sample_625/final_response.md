### Findings

A direct prospective study (PMID: **25092992**) evaluated 60 patients with type 2 diabetes already receiving metformin 500 mg/day:

- **Pioglitazone:** 15 mg/day, 30 patients  
- **Comparator:** Glimepiride 1 mg/day, 30 patients  
- **Duration:** 6 months  

CAVI **decreased significantly** in the pioglitazone group, while it remained unchanged in the glimepiride group. The between-group difference in CAVI change was also statistically significant. Blood pressure decreased in both groups without a significant between-group difference, suggesting the CAVI result was not explained solely by blood-pressure lowering.

The evidence is limited because it comes primarily from one small study with 30 participants per group, and the retrieved record did not provide reliable post-treatment CAVI values or the absolute magnitude of change.

**Conclusion:** Pioglitazone appears to improve CAVI when added to metformin in patients with type 2 diabetes, but certainty is limited by the small single-study evidence base.
