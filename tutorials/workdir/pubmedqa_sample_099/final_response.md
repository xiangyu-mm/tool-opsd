Yes. The multicentre US registry study by Christopoulos et al. (PMID: 25442459; *Can J Cardiol*, 2014) evaluated 636 CTO PCI procedures:

- **Procedural success:** LCX CTOs: **84.6%**, versus **91.7%** for RCA and **94.7%** for LAD (**P = 0.016**).
- **Major complications:** LCX: **4.3%**, versus 1.0% for RCA and 2.3% for LAD; this was a **nonsignificant trend** (**P = 0.07**).
- **Procedural efficiency:** LCX interventions required greater fluoroscopy exposure and substantial contrast use.

Thus, circumflex CTO PCI was associated with significantly lower procedural success and reduced efficiency, although the evidence for higher major-complication rates did not reach statistical significance.

FINAL DECISION: YES
