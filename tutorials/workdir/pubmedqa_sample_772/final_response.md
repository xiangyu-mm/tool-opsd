Yes. A primary study found that γδ TCR–expressing T cells isolated from mice with Coxsackievirus B3 myocarditis killed cultured cardiac myocytes. Blocking experiments indicated the γδ T cells acted predominantly through Fas-mediated killing, and they induced more myocyte apoptosis in vitro than αβ T cells.

**Evidence:** Huber SA. “T cells expressing the gamma delta T cell receptor induce apoptosis in cardiac myocytes.” *Cardiovascular Research*. 2000. PMID: [10728379](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10728379/).

This supports a direct effect in the studied experimental mouse and cell-culture context; it does not by itself establish how often γδ T cells cause cardiomyocyte apoptosis in human heart disease.

FINAL DECISION: YES
