A six-year ADDITION-DK analysis of 1,481 people with screen-detected type 2 diabetes found that change in serum methylglyoxal was inversely associated with change in eGFR: each doubling in methylglyoxal change corresponded to a 1.6 mL/min/1.73 m² lower eGFR change.
