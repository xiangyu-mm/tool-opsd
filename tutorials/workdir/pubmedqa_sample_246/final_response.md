Yes. A 2015 *Acta Diabetologica* study (PMID: 25430706) used exome sequencing in two DCCT participants with type 1 diabetes and hypobetalipoproteinemia. It identified heterozygous APOB mutations predicted to introduce premature stop codons at amino acids 1333 and 3680. Both participants had very low LDL-C (36 and 28 mg/dL), and each variant was inherited from a parent.

This supports APOB loss of function as a plausible explanation in those two cases. The reported evidence does not include functional validation or establish causality beyond them.

FINAL DECISION: YES
