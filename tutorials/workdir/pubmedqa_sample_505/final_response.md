### Evidence summary

The available evidence does **not** show that sex/gender meaningfully changes the usual clinical outcomes of eccentric rotational acetabular osteotomy (ERAO) for hip dysplasia:

- **Matched comparative study:** 36 men versus 72 women had nearly identical postoperative Harris Hip Scores (94.1 vs 93.5). Survival was numerically lower in men (92.8% vs 98.1%), but the difference was not statistically significant. Men tended to have slightly less CE-angle correction, without a demonstrated clinical disadvantage.  
  *(PMID:25096451; source: `call_MDPpeEQzMUmtDOSLzh3MErv`)*

- **Long-term prognostic cohort:** In 273 hips followed for at least 5 years, sex was not an independent predictor of osteoarthritis progression. Predictors included BMI, operative era, postoperative CE angle, femoral-head position, and concomitant valgus osteotomy.  
  *(PMID:17308472; source: `call_PC1FPqYLzQYcWh1A5iRa5kVZ`)*

- **Complications:** Pubic/ischial stress fractures occurred only in women in one predominantly female cohort, but multivariable analysis identified pubic discontinuity—not sex—as the independent predictor. Fractures united and did not worsen final outcomes.  
  *(PMID:21290153; source: `call_BWoik6s5Ey5kpv1gYoUFaJz9`)*

- **Possible range-of-motion difference:** A recent CT-based virtual ERAO study found higher simulated failure rates for adequate flexion and internal rotation in men, associated with lower femoral-neck anteversion. However, this was a simulation study, not a clinical postoperative outcomes study, and requires validation.  
  *(PMID:41371542; source: `call_x7SAPGvrlVXtEm65B43O6ziG`)*

Important limitations are the small number of male patients in most ERAO cohorts and the fact that the studies evaluate biological sex rather than gender identity. Thus, a sex-related effect cannot be completely excluded, particularly for postoperative bony range of motion, but it is not currently established for clinical scores, radiographic correction, osteoarthritis progression, complications, or hip survival.
