Yes—**the highest-dose phytochemical blend provided protection against liver damage** in aged ovariectomized rats.

The study (Obesity, 2015; PMID: 25451038) tested genistein, resveratrol, and quercetin, with vitamin D. The highest-dose combination prevented the ovariectomy-associated increase in serum alanine aminotransferase (ALT), a marker of liver injury. However, some blends also increased serum free fatty acids and hepatic triglycerides, so the protection was **partial rather than complete prevention of fatty liver or all hepatic abnormalities**.
