Yes. Molassiotis et al. examined unmet supportive-care needs in a UK multisite, cross-sectional survey of 472 people with stage I–III cutaneous melanoma, 3 months to 5 years after complete resection.

- **Prevalence:** 123 participants (27%) reported at least one unmet need, mostly at a low level.
- **Most common concerns:** fear of recurrence (29%), uncertainty about the future (25%), insufficient information about recurrence risk (24%), and about what might happen if melanoma spread (20%).
- **Correlates:** Greater unmet need was associated with nodal disease, anxiety or depression, younger age (OR 2.23), and leaving school early (OR 4.85). Better emotional and social well-being were associated with fewer unmet needs. Sex and tumour thickness were not associated.

Because this was a cross-sectional survey, these are associations, not evidence that the correlates cause unmet needs.  
**Source:** Molassiotis A, et al. *Annals of Oncology*. 2014. PMID: 25081900. https://doi.org/10.1093/annonc/mdu366
