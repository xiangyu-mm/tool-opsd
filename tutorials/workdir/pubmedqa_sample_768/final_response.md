Yes. The primary study (PMID: 10735775) found that epithelium-dependent bronchodilatory activity was preserved in pig bronchioles after 1.5 hours of normothermic cardiopulmonary bypass.

In isolated ~100-µm bronchiolar segments, epithelial denudation reduced isoproterenol-induced relaxation, confirming an epithelial component. Responses measured 30 minutes after CPB were not significantly different from pre-CPB responses. Nitroprusside relaxation, used as an epithelium-independent comparator, was unaffected by epithelial removal.

FINAL DECISION: YES
