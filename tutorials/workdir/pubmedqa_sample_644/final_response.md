A primary study (Zhang et al., *Tumori*, 2014; PMID: 25076250) found that silencing ID2 enhanced arsenic trioxide (ATO)-induced proliferation inhibition in Eca109 esophageal squamous-cell carcinoma and BxPC3 pancreatic-carcinoma cells.
