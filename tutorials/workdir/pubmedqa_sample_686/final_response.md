**Yes.** In obese WNIN/Ob rats, vitamin A-enriched diets modulated pathways related to reverse cholesterol transport. In the 20-week study, supplementation reduced plasma HDL cholesterol and increased hepatic SR-BI expression and lipase activity; at 52 and 129 mg vitamin A/kg diet, it also reduced plasma LCAT activity and increased hepatic ABCA1 expression. The authors concluded that RCT was *presumably* improved. This was an inference from pathway-related measures, not a direct measurement of whole-body cholesterol flux.
