In a 2016 in-vitro study, human sinonasal air–liquid-interface epithelial cultures showed a rapid, NOS-mediated signal after exposure to *Staphylococcus epidermidis* conditioned medium, measured using DAF-FM fluorescence. The response was weaker than with *S. aureus* conditioned medium (PMID: 27509402).

This supports production of an NO-related signal by cultured upper-airway epithelium in response to bacterial secreted products. It does not establish the response to direct contact with live bacteria or quantify nitric oxide production in intact humans.

FINAL DECISION: YES
