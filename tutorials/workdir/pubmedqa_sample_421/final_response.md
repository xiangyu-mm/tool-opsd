## Findings

The directly relevant study is:

- **Alnouti et al. (2016)**, *“Addition of Dexamethasone Alters the Bile Acid Composition by Inducing CYP8B1 in Primary Cultures of Human Hepatocytes”*  
- **PMID: 27493455**

In primary human hepatocyte cultures, dexamethasone:

- Increased **CYP8B1 mRNA** expression.
- Increased formation of **cholic acid**.
- Decreased formation of **chenodeoxycholic acid**.
- Therefore shifted bile-acid composition toward greater 12α-hydroxylation, consistent with CYP8B1 induction.

The evidence supports the association between dexamethasone exposure, CYP8B1 transcriptional induction, and altered bile-acid composition. However, the available abstract does not report CYP8B1 protein or enzyme activity, and no CYP8B1 loss-of-function experiment was described; therefore, direct causality is mechanistically plausible but not definitively proven.

FINAL DECISION: YES
