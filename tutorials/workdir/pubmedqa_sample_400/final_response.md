**Yes, in the tested mouse models.** In CT-26 and Lewis lung carcinoma tumor-bearing mice, salidroside reportedly preserved tumor-free body weight, reduced adipose and gastrocnemius muscle loss, lowered tumor burden, and prolonged survival. The study also reported increased gastrocnemius mTOR and phosphorylated mTOR, alongside increased myosin heavy chain; related changes were observed in TNF-α-treated C2C12 myotubes.

These results support mTOR signaling as a mechanism, but the reported marker changes alone do not prove that mTOR activation is necessary for the benefits. This is preclinical evidence, not evidence of efficacy in people.
