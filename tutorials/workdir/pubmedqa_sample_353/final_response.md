**Yes—one study found an association between management on a general/colorectal surgery service and lower inpatient mortality among patients with *C. difficile*.**

Stokes et al. studied 1,175 patients at a single tertiary-care center. Mortality was **2.6%** for patients admitted to surgery services versus **6.8%** for those admitted to medical services (*p* = 0.028). The adjusted odds ratio was **0.18** (95% CI 0.05–0.58); the difference also remained after propensity-score matching.

This was a retrospective, single-center study, so it cannot establish that the service or specialty caused the mortality difference. The groups differed in age and comorbidity, and higher colectomy rates in the surgery group may have contributed. The finding supports an association, not a general guarantee of better outcomes with surgeon management.

**Source:** Stokes AL et al. *Diseases of the Colon & Rectum*. 2016. PMID: 27505114. DOI: 10.1097/DCR.0000000000000643.

FINAL DECISION: YES
