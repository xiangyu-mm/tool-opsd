Yes. In the TSCCA tongue squamous cell carcinoma cell model, siRNA-mediated ANGPTL4 downregulation—confirmed by qRT-PCR and western blot—reduced both:

- **Cell migration**
- **Cell proliferation**

This direct evidence comes from **Wang et al., 2016 (PMID: 27505034)**. The study did not report a rescue experiment or definitive downstream mechanism, so the causal mechanism remains incompletely characterized. Broader HNSCC studies also support roles for ANGPTL4 in migration and proliferation, but they are less TSCC-specific.

FINAL DECISION: YES
