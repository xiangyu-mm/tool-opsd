Yes. In a 2014 case report (PMID: 25075066), a heavily pretreated breast-cancer patient with extensive hepatic infiltration developed life-threatening liver failure despite chemotherapy. After compassionate-use administration of **Legasil®**, a silibinin-containing formulation, the patient experienced clinical and liver-function improvement sufficient to continue palliative chemotherapy.

However, this was a single uncontrolled case, and concurrent treatment effects or spontaneous fluctuation cannot be excluded. Thus, the report supports a **possible clinical benefit**, not definitive proof that silibinin alone caused the improvement.

FINAL DECISION: YES
