Yes. The paper exists:

- **Title:** *Early experiences with a new three-dimensional annuloplasty ring for the treatment of functional tricuspid regurgitation*
- **Authors:** Ratschiller T, Guenther T, Guenzinger R, Noebauer C, Kehl V, Gertler R, Lange R
- **Journal/year:** *The Annals of Thoracic Surgery*, 2014
- **PMID:** 25443010
- **DOI:** 10.1016/j.athoracsur.2014.07.023

It reports a retrospective series of **200 consecutive patients** undergoing repair of functional tricuspid regurgitation with the **Medtronic Contour 3D annuloplasty ring**. At a mean follow-up of 1 year, tricuspid regurgitation improved substantially, with **4.3%** having residual grade II or greater regurgitation at discharge and **90.9% freedom from recurrent grade II or greater regurgitation at 2 years**. The study also reported **6% 30-day mortality**, **98.5% freedom from tricuspid-valve-related reoperation at 2 years**, no ring dehiscence, and a **7% permanent pacemaker rate**.

Thus, the article directly addresses early clinical experience with a new three-dimensional annuloplasty ring for functional tricuspid regurgitation.

FINAL DECISION: YES
