Yes. A primary study (PMID: **10752934**) used **in situ hybridization with ³⁵S-labeled PAI-1/SERPINE1 riboprobes** on rodent eye sections and reported that, in the adult eye, PAI-1 mRNA was localized **exclusively to epithelial cells of the ciliary processes**, particularly near their apices.

Important qualification: during development, PAI-1 mRNA was also transiently detected in the retinal ganglion-cell layer. Thus, the ciliary epithelium is the reported exclusive ocular site in the **adult** rodent eye; the available abstract does not specify whether the rodent was rat or mouse.

FINAL DECISION: YES
