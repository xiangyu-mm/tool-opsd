The presentation is classic for **Meigs syndrome**: an ovarian fibroma associated with ascites and a right-sided pleural effusion. Ovarian fibromas are benign sex-cord stromal tumors composed of **bundles of spindle-shaped fibroblasts**, often with abundant collagen.
