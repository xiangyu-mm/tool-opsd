Evidence from the strongest direct comparative cohort suggests:

- Septic revision THA had greater **unadjusted** blood loss: 2533 vs 1974 mL.
- Transfusion was more frequent: 90% vs 73%.
- However, after adjustment for revision complexity, age, ASA status, preoperative hemoglobin, and related factors, infection was **not independently associated** with either blood loss or transfusion.
- A larger TXA cohort also showed higher transfusion rates in septic cases, but it was not designed to establish infection as an independent risk factor.

Thus, infection is associated with greater crude bleeding and transfusion requirements, but the available evidence does not support infection itself as an independent risk factor.
