Yes. In the Tunisian study by Boudaya et al. (PMID: **25438689**), the **CC genotype of the PTGS2/COX-2 T8473C polymorphism** in the 3′-UTR was associated with decreased nasopharyngeal carcinoma risk:

- **OR:** 0.37  
- **95% CI:** 0.17–0.81  
- **p:** 0.013  
- Population: 189 patients with nasopharyngeal carcinoma and 237 controls

The C allele was also associated with reduced risk (OR 0.72, 95% CI 0.53–0.97, p=0.032). However, this protective association concerns **T8473C**, not the distinct **−765G>C promoter polymorphism**, for which C-allele carriage was associated with increased risk in another Tunisian study (PMID: 17968676).

FINAL DECISION: YES
