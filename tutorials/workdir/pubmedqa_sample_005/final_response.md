Yes. A retrospective study at the Autonomous University of Hidalgo State, a public university in Mexico, reviewed 150 erupted third-molar extractions in 83 patients. The reported reasons were:

- Prosthetic: **44.0%**
- Orthodontic: **24.7%**
- Dental caries: **20.0%**
- Periodontal disease: **11.3%**

Source: *“Reasons why erupted third molars are extracted in a public university in Mexico,”* **The West Indian Medical Journal** (2014), PMID: 25429481.
