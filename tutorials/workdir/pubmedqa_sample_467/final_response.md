Yes. The direct study **PMID:27491861 (Molecular Oncology, 2016)** supports both associations in ER-positive/luminal breast cancer:

- **PIK3CA genotype:** LRG1 mRNA expression was higher in **PIK3CA-mutant** than PIK3CA-wild-type tumors. LRG1 was among nine transcripts associated with PIK3CA status in both a 286-tumor TCGA dataset and an 84-patient metastatic breast-cancer cohort.
- **Aromatase-inhibitor outcome:** In a 25-patient neoadjuvant letrozole dataset, LRG1 mRNA decreased after treatment. In the 84-patient first-line AI-treated metastatic cohort, baseline LRG1 expression was associated with **progression-free survival and overall survival**; the PFS association remained after adjustment for traditional predictive factors.

The evidence supports association, but the available abstract does not provide LRG1-specific hazard ratios, effect sizes, or p-values, and it does not definitively establish LRG1 as a treatment-specific predictive biomarker rather than a prognostic marker.

FINAL DECISION: YES
