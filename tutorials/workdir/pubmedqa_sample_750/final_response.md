The prospective cohort study (PMID: 10727759) found **similar observed cardiovascular-event risks**. Among patients followed for a median of 57 months, events occurred in 31/151 (20.5%) with normohomocysteinaemia and 11/52 (21.2%) with vitamin-treated hyperhomocysteinaemia. The adjusted relative risk for the treated group versus the normohomocysteinaemic group was 0.96 (95% CI 0.48–1.92).

This supports similar observed risks, but the wide confidence interval means the study does **not** establish equivalence or prove that vitamin treatment caused the similar outcomes.
