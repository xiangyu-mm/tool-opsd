The evidence supports a correlation.

The directly relevant study, **PMID:10728358**, examined rabbit atrial terminal-crest preparations from neonatal through adult stages. During postnatal maturation:

- Cx43 gap-junction area at myocyte termini increased approximately **3.5-fold**.
- Cx43 on lateral cell borders remained relatively stable.
- Longitudinal impulse-propagation velocity increased approximately **2.4-fold**.
- Transverse propagation velocity changed little.
- Consequently, electrical anisotropy increased.

Thus, increased longitudinal propagation and anisotropy coincided with preferential redistribution of Cx43 toward the cell termini, consistent with enhanced longitudinal intercellular coupling. However, total Cx43 amount per myocyte changed little, and the study was observational rather than an intervention, so it demonstrates **correlation rather than causation**. The search identified no additional directly matching primary study beyond this principal report.
