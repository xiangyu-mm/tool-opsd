Yes. The 2014 study by Villalonga et al. directly supports this claim:

- **Compound:** ZBW1, an anthraquinonyl glycoside containing a mannose ligand and an anthraquinone group.
- **Electrode:** Nano-graphene-coated screen-printed electrodes.
- **Lectin:** Mannose-binding Concanavalin A.
- **Function:** The anthraquinone moiety binds graphene and produces a measurable voltammetric signal. This signal was used to quantify glycoside loading and select electrodes within a consistent signal range, thereby standardizing electrode functionalization.
- **Detection:** Concanavalin A binding was measured by electrochemical impedance spectroscopy using ferri/ferrocyanide as the external redox probe.

Thus, the anthraquinonyl glycoside was used specifically for electrode quality control and standardization, in addition to providing lectin recognition.
