The study supports **yes**. In a prospective pretest/posttest study of 73 team members, two hours of teamwork training was followed by higher TEAM scores during simulated cardiopulmonary arrest events. Participant-rated scores rose from 3.2 to 3.7, and trained-observer scores rose from 3.0 to 3.7; both changes were statistically significant (P < .001).

This is encouraging evidence, but the study did not report a control group, so it cannot establish with high confidence that training alone caused the improvement. **PMID: 27509565.**
