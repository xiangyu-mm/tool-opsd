**Yes—in the specific experimental sense studied.** When participants lay on their side, the usual time-dependent downward drift in remembered target location did not emerge; the authors attributed this to idiotropic dominance (PMID: 25095771, *The dynamic representation of gravity is suspended when the idiotropic vector is misaligned with gravity*).

This does **not** mean all perception or representation of gravity stops. A related centrifuge study found that vestibular stimulation disrupted the *time course* of representational gravity without changing its direction (PMID: 27595305, *Vestibular stimulation interferes with the dynamics of an internal representation of gravity*). Thus, “suspended” best describes that particular dynamic visual effect under the tested conditions.
