Yes. In ex vivo experiments, PD-1/PD-L1 pathway blockade increased proliferation of hypoproliferative sarcoidosis CD4+ T cells after T-cell receptor stimulation.

Key evidence:

- **PMID: 25073001** — The study explicitly reported that blockade of the PD-1 pathway restored proliferative capacity in sarcoidosis lymphocytes with reduced proliferation.
- **PMID: 27564547** — A mechanistic follow-up confirmed impaired proliferation in sarcoidosis CD4+ T cells, found an inverse correlation between PD-1 expression and proliferation (**r = −0.70, P < 0.001**), and showed normalization of PI3K/AKT/mTOR-related signaling after blockade.

These findings demonstrate an **ex vivo cellular rescue**, not proven clinical benefit from PD-1 blockade as a sarcoidosis treatment.

FINAL DECISION: YES
