**No increased thrombotic risk was demonstrated in the mouse evidence reviewed.** The most direct study tested 2bF8trans mice, which express human FVIII specifically in platelets on an FVIII-null background (PMID: 34587639). It found clot strength and stability were not statistically different from wild type, although thrombi were more stable than those of FVIII-null controls under physiological shear. At high, pathological shear, thrombi were smaller and had less fibrin than in wild-type mice.

These experiments measured clot and injury-induced thrombus characteristics; they do **not** establish that spontaneous thrombosis never occurs or exclude risk in other settings. So the results suggest restored, localized hemostasis—not an apparent excess thrombotic risk in the tested mice. **FINAL DECISION: NO**
