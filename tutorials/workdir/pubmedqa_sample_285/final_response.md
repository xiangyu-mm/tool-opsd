Yes. A Chinese post-hoc observational study (PMID: 27497684) found higher serum copeptin in participants with type 2 diabetes than in controls (9.4 vs 4.1 pmol/L; P<0.0001), with an adjusted association with diabetes (OR 1.312, 95% CI 1.204–1.403).

Among 306 participants with diabetes, higher copeptin was also associated with diabetic retinopathy (adjusted OR 1.117, 95% CI 1.072–1.241) and nephropathy (adjusted OR 1.259, 95% CI 1.198–1.323). The study did not establish associations with cardiovascular complications. Because the evidence identified is limited to one observational analysis, these findings indicate association, not causation.

FINAL DECISION: YES
