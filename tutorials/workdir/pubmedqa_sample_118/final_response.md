Yes—available evidence supports RFA as a thyroid-function-preserving treatment for patients with bilateral benign thyroid nodules, particularly when nodules are treated in separate sessions.

- In a bilateral-nodule study of 18 patients (PMID: **25446422**), serum thyroid hormone levels showed no significant change from baseline at a mean follow-up of 18.1 months. Nodule volume decreased by approximately 76%, with improvement in symptoms and cosmetic concerns.
- A later small case series evaluated bilateral RFA in a single session (PMID: **37667677**) and reported no complications, but it did not provide thyroid-function laboratory outcomes.
- Therefore, the strongest evidence supports preservation of thyroid function after staged bilateral RFA; evidence for same-session bilateral treatment remains limited. The available studies are small and do not establish that thyroid function is preserved in every patient.

FINAL DECISION: YES
