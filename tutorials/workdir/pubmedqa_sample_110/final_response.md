Evidence indicates that **rs3746804 in C20orf54/SLC52A3 is associated with ESCC susceptibility**:

- In a Chinese case-control study of **434 ESCC cases and 554 controls**, the CT genotype was less frequent among cases than controls.
- Compared with CC, CT was associated with reduced ESCC risk: **OR 0.630, 95% CI 0.481–0.826**.
- CT+TT versus CC also showed reduced risk: **OR 0.654, 95% CI 0.507–0.844**.
- The findings were reported in PMID **23327963** and PMID **25427582**, but these appear to represent the **same underlying cohort**, not independent replications.
- Although rs3746804 is annotated as a functional missense variant (**p.P267L**), the available reports do not demonstrate a specific molecular mechanism or experimental functional assay.

Thus, the variant appears to modify susceptibility, with the C/T genotype associated with lower ESCC risk, but the evidence is limited by lack of independent replication and incomplete functional validation.
