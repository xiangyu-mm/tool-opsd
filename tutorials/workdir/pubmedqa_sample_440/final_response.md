**No.** The most directly relevant study, a retrospective cohort of 837 men with nonmetastatic (M0/Mx) castration-resistant prostate cancer in five VA hospitals, found no significant difference between Black and non-Black men in development of metastases or bone metastases; race comparisons were nonsignificant (\(P>0.2\)). The authors concluded race was not prognostic once men had M0/Mx CRPC (PMID: 27505036).

A later real-world study of 406 men with nmCRPC starting apalutamide also reported descriptively similar metastasis-free outcomes for Black and non-Black patients, although it did not report an adjusted race effect estimate (PMID: 39267888). These findings do not establish that race independently predicts metastasis in this setting. The evidence is observational, so it supports “not shown to predict,” rather than proving there can never be a difference.
