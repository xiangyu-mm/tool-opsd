Yes. The 2014 study by Di et al. (Genome Medicine; PMID: 25452766) reconstructed an HIF-1-centered network in AML samples, including APL cases. The APL-specific subnetwork showed:

- Reduced expression of adhesion-related genes  
- Increased extracellular-matrix, motility, migration, and invasion programs  
- A transcriptional pattern resembling EMT-related biology  
- Reversal of this pattern after ATRA-induced differentiation of NB4 cells  

Thus, the HIF-1 network reveals **EMT-like characteristics in APL**. However, the evidence is primarily computational and transcriptional: it does not prove canonical EMT, epithelial-to-mesenchymal conversion, functional invasion, or direct causal dependence on HIF-1.

FINAL DECISION: YES
