**No.** Available comparative clinical evidence does not show that preserving the rectus femoris origin compromises acetabular reorientation during PAO.

- In a retrospective cohort of 75 patients (31 rectus-sparing, 44 rectus-takedown), postoperative radiographs showed no compromised correction; the authors also reported that fragment mobilization and final positioning were not impaired (**PMID: 25091227**).
- A separate retrospective matched study of 64 patients found similar improvement in lateral and anterior center-edge angles and Tönnis angle with rectus preservation versus the classic approach (**PMID: 25053288**).

**Caveat:** Both studies were retrospective, so the evidence supports comparable radiographic correction but does not establish equivalence as strongly as a prospective randomized comparison would.
