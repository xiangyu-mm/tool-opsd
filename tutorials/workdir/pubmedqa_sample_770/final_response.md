**Yes.** A directly relevant human study found significantly reduced B-cell numbers in pre-dialysis uremic and hemodialysis patients compared with healthy controls. When peripheral blood lymphocytes were cultured without stimulation, apoptosis increased—especially in B cells—and the proportion of apoptotic B cells correlated with the degree of B-cell lymphopenia. The apoptosis phenotype was accompanied by lower Bcl-2 expression (PMID: 10727545, *Nephrology Dialysis Transplantation*, 2000).

This supports a relationship between uremic B-cell lymphopenia, accelerated in-vitro apoptosis, and reduced anti-apoptotic Bcl-2. It does **not** prove that reduced Bcl-2 causes the lymphopenia: the study did not manipulate Bcl-2 or perform a rescue experiment. The reported finding concerns Bcl-2; it does not establish dysregulation across the broader Bcl-2 family.

FINAL DECISION: YES
