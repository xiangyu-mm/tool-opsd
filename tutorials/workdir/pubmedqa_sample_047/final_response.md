Yes. The directly relevant study (PMID:25449986) transplanted blotchy or control marrow into irradiated LDLR−/− mice, followed by angiotensin II infusion (1,000 ng/kg/min for 4 weeks).

- **Blood copper:** No significant differences in plasma or hematopoietic-cell copper; blotchy marrow was dispensable.
- **Aortic pathology:** No significant differences in aortic diameters or rupture incidence (rupture comparison, P=0.10); marrow was dispensable overall.
- **Inflammatory mediators:** Blotchy marrow recipients had significantly lower plasma MIP-1β, MCP-1, MCP-3, MCP-5, TIMP-1, and VEGF-A (P<0.05), demonstrating a marrow-dependent effect.

The wording “required for inflammatory mediator production” should be interpreted cautiously: blotchy marrow contributed to the altered/reduced mediator production rather than being required for mediator production in an absolute sense.

FINAL DECISION: YES
