**Yes—in a mouse model.** In the study, 51 mice with acute myocardial injury began continuous apelin-13 infusion one week after injury (0.5 μg/g/day for four weeks). Compared with saline, apelin-13 was associated with reduced MRI-estimated peri-infarct volume (15.1% to 7.4%) and scar volume (34.3% to 25.1%). LVEF changed from 21.9% to 23.1% with apelin-13, versus 23.5% to 16.9% with saline (p=0.02); the reported advantage persisted after infusion stopped.

This supports peri-infarct salvage and preservation of function **in this animal experiment**, not established efficacy in people. **Source:** *Apelin-13 infusion salvages the peri-infarct region to preserve cardiac function after severe myocardial injury.* PMID: 27500765; DOI: 10.1016/j.ijcard.2016.07.263.
