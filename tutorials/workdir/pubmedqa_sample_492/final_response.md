**Yes.** A published case report documents a 24-year-old endodontist who sustained a chemical burn to her left eye while using an operating microscope during root-canal treatment. A pressurized irrigation cannula burst, exposing her eye directly to **3.5% sodium hypochlorite**. A corneal ulcer was diagnosed eight days later; her vision recovered without sequelae by four weeks.

The report shows that an operating microscope does not prevent this kind of splash injury. It is a single case report, not evidence of how often it occurs.  
Source: *Journal of Endodontics* (2014), “Sodium hypochlorite chemical burn in an endodontist's eye during canal treatment using operating microscope,” PMID 25069948; DOI: 10.1016/j.joen.2014.01.026.
