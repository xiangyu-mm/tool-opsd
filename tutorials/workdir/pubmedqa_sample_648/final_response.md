**Yes.** A 2014 study in rheumatoid arthritis (RA) patient samples and cell models reported that methotrexate (MTX) induces lincRNA-p21 and reduces NF-κB activity. In cell experiments, MTX’s reduction of TNFα-stimulated NF-κB activity was reported to depend on DNA-PKcs and lincRNA-p21; lower lincRNA-p21 also increased NF-κB activity. The patient-sample comparisons are associative, while the cell experiments provide mechanistic support. This finding is specific to the studied RA-related contexts and does not by itself establish the pathway in every disease or tissue.

Source: PMID **25077978**, “Methotrexate inhibits NF-κB activity via long intergenic (noncoding) RNA-p21 induction” (*Arthritis & Rheumatology*, 2014).
