The most likely diagnosis is **Ménière disease**, suggested by episodic vertigo, nausea, horizontal nystagmus, and progressive unilateral hearing loss. Unlike BPPV, Ménière disease causes auditory symptoms.

Initial treatment is conservative lifestyle modification, especially **dietary sodium restriction**. Diuretics or betahistine may be added, while intratympanic gentamicin and surgery are reserved for refractory disease.
