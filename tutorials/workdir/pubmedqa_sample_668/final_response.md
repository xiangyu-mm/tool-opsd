**Yes—approximately.** A prospective cohort study of elite male footballers from 46 teams in 10 European countries found that concussion was associated with a **47% higher hazard of subsequent injury during the following year** after adjustment for the number of injuries in the preceding year (HR 1.47, 95% CI 1.05–2.05). The study recorded 66 concussions. This supports “about 50%,” but the estimate is a **hazard ratio**, not a claim that every player’s absolute probability of injury rises by 50%. The comparison was with injury risk following other injuries.
