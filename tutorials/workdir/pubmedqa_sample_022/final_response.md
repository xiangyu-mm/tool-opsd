Yes. Saikosaponin B2 is a naturally occurring oleanane-type pentacyclic triterpenoid saponin found in *Bupleurum* species.

Primary evidence (Lin et al., *Journal of Hepatology*, 2015; PMID: 25450204) directly demonstrated inhibition of HCV entry: it neutralized HCV particles, reduced viral attachment, inhibited entry/fusion and E2-mediated binding, and blocked infection of primary human hepatocytes. A 2019 study (PMID: 30920421) provided additional support.

The available abstract describes the inhibition as potent and non-cytotoxic, although it does not report a numerical entry IC₅₀ or EC₅₀. Thus, “efficiently” is supported qualitatively, not by a retrievable quantitative potency value.

FINAL DECISION: YES
