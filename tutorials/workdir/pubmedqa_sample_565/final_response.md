A 2014 study of 312 patients with rheumatoid arthritis found that patients carrying the full-length KIR2DS4 gene were less likely to respond to methotrexate than KIR2DS4f-negative patients, using DAS28 criteria. The association was strongest in patients with intermediate ESR (20–33 mm/h); the authors reported no effect in the low- or high-ESR groups.

This supports a reduced chance of response, but it is an association from one cohort, not proof that the gene causes treatment failure. The abstract does not provide an effect size. **Source:** PMID 25069714; DOI: 10.1186/1471-2474-15-256.
