- **Study:** *Serum interleukin-10 but not interleukin-6 is related to clinical outcome in patients with resectable hepatocellular carcinoma* (Annals of Surgery, 2000; **PMID: 10749617**).
- **Population:** 67 patients undergoing potentially curative hepatic resection for HCC.
- **IL-10:** High preoperative serum IL-10 was associated with worse disease-free survival and remained an independent predictor of post-resection outcome in multivariate analysis.
- **IL-6:** Although serum IL-6 levels changed after resection, preoperative IL-6 was not significantly associated with disease-free survival.

Thus, in this cohort, serum IL-10—but not serum IL-6—was related to clinical outcome. The available abstract does not provide the exact hazard ratio or *P* value.
