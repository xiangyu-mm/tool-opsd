Yes—available direct evidence indicates that indirect moxibustion decreases renal arterial resistive index (RI), at least transiently:

- **Healthy adults (PMID: 25077574):** RI decreased from **0.578** at baseline to **0.546 immediately after** treatment and **0.547 at 10 minutes**; both reductions were statistically significant.
- **Patients with chronic kidney disease (PMID: 26983055):** RI decreased from **0.590** to **0.565 immediately after** and **0.561 at 10 minutes**; the reduction was significantly greater than with the control condition (**p=0.001**).
- A further CKD study (PMID: 30585470) also reported significantly reduced renal RI after moxibustion.

These studies assessed **short-term Doppler ultrasound changes** after treatment; they do not establish sustained reductions or improved long-term kidney outcomes.

FINAL DECISION: YES
