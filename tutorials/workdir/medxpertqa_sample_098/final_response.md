The presentation is classic for **rhino-orbital-cerebral mucormycosis**, given poorly controlled diabetes/HIV, sinus involvement, and black eschar from angioinvasive tissue necrosis.

A severe complication is extension into the **orbit or cavernous sinus**, which can involve cranial nerves controlling eye movement. Therefore, **impaired extraocular movements**—ophthalmoplegia—is the best answer. Altered mental status would suggest even further cerebral extension, but is less characteristic of the immediate orbital/cavernous-sinus complication being tested.
