Yes. In a preclinical study, adding dasatinib to cetuximab plus fractionated radiotherapy **worsened tumour control** in both FaDu- and A431-derived xenografts. The regimen included 30 Gy in 10 fractions; the triple combination increased tumour growth, DNA synthesis, and angiogenesis compared with cetuximab plus radiotherapy.

**Source:** Baro et al., *British Journal of Cancer* (2014), PMID: 25077442; DOI: 10.1038/bjc.2014.432. This is evidence from xenograft models, not a clinical treatment recommendation.
