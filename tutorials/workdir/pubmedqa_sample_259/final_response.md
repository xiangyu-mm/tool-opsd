Yes. The directly relevant study (Heinonen et al., 2017; PMID: 27491313) found that the **COMT Val/Val genotype**—the high-activity form of the Val158Met polymorphism—was associated with multiple leiomyomas:

- **Multiple vs solitary leiomyomas:** OR 2.61, 95% CI 1.09–6.24, *p* = 0.028  
- **Multiple leiomyomas vs controls:** OR 2.91, 95% CI 1.20–7.06, *p* = 0.016  

The association was reported for patients whose tumors were **MED12 exon 2 mutation-positive and those whose tumors were MED12-negative**. However, the study did not demonstrate a statistically significant difference in COMT genotype distribution by MED12 status itself (OR 0.77, 95% CI 0.23–2.53, *p* = 0.662). Thus, MED12 status did not appear to modify the association, although the subgroup sizes were small.

Importantly, this finding concerns **multiplicity**, not leiomyoma risk overall: the association with all leiomyomas was not statistically significant in that study, and broader COMT–leiomyoma studies have yielded inconsistent results.

FINAL DECISION: YES
