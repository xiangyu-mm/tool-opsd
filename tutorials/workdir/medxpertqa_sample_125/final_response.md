The diagnosis is **disseminated *Taenia solium* cysticercosis**, involving the brain (neurocysticercosis) and eye.

- **Seizures plus multiple ring-enhancing parenchymal lesions** are classic for neurocysticercosis.
- **Subretinal cysts** indicate ocular cysticercosis.
- **CSF eosinophilia** supports a helminthic infection.
- HIV-negative status makes cerebral toxoplasmosis less likely.
- Importantly, cysticercosis results from ingesting ***T. solium* eggs** via fecal contamination from a human tapeworm carrier. Eating undercooked pork causes intestinal taeniasis, not tissue cysticercosis.
