**Yes.** The 2017 study (PMID 27499270) followed 110 patients with paroxysmal AF after second-generation cryoballoon ablation; 20 (18%) had recurrence over a mean of 9 months. Recurrence rose across lower eGFR categories: 7% with eGFR ≥90, 12% with eGFR 60–89.9, and 46% with eGFR 30–59.9 mL/min/1.73 m². Higher eGFR was associated with lower recurrence hazard (HR 0.97, 95% CI 0.93–0.99; p=0.047). [Source: `call_7zxwgWaMEEajIlD90iKFRMEB`]

Non-pulmonary-vein ectopic beats were also associated with recurrence (HR 2.92, 95% CI 1.03–8.27) and were more common at lower eGFR.
