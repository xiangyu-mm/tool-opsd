**Yes, in the study found.** In a historical prospective safety-net cohort of 1,240 females aged 10–26 who had received at least one HPV4 dose, on-time completion of all three doses was less common among those with class II/III obesity than among those with normal BMI: **18% vs 30%**. The odds of timely completion were lower for the class II/III obesity group (**OR 0.55, 95% CI 0.37–0.83**).

This supports poorer HPV4 adherence among participants with severe obesity, but it is observational and does not establish that adherence declines steadily with every increase in BMI.
