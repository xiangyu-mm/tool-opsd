Yes. In NK1R-knockout mice, atomoxetine reduced hyperactivity at 3 mg/kg in the light/dark exploration box and impulsivity at 10 mg/kg in the 5-choice serial reaction-time task. It did not affect attention, and the reported behavioral reductions were not seen in wild-type mice.
