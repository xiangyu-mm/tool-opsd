Yes. The direct evidence comes from a structural-MRI voxel-based morphometry study of 233 HIV-positive participants (PMID: 27497320). Participants with HIV-associated distal neuropathic pain had smaller **left ventral posterior cingulate cortex gray-matter volume** than those without pain:

- Peak: **P = 0.017**
- **t = 5.15**
- MNI coordinates: **x = −6, y = −54, z = 20**
- Greater pain intensity was also associated with smaller volumes in pain-processing cortical regions.

A later diffusion-MRI study found neuropathy-related white-matter abnormalities but did not independently test ventral posterior cingulate gray-matter volume. Thus, the evidence supports an **observational association**, not causation.

FINAL DECISION: YES
