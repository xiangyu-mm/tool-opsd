The study **did not find an association** between IFNL3 genotype and long-term clinical outcomes in patients with HCV-related compensated cirrhosis.

- **Study:** Savino et al., *Antiviral Research* (2015), PMID: **25446338**
- **Population:** 264 patients with compensated HCV cirrhosis
- **Median follow-up:** 14.8 years
- **Outcomes:** Decompensation, hepatocellular carcinoma, liver transplantation, death, or end of follow-up
- **Finding:** IFNL3 genotype was **not associated with disease progression** in either univariate or multivariate analyses.
- **Important distinction:** The CC genotype strongly predicted interferon treatment response/SVR (40% vs 10% for CT and 13% for TT; **p<0.0001**), but it did **not** predict long-term clinical outcome.
