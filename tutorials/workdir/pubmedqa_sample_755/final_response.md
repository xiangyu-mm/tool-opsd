**Yes—with an important qualification.** A study of peripheral-blood monocytes from SLE patients reported significantly lower CD14 and HLA-DR expression and higher TNF-α secretion than in healthy-donor monocytes. In a three-day culture, GM-CSF plus IL-4 reduced TNF-α secretion but did not produce monocyte-derived dendritic cells, suggesting **limited differentiation flexibility toward dendritic cells under those conditions**.

This supports markedly altered phenotype and function, and restricted flexibility in the specific assay. It does **not** by itself show broad lineage switching or establish how common or severe these changes are across all people with SLE; the abstract gives no effect sizes or sample size.

**Source:** Steinbach et al., *Annals of the Rheumatic Diseases* (2000), PMID: [10733475](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10733475/).

FINAL DECISION: YES
