**Conclusion:** Available direct evidence does **not demonstrate an increased AKI risk** from short-course prophylactic gentamicin for open fractures. In one retrospective comparison, AKI occurred in **4.0%** of patients receiving cefazolin plus gentamicin versus **4.8%** with cefazolin alone (p=0.599; tool evidence `call_6Zeg3FlYpYigUc3hiFej3lYH`). Trauma-related factors—including hypotension, injury severity, ICU admission, and contrast exposure—can also contribute to AKI.
