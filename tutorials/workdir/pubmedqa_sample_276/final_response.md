### Evidence summary

Two retrospective comparative studies evaluated mediastinal omental transposition during open Ivor-Lewis esophagectomy:

- **Infection:** Both studies found fewer or less severe intrathoracic infections with omental transposition.
  - 2016 cohort: **30.6% vs 48.3%** infection, *P* = 0.009; infection severity was also lower, *P* = 0.005.
  - 2014 cohort: **39.3% vs 56.2%** infection; among infected patients, combined sepsis was **57.6% vs 86.1%**, *P* < 0.05.
- **Pharmacy cost:** The 2016 study reported lower pharmacy costs: **¥21,668 vs ¥27,012**, *P* = 0.010.
- **Limitations:** Both studies were retrospective, single-center cohorts. Pharmacy-cost reduction was demonstrated in only one study, and **total hospital cost was not significantly reduced**.

Thus, the available evidence supports reductions in postoperative intrathoracic infection severity and pharmacy cost, but the evidence is observational and not definitive proof of causality.
