**Qualified answer: Yes—on several measured PCOS-related outcomes, but not across the board.** In a small randomized, placebo-controlled pilot trial of women with PCOS undergoing ICSI, metformin plus NAC did **not** significantly reduce LH, total testosterone, cholesterol, or triglycerides compared with placebo. It did reduce malondialdehyde, insulin, and leptin, so it would be inaccurate to say the combination had no effects at all. The trial randomized 80 participants, with 20 dropping out, which also limits confidence in the findings (PMID: 25083175).

A related ICSI trial reported improvements in oocyte and embryo quality with **NAC alone**; that result should not be taken as evidence that the combination improves those outcomes (PMID: 25482371).

**Bottom line:** The claim is supported in the limited sense that the combination failed to improve several measured hormonal and lipid markers. The pilot evidence does not establish that it fails to improve every manifestation or clinical outcome.
