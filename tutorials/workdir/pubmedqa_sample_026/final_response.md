**Conclusion:** The available Korle-Bu study does **not** support human enteroviruses as the cause of the neurological problems studied. Owusu et al. examined 82 children with fever-associated convulsions or acute encephalopathy. Enterovirus isolation was reported at about 0.67%; the identified poliovirus was normal Sabin vaccine virus. The authors concluded that non-polio enteroviruses were not the cause of neurological morbidity in that cohort (PMID: [25426190](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25426190/)).

This is evidence about that particular cohort, not proof that enteroviruses never cause neurological disease in children. The study was limited in size and did not report a separate control group.
