Yes. Dutilh et al. analyzed **274 complete *Vibrio cholerae* genomes** using a microbial genome-wide association approach (GWAS-M), incorporating genomic features such as SNPs, protein families, functions, and prophages.

The three niche dimensions were **space, time, and habitat**. The authors reported that genomic variation—particularly mobile functions, including **phages, prophages, transposable elements, and plasmids**—underlay the observed metadata structuring across those dimensions. They interpreted this as contributing to local endemicity, temporal divergence, and invasion of new habitats.

**Source:** Dutilh BE et al. “Comparative genomics of 274 *Vibrio cholerae* genomes reveals mobile functions structuring three niche dimensions.” *BMC Genomics*. 2014;15:654. PMID: 25096633. DOI: 10.1186/1471-2164-15-654.
