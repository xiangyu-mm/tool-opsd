Yes. In the PARIS prospective birth cohort, allergen-specific IgE was measured at 18 months and 1,525 infants were grouped into sensitization profiles. The polysensitized profile comprised 1.6% of infants. At age 2, allergic multimorbidity increased across profiles of greater sensitization severity (p-trend <0.001); at age 6, multimorbidity was also more frequent in the more sensitized profiles.

This supports an association, not proof that polysensitization causes multimorbidity. The abstract does not report an effect size or adjusted odds ratio.

**Source:** Gabet et al., “Early polysensitization is associated with allergic multimorbidity in PARIS birth cohort infants,” PMID: 27501330.
