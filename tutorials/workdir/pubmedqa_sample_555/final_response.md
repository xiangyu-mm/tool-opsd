Yes—in the study directly addressing this question, the benefit of starting soy after menopause depended on pretreatment plaque burden. In a monkey model, soy begun after ovariectomy reduced iliac-artery atherosclerosis progression and coronary plaque burden **only in animals whose plaques were below the median size at treatment initiation** (P=.038 and P=.0001, respectively); animals with larger plaques were not protected. Soy fed both before and after ovariectomy also reduced coronary atherosclerosis versus control (P=.008).

This is evidence from a nonhuman-primate model, so it does not establish that the same stage-dependent effect occurs in postmenopausal women. PMID: 25072952.

FINAL DECISION: YES
