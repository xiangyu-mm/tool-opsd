**The study reports that HEK-conditioned medium reversed or prevented kainic-acid-induced hippocampal damage.** In adult CF1 mice, it was infused intravenously on days 3 and 5 after kainic acid; later histology was reported as comparable to normal controls, with improved behavioral measures and increased EPO and Bcl-2 mRNA (PMID: 25442789).

There is an important qualification: although the title says “reverses,” the paper’s conclusion describes prevention, and the reported design does not establish that neuronal loss was measured before treatment and then reversed. So the evidence supports a beneficial post-insult effect and apparent restoration of hippocampal structure, but not definitive reversal of documented, established damage.

FINAL DECISION: YES
