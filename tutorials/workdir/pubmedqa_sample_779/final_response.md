**Yes.** In anesthetized pigs subjected to graded hemorrhage, the selective angiotensin II type 1 (AT1) blocker **candesartan** was associated with better mesenteric and jejunal mucosal perfusion than control. At 40% blood-volume hemorrhage, the study reported mesenteric blood flow **221% higher** and jejunal mucosal perfusion **244% higher** than control values. A second porcine study also found preserved mesenteric and jejunal mucosal perfusion when candesartan was given after hypovolemia had begun.

This is evidence from acute animal experiments, not proof of benefit in people. The accessible abstracts do not report the candesartan dose.

FINAL DECISION: YES
