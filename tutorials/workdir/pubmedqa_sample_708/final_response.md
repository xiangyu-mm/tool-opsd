Luo and Zhao treated **10 patients (14 knees)** and followed them for a mean of **16.3 months** (range 12–25). They reported:

- Mean Lysholm score improved from **19.5 to 84.6**.
- **13 of 14 knees** had good or excellent Knee Society scores; one was fair.
- Functional results were better in patients operated on early (within two weeks) and in younger patients.
