**Yes—with a caveat about causality.** In rat and human alveolar epithelial cell models, hyperoxia reduced LAT1 expression and LAT-system transport alongside reduced exogenous-NO-associated S-nitrosothiol accumulation and soluble guanylyl cyclase activation (PMID: 25089378). A supporting study found that LAT transport contributes to cellular uptake of NO-related S-nitrosothiols (PMID: 18441097).

Together, these findings implicate reduced LAT1 function as a mechanism by which hyperoxia inhibits NO effects. However, the retrieved evidence does not establish LAT1 as the sole or genetically proven mediator; a separate study reporting hyperoxia–NO cytotoxicity did not test LAT1 (PMID: 9530177).
