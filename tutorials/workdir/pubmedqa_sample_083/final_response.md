**Yes.** A 2014 primary study reported that pPLAIIIδ influences cell morphology and organ growth in **both Arabidopsis and Brassica napus**. Overexpression inhibited longitudinal growth while promoting transverse growth in most organs. In Arabidopsis, knockout plants had greater hypocotyl cell elongation, while overexpression broadened hypocotyl cell growth and reduced leaf pavement-cell polarity.

The study also found that altered pPLAIIIδ expression disturbed endogenous auxin distribution and changed sensitivity to IAA-promoted hypocotyl elongation. Auxin-induced genes were more highly expressed in overexpression plants under the reported treatments. These results support an influence on auxin-responsive morphology and organ size, though they do not establish that every phenotype is caused exclusively by auxin.

**Source:** Dong et al., *BMC Plant Biology* (2014), PMID: 25428555; DOI: 10.1186/s12870-014-0332-1.
