**Yes—available evidence supports that WNT2 promotes cervical carcinoma metastatic behavior and EMT.** A 2016 *PLOS ONE* study found that high WNT2 expression was associated with pelvic lymph-node metastasis in cervical cancer specimens. In SiHa cervical cancer cells, WNT2 knockdown reduced motility and invasion and reversed EMT, with effects linked to the WNT2/β-catenin pathway.

The evidence supports this role, but the clinical association alone does not prove that WNT2 causes metastasis in patients; the direct perturbation evidence described was cell-based.

**Reference:** “WNT2 Promotes Cervical Carcinoma Metastasis and Induction of Epithelial-Mesenchymal Transition.” *PLOS ONE* (2016). PMID: 27513465. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0160414
