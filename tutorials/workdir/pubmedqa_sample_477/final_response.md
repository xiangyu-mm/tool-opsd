**Yes.** The in-vitro study in human metastatic melanoma cell lines A375 and 518a2 supports autocrine 15d-PGJ2 as a mediator of simvastatin-induced apoptosis. Simvastatin induced p38- and COX-2-dependent 15d-PGJ2 synthesis, and 15d-PGJ2 accumulated in the culture medium at levels reported to activate caspase-8 and mitochondrial apoptosis. Crucially, inhibiting lipocalin-type PGD synthase, an enzyme required for 15d-PGJ2 synthesis, abolished simvastatin’s apoptotic effect—evidence for necessity, not merely association.

This conclusion is limited to the reported cell models and in-vitro experiments; it does not establish clinical efficacy in patients. The paper also reports that primary human melanocytes were not affected under the tested conditions.
