**Conclusion:** Yes. Direct promoter-reporter experiments support ITF/TFF3 increasing transcriptional activity of its own promoter. In the 2016 study, STAT3 involvement was supported by promoter-site mutation, STAT3 occupancy at the promoter, and STAT3 gain/loss experiments (PMID: 27453253). A second study reported that AG490 reduced ITF-stimulated promoter-reporter activity (PMID: 25432365), consistent with a JAK/STAT contribution.

The qualification is that the evidence for STAT3-dependent promoter regulation is stronger than the evidence specifically proving JAK-mediated STAT3 activation: the retrieved studies do not fully establish that biochemical step, and AG490 alone is not definitive proof of pathway specificity.
