**Yes—in the tested preclinical models, pharmacological autophagy modulation enhanced NDV/FMW-mediated antitumor activity, but the effective direction depended on the resistance phenotype.** In cisplatin-resistant A549/DDP cells, autophagy inhibition with chloroquine enhanced NDV activity; in paclitaxel-resistant A549/PTX cells, the autophagy inducer rapamycin enhanced it. The study also reported potentiation in mice bearing the corresponding resistant tumors. Autophagy modulation did not increase viral progeny, so the enhanced effect was not attributed to greater virus yield. (PMID: 25078870; search evidence: `call_C8Z2exHr0h8vYyrHz8PrAYCt`, `call_dsnGNHcAV8mm04rrW15jkkQk`.)

This is preclinical evidence from specific resistant lung cancer models, not proof of clinical benefit or a universal rule about whether autophagy should be inhibited or induced.
