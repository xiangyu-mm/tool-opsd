Yes. In a 2015 in-vitro study, vitamin D attenuated aPL-induced inflammatory responses in both HTR8 human first-trimester trophoblast cells and primary trophoblast cultures. It also significantly inhibited LMWH-induced sFlt-1 release.

The result is limited to cell-culture experiments: it does not show that vitamin D reverses these effects or improves outcomes in pregnant patients. The study also found vitamin D did not alter aPL-mediated changes in angiogenic factors in primary trophoblasts.

**Study:** Gysler SM et al. “Vitamin D reverses aPL-induced inflammation and LMWH-induced sFlt-1 release by human trophoblast.” 2015. PMID: 25070806; DOI: 10.1111/aji.12301.

FINAL DECISION: YES
