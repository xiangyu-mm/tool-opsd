Yes—based on the directly relevant study (PMID:25074630), elective surgeon changes increased mean OR turnover time from **43.6 to 51.0 minutes**, a statistically significant **7.4-minute increase** (95% CI, 6.8–8.1; P<0.0001).

However, the authors found that this added time was **far less than one hour per OR day** and did not allow an additional elective case even if eliminated. The effect varied by service, procedure type, time of day, and turnover frequency. Thus, the impact is not literally zero, but it was operationally negligible in that institution’s scheduling context.

FINAL DECISION: YES
