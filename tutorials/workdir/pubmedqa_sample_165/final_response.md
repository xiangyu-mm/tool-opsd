Yes. The exact publication is:

- **Title:** *First registry results from the newly approved ACURATE TA™ TAVI system*
- **PMID:** 25425552
- **DOI:** 10.1093/ejcts/ezu367
- **Journal/year:** *European Journal of Cardio-Thoracic Surgery*, 2015

It reported the first **250 post-CE-mark patients** from a 17-center, multicountry registry. Procedural success was **98%**, 30-day mortality **6.8%**, stroke **2.8%**, moderate paravalvular leak **2.3%**, and new pacemaker implantation **10.0%**. The authors characterized the short-term safety and efficacy as comparable to, or better in some respects than, existing TAVI devices.
