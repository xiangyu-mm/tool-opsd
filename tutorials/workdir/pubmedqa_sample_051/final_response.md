Yes—available evidence indicates that *Pseudomonas aeruginosa* is found predominantly in household drains in both CF and non-CF homes.

The key comparative study (PMID: 25443472) sampled 11,674 household environmental sites across 7 CF homes and 8 non-CF homes. *P. aeruginosa* was recovered from 28% of sampled drains at least once, and modeled recovery from drain samples was 6.3%—more than eightfold higher than from any other household-environment category. A review (PMID: 32684992) similarly identifies shower drains, kitchen and bathroom hand-basin drainpipes, and toilet drainpipes as common household sources.

Important limitation: the published summary does not report separate drain-positivity rates for CF versus non-CF homes, nor does it rank sink, shower, and toilet drains individually. Thus, the evidence supports drain predominance overall and across the combined CF/non-CF comparison, but not a precise comparison between the two household groups.

FINAL DECISION: YES
