The primary study (PMID: 10727665) found that the estrogen receptor-alpha (ESR1/ERα) promoter was apparently unmethylated in normal, in situ human aortic smooth muscle cells but became methylated after explantation and proliferation in culture. Southern blot, COBRA, and RLGS-M analyses supported a selective locus-level methylation change rather than a generalized increase in methylation.

However, “selective” referred to particular genomic loci; the study did not establish specificity compared with other cell types.

FINAL DECISION: YES
