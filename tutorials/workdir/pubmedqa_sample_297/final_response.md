In a prospective cohort of 115 adults with decompensated cirrhosis undergoing transplant evaluation, 24-hour urinary sodium was independently inversely associated with Doppler-defined diastolic dysfunction (OR 0.98, 95% CI 0.97–0.99; p=0.004; PMID 27493103). Diastolic dysfunction was present in 66 patients (57.3%). The available report does not specify the odds-ratio scaling or a urinary sodium cutoff.

This supports an association between diastolic dysfunction and lower urinary sodium excretion, but the evidence identified is from one cohort and does not establish causality or a mechanism. A separate cohort did not assess urinary sodium, so it does not replicate the association.
