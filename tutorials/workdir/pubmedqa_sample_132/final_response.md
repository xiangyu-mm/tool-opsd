Yes. Lowe et al. (2015, PMID: 25450500) found that modifying ovalbumin protocols in guinea-pigs restored or enhanced both functional and inflammatory responses:

- Increasing challenge concentration restored airway hyperresponsiveness, increased the early asthmatic response, and restored eosinophilia.
- Increasing sensitisation strength increased total inflammatory cells, particularly eosinophils.
- Increasing adjuvant concentration or extending the sensitisation–challenge interval revealed a late asthmatic response and increased lymphocytes and eosinophils.

However, the responses were partly dissociated: no single adjustment restored every endpoint, and early, late, functional, and inflammatory responses did not always change together.

FINAL DECISION: YES
