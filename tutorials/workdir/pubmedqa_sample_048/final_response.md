The directly relevant retrospective cohort (PMID: 25424123) found that men with **less advanced disease at salvage ADT initiation**—defined mainly as **PSA doubling time >3 months and no distant metastases**—had lower subsequent mortality after biochemical failure following salvage radiotherapy.

Compared with men with more advanced disease, the more-advanced group had:

- **Prostate-cancer-specific mortality:** HR 2.8 (95% CI 1.4–5.6; p=0.005)
- **Overall mortality:** HR 1.9 (95% CI 1.0–3.5; p=0.04)

A multivariable analysis similarly found that PSADT <3 months predicted prostate-cancer-specific mortality (HR 4.2) and overall mortality (HR 2.9).

The evidence is observational and supports a **prognostic association**, not proof that starting ADT earlier causes improved survival. The key markers of advanced disease were rapid PSA doubling and distant metastasis, rather than clinical T stage.

FINAL DECISION: YES
