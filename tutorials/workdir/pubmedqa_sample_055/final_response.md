**No evidence retrieved supports the claim that cenicriviroc redistributes HIV from intracellular compartments into extracellular plasma.** The studies reviewed do not establish a CVC-caused plasma HIV-RNA increase paired with an intracellular HIV-DNA decline.

- In an open-label 24-week study of people with HIV on stable ART and undetectable plasma HIV RNA (<50 copies/mL), the reported CVC-associated changes were in fibrosis biomarkers—not viral redistribution or intracellular HIV DNA (PMID: 32013805).
- In an in-vitro blood–brain barrier model, CVC reduced transmigration of HIV-bearing monocytes. That finding concerns cell trafficking, not release of virus into plasma (PMID: 33727362).

Thus, redistribution remains **un demonstrated by the evidence reviewed**; these findings do not prove it is impossible, but they do not support using it to explain plasma viral-load or intracellular-DNA changes.

FINAL DECISION: NO
