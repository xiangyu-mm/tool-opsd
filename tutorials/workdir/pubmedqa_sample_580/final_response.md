Yes. The primary study (PMID:25071375) found that sirolimus induction monotherapy in nephrotoxic serum nephritis rats produced significant benefits in several renal outcomes:

- Reduced kidney enlargement by 41% and improved creatinine clearance by 50%.
- Reduced glomerular cell proliferation by 53% and tubulointerstitial proliferation by 70%.
- Reduced segmental fibrinoid necrosis by 69%, glomerular tuft enlargement, IgG deposition, and periglomerular myofibroblast accumulation.
- Attenuated tubulointerstitial injury markers, including ED-1-positive cells, α-smooth-muscle-actin-positive cells, and tubular vimentin expression.

However, the benefits were selective rather than comprehensive: proteinuria, crescent formation, and macrophage accumulation were not significantly improved. Thus, the evidence supports the stated claim, particularly for tubulointerstitial injury.

FINAL DECISION: YES
