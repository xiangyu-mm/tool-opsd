**Yes—HDL-C appears to modify the association between CRP and coronary artery calcium (CAC).** In a study of 12,030 Korean men, the highest versus lowest CRP group was associated with higher odds of CAC>0 in the lowest HDL-C quartile (OR 1.41, 95% CI 1.04–1.90; P=0.027), but not in the highest HDL-C quartile (OR 0.80, 95% CI 0.46–1.39; P=0.425). The study concluded that the CRP–CAC association differed by HDL-C level.

**Qualification:** The available abstract does not report the formal CRP×HDL-C interaction P value, so these subgroup results support—but do not independently establish statistically significant interaction. This evidence is from one occupational cohort of men and may not generalize to other populations.
