Yes. A qualitative study directly examined youth perceptions of comprehensive adolescent health services through the Boston HAPPENS program:

- **Study:** Rosenfeld et al., “Youth perceptions of comprehensive adolescent health services through the Boston HAPPENS program.”
- **Citation:** *Journal of Pediatric Health Care*, 2000; PMID: **10736140**
- **Participants:** 18 youth aged 12–24 years, interviewed in four focus groups.
- **Services assessed:** Medical care; mental-health and substance-abuse care; HIV prevention and care; case management; and financial assistance.
- **Findings:** Youth generally felt comfortable receiving care and appreciated the program’s comprehensive services. They also recommended more individualized mental-health counseling and additional recreational and peer-support opportunities, particularly for youth living with HIV.

Thus, the evidence directly supports that youth perceptions of Boston HAPPENS’ comprehensive services were studied and were generally positive, with specific areas identified for improvement.

FINAL DECISION: YES
