**Yes.** The study found that HIF-2α contributes to the suppression of sprout formation under prolonged hypoxia. Human microvascular endothelial cells cultured at **1% oxygen for 14 days** did not sprout into 3D fibrin matrices after VEGF-A/TNFα stimulation. **Silencing HIF-2α partially restored sprouting**; silencing HIF-1α or HIF-3α did not. The rescue was partial, so HIF-2α was not the only factor involved; reduced uPA also contributed to impaired sprouting.

Evidence: Nauta et al., *PLoS One* (2016), PMID **27490118**, DOI [10.1371/journal.pone.0160700](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0160700). PubMed lists a correction as PMID 27657501.
