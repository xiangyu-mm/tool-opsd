**Yes.** In a retrospective study of 190 peritoneal dialysis patients, chest-X-ray aortic arch calcification grades 2–3 were associated with higher all-cause mortality after adjustment for age, sex, dialysis duration, diabetes, blood pressure, and prior cardiovascular disease. Compared with grade 0, the adjusted hazard ratios were **2.74** (95% CI 1.04–7.21) for grade 2 and **3.29** (95% CI 1.16–9.36) for grade 3. Grades 2–3 were also associated with cardiovascular mortality, although that estimate was very imprecise (HR 36.05; 95% CI 3.49–372).

This supports severe aortic arch calcification as a **mortality risk marker** in PD patients. The conclusion rests on a single relatively small cohort, so it does not establish causation or guarantee predictive performance in other populations. Methodological commentaries on the study were also published.

**Key source:** PMID 27497908, *Severe aortic arch calcification predicts mortality in patients undergoing peritoneal dialysis*.
