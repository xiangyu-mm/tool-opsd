A Chinese case–control study of 300 acute anterior uveitis (AAU) patients and 300 healthy controls reported **CFI rs7356506** as protective: the authors reported an odds ratio of 0.684 for the A allele (95% CI 0.534–0.876; corrected *p*=0.03) and 0.624 for AA homozygosity (95% CI 0.452–0.862; corrected *p*=0.04). The association was also reported in analyses stratified by sex and HLA-B27 status.

This supports a protective **association** in the studied Chinese cohort; it does not establish causality or by itself confirm the result across populations. (Wang et al., *Br J Ophthalmol*, 2014; PMID: 25075123; DOI: 10.1136/bjophthalmol-2014-305296.)
