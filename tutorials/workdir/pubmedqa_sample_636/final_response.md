**Yes—there is a published Korean adult reference estimate**, though it is provisional rather than a broadly validated laboratory reference interval.

A 2014 study of healthy, nonasthmatic Korean adults reported an **upper 90th percentile of 3.5%** for induced-sputum eosinophils (PMID: 25097850). Of 208 adults recruited, only 81 had adequate samples for analysis; 107 did not produce sputum and 20 samples were inadequate. The reported values also varied with atopy: median 1.6% (range 0–11.0%) in atopic participants versus 0% (range 0–3.6%) in non-atopic participants. The **3.5% value is a healthy-population upper-percentile estimate**, not necessarily a complete or universally transferable reference interval. Available study details do not establish whether the percentage denominator was explicitly restricted to nonsquamous cells. Clinical thresholds such as **>3%** or **≥2%** used in Korean asthma studies are disease-phenotyping or biomarker cutoffs, not normal reference limits.
