Yes. In a randomized, double-blind study of 200 outpatients having day-case diagnostic knee arthroscopy under spinal anesthesia, **50 mg oral diclofenac given one hour before surgery reduced pain at eight hours**: mean VAS 19 ± 22 versus 32 ± 28 with placebo (*P* = 0.001). In contrast, **intra-articular ropivacaine (20 mL of 0.5%) did not significantly reduce postoperative pain** compared with saline.

The finding supports the question’s claim for the measured eight-hour outcome. The abstract does not establish a significant diclofenac benefit at later time points.

**Source:** PMID 10730731, “Diclofenac premedication but not intra-articular ropivacaine alleviates pain following day-case knee arthroscopy” (2000).

FINAL DECISION: YES
