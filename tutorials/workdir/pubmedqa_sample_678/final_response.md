**Study:** Choi et al., *Factors influencing the miss rate of polyps in a tandem colonoscopy study* (2014), PMID: 25073668.

- **Design:** Retrospective tandem-colonoscopy study
- **Population:** 395 patients, 2,068 polyps
- **Miss rates:** 59.2% per patient and 27.9% per polyp

Factors significantly associated with missed polyps were:

- Afternoon examination: OR 1.632, *P* = 0.046
- Size <5 mm: OR 4.595, *P* < 0.001
- Size 5–10 mm: OR 3.447, *P* < 0.001
- Flat morphology: OR 2.406, *P* < 0.001
- Sessile morphology: OR 1.768, *P* = 0.002
- Left-colon location: OR 1.391, *P* = 0.007

Endoscopist experience and patient characteristics were not significantly associated with the miss rate. The findings are relevant but should be interpreted cautiously because the study was retrospective and involved a selected population undergoing repeat colonoscopy for polyp removal.
