Yes. Direct BAV-specific evidence comes from a 2016 observational study of 139 patients (PMID: 27513417). Among patients with ascending aortic dilatation (diameter ≥3.9 cm), higher neutrophil-to-lymphocyte ratio was inversely correlated with ejection fraction:

- **Correlation:** r = −0.467  
- **P-value:** 0.033  

This suggests that higher NLR is related to lower ejection fraction, but the evidence is limited to a subgroup, lacks an adjusted effect estimate, and does not prove causation or establish the relationship in all BAV patients.

FINAL DECISION: YES
