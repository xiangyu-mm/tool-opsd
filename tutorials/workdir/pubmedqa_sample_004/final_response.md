Yes. The primary study by Liu et al. reported that the **A allele of NCAM1 promoter variant rs2301228** was significantly associated with schizophrenia in Han Chinese participants. The association was found in a Hunan discovery cohort (986 cases, 1,040 controls) and validated in a Yunnan replication cohort (564 cases, 547 controls). In luciferase assays, the A allele also significantly reduced NCAM1 transcription.

The evidence supports the allele as a reported risk marker in those cohorts, but the retrieved abstract did not provide odds ratios, confidence intervals, or exact p-values. The validation was within the same two-stage study; the available search results did not identify an independent external replication.

Source: Liu et al., *Schizophrenia Research* (2014), PMID 25445624.
