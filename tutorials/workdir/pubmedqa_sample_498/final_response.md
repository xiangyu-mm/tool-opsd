**Yes.** In the Chinese phase III BEYOND randomized trial, 276 patients with advanced or recurrent non-squamous NSCLC received carboplatin–paclitaxel with either bevacizumab or placebo. Adding bevacizumab improved:

- **Median progression-free survival:** 9.2 vs 6.5 months (HR 0.40; 95% CI 0.29–0.54; P<0.001)
- **Objective response rate:** 54% vs 26%
- **Median overall survival:** 24.3 vs 17.7 months (HR 0.68; 95% CI 0.50–0.93; P=0.0154)

A Chinese retrospective cohort also reported longer PFS with bevacizumab plus pemetrexed–platinum, though its nonrandomized design means confounding is possible (PMID: 31814679). The randomized trial reported the regimen was well tolerated, but the retrieved evidence did not provide detailed adverse-event rates. These results support efficacy in the studied population; individual suitability depends on clinical factors.

**Key evidence:** BEYOND trial, PMID: 26014294; observational cohort, PMID: 31814679.

FINAL DECISION: YES
