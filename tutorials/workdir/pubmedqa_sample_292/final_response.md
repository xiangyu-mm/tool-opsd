**Yes—short-term relief of dyspnoea during NIV was associated with lower measured pressure-pain thresholds in an ALS study.** In 18 patients with ALS and chronic respiratory failure, deltoid pressure-pain thresholds fell by a median of **24.5–33.0%** after 30 and 60 minutes of NIV compared with unassisted breathing (**p<0.05**).

This was a small, short-term study, so it does not establish that NIV causes clinically important pain or that the effect persists. The authors noted that the clinical implications remain undetermined.

**Source:** *Thorax* (2017), “Relieving dyspnoea by non-invasive ventilation decreases pain thresholds in amyotrophic lateral sclerosis,” PMID: 27507899. https://doi.org/10.1136/thoraxjnl-2016-208544
