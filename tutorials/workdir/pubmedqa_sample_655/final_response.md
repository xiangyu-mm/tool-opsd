The available direct comparative evidence does **not** show that adding a polyurethane meniscal scaffold (Actifit) improves outcomes after open-wedge high tibial osteotomy (OWHTO).

A prospective comparative study of 60 patients followed for a mean of 31.2 months found that both groups improved, but WOMET improvement and pain reduction favored OWHTO without the scaffold. IKDC improvement and patient satisfaction did not differ significantly. The study was small and nonrandomized, and the available report did not provide comparative structural imaging outcomes or complication rates. Thus, it does not establish long-term structural benefit or harm.

**Conclusion:** No demonstrated clinical improvement from adding the scaffold; the evidence remains limited, particularly for long-term and structural outcomes.

FINAL DECISION: NO
