The population-based Olmsted County study (PMID: 27492913) evaluated 233 incident Type 1 diabetes cases from 1994–2010.

- Overall incidence: **9.2 per 100,000 person-years** (95% CI 8.0–10.4)
- Incidence among individuals younger than 20: **19.9 per 100,000 person-years** (95% CI 16.6–23.2)
- Temporal trend: **not statistically significant** (Poisson regression, **P = .45**)
- Pattern: an initial rise followed by a plateau

Thus, the study did **not** find that Type 1 diabetes incidence was continuing to increase in this population.
