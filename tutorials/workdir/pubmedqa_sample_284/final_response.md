**Yes.** The directly relevant J-DOPPS III/IV cohort included 2,606 Japanese adults receiving hemodialysis. Among patients with **CRP <0.3 mg/dL**, adjusted all-cause mortality showed a **U-shaped relationship** across ferritin categories (<50, 50–99.9, 100–199.9, and ≥200 ng/mL), consistent with increased mortality at both low and high ferritin levels. Similar findings were reported when patients were stratified by albumin at 3.8 g/dL. The pattern was not significant in the higher-CRP subgroup.

This supports the association in a lower-inflammation subgroup, but the available report does not provide category-specific adjusted hazard ratios; it also does not establish causation.
