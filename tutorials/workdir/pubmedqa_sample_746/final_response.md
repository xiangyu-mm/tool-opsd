**Yes—in animal models.** A study using intramuscular naked plasmids encoding furin and modified rat proinsulin increased plasma insulin and C-peptide and reduced hyperglycemia in streptozotocin-diabetic mice (PMID: [10738567](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10738567/)). Related studies found reduced glucose and improved survival after electroporation-enhanced muscle delivery of insulin constructs (PMID: [12378405](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12378405/)), and reduced diabetes occurrence and hyperglycemia after delivery of a calcitonin gene-related peptide plasmid in an autoimmune mouse model (PMID: [12594852](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12594852/)).

These are **preclinical mouse findings**, and some studies used electroporation to improve plasmid uptake. The evidence supports amelioration in experimental models, but does not establish efficacy in people.

FINAL DECISION: YES
