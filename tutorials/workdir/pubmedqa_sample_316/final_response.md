“bRCA1” is most likely a typo or capitalization variant of **BRCA1/BRCA-1**.

Evidence from the key study, **Pękala et al., 2016 (PMID: 27496484)**:

- Explanted myocardium from **10 men with end-stage idiopathic dilated cardiomyopathy (IDCM)** was compared with tissue from 10 male donor controls.
- BRCA1 protein was assessed by semiquantitative immunohistochemistry.
- IDCM myocardium showed **significantly higher and heterogeneous cardiocyte BRCA1 staining**:
  - intense staining in hypertrophied cardiocytes;
  - weak staining in typical cardiomyopathic cardiocytes.
- The authors interpreted this heterogeneous pattern as reflecting **myocardial adverse remodeling**.

However, the evidence is limited:

- very small, all-male, single-center study;
- cross-sectional analysis of end-stage transplant hearts;
- no serial imaging or longitudinal outcomes;
- no direct assessment of fibrosis burden;
- no demonstration that BRCA1 predicts future adverse remodeling or left-ventricular reverse remodeling;
- no proof that BRCA1 causes remodeling.

Therefore, BRCA1 **does appear to reflect established myocardial adverse remodeling in end-stage IDCM**, but it is not yet a validated predictive biomarker for remodeling progression or recovery.

FINAL DECISION: YES
