### Structural change

\[
\mathrm{-CH_2CH_2-O-Si(CH_3)_2C(CH_3)_3}
\]

to:

\[
\mathrm{-CH_2CH_2-OH}

The rest of the molecule—including the ester, thiophene, methyl group, and cyclic urea/lactam moiety—remains unchanged. This is therefore a selective **TBDMS desilylation**.
