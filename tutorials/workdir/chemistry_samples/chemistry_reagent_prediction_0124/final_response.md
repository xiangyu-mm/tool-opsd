## Reaction assignment

The transformation is an **amide coupling** between:

- **2-Aminopyridine**: `Nc1ccccn1`
- **2-(5-Bromopyridin-2-yl)acetic acid**: `O=C(O)Cc1ccc(Br)cn1`

The product is:

**N-(pyridin-2-yl)-2-(5-bromopyridin-2-yl)acetamide**

The new bond forms between the **carboxylic-acid carbonyl carbon** and the **exocyclic amino nitrogen** of 2-aminopyridine. The pyridine ring nitrogen is not the site of acylation.

## Most plausible reagent set

A suitable primary combination is:

- **TBTU**, approximately 1.0–1.2 equivalents  
- **DIPEA** or Hünig’s base, approximately 2–3 equivalents  
- **Dry THF** as solvent  
- 2-Aminopyridine, approximately 1.0–1.2 equivalents  
- Room temperature, typically several hours to overnight

A representative setup would be:

> 2-(5-Bromopyridin-2-yl)acetic acid + 2-aminopyridine + TBTU + DIPEA in THF, at room temperature.

The acid may first be activated with TBTU/DIPEA, followed by addition of 2-aminopyridine.

## Reasonable alternatives

Other standard amide-coupling systems could also work:

1. **HATU + DIPEA**  
   Solvent: DMF or THF/DMF

2. **EDC·HCl + HOBt or Oxyma + DIPEA/NMM**  
   Solvent: DMF or dichloromethane

3. **PyBOP + DIPEA**  
   Solvent: dichloromethane or DMF

4. **Acid chloride route**  
   Convert the acid to its acid chloride using **oxalyl chloride** or **thionyl chloride**, then react with 2-aminopyridine and **triethylamine or DIPEA**.

## Recommended answer

**Best initial choice:**  
**TBTU, DIPEA, dry THF, room temperature.**

The borane reduction conditions associated with the same acid are not relevant to this reaction because the desired transformation is amide formation, not reduction.
