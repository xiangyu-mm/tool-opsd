### Alternative reagent systems

Depending on substrate stability:

1. **LiOH, THF/MeOH/H₂O**  
   - Often mild and effective for ester hydrolysis.
   - Useful when minimizing degradation of sensitive heterocycles.

2. **KOH, aqueous ethanol or MeOH/H₂O**  
   - Stronger basic saponification conditions.
   - Suitable if the ester is relatively unreactive.

3. **TMSI or BBr₃**  
   - Can cleave methyl esters under specialized conditions, but generally less preferable here because of potential sensitivity of heteroaromatic and N–N-containing groups.

4. **HCl or H₂SO₄ in aqueous alcohol**  
   - Acidic ester hydrolysis is possible, but may risk protonation, decomposition, or alteration of acid-sensitive heterocycles.

### Best practical choice

Use:

**NaOH, MeOH/H₂O, room temperature or mild heating, then HCl acidification.**

A milder alternative is:

**LiOH, THF/MeOH/H₂O, followed by HCl workup.**

The expected product is the **free carboxylic acid**, while the other carbonyl and heterocyclic functionalities should remain intact under controlled hydrolysis conditions.
