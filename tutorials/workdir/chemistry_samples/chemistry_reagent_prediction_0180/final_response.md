- **Triphenylphosphine (PPh₃)**
- **DIAD** or **DEAD**
- Aprotic solvent such as **THF**

\[
\text{menthol}+\text{dihydroxybenzene}+\mathrm{PPh_3}+\mathrm{DIAD}
