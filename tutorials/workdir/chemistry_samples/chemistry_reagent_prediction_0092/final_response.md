The transformation is **`CC(=O)NCCCC(O)Ph` → `CC(=O)NCCCC(=O)Ph`**: a secondary benzylic alcohol is oxidized to a ketone, while the amide and phenyl ring remain unchanged.

Plausible reagents include:

- **Dess–Martin periodinane (DMP)**, typically in dichloromethane
- **PCC**, typically in dichloromethane
- **Swern oxidation**: DMSO, oxalyl chloride, then triethylamine
- **TPAP/NMO** (ruthenium-catalyzed oxidation)
