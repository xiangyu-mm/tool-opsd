The most likely reaction is **acidic Boc deprotection** of the piperidine nitrogen.

### Transformation

The starting material is a **N-Boc-protected heteroaryl-substituted piperidine**:

```text
CC(C)(C)OC(=O)N1CCCC(Cc2ncccn2)C1
```

The reagents are consistent with **HCl in diethyl ether/DCM**, which removes the tert-butoxycarbonyl (Boc) group.

### Expected covalent product

```text
c1cnc(CC2CCCNC2)nc1
```

In other words:

```text
N-Boc piperidine  →  secondary piperidine amine
```

The isolated material would likely be obtained as the **piperidinium hydrochloride salt**:

```text
[c1cnc(CC2CCC[NH2+]C2)nc1]Cl−
```

The neutral free-amine SMILES above represents the covalent product; chloride is expected to function as the counterion rather than participate in a new covalent bond.
