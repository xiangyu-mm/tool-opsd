### Reaction interpretation

The bracketed input is **SELFIES**, not ordinary SMILES. Decoding gives:

- **CCI**: ethyl iodide, an alkylating agent  
- **O=CC=1SC=C(Br)C=1C(=O)O**: bromo-formyl thiophene carboxylic acid  
- **CN(C)C=O**: DMF, solvent  
- **K₂CO₃**: base  

Potassium carbonate deprotonates the carboxylic acid, and the resulting carboxylate undergoes **SN2 ethylation** with ethyl iodide. The carboxylic acid is therefore converted to its ethyl ester.
