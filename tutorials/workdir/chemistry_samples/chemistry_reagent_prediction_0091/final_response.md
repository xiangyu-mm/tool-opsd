### Reaction identification

The transformation is:

\[
\mathrm{R{-}CH_2NO_2 \rightarrow R{-}CH_2NH_2}
\]

The carbon-bound nitro group is reduced to a primary aminomethyl group. The bicyclic tertiary amine remains unchanged; this is **not an amine N-oxide reduction**.

### Possible reagent systems

| Priority | Reagents and conditions | Comment |
|---|---|---|
| **1 — Best-supported** | **Fe, HCl, EtOH** | Directly represented for this substrate/product transformation; likely forms the amine hydrochloride salt. |
| 2 | **Fe, NH₄Cl, EtOH/H₂O** | Common milder iron-mediated nitro reduction alternative. |
| 3 | **H₂, Pd/C** | Standard catalytic hydrogenation of aliphatic nitro groups; use mild pressure and monitor for catalyst compatibility. |
| 4 | **H₂, Pt/C or Raney Ni** | Alternative catalytic hydrogenation systems. |
| 5 | **Zn, NH₄Cl, EtOH/H₂O** | Mild metal-mediated reduction. |
| 6 | **SnCl₂·2H₂O, HCl, EtOH** | Effective nitro reduction, usually followed by basification or neutralization to isolate the free amine. |

### Recommended starting point

Use:

**Iron powder, hydrochloric acid, ethanol; heat or stir until nitro-group consumption is complete.**

The product may initially be obtained as the **amine hydrochloride salt**, so basification may be required if the free amine is desired.
