### Inferred transformation

The reaction is most consistent with **methyl ester hydrolysis (saponification)**:

\[
\mathrm{R{-}C(=O)OCH_3 \rightarrow R{-}C(=O)OH}
\]

The nitrile and heteroaromatic portions appear to remain unchanged. This assignment is supported by the close reaction record. **Source: `call_zelU1jd3IEVZBBzwgv0JgLJi`.**

### Recommended reagents

**Primary choice:**

- **NaOH**
- **EtOH/H₂O** or aqueous ethanol
- Stir at room temperature to mild heating until ester consumption
- Acidic workup with **HCl**, **H₂SO₄**, or **citric acid** to obtain the free carboxylic acid

A close database match used **NaOH in ethanol for approximately 1.5 h**, giving the acid product in **63% yield**.  
**Source: `call_zelU1jd3IEVZBBzwgv0JgLJi`.**

### Other plausible reagent sets

1. **LiOH·H₂O, THF/MeOH/H₂O**  
   - Often a mild and effective option for ester hydrolysis.
   - Acidify after completion.

2. **KOH, aqueous EtOH or MeOH**  
   - A stronger saponification alternative to NaOH.
   - Follow with acidic workup.

3. **Na₂CO₃ or K₂CO₃, aqueous alcohol or DMSO**  
   - Milder basic conditions; potentially preferable if the heteroaromatic scaffold is base-sensitive.
   - A related record used carbonate-type inorganic conditions in DMSO and reported approximately **67% yield**, although this is a less direct match.

### Best starting condition

**NaOH, EtOH/H₂O, room temperature to gentle reflux, followed by acidification.**

This should be the first condition screened because it is directly supported by the closest reaction precedent. Avoid strongly oxidative additives; the required transformation is simple ester hydrolysis, not an oxidation.
