### Predicted major product

The carboxylic acid hydroxyl is replaced by the aminocyclopropyl group:

```text
COC1=CC(N2N=C(C3=C(F)C=CC=C3Cl)[NH1]C2=O)=CC=C1C(=O)NC1(C2=CC=C(C(F)(F)F)C=C2)CC1
```

In words, this is the corresponding **carboxamide bearing a 1-(4-trifluoromethylphenyl)cyclopropan-1-yl substituent on nitrogen**.

The key newly formed bond is:

```text
Ar–C(=O)–OH  +  H2N–C(cyclopropyl)(4-CF3-phenyl)
              →
Ar–C(=O)–NH–C(cyclopropyl)(4-CF3-phenyl)
```

This assignment is substantially more likely than a Suzuki coupling: the final component is **tetrafluoroborate**, not an organotrifluoroborate carbon reagent, while the uronium species is the amide-coupling reagent.
