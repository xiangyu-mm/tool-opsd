The transformation is most consistent with **reduction of an aromatic nitro group to an aniline**:

\[
\mathrm{Ar{-}NO_2 \longrightarrow Ar{-}NH_2}
\]

The methyl and nitrile groups are retained; the nitrile is not reduced.

### Most likely reagents

1. **Tin(II) chloride**
   - \(\mathrm{SnCl_2\cdot 2H_2O}\)
   - In ethanol or aqueous ethanol
   - With \(\mathrm{HCl}\) or acetic acid
   - Followed by basification during workup

   Example:
   \[
   \mathrm{SnCl_2\cdot 2H_2O,\ EtOH/HCl}
   \]

2. **Iron-mediated reduction**
   - Iron powder, \(\mathrm{Fe}\)
   - In acetic acid/ethanol or aqueous ethanol
   - Alternatively, \(\mathrm{Fe/NH_4Cl}\) in aqueous ethanol

   Example:
   \[
   \mathrm{Fe,\ AcOH/EtOH}
   \]

### Possible but less directly supported alternative

- Catalytic hydrogenation: \(\mathrm{H_2/Pd-C}\) or \(\mathrm{H_2/Raney\ Ni}\)

However, the strongest evidence for this specific substrate–product pair supports **\(\mathrm{SnCl_2}\)** and **Fe-based reduction**. The available records do not establish a unique temperature, reaction time, or yield.
