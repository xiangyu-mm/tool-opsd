\[
\mathrm{Ar{-}CHO \rightarrow Ar{-}CH_2OH}
\]

- **Sodium borohydride, NaBH₄**
- **Solvent:** tetrahydrofuran (THF)
- **Likely workup:** aqueous ammonium chloride or dilute acid
