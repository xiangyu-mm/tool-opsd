The substrate undergoes **selective desilylation** of the terminal primary silyl ether.

The transformation is:

\[
\text{–CH}_2\text{CH}_2\text{CH}_2\text{–O–SiR}_3
\;\longrightarrow\;
\text{–CH}_2\text{CH}_2\text{CH}_2\text{–OH}
\]

The aryl silyl ether, ester, alkene, methoxy group, methyl substituents, and lactone are retained.
