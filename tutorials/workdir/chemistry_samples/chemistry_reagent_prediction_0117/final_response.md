### Reaction interpretation

- **Reactant:** methyl 6-(methylthio)-1H-indole-2-carboxylate  
- **Product:** 6-(methylthio)-1H-indole-2-carboxylic acid  

The methylthio substituent and indole ring remain unchanged. The transformation is:

\[
\mathrm{ArCO_2Me \rightarrow ArCO_2H}
\]

### Most likely reagents

- **NaOH, aqueous**
- Water or aqueous ethanol/methanol
- Subsequent acidification with **dilute HCl** or another mineral acid

\[
\mathrm{NaOH,\ H_2O\ or\ EtOH/H_2O;\ then\ HCl\ workup}
\]

The reaction mixture is typically heated at room temperature to reflux until ester hydrolysis is complete, then acidified to precipitate the carboxylic acid.

This is **not** a thioether-forming reaction. The `Ar–S–CH₃` group is already present in the reactant and is retained in the product. Avoid oxidative reagents such as peroxides or peracids, which could convert the thioether to sulfoxide or sulfone.
