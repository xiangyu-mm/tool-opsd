### Analysis

- `[C][O][C][Cl]` = chloromethyl methyl ether (MOMCl), a methoxymethylating reagent.
- `[H−].[Na+]` = sodium hydride, a strong base.
- The aromatic substrate is **3,4-dihydroxybenzaldehyde** (protocatechualdehyde).
- THF and DMF are solvents.

NaH deprotonates one phenolic OH, and the resulting phenoxide undergoes an SN2 reaction with MOMCl. The aldehyde remains unchanged.

### Expected product

The likely product is a **mono-MOM-protected 3,4-dihydroxybenzaldehyde**:

**3-hydroxy-4-(methoxymethoxy)benzaldehyde**  
or its regioisomer, **4-hydroxy-3-(methoxymethoxy)benzaldehyde**.

A representative SMILES is:

```text
O=Cc1cc(O)c(OCOC)cc1
```

where `OCOC` represents the methoxymethoxy group, `Ar–O–CH2–O–CH3`.

The alternative regioisomer can be represented as:

```text
O=Cc1cc(OCOC)c(O)cc1
```

Thus, the key transformation is:

```text
Ar–OH  →  Ar–O–CH2–O–CH3
```

with the benzaldehyde group retained. If excess MOMCl were used, the corresponding bis-MOM-protected aldehyde could also form, but the supplied reactant set most directly supports mono-protection.
