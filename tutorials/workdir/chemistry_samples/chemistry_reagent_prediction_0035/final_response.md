Typical conditions:

- **Reagent:** BBr₃, approximately 1–2 equivalents per methoxy group  
- **Solvent:** Anhydrous CH₂Cl₂

### Possible alternatives

1. **BCl₃ / CH₂Cl₂**  
   A potentially milder Lewis-acid alternative for aryl ether cleavage.

2. **AlCl₃ with ethanethiol or another thiol**  
   Can promote cleavage of aryl methyl ethers, although conditions may be less convenient.

3. **Concentrated HBr or HI, heat**  
   Possible but generally harsher and less chemoselective.

4. **Pyridine hydrochloride, high temperature**  
   An older method for demethylating aryl methyl ethers.
