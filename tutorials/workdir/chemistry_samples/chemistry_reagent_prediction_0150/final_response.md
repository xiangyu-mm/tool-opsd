### Transformation identified

The reaction is an **amide coupling**:

- Acid component: **2-[(3-chloro-4-fluorophenyl)amino]acetic acid**  
  `O=C(O)CNH–C6H3(Cl)(F)`
- Amine component: a chiral **aminopiperidine-fused N-heterocycle**
- Product: **N-acylation of the piperidine nitrogen**

The piperidine nitrogen is the most likely nucleophile; the heteroaromatic NH remains unacylated.

### Plausible reagent systems

Most likely options, in approximate order:

1. **HATU / DIPEA**
   - HATU, 1.0–1.2 equiv
   - DIPEA, 2–4 equiv
   - DMF or NMP
   - Room temperature
   - Often the best choice for coupling a hindered or heteroatom-rich amine.

2. **T3P / DIPEA**
   - T3P, typically 1.5–2.5 equiv
   - DIPEA or triethylamine
   - Ethyl acetate, DMF, or dichloromethane
   - A practical alternative with relatively clean byproducts.

3. **EDCI·HCl / Oxyma**
   - EDCI·HCl, 1.1–1.5 equiv
   - Oxyma, 1.1–1.5 equiv
   - DIPEA or N-methylmorpholine
   - DMF or dichloromethane
   - Safer modern alternative to EDCI/HOBt.

4. **COMU / DIPEA**
   - COMU, 1.1–1.3 equiv
   - DIPEA, 2–4 equiv
   - DMF
   - Effective for difficult amide formations.

5. **Acid chloride route**
   - Convert the acid to its acid chloride using:
     - **Oxalyl chloride**, optionally catalytic DMF, or
     - **Thionyl chloride**
   - Then react with the aminopiperidine using **DIPEA**, triethylamine, or 2,6-lutidine.
   - Typical solvents: dichloromethane or THF.

### Most likely specific procedure

A reasonable first choice would be:

> **2-[(3-chloro-4-fluorophenyl)amino]acetic acid + aminopiperidine heterocycle + HATU + DIPEA in DMF at room temperature**

Alternatively, the transformation could have been performed through the corresponding acid chloride:

> **Oxalyl chloride/DMF, then aminopiperidine + DIPEA**

Because the product specifically contains the **N-acylated piperidine**, a standard peptide-coupling reagent is more likely than a palladium-, copper-, or alkylation-based reagent. The exact reagent cannot be uniquely determined from the reaction structures alone.
