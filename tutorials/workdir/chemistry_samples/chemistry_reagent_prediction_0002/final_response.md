The transformation is **hydrolysis of a methyl ester to the corresponding carboxylic acid**:

\[
\mathrm{R{-}C(=O)OCH_3 \rightarrow R{-}C(=O)OH}
\]

The substrate is consistent with **methyl 3-(cyclododecyl)propanoate**, and the product is **3-(cyclododecyl)propanoic acid**. The cyclododecyl ring and carbon skeleton remain unchanged.

### Preferred procedure

A practical first choice would be:

1. Dissolve the methyl ester in **methanol/water**.
2. Add approximately **1.5–3 equivalents of NaOH**.
3. Heat at about **50–55 °C** and monitor by TLC, LC–MS, or NMR.
4. Remove methanol if needed and acidify the aqueous mixture with **dilute HCl** to approximately pH 2.
5. Extract the liberated carboxylic acid with an organic solvent or isolate it by appropriate precipitation/extraction.

The retrieved record specifically used **NaOH in methanol/water at approximately 55 °C**, followed by acidic workup; its reported yield was **25.1%**. This yield should be regarded as record-specific rather than a guaranteed outcome. Evidence: `call_RPI3UwKfY7B5rxdz20uvJkG8`.

**Best initial recommendation:** **NaOH, MeOH/H₂O, approximately 55 °C, followed by HCl acidification.** If other base-sensitive functional groups are present, consider **LiOH in THF/H₂O at lower temperature**.
