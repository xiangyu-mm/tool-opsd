### Best practical starting condition

**TBAF in dry THF at 0 °C to room temperature**, followed by aqueous or ammonium chloride workup.

Because the molecule contains a phosphate triester and multiple unsaturated groups, avoid unnecessarily strong acid, prolonged heating, or large excesses of fluoride. Monitor the reaction by TLC or LC–MS to minimize possible phosphate cleavage or other side reactions.
