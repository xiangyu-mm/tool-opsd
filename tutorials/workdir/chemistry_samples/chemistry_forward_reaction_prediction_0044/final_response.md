The probable reaction is a **cesium-carbonate-promoted nucleophilic aromatic substitution (SNAr)**.

- **Nucleophile:** ethanol → ethoxide  
- **Substrate:** activated difluoro-nitrobenzoic acid  
- **Transformation:** one aryl **F** is replaced by **–OCH₂CH₃**; the other F and the carboxylic acid remain intact.
- **Cs₂CO₃:** base that generates ethoxide  
- **DMF/water/chloride:** solvent or work-up components

**Ethoxy-fluoro-nitrobenzoic acid**, represented by:

```text
CCOc1cc(C(=O)O)c(F)cc1[N+](=O)[O-]
```
