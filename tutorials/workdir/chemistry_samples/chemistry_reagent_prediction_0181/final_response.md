### Potential alternatives

Depending on substrate sensitivity, possible alternatives include:

- **Borane–THF complex (BH₃·THF)** — effective for amide reduction and often more chemoselective.
- **Sodium bis(2-methoxyethoxy)aluminum hydride (Red-Al)** — a soluble hydride alternative to LAH.
- **DIBAL-H** — may reduce amides under specially controlled conditions, but is generally less straightforward for complete conversion to the amine.

**Best primary choice:** **LiAlH₄ in dry ether or THF**, followed by a controlled workup.
