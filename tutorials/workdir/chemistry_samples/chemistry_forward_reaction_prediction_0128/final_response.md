A plausible product is formed by **N-alkylation of the free piperidine nitrogen** with 2-chloroethyl methyl ether.

### Reaction
\[
\text{Piperidine–NH} + \mathrm{ClCH_2CH_2OCH_3}
\rightarrow
\text{Piperidine–NCH_2CH_2OCH_3}
\]

The potassium carbonate/KI system promotes an SN2 substitution. The piperidine nitrogen is the most plausible nucleophile because the aryl oxygen is already an ether and the piperazine nitrogen is benzoyl-protected.

### Predicted product
**1-Benzoyl-4-{[4-[(1-(2-methoxyethyl)piperidin-4-yl)oxy]phenyl]methyl}piperazine**

**SMILES:**
```text
COCCN1CCC(Oc2ccc(CN3CCN(C(=O)c4ccccc4)CC3)cc2)CC1
```

Thus, the key structural change is replacement of the piperidine **N–H** by a **2-methoxyethyl group**.
