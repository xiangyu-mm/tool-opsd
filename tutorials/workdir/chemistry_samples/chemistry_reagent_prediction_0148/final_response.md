## Reaction interpretation

The transformation is:

**2,5-difluoroacetophenone → 1-(2,5-difluorophenyl)ethanol**

In SMILES:

```text
CC(=O)c1ccc(F)cc1F → CC(O)c1ccc(F)cc1F
```

This is a **ketone reduction to a secondary benzylic alcohol**. The aryl fluorides and carbon skeleton remain unchanged.

## Recommended reagents

### Preferred conditions

```text
NaBH4, MeOH/THF
then aqueous workup
```

Typical conditions:

- Sodium borohydride: approximately **1.0–1.5 equivalents**
- Solvent: methanol, optionally diluted with tetrahydrofuran
- Temperature: **0 °C to room temperature**
- Quench/workup: water or dilute aqueous solution, followed by extraction

An exact ORD precedent for this substrate-to-product conversion uses **NaBH₄ with methanol and tetrahydrofuran**, with water present in the reaction record. Source: `call_QtIk9k7TQX8OA2cLSvSnzSOH`.

## Alternative reagent systems

| Reagent | Possible conditions | Comments |
|---|---|---|
| **LiAlH₄** | Dry THF or ether, then careful aqueous workup | Effective but stronger and less convenient than NaBH₄ |
| **DIBAL-H** | Toluene or dichloromethane, low temperature | Usually unnecessary for a simple ketone |
| **BH₃·THF** | THF, controlled temperature | Can reduce ketones but is less standard here |
| **Transfer hydrogenation** | Isopropanol with a suitable catalyst | Possible, but catalyst-dependent |

## Best answer

Use:

```text
1. NaBH4, MeOH/THF, 0 °C → room temperature
2. Aqueous workup
```

This should give **1-(2,5-difluorophenyl)ethanol**, potentially as a racemic mixture because reduction creates a new stereocenter. The reaction is **not** an enol-ether formation.
