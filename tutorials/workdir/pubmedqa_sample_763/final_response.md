Yes. The directly relevant study by Sykes et al. (Transplantation, 2000; PMID: 10755549) examined α1,3-galactosyltransferase-deficient (GGTA1/GalT−/−) mice treated in vivo with depleting anti-CD4 and anti-CD8 antibodies.

T-cell depletion significantly increased:

- Serum anti-GALα1,3GAL IgM
- Total serum IgM
- Anti-GALα1,3GAL IgM-producing cells in the spleen and bone marrow

However, it did not significantly increase anti-GALα1,3GAL IgG or total IgG. Thus, the enhancement was selective for the natural-antibody IgM response.
