Yes. A retrospective study directly compared failure rates among three ICD lead groups in 297 implants:

- **Durata:** 0.20% per patient-year; 5-year survival 98.9%
- **Linox/LinoxSmart:** 0.95% per patient-year; 5-year survival 100%
- **Sprint Quattro:** 1.84% per patient-year; 5-year survival 87.5%

Sprint Quattro had significantly worse survival than Durata (*p*=0.011) and Linox/LinoxSmart (*p*=0.028). Durata and Linox/LinoxSmart did not differ significantly (*p*=0.393). The study grouped Linox and LinoxSmart together, so it compared three lead groups rather than three individual models. **Reference: PMID 31691064.**
