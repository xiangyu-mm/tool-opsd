In the available direct study, QRS duration was **not associated** with left-ventricular twisting or untwisting in chronic systolic heart failure. Among 100 patients with LVEF ≤35%, speckle-tracking echocardiography showed no significant differences across QRS-duration groups in global twist, peak twist rate, or peak untwist rate; regression also found no relationship with QRS duration (all P>0.05; PMID: 25077412).

This supports **no measurable effect of QRS duration** on these rotational measures in the studied population. The evidence is limited, and QRS duration should be distinguished from LBBB morphology itself.
