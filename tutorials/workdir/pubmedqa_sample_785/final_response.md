Yes. In the primary study by Weller et al. (PMID: 10752962), human RPE cells cultured on bovine retinal explants caused substantial contraction:

- **IL-10** alone inhibited contraction by approximately **63%** versus untreated controls.
- **Antibodies against PDGF and TGF-β2** inhibited contraction by **up to 70%**.
- The antibodies also blocked the contraction-promoting effects of exogenous PDGF and TGF-β2 (**P < 0.01**).

The abstract does not clearly separate the exact effects of anti-PDGF alone, anti-TGF-β2 alone, and their combination, but collectively the evidence supports inhibition by all listed treatments.

FINAL DECISION: YES
