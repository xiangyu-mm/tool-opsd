A directly relevant human study (PMID:10731497) evaluated 12 men with severe hypertriacylglycerolemia before and after 6 weeks of fish-oil n-3 fatty-acid ethyl esters (~3.4 g/day).

- Postprandial chylomicrons decreased by **49–64%** at 4–8 hours.
- Postprandial VLDL decreased by **36–43%** at 4–8 hours.
- Large chylomicron remnants decreased by **19% at 6 hours** and **43% at 8 hours**.
- Small chylomicron remnants decreased by **31% only at 8 hours**.

Thus, chylomicrons and VLDL were reduced earlier and generally more consistently than chylomicron remnants. The evidence comes from a small before–after study, so the conclusion is supportive rather than definitive.

FINAL DECISION: YES
