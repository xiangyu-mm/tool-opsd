A single direct study in 30 premature infants compared:

- **5% phenylephrine + 0.5% tropicamide**
- **0.5% tropicamide alone**

The combination produced:

- **Pupil surface area:** 43.8% vs 23.1% of corneal surface area — approximately **1.90 times greater** (*p* < 0.0001).
- **Pupil diameter:** 65.9% vs 47.3% of corneal diameter — approximately **1.39 times greater**, not twofold.
- **Peripheral retinal visualization:** 100% vs 53.3% of eyes (*p* < 0.0001).

Thus, the claim is essentially correct **when efficacy is measured by normalized pupil surface area**, but not when measured by linear pupil diameter. The evidence is based on one small study in premature infants, with limited safety and generalizability data.

FINAL DECISION: YES
