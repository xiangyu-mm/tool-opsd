Yes—clinical results were published for the Contura multilumen balloon breast brachytherapy Phase IV registry trial:

- **Publication:** Shaitelman et al., *Int J Radiat Oncol Biol Phys* (2014), **PMID: 25442036**
- **Population:** 342 evaluable patients from 23 institutions
- **Follow-up:** Median 36 months; range 1–54 months
- **Outcomes:**  
  - 3-year local recurrence-free survival: **97.8%**
  - 10 ipsilateral breast tumor recurrences
  - Good-to-excellent cosmesis: **88%**
  - Infection: **8.5%**
  - Symptomatic seroma: **4.4%**
  - No grade 4 toxicity reported

However, despite the article’s title calling these “long-term results,” the available evidence is more accurately described as **intermediate-term**. I did not identify mature 5-year or 10-year outcomes, nor separate long-term overall-survival or distant-metastasis-free-survival analyses, for this 342-patient registry cohort.
