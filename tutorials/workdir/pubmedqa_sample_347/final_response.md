**Yes—organ-space infection was less frequent in the air-leak-test group in the identified study.** In a retrospective, single-center cohort of 318 patients undergoing major hepatectomy without biliary-enteric anastomosis, infection occurred in **5.2% with testing (n=210)** versus **13.0% without testing (n=108)** (*p*=0.015). That is an observed absolute difference of 7.8 percentage points.

This supports an association, not definitive proof that the test itself caused the reduction: the study was observational, and the groups differed in the proportion undergoing trisegmentectomy. The result is from Tran Cao et al., *Journal of Gastrointestinal Surgery* (2017), [PMID 27496092](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27496092/), DOI: 10.1007/s11605-016-3209-9.

FINAL DECISION: YES
