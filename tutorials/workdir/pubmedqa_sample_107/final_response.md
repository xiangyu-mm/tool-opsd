Delayed inflammatory reactions in red tattoos have been associated with **azo pigments**; pigment analyses of chronic reactions have identified azo pigments including Pigment Red 22, 170, and 210 (PMID: 31626330). A small clinical series also reported reactions involving **quinacridones** Violet 19 and Red 122 (PMID: 25441375), though evidence specifically establishing those purified pigments as the cause is more limited.

The evidence is not definitive for every case: tattoo inks contain mixtures, and patch tests are often negative. Formation of allergenic compounds in the skin has been proposed as one explanation (PMID: 25040844).

FINAL DECISION: YES
