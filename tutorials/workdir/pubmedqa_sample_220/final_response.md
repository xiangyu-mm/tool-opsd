**Evidence:** Azhdari-Zarmehri et al. tested rats using 2% formalin and reported that naloxone (1 mg/kg) reduced phase-2A nociception but **did not alter delayed termination** of the response. A second formalin injection 60 minutes after the first diminished nociceptive behavior, whereas a 5-minute interval did not. The authors interpreted this as evidence for an endogenous inhibitory mechanism other than opioid-receptor activation.

**Qualification:** This supports the claim under the study’s conditions; it does not establish that opioid signaling can never contribute to formalin-test responses.  
Source: [PMID 25436084](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25436084/); DOI: 10.1016/j.brainres.2012.08.013.
