Yes. In a direct study of healthy human aortic valve interstitial cells (AVICs), Deng et al. found that adult AVICs produced higher inflammatory and osteogenic marker levels than pediatric AVICs after stimulation with lipopolysaccharide, a TLR4 agonist. TLR4 protein levels were similar between age groups; the stronger adult response was associated partly with lower STAT3 activation.

This supports greater adult AVIC responses in the tested cell-culture conditions, though it is evidence from a specific adult-versus-pediatric comparison rather than every possible TLR4 stimulus or clinical setting. **Source:** Deng et al., *Annals of Thoracic Surgery* (2015), PMID: 25442996.

FINAL DECISION: YES
