**Yes.** A 2015 *Addictive Behaviors* study recruited 49 adults at campus drinking venues and reported that higher blood alcohol concentration negatively predicted money won on the **final two blocks** of the Iowa Gambling Task. The analysis accounted for trait impulsivity and habitual alcohol use, which the researchers treated as a proxy for alcohol tolerance, along with other covariates.

This was a small, observational study, so it supports an adjusted association in that sample and setting, not proof that BAC caused lower winnings. [PubMed: PMID 25452056](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25452056/)
