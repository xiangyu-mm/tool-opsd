**Yes—there is limited human evidence that endometriosis can involve the decidua at the fetal-membrane interface.** In a small study of 11 pregnant women with severe endometriosis and 11 controls, investigators reported endometriotic-like glandular lesions on the decidual side of the choriodecidua in tissues collected at term during prelabor cesarean delivery (PMID: 25432921). They also observed molecular differences between groups.

The finding is preliminary: the study does not establish which specific decidual layer was involved, whether the changes impair fetal-membrane function, or how often this occurs. So this supports possible involvement—not a claim that endometriosis routinely affects the membrane-associated decidua.

FINAL DECISION: YES
