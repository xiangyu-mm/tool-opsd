In the directly relevant study, a retrospective cohort of 119 non-Asian women with chronic hepatitis B was assessed for advanced fibrosis/cirrhosis by liver stiffness measurement. Neither historical nor current HBV DNA nor quantitative HBsAg was reported as an independent predictor. The reported estimates included historical HBV DNA (RR 0.34, 95% CI 0.05–2.44) and qHBsAg >1,000 IU/mL (RR 0.35, 95% CI 0.11–1.11), both statistically nonsignificant.

Interpret this as **no association detected**, not proof that no association exists: only 14 women met the elastography-defined F3–F4 outcome, estimates were imprecise, and the outcome was not biopsy-confirmed cirrhosis. The abstract did not provide separate numerical estimates for current HBV DNA or current qHBsAg.
