The available comparative evidence does **not** show increased urinary *Chlamydia trachomatis* prevalence.

- Cristea et al. (2019, PMID: **30988725**) studied 27 patients with spondyloarthritis, including **24 men**, and 26 healthy controls.
- Urine was tested using a *C. trachomatis* real-time PCR assay.
- Positive urine results were **0/27 (0%) in patients versus 0/26 (0%) in controls**.
- The study included spondyloarthritis rather than an ankylosing-spondylitis-only cohort, so the evidence is limited for strictly defined AS. However, it provides no indication of increased urinary prevalence.

FINAL DECISION: NO
