The finding supports an **association**, not by itself a causal role for methylation in drought tolerance. A separate 2024 whole-genome bisulfite sequencing study found drought-linked CHH hypermethylation in *Populus nigra*, but that is a different species and should be treated as contextual rather than direct evidence for *P. trichocarpa*.
