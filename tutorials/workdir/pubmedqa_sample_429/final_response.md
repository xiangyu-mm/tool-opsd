A primary study titled *“CUB domain-containing protein 1 and the epidermal growth factor receptor cooperate to induce cell detachment”* (2016; **PMID: 27495374**) reports that CDCP1 and EGFR cooperate to promote breast-cancer-cell detachment and disrupt adherens junctions. The proposed mechanism involves a CDCP1–Src–EGFR complex and Src-dependent EGFR transactivation. The response varied by cell context, so the finding should not be assumed to apply universally.
