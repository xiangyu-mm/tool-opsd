Yes—**in the cited study, MPA-induced GTP depletion was accompanied by effects on both ATP and pyrimidine metabolism** in mitogen-stimulated primary human T lymphocytes.

- **ATP:** ATP levels decreased by up to approximately **50%**, in a concentration-dependent manner, while GTP fell to about **10% of unstimulated-cell levels**.
- **Pyrimidines:** MPA altered pyrimidine metabolism: uridine pools increased, CTP remained near resting-cell levels, precursor incorporation changed, and uridine uptake/salvage decreased.
- **Qualification:** The data indicate altered pyrimidine synthesis/metabolic balance—not necessarily a complete, uniform blockade of de novo pyrimidine synthesis.

Evidence: PMID **10755546**, *Transplantation* (2000).

FINAL DECISION: YES
