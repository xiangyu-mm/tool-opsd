Yes. A 2014 study in the *Indian Journal of Pharmacology* (PMID: 25097278) reported that sildenafil enhanced the peripheral antinociceptive effect of ellagic acid in rats undergoing the formalin test.

Specifically, subeffective sildenafil doses (25 or 50 μg/paw) potentiated the effect of subeffective ellagic acid (60 μg/paw) compared with ellagic acid alone. The enhancement was observed in both the early neurogenic and late inflammatory phases.

FINAL DECISION: YES
