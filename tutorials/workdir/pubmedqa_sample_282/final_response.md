### Evidence summary

Yes. The primary study was a 2016 cattle study (*Clinical and Experimental Vaccine Research*, PMID: **27489804**) comparing:

- Commercial W/O/W oil-adjuvanted FMD vaccine plus **QS-21**
- The same oil-adjuvanted vaccine without QS-21

Key findings:

- Six cattle were included per group.
- Type O neutralizing antibodies were detected by day 3 in **4/6 QS-21 cattle versus 3/6 controls**.
- The QS-21 group showed a strong neutralizing-antibody response by approximately day 8, with rapid increases continuing to about day 28.
- The paper reported a **statistically significant early antibody increase**, particularly in virus-neutralizing antibodies.
- Exact antibody titers and P values for individual days such as 7, 14, or 21 were not available in the retrieved record.

The evidence therefore supports enhancement of the early antibody response, although the study was small and the precise magnitude at each early time point is unclear.

FINAL DECISION: YES
