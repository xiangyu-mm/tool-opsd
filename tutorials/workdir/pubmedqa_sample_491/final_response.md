**Yes—but only as an observational association for early, short-term survival.** In a cohort of 522 adult liver-transplant recipients, higher early post-transplant recipient GGT was associated with better 90-day survival. Median peak GGT was **293 U/L in patients surviving beyond 90 days versus 172 U/L in non-survivors** (*p*<0.0001); Kaplan–Meier analysis also favored higher GGT for 90-day survival (*p*<0.001). (Salomone et al., 2014; PMID: [25075298](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25075298/))

This does **not** show that elevated GGT causes better survival. The association was time-dependent: the report found the opposite direction for late post-transplant GGT and five-year survival. The result concerns recipient GGT after transplantation, not donor or pre-transplant GGT.
