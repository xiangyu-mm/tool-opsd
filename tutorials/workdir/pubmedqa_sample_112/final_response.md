The study is **PMID 25427271**, a case-control pilot study of 30 systemically healthy urban Indian men:

- 15 had generalized, severe, chronic periodontitis.
- 15 had clinically healthy periodontium.
- The periodontitis group had significantly lower hemoglobin, RBC count, packed-cell volume/hematocrit, and MCHC.
- They also had significantly higher MCV and ESR.
- Group comparisons used ANCOVA with age as a covariate.
- The authors concluded that severe periodontal disease “can be linked with anemic status.”

However, the accessible abstract did not report ferritin, serum iron, transferrin, TIBC, or transferrin saturation. Therefore, it supports an association with anemia-related hematologic changes, but does not definitively prove classical anemia of chronic disease or causation.
