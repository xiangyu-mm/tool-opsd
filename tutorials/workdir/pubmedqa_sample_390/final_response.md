Yes. A 2016 study (PMID: 27506200) developed tissue-specific BirA/Avi-tag affinity purification followed by mass spectrometry in *C. elegans*. Applying it to DLG-1/Discs large identified candidate interaction partners including:

- **ATAD-3**, an AAA-type ATPase; the interaction domains were mapped, and the interaction was shown to contribute to development.
- **MAPH-1.1**, which specifically co-purified with neuronal DLG-1 and was proposed as a neuron-specific, microtubule-associated interaction partner.

The approach was validated using known LGL-1 partners PAR-6 and PKC-3, supporting its ability to identify bona fide protein interactions.
