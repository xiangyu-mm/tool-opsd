A direct clinical study in acromegaly found a **high correlation** between:

- **Percent tumor volume resected**, measured by 3D MRI  
- **Percent reduction in plasma growth hormone (GH)** after surgery  

This was reported in **11 patients** with incomplete resection and no medical therapy; the association was statistically significant (**p = 0.006; PMID: 25423276**).

However, the evidence is limited because the cohort was small and selected, and the accessible report did not provide the actual correlation coefficient. Also, this relationship concerns **within-patient percentage changes**, not simply baseline tumor size versus baseline GH.

Thus, the available direct evidence supports a close correlation, but not as a universally precise predictor for every patient.

FINAL DECISION: YES
