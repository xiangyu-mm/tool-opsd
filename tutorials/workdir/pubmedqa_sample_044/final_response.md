**Yes, platelet activation appears to be associated with myocardial infarction in hospitalized patients with pneumonia, based on limited observational evidence.**

In a cohort of 278 hospitalized patients with community-acquired pneumonia, platelet-activation markers—including soluble P-selectin, soluble CD40 ligand, and thromboxane B₂—were higher in patients with MI; several were independent predictors in the study’s regression analysis (PMID: 25444147). Another CAP study assessed urinary 11-dehydro-thromboxane B₂ and reported 31 MIs during hospitalization, though the available results do not show the specific marker comparison by MI status (PMID: 29577968).

These findings support an **association**, not proof that platelet activation causes MI. The studies also do not establish whether the events were type 1 or type 2 MI.
