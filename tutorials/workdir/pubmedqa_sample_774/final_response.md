**Yes—recipient natural killer (NK) cells contribute substantially to the early chemokine response in murine allogeneic skin grafts.** In the primary study (PMID: 10755559), depleting NK cells before grafting reduced early KC/CXCL1, MIP-1α/CCL3, and MIP-1β/CCL4 mRNA by approximately 60–70%, and also lowered day-4 chemokine protein. The response was present in athymic recipients, indicating it did not require T cells.

The evidence supports NK cells **amplifying** the early response, rather than establishing that they are its only source or that the response is entirely NK-cell-dependent. The conclusion rests on depletion experiments in one primary study.
