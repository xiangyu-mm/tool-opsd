**No.** In the canine cruciate-deficiency model, synovial-fluid glycosaminoglycan/proteoglycan concentration **did not correlate with chondropathy severity** and **did not predict later cartilage damage**.

The study followed 19 adult dogs after anterior cruciate ligament transection. Chondropathy was assessed arthroscopically after 16 weeks and at postmortem after 32 weeks. The 16-week synovial-fluid proteoglycan concentration did not predict damage at 32 weeks. Adjusting for joint clearance did not improve the result.

**Source:** “Synovial fluid glycosaminoglycan concentration does not correlate with severity of chondropathy or predict progression of osteoarthritis in a canine cruciate deficiency model.” *The Journal of Rheumatology*. 2000; PMID: [10743821](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10743821/).

This conclusion applies to the experimental canine model studied; it does not establish that every synovial-fluid biomarker is uninformative.

FINAL DECISION: NO
