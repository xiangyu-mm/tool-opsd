A direct rat study (PMID: 27504985, *Psychoneuroendocrinology*, 2016) found that 8 weeks of clinically relevant olanzapine-depot treatment caused a **biphasic, time-dependent change in serum leptin**. Leptin rose markedly early, while increased adiposity emerged later, indicating that the endocrine alteration was not simply a consequence of established fat accumulation.

No time-dependent changes were reported for ghrelin, FGF-21, IL-1α, IL-6, or TNF-α; effects on adiponectin, resistin, and visfatin were not established in the available report.

FINAL DECISION: YES
