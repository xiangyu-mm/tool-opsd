**Yes—as a preclinical candidate.** In the clearest study, PR8/NIBRG-93 carrying three substitutions in the HA segment’s 3′ noncoding promoter region (G3A, U5C, C8U) showed increased HA expression and attenuation. In mice, it protected against lethal challenge and reduced virus shedding. The engineered mutations persisted through 10 egg passages, although an additional change appeared at a later passage. ([PMID 25087607](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25087607/))

A separate mouse study found improved challenge protection when HA promoter substitutions were combined with NS1 truncation, but that result does not establish promoter mutations alone as the cause of attenuation. ([PMID 22398291](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22398291/))

Thus, the findings support an effective **experimental** live attenuated vaccine candidate, but the evidence is preclinical and does not establish safety or effectiveness in humans.

FINAL DECISION: YES
