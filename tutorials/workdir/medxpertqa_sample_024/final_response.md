The **three-bubble sign** indicates **proximal jejunal atresia**: gas is present in the stomach, duodenum, and dilated proximal jejunum. Jejunal atresia results from an **in utero mesenteric vascular insult**, causing ischemia, necrosis, and resorption of a bowel segment.

Failed luminal recanalization more typically causes **duodenal atresia**, which produces a double-bubble sign.
