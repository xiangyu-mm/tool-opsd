The available evidence supports **yes**.

A 2014 in-vitro study (PMID: **25070816**) examined the ADI-sensitive human lymphoma cell line **Ramos**. Arginine deiminase (**0.01 U/mL**) was administered with increasing concentrations of L-arginine (**10, 30, and 50 mM**). L-arginine **dose-dependently enhanced ADI-induced growth inhibition**, assessed by MTT cell-survival testing.

The study also measured **NF-κB p65/RELA** and **p53/TP53** expression by Western blot and attributed the enhanced response to changes involving these proteins. However, the accessible abstract does not specify the direction of expression changes or establish causality through inhibition/knockdown experiments. Therefore, the strongest conclusion is that L-arginine enhances ADI-mediated growth inhibition in this lymphoma model, with NF-κB p65 and p53 expression implicated mechanistically.

FINAL DECISION: YES
