Yes. The relevant mouse study is **“MST1 coordinately regulates autophagy and apoptosis in diabetic cardiomyopathy in mice”** (*Diabetologia*, 2016; **PMID: 27510910**).

It identified mST1/MST1 as **mammalian sterile 20-like kinase 1 (MST1/STK4)**. In diabetic mice:

- **MST1 overexpression** suppressed autophagy and increased cardiomyocyte apoptosis.
- **MST1 knockout or reduction** enhanced autophagic flux and decreased apoptosis.
- These changes were accompanied by corresponding worsening or improvement in cardiac dysfunction.

Thus, the evidence supports MST1 as a coordinated regulator of both impaired autophagy and increased apoptosis in diabetic cardiomyopathy, although the study demonstrates coordinated biological regulation rather than necessarily proving that one process directly causes the other.

FINAL DECISION: YES
