A clinical study of 145 patients with colorectal cancer found that, among those with Dukes C disease, high MCM7 expression (immunohistochemical labeling index ≥76%) was associated with significantly worse recurrence-free survival after curative surgery. Overall survival was also worse in the MCM7-positive group.

However, the available abstract does not provide the hazard ratio or p-value and does not establish whether MCM7 was an independent risk factor after multivariable adjustment. Thus, the evidence supports MCM7 as a recurrence-associated prognostic marker, but the strength of its independent predictive value remains uncertain.

Source: Ishibashi et al., *Anticancer Research*, 2014; PMID: 25075101.
