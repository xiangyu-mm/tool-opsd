**Yes, with an important distinction:** in an experimental tubule-generation study, tannic-acid-containing fixation for electron microscopy revealed extracellular matrix in the newly formed interstitium that was not visible with conventional fixation. The authors interpreted the finding as evidence of abnormal features in that interstitium during renal-tubule regeneration (PMID: 25517030). A related study also reported a distinct tannic-acid labeling pattern in regenerating versus mature tubules (PMID: 22337593).

This does **not** mean tannic acid specifically labels abnormal cells; it reveals extracellular structures. Thus, the evidence supports abnormal **interstitial matrix development** coinciding with tubule regeneration, not abnormal cell development as a direct labeling readout.
