The most likely diagnosis is an **ovarian endometrioma (“chocolate cyst”)**, a manifestation of endometriosis.

- Cyclic premenstrual pelvic pain is characteristic.
- A homogeneous, low-level “ground-glass” echo pattern on ultrasound is typical.
- These cysts are generally benign, not usually malignant.
- Recurrence after treatment is common.
- Hormonal therapy may control symptoms but is not appropriate as first-line treatment for every patient.
- Symptomatic, persistent, or clinically significant endometriomas are commonly managed surgically, usually by laparoscopic cystectomy.
