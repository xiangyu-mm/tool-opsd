Yes. A 2015 rat study (PMID: 25428031) found that pretreatment with Shinzami Korean purple-fleshed sweet potato extract attenuated hepatic ischaemia–reperfusion injury.

Compared with untreated hepatic ischaemia–reperfusion controls, the extract:

- Reduced serum AST and ALT
- Increased hepatic glutathione, superoxide dismutase, and glutathione peroxidase activity
- Reduced histological damage, including distortion, hemorrhage, necrosis, and inflammatory-cell infiltration

The evidence comes from one directly relevant experimental study, and the accessible report does not provide full dose, preparation, or quantitative effect-size details. Thus, it supports prevention or attenuation of liver damage in this rat model, but not necessarily clinical benefit in humans.

FINAL DECISION: YES
