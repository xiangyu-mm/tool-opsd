The relevant study was **PMID: 25085325**, which compared otosclerotic stapedial osteoblasts (OSO) with normal stapedial osteoblasts (NSO) and normal-bone osteoblasts.

Untreated OSO showed:

- Increased cell adhesion
- Reduced proliferation
- Increased calcification/mineralization
- Markedly reduced osteopontin expression—reported as **9.4-fold lower than NSO at day 14**
- Increased RANKL/OPG signaling

Alendronate, tested at **10⁻¹⁰ to 10⁻⁸ M**, produced dose-dependent normalization of the principal functional differences: adhesion, proliferation, and mineralization were brought to levels comparable with NSO and control osteoblasts. It also increased osteopontin and reduced the RANKL/OPG ratio, which the authors described as normalization of the abnormal remodeling profile.

The evidence does not establish normalization of every osteoblast-marker gene, but it does support normalization of the major measured cellular phenotypes.

FINAL DECISION: YES
