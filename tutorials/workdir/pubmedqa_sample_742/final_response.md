The available evidence indicates that **bosentan does not improve survival** in severe experimental pancreatitis in rats.

- In the directly relevant study (PMID: **10732291**), rats with severe taurocholate-induced necrotizing pancreatitis received bosentan **20 mg/kg IV every 12 hours for 3 days**, starting 1 hour after induction.
- Seven-day survival was:
  - **4% taurocholate:** 62% with bosentan vs 77% with vehicle
  - **5% taurocholate:** 20% with bosentan vs 27% with vehicle
- The differences were not statistically significant, and survival numerically favored the vehicle group.
- A separate study reported improved survival with **LU-135252**, but that is a different endothelin receptor antagonist and does not establish efficacy for bosentan.

FINAL DECISION: NO
