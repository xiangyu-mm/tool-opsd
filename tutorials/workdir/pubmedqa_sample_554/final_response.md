**Yes—in the reported cohort.** A 2014 study of 312 patients with **oral cavity** squamous cell carcinoma (not oropharyngeal cancer) found that quantitative PCR-measured HPV-16/18 E7 load of **at least 15 copies per genome** was independently associated with 5-year distant metastasis. Risk groups combining viral load with adverse clinical features had reported 5-year distant-metastasis rates of **74% (high risk), 17% (intermediate), and 1% (low)**. [PMID: 25097016; sources: `call_86KAT7pkKeE3LDZ0ojNlmH5d`, `call_3HRxPkJcqGiJxZ1gPukL0Sax`]

This supports a predictive association in that study, but the available summary does not provide separate effect estimates for HPV-16 versus HPV-18, and a single cohort does not establish broad clinical validation.
