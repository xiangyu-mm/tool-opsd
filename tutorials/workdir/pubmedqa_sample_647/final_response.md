Yes. A 2014 study of 54 surgically resected pancreatic IPMN specimens (181 lesions) found that EZH2 expression increased with lesion grade and was higher in carcinoma in situ/invasive lesions than in adenoma or borderline lesions. EZH2-positive malignant cells lacked p27Kip1, and EZH2 and p27Kip1 mRNA levels were inversely correlated (p=0.0109); malignant lesions also had lower p27Kip1 mRNA (p<0.05). EZH2 expression correlated with Ki-67 proliferation (p<0.0001). (PMID: 25084021)

**Qualification:** These tissue and expression findings support an association consistent with EZH2-linked p27Kip1 downregulation, but the study was observational and does not by itself prove that EZH2 causes malignant progression through that mechanism.

FINAL DECISION: YES
