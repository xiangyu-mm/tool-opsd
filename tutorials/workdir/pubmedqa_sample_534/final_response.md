**Yes.** In healthy male Fischer 344 rats, aerosolized DPP-4 inhibitors L-isoleucine-thiazolidide (L-Ile-Thia) and L-valine-pyrrolidide (L-Val-Pyrr) produced different surfactant-protein expression patterns. SP-A and SP-D mRNA increased with L-Ile-Thia at 6 hours; with L-Val-Pyrr, SP-D and SP-B mRNA increased at different time points. L-Val-Pyrr also significantly increased SP-A, SP-B, and SP-C protein at 6 hours. These results support differential effects by inhibitor and protein, though the retrieved abstract does not provide numerical effect sizes or report a significant SP-D protein increase.
