A 2014 study specifically examined capsaicin during 3T3-L1 preadipocyte differentiation and reported that low-dose capsaicin increased brown-fat marker-gene expression and induced a brown-like (“brite”) phenotype. The effects were dose-dependent, so this does not mean all capsaicin concentrations induce browning.

**Source:** *Capsaicin induces “brite” phenotype in differentiating 3T3-L1 preadipocytes*, PLOS ONE (2014), PMID: [25072597](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25072597/).
