**No.** Geller and Cook’s family-based transmission disequilibrium tests found **no preferential transmission of the low-activity COMT allele** in the ultradian rapid-cycling subgroup or in the full prepubertal/early-adolescent bipolar sample. The study included 52 probands, 75% of whom had ultradian rapid cycling; the abstract reports nonsignificant tests. This means the study did not find transmission disequilibrium—it does not prove that no association could exist in other samples or age groups.
