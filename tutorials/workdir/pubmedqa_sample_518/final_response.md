Yes. Direct human evidence indicates that parathyroid hormone (PTH) is associated with LV mass after aortic valve replacement (AVR). In a study of 195 patients assessed an average of 8 years after AVR, higher PTH was independently associated with higher LV mass index (p=0.003) and LV hypertrophy (p=0.02). In a separate hemodialysis cohort, higher preoperative PTH was associated with less LV-mass regression after AVR.

These are observational findings; they do not establish causation, and the available evidence does not confirm a TAVR-specific association. Sources: PMID 25095827; PMID 24760625.

FINAL DECISION: YES
