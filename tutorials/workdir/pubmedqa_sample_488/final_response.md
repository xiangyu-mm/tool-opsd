**No—under the conditions studied, successive moderate hypoxia did not significantly affect glucose metabolism or overall substrate oxidation.**

The matching study compared eight healthy young men during a 7-hour resting trial in normoxia (FiO₂ 20.9%) and moderate hypoxia (FiO₂ 15.0%). They ate two standardized meals during each trial. Glucose and insulin rose after meals, but the responses and their 7-hour areas under the curve did not differ significantly between conditions. Average respiratory exchange ratio (RER)—a measure used to estimate substrate oxidation—also did not differ. Oxygen consumption and carbon dioxide production were higher at one time point during hypoxia, but this did not change RER or the overall averages.

The result is limited to a small sample and this short, resting exposure; it does not establish that longer or more severe hypoxia, or hypoxia combined with exercise, has no metabolic effects.

**Reference:** “Successive exposure to moderate hypoxia does not affect glucose metabolism and substrate oxidation in young healthy men.” PMID: 25089253.
