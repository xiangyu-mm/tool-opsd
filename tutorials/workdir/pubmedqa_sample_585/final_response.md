The available randomized trial supports Vagifem as superior to vaginal Premarin for increasing endometrial thickness in patients with refractory endometrium undergoing frozen-thawed embryo-transfer cycles.

- **Study:** Zolghadri et al., *Iranian Journal of Reproductive Medicine*, 2014; PMID: **25071850**, PMCID: **PMC4111890**
- **Design:** Randomized clinical trial
- **Participants:** 60 patients; 30 received vaginal Vagifem and 30 received vaginal Premarin
- **Outcome:** Endometrial thickness measured after 14 days
- **Result:** A statistically significant difference favored Vagifem (**p < 0.001**)
- **Authors’ conclusion:** Vagifem was superior to Premarin for inducing endometrial thickening.

However, the reported means are presented inconsistently in the abstract: **5.93 ± 0.38 versus 6.74 ± 0.32 mm**, while the text identifies Vagifem as the thicker group. This appears to be a group-ordering or typographical error. Also, the trial was small and primarily assessed endometrial thickness—not implantation, clinical pregnancy, miscarriage, or live-birth rates—so superiority for reproductive outcomes remains unproven.

FINAL DECISION: YES
