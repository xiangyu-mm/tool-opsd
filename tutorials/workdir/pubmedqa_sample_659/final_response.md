The evidence supports **yes** for maturation of GDF15, with an important qualification.

- Qiu et al. (JCEM 2014, **PMID:25093616**) identified a direct intracellular interaction between MMP26 and GDF15 using a placental yeast two-hybrid screen, validated by co-immunoprecipitation and immunofluorescence.
- The study reported that MMP26 binding and enzymatic activity promote processing/maturation of pro-GDF15 in trophoblast-related models, including HTR8/SVneo cells.
- Independent studies confirm that MMP26 and GDF15 are expressed in human placental trophoblast populations, while BeWo trophoblasts secrete GDF15. However, those studies did not establish that MMP26 is required for GDF15 secretion or overall production.
- The retrieved evidence does not provide a definitive MMP26 knockdown/knockout-rescue experiment or detailed cleavage-site analysis. Thus, the strongest conclusion is that MMP26-dependent interaction is supported for **pro-GDF15 maturation**, not necessarily for all aspects of GDF15 secretion.

FINAL DECISION: YES
