**Yes—for long-term mortality, but the evidence is limited.** In a prospective, single-center study of 126 patients with heart failure undergoing CRT, baseline plasma C3a above 165 ng/mL independently predicted 5-year mortality (HR 4.21, 95% CI 1.65–10.72; p=0.003; PMID: 27492980). C3a also improved reported risk reclassification beyond other factors.

This does **not** establish that C3a predicts CRT response, reverse remodeling, or hospitalization, nor that post-CRT C3a independently predicts outcomes. A separate study of only eight patients did not evaluate prognostic value (PMID: 16884512). Thus, C3a is a promising mortality prognostic marker, but validation in larger independent cohorts is needed.
