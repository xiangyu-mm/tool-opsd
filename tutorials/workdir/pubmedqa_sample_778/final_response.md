Yes. A direct comparative study of 35 patients with postherpetic neuralgia (PHN) and 34 patients with peripheral vestibular disease found different psychological profiles:

- **PHN patients:** significantly more symptoms of major depression and somatization.
- **Peripheral vestibular disease patients:** significantly more acute distressing somatic symptoms.
- **Formal psychiatric diagnoses:** no significant difference between groups.

The study used SCL-90-R measures and psychiatrist-administered diagnostic interviews. However, the available abstract does not report group means, effect sizes, or exact p-values, and the sample was small. Thus, the evidence supports a difference in symptom patterns—not necessarily a difference in the prevalence of diagnosable psychiatric disorders.

FINAL DECISION: YES
