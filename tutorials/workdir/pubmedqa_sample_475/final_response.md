Yes. Direct evidence supports topical vitamin K1 promoting repair of full-thickness skin wounds in rats:

- **PMID:25097279 (2014)**: In an excisional full-thickness wound model, 1% and 2% vitamin K1 cream applied daily improved wound contraction, re-epithelialization, hydroxyproline content, tensile strength, and histopathology versus controls (**P < 0.01**).
- **PMID:40187107 (2025)**: In diabetic rats, 1% topical vitamin K cream reduced wound size and improved collagen/hydroxyproline levels, oxidative and inflammatory markers, and histologic healing.

The evidence is based on animal studies and does not establish effectiveness in humans.

FINAL DECISION: YES
