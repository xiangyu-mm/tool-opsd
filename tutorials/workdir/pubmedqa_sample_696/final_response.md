The case-control study **“Chronic cerebrospinal venous insufficiency is not associated with chronic venous disorders”** (*Phlebology*, 2015; PMID: 25081746) compared 55 adults with CCSVI with 186 controls without CCSVI.

The study found similar chronic venous disorder prevalence between the groups and no statistically significant association. The reported odds ratios were 0.6 (95% CI 0.2–2.2) for CEAP class 1, 0.9 (95% CI 0.2–4.5) for CEAP class 2, and 1.0 (95% CI 0.6–1.9) for family history of varicose veins. The confidence intervals include 1, consistent with no statistically significant association. The authors concluded that CCSVI was not associated with chronic venous disorders or family history of varicose veins.

This is evidence from a case-control study; it does not prove that no association exists in every population, but it does not support an association in the studied groups.

FINAL DECISION: NO
