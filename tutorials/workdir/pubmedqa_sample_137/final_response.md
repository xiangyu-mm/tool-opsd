Yes. Two Taiwanese population-based cohort studies found higher coronary heart disease (CHD) incidence among people with fibromyalgia:

- **PMID 25436616**: revascularization-defined CHD (PCI or CABG); adjusted HR **2.11** (95% CI **1.46–3.05**).
- **PMID 26366998**: 61,612 fibromyalgia patients versus 184,834 matched controls; adjusted HR **1.47** (95% CI **1.43–1.51**) over a mean follow-up of 8.86 years.

The studies were observational, so they demonstrate an association rather than proving that fibromyalgia causes CHD. Both used Taiwanese health-claims data, and residual confounding or differences in diagnosis and healthcare utilization remain possible.

FINAL DECISION: YES
