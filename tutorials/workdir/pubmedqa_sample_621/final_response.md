Yes. The available evidence suggests that adalimumab significantly reduces acute anterior uveitis recurrence or flare risk in ankylosing spondylitis:

- A nationwide cohort found lower recurrence risk versus no biologic-DMARD exposure: adjusted HR 0.798 (95% CI 0.659–0.968).
- Compared with adalimumab, etanercept had significantly higher uveitis risk: HR 1.793 (95% CI 1.403–2.292).
- A Korean cohort likewise found higher uveitis incidence with etanercept than adalimumab, including among patients with previous uveitis (IRR 1.86).

However, the strongest randomized evidence supports anti-TNF monoclonal antibodies as a class rather than adalimumab specifically, and the available studies do not provide adalimumab-specific absolute recurrence rates. Thus, the conclusion is supported but based substantially on observational comparative evidence.

FINAL DECISION: YES
