The presentation is most consistent with **erysipelas**, a superficial form of nonpurulent skin infection characterized by:

- Sharply demarcated erythema  
- Warmth and significant pain  
- Fever and tachycardia  
- No fluctuance, drainage, or abscess  

Erysipelas is classically caused by **β-hemolytic streptococci, especially *Streptococcus pyogenes* (Group A Streptococcus)**. Although *Staphylococcus aureus* can infect peripheral IV sites, it more commonly produces purulence, abscesses, or drainage.

The other organisms generally require specific exposures or risk factors such as bites, burns, water exposure, immunocompromise, necrotic tissue, or chronic wounds.
