A preclinical study found **no detectable toxicity to normal brain cells under the conditions tested**. Healthy, immunocompetent rats received intracerebral lentiviral HSV-Tk.007 and 14 days of ganciclovir. The researchers reported that transduced normal brain cells survived, with no significant treatment-control differences in behavior or transduced-cell counts and no observed brain-cell inflammation or apoptosis (PMID: 27490042).

This is reassuring evidence, but it does not establish that the therapy is risk-free in humans or under different doses, treatment durations, or clinical conditions. **For the tested rat model and regimen, the study’s answer is no.**

FINAL DECISION: NO
