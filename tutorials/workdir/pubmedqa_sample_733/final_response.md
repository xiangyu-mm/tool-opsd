Yes. A primary in-vitro study (PMID: 10752932, IOVS 2000) reported that **cholestanol (5α-cholestan-3β-ol; dihydrocholesterol)** induced apoptosis in both:

- **Corneal endothelial cells**
- **Lens epithelial cells**

At 10 µg/mL, cholestanol caused reduced viability, increased TUNEL-positive nuclei, time-dependent apoptotic DNA fragmentation, and increased ICE and CPP32/caspase-3-like activity. Cholesterol and ethanol vehicle controls did not produce the same apoptotic response.

The evidence is limited to an in-vitro study and does not establish effects at physiological concentrations or in vivo.

FINAL DECISION: YES
