The available evidence supports an association.

- **Ethnic Kazakh population:** In Xinjiang, China, the 2014 study (PMID **25448630**) included 800 ethnic Kazakhs and found that CMV seropositivity was independently associated with essential hypertension in Kazakh men; a CMV gB2+gB3 genotype combination was associated with hypertension in Kazakh women. A follow-up study of 720 ethnic Kazakhs (PMID **29752343**) found higher HCMV seropositivity among hypertensive participants (**P=0.04**) and correlations between HCMV IgG levels and blood pressure.
- **Han Chinese population:** The same 2014 study found that CMV antibody titers were independently associated with hypertension and blood pressure in Han participants. A 2017 study of 563 Han Chinese participants (PMID **28837559**) found higher CMV IgG levels in hypertension, increasing levels with hypertension grade and target-organ damage, and positive correlations with arterial blood pressure.
- **Limitations:** These are observational, largely case-control/cross-sectional studies, so they demonstrate association rather than causation. Also, the Kazakh studies involved **ethnic Kazakhs living in Xinjiang, China**, not a nationally representative Kazakhstan-resident cohort.
