Yes. The study directly relevant to this question (PMID 25436996) found elevated CD100/Sema4D on B cells in people with chronic hepatitis C. In a longitudinal study of 20 patients receiving pegylated interferon-α plus ribavirin, treatment further increased B-cell CD100 among early virological responders; in-vitro interferon-α exposure also induced it. CD100 levels moved toward normal after sustained virological response.

This supports a relationship between interferon-α-induced B-cell CD100 up-regulation and chronic HCV infection or its treatment response. It does **not** establish that HCV itself directly induces CD100 on B cells, or that CD100 causes viral clearance.

FINAL DECISION: YES
