Yes. The primary human study (Lankelma et al., 2016; PMID: 27489950) evaluated 12 healthy young men who received ciprofloxacin, vancomycin, and metronidazole for 7 days.

Key findings:

- Antibiotics significantly reduced gut microbial diversity.
- One day after treatment, isolated mononuclear cells produced less TNF-α after ex vivo lipopolysaccharide stimulation.
- The reduced TNF-α response returned to baseline six weeks after antibiotics were stopped.

This was a small proof-of-principle study, so it supports a reversible association rather than proving a permanent or universal effect.

FINAL DECISION: YES
