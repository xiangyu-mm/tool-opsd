The marker was conjunctival ultraviolet autofluorescence (CUVAF): the later Raine Study review describes a negative relationship between myopia and this objective measure of sun exposure, with smaller CUVAF area indicating less exposure (PMID: 35570945).

This supports an inverse association, not proof that sun exposure itself prevents myopia; the evidence is observational.
