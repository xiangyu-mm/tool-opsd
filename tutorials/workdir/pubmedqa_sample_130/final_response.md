## Evidence summary

The key primary study is:

- **PMID: 25424483**, *Molecular Brain* (2014)
- Model: **primary cultured rat microglia**
- Treatment: **simvastatin, 20 μM**
- Simvastatin reduced cellular cholesterol by approximately **18%** without detectable toxicity.

The study found two distinct response patterns:

1. **Cytokine/trophic-factor release:** Simvastatin altered release of mediators including **IL-1β, TNF-α, and BDNF**. These secretory effects were reported to depend on cellular cholesterol.
2. **Phagocytosis:** Simvastatin inhibited microglial phagocytic activity, but this inhibition was reported to be **cholesterol-independent**.

Because cholesterol depletion affected mediator release but was not required for the phagocytic inhibition, the authors concluded that the two effects operate through **separable mechanisms**. The evidence is pharmacologic/mechanistic rather than genetic: the study did not establish the precise molecular pathways using knockout, knockdown, rescue, or epistasis experiments.

Thus, the findings support **functional or operational separability**, while leaving the exact molecular identities of the two mechanisms unresolved.

FINAL DECISION: YES
