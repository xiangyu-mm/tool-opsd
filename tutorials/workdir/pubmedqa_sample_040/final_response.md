- **Population:** 510 subjects with active subfoveal choroidal neovascularization secondary to neovascular AMD.
- **Comparison:** Multiple pazopanib eye-drop regimens versus monthly intravitreal ranibizumab; placebo eye drops with as-needed ranibizumab were also included.

- **Findings:** Pazopanib-containing regimens maintained visual acuity comparably to ranibizumab, but did not achieve the prespecified ≥50% reduction in rescue ranibizumab injections. The study concluded that pazopanib eye drops offered **no therapeutic benefit beyond ranibizumab alone**.
