Yes—one discovery GWAS identified an **NBEA variant, rs1160720**, associated with migraine among people with bipolar disorder.

- **Study:** Jacobsen et al., 2015, *Journal of Affective Disorders*; **PMID: 25451450**
- **Comparison:** 460 bipolar patients with migraine versus 914 bipolar patients without migraine.
- **Variant:** Intronic **NBEA rs1160720**
- **Association:** *P* = 2.97 × 10⁻⁸; OR = 1.82; 95% CI 1.47–2.25.
- **Interpretation:** This was a genome-wide significant discovery signal for **migraine comorbidity in bipolar disorder**, not evidence that NBEA is a general bipolar-disorder risk gene.
- **Limitation:** The association was not replicated in a follow-up sample of 289 migraine cases and was not supported by a large general-migraine GWAS meta-analysis.
