The available rodent evidence supports that **resveratrol treatment can prevent or reduce hippocampal neurodegeneration after traumatic brain injury**:

- In 7-day-old rats with contusion TBI, a single 100 mg/kg intraperitoneal dose immediately after injury significantly reduced neuron loss in both hippocampi at 2 weeks (PMID: 17531385).
- Other rat TBI studies reported improved hippocampal histopathology and reduced oxidative/inflammatory markers after resveratrol, although neuronal survival was not consistently quantified (PMIDs: 27509464, 38189498).

This conclusion is strongest for experimental rat models and should not be generalized directly to humans. The most direct study used immature rats and did not provide effect sizes or cell counts.

FINAL DECISION: YES
