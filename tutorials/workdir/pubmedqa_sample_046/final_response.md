Yes—**in the available rat model**, diet-induced obesity prevented development of most acute traumatic coagulopathy changes.

In PMID **25423535**, obesity-prone rats fed a high-fat diet did not develop the shock-associated prolongation of TEG R/K times or reductions in α-angle and coagulation index seen in control and obesity-resistant rats. However, maximum amplitude decreased in all groups. The study was small, preclinical, and phenotype-specific, so it does not establish the same effect in humans.

FINAL DECISION: YES
