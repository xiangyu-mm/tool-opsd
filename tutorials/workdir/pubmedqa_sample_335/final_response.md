**Yes—preliminary evidence suggests plasma Galectin-9 reflects malaria severity.** In one Thai cohort of 50 acute malaria patients (9 severe, 41 uncomplicated), median plasma Galectin-9 was higher in severe cases at presentation: **923 vs 617 pg/mL** (*P*=0.03). It remained higher on day 7: **659 vs 348 pg/mL** (*P*=0.02), and levels declined over follow-up. Higher levels were also associated with a renal dysfunction marker and acute inflammatory mediators. [PMID: 27515948; search result `call_j0hzBoYBLna4KtgsuJInTVdd`]
