**Yes—specifically for acetylcholine-mediated vasodilation.** In an ex-vivo rat middle cerebral artery study after 2 hours of focal ischemia, the increase in diameter at the tested acetylcholine concentration was **8±2%** with ischemia plus rtPA versus **17±3%** with ischemia alone (control: **23±4%**). This indicates greater attenuation in the presence of rtPA (PMID: 10754003).

The effect was not uniform across the tested responses: the study did **not** find further worsening of serotonin-mediated vasoconstrictor sensitivity with rtPA.
