In the directly relevant 2016 cohort study, 77 high-risk patients colonized with colistin-resistant, KPC-producing *Klebsiella pneumoniae* were followed for 180 days; 44 received oral aminoglycoside decontamination. After multivariable analysis including a propensity score, decontamination was associated with lower mortality (HR 0.18, 95% CI 0.06–0.55) and fewer KPC-*K. pneumoniae* infections (HR 0.14, 95% CI 0.02–0.83).

This is evidence of an association, not proof that treatment caused the reduction: the study was small and nonrandomized, so residual confounding is possible. **Source:** PMID 27494911.
