**Yes.** A direct in-vitro study in primary rat vascular smooth muscle cells found that ezetimibe reduced cholesterol accumulation in cells loaded with cholesterol–methyl-β-cyclodextrin. At 3 μmol/L, it significantly lowered intracellular total cholesterol, cholesterol ester, and free cholesterol.

MAPK involvement is supported by reduced ERK1/2 phosphorylation with ezetimibe and by similar changes after treatment with the ERK1/2 inhibitor PD98059. The authors therefore concluded that the effect was *possibly* mediated through MAPK signaling; the evidence supports this mechanism but does not establish it definitively.

**Source:** Qin L, et al. *Acta Pharmacol Sin.* 2014;35(9):1129–1136. PMID: 25087996. doi:10.1038/aps.2014.10.
