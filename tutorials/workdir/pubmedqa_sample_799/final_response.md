Evidence supports that dietary vitamin B6 supplementation prevents ethanol-induced hypertension in rats.

- **Direct study:** PMID **10726110**, *“Dietary vitamin B6 supplementation prevents ethanol-induced hypertension in rats.”*
- **Model:** Female Wistar-Kyoto rats, 14 weeks.
- **Exposure:** 1% ethanol in drinking water.
- **Intervention:** Diet containing approximately **20× normal vitamin B6**.
- **Outcome:** Ethanol significantly increased systolic blood pressure, whereas high-B6 supplementation prevented this increase. It also prevented associated platelet calcium elevation, tissue aldehyde-conjugate accumulation, and renal vascular smooth-muscle hyperplasia.
- **Limitations:** The available abstract does not report numerical blood-pressure values, exact ethanol intake, or the specific B6 vitamer. This is evidence from a single rat study and does not establish effectiveness in humans.

FINAL DECISION: YES
