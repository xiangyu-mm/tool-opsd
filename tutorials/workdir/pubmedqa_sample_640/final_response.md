## Evidence summary

The most direct evidence is a randomized controlled trial in 102 adults with non-specific neck pain (PMID: 25097535; Pakistan Journal of Medical Sciences, 2014):

- **SNAGs + isometric exercise:** 51 participants  
- **SNAGs alone:** 51 participants  
- Treatment lasted approximately six weeks.

### Findings

- **Disability:** SNAGs plus isometric exercise produced greater improvement in Neck Disability Index scores:
  - Combination: **40 → 15**
  - SNAGs alone: **42 → 30**
  - Between-group **p = 0.003**
- **Pain:** A statistically significant between-group result was reported (**p = 0.013**), but both groups were reported to improve from **VAS 7 → 4**, making the magnitude and reliability of the added pain benefit unclear.
- **Range of motion:** Not reported in this direct trial.
- **Long-term outcomes:** Not assessed.

## Interpretation

The study supports a likely additional **disability benefit** when isometric exercise is added to SNAGs. However, confidence is limited because this conclusion relies mainly on one trial with incomplete methodological reporting, inconsistent treatment-duration descriptions, questionable pain-result reporting, no range-of-motion data, and no long-term follow-up.

Other evidence is less definitive:

- A separate randomized trial found SNAGs were not superior to other manual therapies for pain, disability, or cervical range of motion (PMID: 24467843).
- A 2025 systematic review found that combined SNAG interventions may be beneficial in some chronic neck-pain populations, but rated the certainty of evidence **very low** (PMID: 40439260).
- Evidence for range-of-motion improvement comes mainly from indirect populations and should not be assumed to apply directly to non-specific neck pain.

Therefore, SNAGs appear effective for short-term improvement in non-specific neck pain, and adding isometric exercise **may enhance disability outcomes**. The evidence for an additional pain benefit is uncertain, and superiority has not been established with high confidence.

**FINAL DECISION: YES**
