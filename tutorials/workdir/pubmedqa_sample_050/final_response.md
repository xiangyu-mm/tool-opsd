Yes, based on the available **preclinical evidence**.

- In a murine HER2/OVA melanoma model, α-galactosylceramide-loaded CD1d–anti-HER2 fusion protein combined with OVA peptide/CpG vaccination produced greater tumor inhibition than either treatment alone, with enhanced dendritic-cell, IL-12, NK, and tumor-specific CD8 responses.
- In B16F10 melanoma, adding α-galactosylceramide to CpG plus melanoma peptides reduced tumor volume approximately fivefold versus comparable formulations without α-galactosylceramide.
- In an HPV TC-1 tumor model, combining α-galactosylceramide and CpG with HPV E6/E7 peptide vaccination produced sustained tumor elimination in more than 85% of mice, whereas either adjuvant alone was insufficient.

Thus, iNKT/CD1d activation appears to significantly enhance therapeutic CpG/peptide vaccination in mouse models. However, the evidence is heterogeneous, mostly evaluates tumor growth rather than rigorously reported survival, and does not establish clinical efficacy in humans.

FINAL DECISION: YES
