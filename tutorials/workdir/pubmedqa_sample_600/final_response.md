**No significant increase was detected** in the Swedish cohort study by Hesselman et al. Among 7,683 women attempting vaginal birth after one prior cesarean, uterine rupture occurred in 103 (1.3%). Single-layer versus double-layer closure had an odds ratio of **1.17 (95% CI 0.78–1.76)**, so the result was not statistically significant.

The confidence interval is fairly wide, however: the study does not establish that the risks are equivalent and does not rule out a moderate increase. **Source:** Hesselman et al., 2015, PMID 25088680, DOI: 10.1111/1471-0528.13015.
