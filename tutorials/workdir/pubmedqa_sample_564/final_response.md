The evidence supports **yes**.

- **Heme oxygenase is required:** In *L. interrogans*, disruption of **hemO** caused poor growth when hemoglobin was the sole iron source (PMID: **18524663**). A later study also linked HemO loss to reduced virulence (PMID: **19114124**).
- **Plastidic-type FNR supports heme utilization:** Purified *L. interrogans* heme oxygenase (LepHO) required the plastidic-type ferredoxin–NADP(+) reductase (LepFNR) as its electron donor for complete heme degradation, releasing free iron and α-biliverdin (PMID: **25092651**).
- **Qualification:** The heme oxygenase requirement was demonstrated genetically and biochemically. LepFNR’s role was demonstrated directly in reconstituted biochemical assays; the cited study did not report a LepFNR knockout or complementation experiment proving its in vivo growth requirement.

Thus, for the described heme-degradation route, both proteins are required or functionally necessary, although the evidence for LepFNR is primarily biochemical rather than genetic.

FINAL DECISION: YES
