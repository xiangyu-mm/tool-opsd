Yes. In cell-culture experiments, myriocin accelerated HSV-2 replication and increased infectious-virus titers. Effects were detectable at 3 nM; at 30 nM, virus production increased by about 1.7 log. The study did not establish the mechanism, and these findings do not demonstrate increased infection in people.

Source: Wang et al., *Life Sciences* (2015), PMID: 25447452; DOI: 10.1016/j.lfs.2014.11.004.

FINAL DECISION: YES
