**Yes for rectal diclofenac:** the evidence supports reduced post-ERCP pancreatitis (PEP) incidence. A meta-analysis of 25 NSAID trials involving 7,688 patients found lower PEP occurrence with NSAIDs overall (pooled estimate 0.598, 95% CI 0.47–0.76) and reported benefit with rectal diclofenac (PMID: 37981523). A real-world study using rectal diclofenac with selective pancreatic-duct stenting also reported lower PEP odds versus historical controls, though the combined intervention limits attribution to diclofenac alone (PMID: 37160838).

**Intramuscular diclofenac:** I did not find route-specific evidence establishing that it reduces PEP incidence. The rectal evidence should not be assumed to apply to the intramuscular route.
