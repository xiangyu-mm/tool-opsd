The primary study by Meldau, Wu, and Baldwin (2014; PMID: 25430398; DOI: 10.1186/1471-2229-14-210) concluded that fatty acid–amino acid conjugates (FACs) are essential for the tested systemic responses in *Nicotiana attenuata*.

Key evidence:

- Wounding alone did not produce detectable systemic SIPK activation or systemic jasmonic acid (JA/JA-Ile) accumulation.
- Applying *Manduca sexta* oral secretion, which contains FACs, to wounds strongly induced SIPK activation and JA/JA-Ile accumulation in selected undamaged systemic leaves.
- The effect was spatially selective and required the wound-associated delivery context; FACs were not shown to be sufficient as a standalone systemic signal.
- Therefore, FACs are necessary components of the wound-plus-herbivore-elicitor pathway leading to systemic SIPK and JA responses.

FINAL DECISION: YES
