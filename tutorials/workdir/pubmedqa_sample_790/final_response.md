A randomized crossover study in 15 young healthy women found that adding **15 g of inulin** to breakfast did **not significantly change serum ionized calcium or intact PTH** over 8 hours. Samples were collected before breakfast and at 2, 4, 6, and 8 hours. The result was similar in comparisons using either cheese or a calcium supplement as the calcium source; the reported 8-hour concentration-curve AUCs did not differ between inulin and control conditions. The study did not provide exact p-values in the available report. (PMID: 10725769)

This supports **no detected acute effect in the tested setting**; it does not establish that inulin has no effects in other populations, at other doses, or over longer periods.
