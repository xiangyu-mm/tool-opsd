- Observed mortality: **9.68%**
- Additive EuroSCORE predicted mortality: **5.0%**  
  - Observed-to-expected ratio: **1.94**
- Logistic EuroSCORE predicted mortality: **5.6%**  
  - Observed-to-expected ratio: **1.73**
- Calibration was poor for both models (**Hosmer–Lemeshow P < 0.001**).
- Underestimation was concentrated in intermediate- and high-risk patients:
  - Intermediate risk: **6.34% observed vs 3.9% predicted**
  - High risk: **17.4% observed vs 7.64% predicted**
- In the low-risk group, EuroSCORE actually overpredicted mortality (**0.7% observed vs 2.0% predicted**).

Thus, in this Mexican valve-surgery cohort, EuroSCORE underestimated mortality overall, particularly for patients at intermediate or high predicted risk. Evidence is based primarily on this single valve-specific cohort, so generalization to all Mexican cardiac surgery populations should be cautious.
