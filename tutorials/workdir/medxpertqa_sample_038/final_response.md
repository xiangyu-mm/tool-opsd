The most likely diagnosis is **bacterial tracheitis**, characterized by high fever, toxic appearance, hypoxemia, inspiratory stridor, retractions, and failure to improve with racemic epinephrine. The most common organism is **Staphylococcus aureus**, often MSSA.

- **Parainfluenza virus type 1** causes typical croup, which generally improves transiently with racemic epinephrine.
- **H. influenzae type b** epiglottitis is unlikely in a fully vaccinated adolescent and would more commonly cause drooling, muffled voice, and tripod positioning.
- The remaining organisms do not fit the acute, severe upper-airway obstruction as well.
