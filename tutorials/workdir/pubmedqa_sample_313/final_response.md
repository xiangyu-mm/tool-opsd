**Likely answer: yes, with an important qualification.** Expression-dependent, often RDR6-dependent and Pol IV-independent, RdDM is associated with transcriptionally active elements. Full-length autonomous elements can produce transcripts that feed this route to small-RNA production and DNA methylation, making them plausible preferential targets compared with elements that do not produce such transcripts. Nuthikattu et al.’s study, *“The initiation of epigenetic silencing of active transposable elements is triggered by RDR6 and 21–22 nucleotide small interfering RNAs”* (Plant Physiology, 2013), is relevant to this model.

That does **not** mean full-length autonomous elements are the only targets, or that every such element is methylated.
