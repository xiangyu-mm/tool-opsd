Yes—statin use was associated with slower progression to cirrhosis in a large observational cohort of 5,985 people with HIV/HCV coinfection. Among participants with ALT ≤40 IU/L, each 30% increase in time with an active statin prescription was associated with a 32% lower cirrhosis hazard (HR 0.68, 95% CI 0.47–0.98).

This is suggestive, not proof that statins prevent cirrhosis: the study was observational, and the reported protective association was limited to that ALT subgroup. The cirrhosis outcome was defined by an ICD-9 diagnosis or APRI >2. **Source:** PMID 27753678, *AIDS* (2016).

FINAL DECISION: YES
