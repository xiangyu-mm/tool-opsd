The primary study analyzed 22 dairy-origin isolates alongside 47 reference genomes and resolved nine clades. Clade IV isolates consistently carried *hblACD* and produced Hbl; clade III-c isolates lacked the genes and did not produce Hbl. Clade IV was significantly more likely to produce Hbl (**p = 0.0001**). However, clade VI isolates carried *hbl* homologues without detectable Hbl, so the association is not deterministic.
