Yes. In the direct study PMID 25070024, the esophageal adenocarcinoma cell lines **PT6216** and **LN6216c**, both showing homogeneously high EGFR polysomy by FISH, were highly sensitive to gefitinib in proliferation and cell-vitality assays (source: `call_3whxRId7wp1dvygLXXT7BAgx`).

However, the evidence is limited: quantitative IC₅₀ values and complete EGFR mutation/expression data were not reported, so high EGFR polysomy should be considered **associated with** gefitinib response rather than proven to predict response independently.

FINAL DECISION: YES
