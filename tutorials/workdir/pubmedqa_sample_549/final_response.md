**The available direct evidence does not show that chromosome instability predicts chemotherapy response in synovial sarcoma.** In a study of 65 patients, genomic complexity was measured by array-CGH; 45 received neoadjuvant anthracycline/ifosfamide chemotherapy. Genomic complexity was **not significantly associated** with either radiologic response (RECIST) or histologic response. It was associated with metastasis-free survival, suggesting a prognostic relationship distinct from chemotherapy sensitivity (PMID: 25070544).

This is a limited retrospective cohort, so it cannot rule out smaller or regimen-specific effects. But on the evidence found, the answer is **no**.
