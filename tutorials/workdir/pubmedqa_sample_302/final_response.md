## Findings

The exact matching study is:

**Liu et al.** “Plasma MicroRNA-126-5p is Associated with the Complexity and Severity of Coronary Artery Disease in Patients with Stable Angina Pectoris.” *Cellular Physiology and Biochemistry*. 2016. **PMID: 27497911**.

- **Population:** 150 participants, including 110 patients with stable angina and angiographically documented coronary disease.
- **Biomarker:** Plasma miR-126-5p measured using qRT-PCR.
- **CAD complexity/extent:** Assessed by the number of diseased vessels and presence of multivessel disease.
- **CAD angiographic complexity/severity:** Assessed using the SYNTAX score.
- **Main finding:** Plasma miR-126-5p was significantly lower in patients with multivessel disease and in those with higher SYNTAX scores. Thus, lower circulating miR-126-5p was associated with greater CAD complexity and severity.
- **Limitations:** The available evidence does not provide detailed effect sizes, confidence intervals, or clear confirmation that the associations remained statistically independent after adjustment for age, dyslipidemia, and diabetes. The study was observational and does not establish causality.

Therefore, the evidence supports an association with **both** coronary disease complexity and angiographic severity, although the strength and independence of the association remain uncertain.
