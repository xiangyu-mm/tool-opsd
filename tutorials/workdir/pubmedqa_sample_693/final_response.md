**Yes.** In isolated, pressurized rat gracilis muscle arterioles, H₂O₂ (10–100 µM) caused concentration-dependent constriction. In the primary study, inhibiting phospholipase A (PLA), cyclooxygenase (COX), or thromboxane A₂ (TXA₂) receptors reduced or reversed the constriction; COX-1 inhibition had a stronger effect than COX-2 inhibition. The authors concluded that endothelial signaling activates PLA and COX-1-dependent TXA₂ production, which promotes constriction (PMID: 25093847).

This supports AA-pathway involvement in this experimental preparation; it does not establish that the same mechanism applies in every vascular bed or physiological setting.
