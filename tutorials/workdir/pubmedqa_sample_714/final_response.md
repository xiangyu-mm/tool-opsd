Yes—**age was associated with specialist management in one adult asthma cohort**. In a Danish nationwide study, among patients aged 18–45 with possible severe asthma, those aged **36–45 had lower odds of specialist management than those aged 18–25** (OR 0.66, 95% CI 0.51–0.85). Overall, 61% of the possible severe-asthma group were managed in primary care alone (PMID: 33986027).

This supports an age-related difference in that setting, but the evidence is limited to younger adults in Denmark with possible severe asthma. It does not establish the pattern for all adults with asthma or for specialist assessment of other kinds.
