**Answer:** Yes, in the preclinical models reported. The 2014 study *“Ibulocydine sensitizes human cancers to radiotherapy by induction of mitochondria-mediated apoptosis”* (PMID: 25082098) reports that ibulocydine plus radiotherapy reduced viability and clonogenic survival in human lung and colon cancer cells, and delayed tumor growth in a xenograft model. Evidence consistent with mitochondria-mediated apoptosis included loss of mitochondrial membrane potential, cytochrome-c release, caspase activation, a reduced Bcl-2/Bax ratio, and attenuation of the response when BCL-2 was restored.

The conclusion is limited: this is one preclinical study, assessed from an abstract-level record. It does not establish clinical radiosensitization or clinical benefit in patients.
