Yes. A 2020 systematic review and meta-analysis of 14 studies involving 108,416 participants found higher odds of Barrett esophagus in people with metabolic syndrome: **OR 1.354 (95% CI 1.145–1.600; P<0.001)**.

The result showed substantial heterogeneity (**I²=81.95%**), so the size of the association may vary across studies. This supports an association, but does not establish that metabolic syndrome causes Barrett esophagus.  
**Source:** Karimian et al., *BMC Gastroenterology* (2020), PMID: 32375671; DOI: 10.1186/s12876-020-01267-2.

FINAL DECISION: YES
