Yes. In mouse forced swim tests, ifenprodil can modify antidepressant-like activity, generally by **potentiating** the reduction in immobility produced by subeffective doses:

- **Imipramine:** potentiated by ifenprodil 10 mg/kg.
- **Fluoxetine:** potentiated by ifenprodil 10 mg/kg.
- **Paroxetine:** potentiated by ifenprodil 0.5 mg/kg.
- **Lithium:** potentiated by ifenprodil 0.5 mg/kg.

However, the effect is not universal: no potentiation was detected with **reboxetine** or **tianeptine** under the tested conditions. The studies did not demonstrate that ifenprodil inhibits antidepressant activity.
