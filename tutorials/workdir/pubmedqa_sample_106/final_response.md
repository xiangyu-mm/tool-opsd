**No general association is supported by the evidence reviewed.** A case-control study of 45 people with CTEPH and 36 chronic pulmonary embolism controls found no differences in free iron, ferritin, soluble transferrin receptor, or hepcidin; the measured iron-status markers were within normal limits (PMID: 25449047).

Iron overload mentioned in a separate report occurred in a patient with thalassemia and CTEPH; it was discussed as a feature of thalassemia, not as an effect of CTEPH (PMID: 30088118).
