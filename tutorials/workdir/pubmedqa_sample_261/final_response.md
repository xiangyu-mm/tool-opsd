In the isolated porcine kidney study (PMID: 27500213), kidneys treated with cyclic Helix B peptide (CHBP; 10.56 nmol/L) showed significantly better renal blood flow, oxygen consumption, and urine output during reperfusion, alongside lower serum potassium and less tissue damage. The study also reported fewer tubular apoptotic and MPO-positive cells and lower caspase-3 expression, with increased HSP70.

This supports CHBP ameliorating ischemia-reperfusion injury in this experimental model. The evidence is limited to an isolated-organ study, and the retrieved abstract did not provide effect sizes or clarify whether the reported *n*=5 was per group or overall.
