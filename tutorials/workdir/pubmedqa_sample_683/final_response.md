Yes. The key study (PMID:25078447) directly compared IVIG treatment in wild-type and IL-11 receptor alpha–deficient mice with MOG35–55-induced EAE.

- In wild-type mice, daily high-dose IVIG nearly prevented EAE.
- IL-11Rα-deficient mice showed only transient initial protection and subsequently developed EAE with incidence and severity similar to untreated controls.
- IVIG also failed to suppress myelin-reactive Th17 cytokine production without an intact receptor.

Thus, an intact IL-11 receptor is required for the **optimal and sustained attenuation** of EAE by IVIG, although some early partial protection may occur independently of it.

FINAL DECISION: YES
