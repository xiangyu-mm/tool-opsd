**Yes—with qualification.** In the Argentine CONAART cohort of 848 people with early arthritis, unemployment was similar for rheumatoid arthritis and undifferentiated arthritis (21.0% vs 18.6%; not statistically significant). Disease activity was the main multivariable correlate of unemployment in both groups, supporting the authors’ conclusion that work disability relates more to arthritis involvement and activity than to the specific diagnostic label.

The measure was primarily **unemployment**, not a comprehensive work-capacity assessment, and the reported abstract does not provide adjusted effect estimates for the diagnosis comparison. The evidence therefore supports the claim in this cohort, but does not establish that diagnosis never matters.
