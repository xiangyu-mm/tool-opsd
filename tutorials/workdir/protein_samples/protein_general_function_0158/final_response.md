## Functional summary

This 106-amino-acid protein is an **exact full-length sequence match** to reviewed UniProt **FlhD (Q3JY54)** from *Burkholderia pseudomallei*. This supports a strong assignment as the **FlhD flagellar transcriptional regulator**.

FlhD acts with FlhC as a master regulator: the complex binds promoter regions and activates class 2 flagellar genes, including *fliA*, which helps drive later flagellar gene expression. FlhD also regulates some non-flagellar operons. The reference annotation places it in the **cytoplasm** and describes an assembly containing **four FlhD and two FlhC subunits**; FlhD forms dimers.
