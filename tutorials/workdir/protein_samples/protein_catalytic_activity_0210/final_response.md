## Catalytic reaction

\[
\text{S-adenosyl-L-methionine} + \mathrm{H^+}
\rightarrow
\text{S-adenosyl 3-(methylsulfanyl)propylamine}
+ \mathrm{CO_2}
\]

The product is commonly called **decarboxylated S-adenosylmethionine** or **dcAdoMet**.

## Biological role

SAMDC performs the committed decarboxylation step in **polyamine biosynthesis**. Its dcAdoMet product donates an aminopropyl group to:

- **Putrescine**, producing **spermidine**
- **Spermidine**, producing **spermine**

These reactions are carried out subsequently by spermidine synthase and spermine synthase, respectively.

## Catalytic explanation

This is a **pyruvoyl-dependent decarboxylase**. The SAMDC proenzyme is processed to generate a covalently attached pyruvoyl cofactor, which participates in substrate decarboxylation and stabilizes reaction intermediates. The enzyme removes the carboxyl group from S-adenosyl-L-methionine, releasing carbon dioxide and forming dcAdoMet.
