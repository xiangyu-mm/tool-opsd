## Functional assignment

Its closest reviewed references align across about 94% of the query, with 49–55% sequence identity. **Reaction catalyzed (reductive direction):**

\[
\text{4-phospho-L-aspartate} + \text{NADPH} + \text{H}^+
\rightarrow
\text{L-aspartate 4-semialdehyde} + \text{phosphate} + \text{NADP}^+
\]

In other words, the enzyme uses **NADPH** to convert 4-phospho-L-aspartate to L-aspartate 4-semialdehyde while releasing phosphate. The product feeds the aspartate-derived biosynthetic routes to **lysine, methionine, and threonine**. The referenced Rhea entry records the reaction as reversible/unspecified in direction.
