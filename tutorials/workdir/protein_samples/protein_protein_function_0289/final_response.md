gp160 is the viral envelope precursor. In the host cell, it assembles mainly as a trimer in the **endoplasmic reticulum**, passes through the **Golgi**, and is cleaved in the trans-Golgi by furin-like proteases into **gp120** and **gp41**. These associate as a trimer of gp120–gp41 pairs to form Env spikes.

- **gp120** binds the host receptor **CD4**, then engages a chemokine coreceptor—typically **CCR5 or CXCR4**—to promote viral entry.
- **gp41** functions as a class I fusion protein, driving fusion of viral and host membranes so the viral contents can enter the host cell.
