## Functional summary

The 538-aa sequence is an **exact sequence match** to reviewed mammalian annotations for **mitochondrial pyruvate dehydrogenase phosphatase 1 (PDP1)**. It contains a detected **PP2C-family phosphatase domain** (residues 203–441) and is assigned **EC 3.1.3.43**.

Its catalytic reaction is hydrolysis of a phosphoserine on the E1α subunit of the pyruvate dehydrogenase complex:

\[
\text{O-phospho-L-seryl–PDH E1}\alpha + \mathrm{H_2O}
\rightarrow
\text{L-seryl–PDH E1}\alpha + \mathrm{P_i}
\]

This dephosphorylation **reactivates E1α**, thereby promoting the complex’s conversion of pyruvate to acetyl-CoA. PDP1 catalyzes the dephosphorylation step; it does **not** itself catalyze pyruvate’s conversion to acetyl-CoA. The reference annotations report Mg²⁺ binding, with Mn²⁺ able to substitute for catalytic activity.
