The assignment is highly confident because:

## Catalytic reaction

GidB methylates the **N7 position of guanine 527 in bacterial 16S rRNA**, using S-adenosyl-L-methionine (SAM) as the methyl donor:

\[
\text{16S rRNA-G527} + \text{SAM}
\rightarrow
\text{16S rRNA-}N^7\text{-methyl-G527} + \text{S-adenosyl-L-homocysteine}
\]

Thus, the enzyme is an **RNA guanine-N7 methyltransferase** involved in maturation or modification of the small ribosomal subunit. It is expected to function in the bacterial cytoplasm.
