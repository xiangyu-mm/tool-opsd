## Functional assignment

The 253-amino-acid protein is very likely **UbiE**, the **S-adenosyl-L-methionine (SAM)-dependent C-methyltransferase** involved in menaquinone and ubiquinone biosynthesis.

### Evidence

- Length: **253 aa**
- Predicted molecular mass: **~27.9 kDa**
- Matches reviewed UbiE proteins over the full sequence:
  - **81–83% amino-acid identity**
  - **100% coverage**
  - E-values approximately **10⁻¹⁵⁰**
- Homologs are assigned EC activities **2.1.1.163** and **2.1.1.201**.

## Catalytic activity

UbiE transfers a methyl group from **SAM** to a quinone or quinol intermediate. SAM is converted to **S-adenosyl-L-homocysteine (SAH)**.

### Menaquinone biosynthesis

\[
\text{2-demethylmenaquinol} + \text{SAM}
\rightarrow
\text{menaquinol} + \text{SAH} + \mathrm{H^+}
\]

This is the final methylation step in the conversion of a demethylated menaquinone precursor to **menaquinol**, a membrane electron carrier.

### Ubiquinone biosynthesis

UbiE can also methylate a polyprenylated benzoquinol precursor:

\[
\text{2-methoxy-6-polyprenylbenzene-1,4-diol} + \text{SAM}
\rightarrow
\text{5-methoxy-2-methyl-3-polyprenylbenzene-1,4-diol}
+ \text{SAH} + \mathrm{H^+}
\]

## Summary

This sequence encodes a **UbiE-type quinone C-methyltransferase**. Its primary role is to methylate late-stage menaquinone and/or ubiquinone precursors using SAM, supporting biosynthesis of respiratory quinones. The sequence evidence strongly supports the UbiE assignment, although the exact substrate usage in the source organism would require genomic context or biochemical testing.
