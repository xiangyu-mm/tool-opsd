The 210-residue protein is **nicotinate-nucleotide adenylyltransferase (NadD)**, also called **NaMN adenylyltransferase**.

### Catalytic reaction

\[
\text{Nicotinate D-ribonucleotide (NaMN)} + \text{ATP}
\rightleftharpoons
\text{deamido-NAD}^{+}\text{ (NaAD)} + \text{diphosphate}
\]

More explicitly:

\[
\text{NaMN} + \text{ATP} + \mathrm{H}^{+}
\rightleftharpoons
\text{NaAD} + \mathrm{PP_i}
\]

The enzyme transfers an **adenylyl group (AMP)** from ATP to NaMN, producing **nicotinic acid adenine dinucleotide**, also known as **deamido-NAD⁺**. This is a step in **NAD⁺ biosynthesis**.

### Evidence

- The sequence is **210 amino acids** long.
- It is an **exact full-length sequence match** to reviewed UniProt protein **A5CX85**.
- The reference protein is annotated as **nicotinate-nucleotide adenylyltransferase**.
- Assigned **EC number: 2.7.7.18**.
- Corresponding Rhea reaction: **RHEA:22860**.
- The sequence contains a **CTP_transf_like domain**, consistent with the nucleotidyl-transferase fold.

**Conclusion:** This protein is very confidently identified as **NadD**, catalyzing the reversible conversion of **NaMN and ATP into NaAD and pyrophosphate** during NAD⁺ biosynthesis.
