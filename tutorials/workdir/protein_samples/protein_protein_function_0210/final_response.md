## Functional summary

The 467-amino-acid sequence is an **exact match** to reviewed *Shewanella oneidensis* cytochrome c-552 (UniProt **Q8EAC7**). This makes its functional assignment especially strong.

- **Likely function:** A multiheme cytochrome c nitrite reductase (EC **1.7.2.2**), which reduces nitrite to ammonia, accepting six electrons. The annotation also reports very low activity toward hydroxylamine and still lower activity toward sulfite.
- **Biological processes:** Nitrogen metabolism, including nitrate/nitrite reduction, and anaerobic electron transport. Its hemes support electron transfer in the reductive reaction.
- **Cellular location:** **Periplasm** of the Gram-negative bacterium, consistent with the reviewed annotation and GO term for the outer-membrane-bounded periplasmic space.
- **Cofactors:** The reference protein is annotated to bind **five covalent heme c groups** and **one Ca²⁺ ion** per monomer.
- **Sequence evidence:** The Cytochrom_C552 Pfam domain was detected at residues 36–461.
