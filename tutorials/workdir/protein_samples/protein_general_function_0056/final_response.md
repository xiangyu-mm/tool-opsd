## Likely function

This protein is the **protein component of bacterial RNase P**. RNase P is a ribonucleoprotein complex consisting of:

1. A catalytic RNA component, commonly called **M1 RNA** or encoded by **rnpB**
2. A small protein subunit such as this one

The protein subunit binds RNA, particularly the 5′ leader region of precursor tRNAs, and helps the catalytic RNA recognize and process substrates. The complex removes the **5′ leader sequence from pre-tRNA**, producing mature tRNA. Some bacterial RNase P complexes can also process other RNA substrates, including 4.5S RNA.
