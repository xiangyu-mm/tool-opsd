Nnr protein (UniProt **P74217**), with **49.5% sequence identity across 94.6% of the query** (E-value 5.69 × 10⁻¹⁵⁹). This strong, broad alignment supports the functional assignment, but it is still homology-based rather than direct experimental evidence for this particular sequence.
