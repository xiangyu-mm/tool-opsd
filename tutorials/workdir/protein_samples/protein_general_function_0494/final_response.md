\[
\text{succinic semialdehyde} + \text{NADPH} + H^+
\rightarrow \text{4-hydroxybutyrate (GHB)} + \text{NADP}^+

- 2-carboxybenzaldehyde  
- 2-nitrobenzaldehyde  
- pyridine-2-aldehyde  
- aflatoxin B1 dialdehyde  
- aromatic quinones, including naphthoquinone and phenanthrenequinone  

Thus, it may contribute both to **GHB production** and to detoxification of reactive aldehydes and aflatoxin-derived intermediates.
