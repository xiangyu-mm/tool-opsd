### Biological role

RimM is a **non-enzymatic accessory factor in bacterial 30S ribosomal-subunit biogenesis**. It contributes to:

- Final assembly and maturation of the 30S subunit, potentially including the ribosomal head
- Efficient processing and maturation of **16S rRNA**
- Interactions with immature/free 30S subunits
- Binding to ribosomal protein **uS19**

RimM may act at multiple stages relative to the 16S-rRNA maturation factor **RbfA**. The curated annotation indicates preferential association with free 30S subunits rather than complete 70S ribosomes.

This is a small, acidic, two-domain bacterial ribosome-assembly protein rather than a metabolic enzyme. Its sequence architecture and exact match strongly support the annotation:
