## Catalytic reaction

MsrA repairs oxidatively damaged proteins by reducing methionine sulfoxide residues back to methionine:

\[
\text{Protein-L-methionine-(S)-sulfoxide}
+ \text{thioredoxin}_{red}
\rightarrow
\text{Protein-L-methionine}
+ \text{thioredoxin}_{ox}
+ H_2O
\]

Thus, the enzyme promotes the **thioredoxin-dependent reduction of peptide-bound L-methionine-(S)-s-oxide to L-methionine**. The corresponding database annotations include Rhea reactions **RHEA:14217** and **RHEA:19993**, represented as reversible oxidation–reduction reactions.

## Biological role

MsrA functions as a **protein-repair enzyme**, protecting cells from oxidative stress by reversing methionine oxidation in proteins. The predicted protein size is approximately **24.0 kDa**, consistent with a bacterial MsrA.
