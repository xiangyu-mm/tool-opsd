## Functional annotation

**Likely protein:** **Isoleucine--tRNA ligase (IleRS)**  
**EC:** 6.1.1.5  
**Confidence:** High, based on strong homology and conserved aminoacyl-tRNA synthetase domains.

### Evidence

- Length: **768 amino acids**
- Predicted molecular mass: **~86.5 kDa**
- Pfam domains:
  - **tRNA-synt_1**: residues ~39–670
  - **tRNA-synt_1g**: residues ~68–211
  - **Anticodon_1**: residues ~717–768
- The sequence matches curated cyanobacterial IleRS proteins with very high similarity, including a reviewed *Prochlorococcus marinus* IleRS reference.
- The domain architecture is characteristic of a **class I aminoacyl-tRNA synthetase**, including the catalytic and anticodon-recognition regions.

### Molecular function

The protein most likely catalyzes:

\[
\text{L-isoleucine} + \text{tRNA}^{\mathrm{Ile}} + \text{ATP}
\rightarrow
\text{isoleucyl-tRNA}^{\mathrm{Ile}} + \text{AMP} + \text{PP}_{i}
\]

Thus, it charges tRNA\(^\mathrm{Ile}\) with isoleucine for use during translation.

IleRS enzymes can also perform editing reactions that improve translational fidelity:

- **Pre-transfer editing:** hydrolysis of incorrectly activated valine-AMP
- **Post-transfer editing:** removal of valine from mischarged Val-tRNA\(^\mathrm{Ile}\)

These editing functions are supported by the curated homolog annotations, though they are inferred for this exact sequence rather than experimentally demonstrated.

### Subcellular localization

**Likely cytoplasmic.**

There is no apparent N-terminal secretion signal or transmembrane region, and the closest curated homologs are annotated as cytoplasmic enzymes. The protein is likely soluble and probably functions as a **monomer**, as reported for the close reference IleRS proteins.

### Cofactor

The closest curated homologs are annotated to bind **one Zn²⁺ ion per subunit**. This zinc is likely associated with the editing/fidelity function of IleRS, but metal binding should be regarded as a homology-based prediction for this sequence.

### Biological processes

The protein is expected to participate in:

- **Isoleucyl-tRNA aminoacylation**
- **Aminoacyl-tRNA biosynthesis**
- **Cytoplasmic translation**
- **Protein biosynthesis**
- **Maintenance of translational accuracy**, through discrimination against valine and editing of mischarged tRNA

### Summary

This sequence encodes a **cytoplasmic bacterial isoleucyl-tRNA synthetase (IleRS)**. It charges tRNA\(^\mathrm{Ile}\) with isoleucine using ATP and likely includes the associated valine-editing activities that prevent mistranslation. The assignment is strongly supported by its class-I aminoacyl-tRNA synthetase domains and close similarity to curated cyanobacterial IleRS proteins, although the catalytic and zinc-binding properties have not been directly tested for this particular sequence.
