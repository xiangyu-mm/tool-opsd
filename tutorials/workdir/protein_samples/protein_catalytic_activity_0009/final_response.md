## Functional identification

The 387-residue protein is **erythronate-4-phosphate dehydrogenase** (**EC 1.1.1.290**), also called **4-phosphoerythronate dehydrogenase**.

The assignment is highly reliable: the sequence is an **exact 100% identity match** to the reviewed UniProt protein **B1KKP1**. It also contains the expected **2-hydroxyacid dehydrogenase domains**:

- 2-Hacid_dh, PF00389  
- 2-Hacid_dh_C, PF02826  

## Catalytic reaction

\[
\text{4-phospho-D-erythronate} + \mathrm{NAD^+}
\rightarrow
\text{(R)-3-hydroxy-2-oxo-4-phosphooxybutanoate}
+ \mathrm{NADH} + \mathrm{H^+}
\]

Thus, the enzyme catalyzes an **NAD⁺-dependent oxidation of the aldehyde/alcohol functionality of 4-phospho-D-erythronate**, producing a phosphorylated 2-oxo acid.

- **EC:** 1.1.1.290  
- **Rhea:** RHEA:18829  
- **Cofactor:** NAD⁺/NADH  
- **Likely organization:** homodimer  
- **Localization:** cytoplasm  

## Biological role

This is the **second step in pyridoxine-5′-phosphate (vitamin B6) biosynthesis** from D-erythrose-4-phosphate.

Overall, the sequence and curated exact-match annotation strongly support **erythronate-4-phosphate dehydrogenase**, rather than a generic 2-hydroxyacid dehydrogenase.
