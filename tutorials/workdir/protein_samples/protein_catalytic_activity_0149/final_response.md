## Functional identification

The 324-residue protein is **succinylglutamate desuccinylase** (**AstE/AstE-like enzyme**), a zinc-dependent hydrolase.

The assignment is highly secure: the sequence is an **exact full-length match** to reviewed *Acinetobacter baumannii* succinylglutamate desuccinylases, with 100% identity and coverage.

## Catalytic reaction

\[
\text{N}^{2}\text{-succinyl-L-glutamate} + \mathrm{H_2O}
\rightarrow
\text{L-glutamate} + \text{succinate}
\]

Thus, the enzyme hydrolytically removes the **succinyl group** from N²-succinylglutamate, yielding free L-glutamate and succinate.

- **EC number:** EC 3.5.1.96  
- **Rhea reaction:** RHEA:15169  
- **Cofactor:** one structural/catalytic **Zn²⁺ ion per subunit**
- **Pathway:** final step of the L-arginine degradation pathway via the arginine succinyltransferase (AST) route
