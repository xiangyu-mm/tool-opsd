- Sequence-specific DNA binding by the processed p50 product.

### 3. C-terminal ankyrin-repeat region

The C-terminal half contains repeated ankyrin-like motifs. In NF-κB1 p105, this region functions as a regulatory module that:

- Interacts with other proteins.
- Promotes cytoplasmic retention of NF-κB complexes.
- Regulates processing of p105 into p50.
- Helps control the availability and activity of NF-κB transcription factors.

The histidine/alanine-rich appearance of this region reflects the composition of ankyrin repeats rather than evidence for a histidine-dependent enzyme.

## Biological function

The likely full-length protein has two related functions:

1. **Precursor/regulator:**  
   The p105 form acts as an inhibitory or cytoplasmic-retention component of NF-κB complexes. It can keep NF-κB proteins inactive outside the nucleus and participate in regulatory protein complexes.

2. **Transcription factor after processing:**  
   Proteasome-dependent processing generates an N-terminal p50-like product. p50 can dimerize with proteins such as RELA/p65, c-Rel, or another p50 molecule and bind κB regulatory DNA elements.

Depending on its dimerization partner, the resulting NF-κB complex can activate or repress transcription.
