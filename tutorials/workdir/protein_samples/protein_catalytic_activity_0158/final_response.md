The physiological reaction is:

\[
\text{4-phospho-L-aspartate} + \text{NADPH} + \text{H}^+
\rightarrow
\text{L-aspartate 4-semialdehyde} + \text{Pi} + \text{NADP}^+
\]

Thus, the enzyme performs an **NADPH-dependent reductive dephosphorylation** of 4-phospho-L-aspartate, producing **L-aspartate 4-semialdehyde (ASA)** and inorganic phosphate.

## Biological role

L-aspartate 4-semialdehyde is a key branch-point intermediate in **aspartate-family amino-acid biosynthesis**. This reaction supplies precursors for:

- **L-lysine** biosynthesis through the diaminopimelate pathway
- **L-methionine** biosynthesis
- **L-threonine** biosynthesis
