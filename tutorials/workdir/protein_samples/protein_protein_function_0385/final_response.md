### Functional summary

This 135-amino-acid protein is most likely a small fungal membrane protein of the **Osw5 family**, specifically **Outer spore wall protein 5**.

- **Topology:** Two closely spaced N-terminal transmembrane helices, approximately residues **2–22** and **25–45**, forming a compact membrane hairpin. The remaining C-terminal region is predicted to be cytoplasmic.
- **Domain/family:** Contains a strong **Osw5 Pfam domain** match, spanning approximately residues 6–74.
- **Composition:** The C-terminal half is relatively polar and includes a low-complexity, serine/proline-rich disordered region, consistent with a possible protein-interaction or assembly-related tail.
- **Best functional evidence:** The sequence is an **exact match** to reviewed UniProt protein **C5DQ25 from *Zygosaccharomyces rouxii***, annotated as **Outer spore wall protein 5**, involved in **spore wall assembly**.

### Proposed biological role

The protein likely participates in **fungal spore-wall biogenesis or maturation**, probably as a small membrane-associated component that helps organize or recruit factors involved in spore-wall assembly. Its C-terminal cytoplasmic tail may mediate interactions with intracellular assembly machinery, although this mechanism is speculative.

**Confidence:** High for Osw5 family membership and association with spore-wall assembly because of the exact sequence match; low-to-moderate for the precise molecular mechanism, which is not established from sequence alone.
