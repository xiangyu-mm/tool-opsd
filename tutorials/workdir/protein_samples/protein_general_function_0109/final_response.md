## Summary

The sequence is an **exact, full-length match** to reviewed rice (*Oryza sativa* subsp. *japonica*) **NTRC—NADPH-dependent thioredoxin reductase** (UniProt **Q70G58**). Thus, the protein identification is supported by an exact sequence match, not merely by distant homology.

- **Likely role:** A chloroplast redox enzyme that combines thioredoxin-reductase activity with a functional thioredoxin domain. It transfers reducing power from NADPH to thioredoxin substrates and is annotated as an electron donor to plastidial 2-Cys peroxiredoxin BAS1 in hydrogen-peroxide scavenging, particularly in the dark.
- **Reaction / enzyme class:** Thioredoxin-disulfide reductase, **EC 1.8.1.9**; the physiological reduction direction is NADPH-dependent reduction of thioredoxin disulfide to thioredoxin dithiol.
- **Cofactor and assembly:** One **FAD** per subunit; annotated to homodimerize under reducing conditions.
- **Localization and architecture:** Plastid/chloroplast. The reference annotation places a chloroplast transit peptide at residues **1–45** and a C-terminal thioredoxin domain at **377–515**.

**Evidence caveat:** The enzyme identity and core functional annotations are tied to the exact rice reference sequence. Additional roles in chlorophyll and starch biosynthesis appear in a related Arabidopsis NTRC annotation, so they are not asserted here as direct evidence for this sequence.
