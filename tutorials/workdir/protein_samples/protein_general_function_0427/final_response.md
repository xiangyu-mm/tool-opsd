## Proposed function

MurE catalyzes the ATP-dependent addition of **meso-diaminopimelate (meso-DAP)** to the peptidoglycan precursor UDP-MurNAc-L-Ala-D-Glu:

\[
\text{UDP-MurNAc-L-Ala-D-Glu}
+ \text{meso-DAP}
+ \text{ATP}
\rightarrow
\text{UDP-MurNAc-L-Ala-D-Glu-meso-DAP}
+ \text{ADP}
+ \text{Pi}
\]

The product is a key precursor for **bacterial peptidoglycan cell-wall synthesis**.

- **Likely metal cofactor:** Mg²⁺  
- **Likely cellular location:** Cytoplasm
