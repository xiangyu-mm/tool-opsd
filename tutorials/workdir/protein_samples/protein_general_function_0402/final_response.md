## Summary

The 314-aa sequence is an **exact full-length match** to reviewed *Gloeobacter violaceus* ribosomal RNA small-subunit methyltransferase H (**RsmH**, UniProt Q7NCQ1). This makes RsmH the strongly supported identification.

- **Function:** Methylates the **N4 position of cytidine 1402 (C1402) in bacterial 16S rRNA**, producing N4-methylcytidine.
- **Reaction:** Uses **S-adenosyl-L-methionine (SAM)** as the methyl donor, yielding S-adenosyl-L-homocysteine.
- **Enzyme classification:** EC **2.1.1.199**; Rhea **RHEA:42928**.
- **Localization:** Cytoplasm, according to the reference annotation.
- **Sequence-derived properties:** Estimated molecular mass **35.2 kDa**, theoretical pI **6.74**, and GRAVY **−0.396**.

The annotation also links RsmH to rRNA cytosine-N4-methyltransferase activity and rRNA base methylation (GO:0071424 and GO:0070475). These functional details come from the exact-sequence reference annotation; the mass and other physicochemical values are sequence-derived estimates.
