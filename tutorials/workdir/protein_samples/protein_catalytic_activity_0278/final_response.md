## Catalytic activity

The protein cleaves the phosphodiester bond between the phosphate group and the polar headgroup of sphingolipids and lysophospholipids. Importantly, it proceeds by **transphosphatidylation**, producing a cyclic phosphate rather than simply hydrolyzing the substrate to a non-cyclic phosphate.

The principal reaction is:

\[
\text{sphingomyelin}
\rightarrow
\text{ceramide-1,3-cyclic phosphate} + \text{choline}
\]

More explicitly:

\[
\text{N-acyl-sphingosylphosphocholine}
\rightarrow
\text{N-acyl-sphingosyl-1,3-cyclic phosphate}
+
\text{choline}
\]

It can also act on related substrates:

\[
\text{ceramide phosphoethanolamine}
\rightarrow
\text{ceramide cyclic phosphate}
+
\text{ethanolamine}
\]

and on lysophosphatidylcholine or lysophosphatidylethanolamine, generating the corresponding cyclic phosphate and releasing choline or ethanolamine.

## Functional summary

This protein is a **secreted, Mg²⁺-dependent sphingomyelinase D/dermonecrotic toxin**. It catalyzes phosphodiester cleavage and intramolecular transphosphatidylation of sphingomyelin and related phospholipids, yielding **cyclic phosphate lipid products plus choline or ethanolamine**. The resulting lipid modifications are associated with dermonecrosis, hemolysis, vascular permeability, edema, inflammation, and platelet effects.

The assignment is high confidence because the submitted sequence is an **exact match to a reviewed UniProt entry**, rather than being inferred only from distant homology.
