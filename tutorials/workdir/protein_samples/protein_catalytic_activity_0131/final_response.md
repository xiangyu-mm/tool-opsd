## Functional identification

The 351-amino-acid sequence is an **exact full-length match** to reviewed mouse **ABHD5** (UniProt **Q9DBL9**), also annotated as **1-acylglycerol-3-phosphate O-acyltransferase**. The reference assigns it **EC 2.3.1.51** activity.

## Catalytic reaction

ABHD5 is annotated as a CoA-dependent lysophosphatidic acid (LPA) acyltransferase. It transfers an acyl group from acyl-CoA onto LPA to form phosphatidic acid:

**1-acyl-sn-glycero-3-phosphate + acyl-CoA → 1,2-diacyl-sn-glycero-3-phosphate + CoA**

The reference reports preference for **1-oleoyl-LPA** as the acceptor and **arachidonoyl-CoA** as the donor, while also listing other LPA and acyl-CoA substrates. This reaction contributes to phosphatidic acid biosynthesis.

**Evidence and caveat:** The sequence match and reaction assignment are strong for this specific protein annotation. ABHD5 is also annotated as a regulator/activator of the lipase PNPLA2 (ATGL); that additional role is distinct from the acyl-transfer reaction above.
