## Catalytic activity

MurE ligase catalyzes the ATP-dependent incorporation of **meso-2,6-diaminopimelate (meso-DAP)** into the UDP-linked peptidoglycan precursor **UDP-N-acetylmuramoyl-L-alanyl-D-glutamate**.

### Chemical reaction

\[
\text{UDP-MurNAc-L-Ala-D-Glu}
+ \text{meso-DAP}
+ \text{ATP}
\rightarrow
\text{UDP-MurNAc-L-Ala-}\gamma\text{-D-Glu-meso-DAP}
+ \text{ADP}
+ \text{P}_{i}
+ \text{H}^{+}
\]

In words, the enzyme forms an amide bond between the terminal γ-carboxyl group of D-glutamate and the amino group of meso-DAP. ATP activates the carboxyl group, and **Mg²⁺** supports nucleotide binding and phosphoryl-transfer chemistry.

The product is the **UDP-MurNAc-tripeptide**, an essential precursor for bacterial cell-wall peptidoglycan. It is subsequently converted into later peptidoglycan intermediates, including the pentapeptide precursor.

## Overall conclusion

This sequence is best assigned as a **meso-DAP-specific MurE ligase** involved in bacterial cell-wall synthesis. The assignment is strongly supported by its domain organization, length, conserved substrate-recognition region, and high similarity to curated MurE homologs. The evidence is primarily comparative and sequence-based; direct biochemical confirmation of this particular protein has not been performed.
