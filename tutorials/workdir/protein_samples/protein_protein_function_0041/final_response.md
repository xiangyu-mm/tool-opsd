## Function

This enzyme removes an N-terminal **5-oxoproline/pyroglutamyl residue** from peptides and proteins:

\[
\text{pyroglutamyl-peptide} \rightarrow \text{peptide with exposed N-terminus} + \text{5-oxoproline}
\]

The second residue is generally not proline. This is a specialized proteolytic or peptide-processing activity rather than a broad-spectrum protease.
