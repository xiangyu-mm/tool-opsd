### Functional assignment

The 402-residue protein is most consistent with a **bifunctional IspD/IspF enzyme** in the non-mevalonate (MEP/DOXP) pathway of isoprenoid biosynthesis.

- **N-terminal module:** IspD, 2-C-methyl-D-erythritol 4-phosphate cytidylyltransferase  
  - Pfam IspD domain: approximately residues 8–236
  - EC **2.7.7.60**

- **C-terminal module:** IspF, 2-C-methyl-D-erythritol 2,4-cyclodiphosphate synthase  
  - Pfam YgbB/IspF domain: approximately residues 242–394
  - EC **4.6.1.12**

### Catalytic reactions

The fused enzyme is predicted to catalyze two consecutive reactions:

1. **IspD reaction**
   
   \[
   \text{2-C-methyl-D-erythritol 4-phosphate}
   + \text{CTP}
   + \mathrm{H^+}
   \rightarrow
   \text{4-CDP-2-C-methyl-D-erythritol}
   + \text{diphosphate}
   \]

2. **IspF reaction**
   
   \[
   \text{4-CDP-2-C-methyl-D-erythritol 2-phosphate}
   \rightarrow
   \text{2-C-methyl-D-erythritol 2,4-cyclic diphosphate}
   + \text{CMP}
   \]

The enzyme is therefore involved in **steps 2 and 4 of MEP-pathway isoprenoid biosynthesis**. The homolog annotations also indicate dependence on a **divalent metal cation**.

### Evidence and confidence

This assignment is supported by:

- Strong matches to reviewed IspD/IspF homologs, with approximately **58–60% sequence identity over ~97% coverage**.
- Clear domain organization matching an N-terminal IspD module and a C-terminal IspF/YgbB module.
- Agreement with curated homolog annotations, EC numbers, and Rhea reaction entries.

Thus, the best concise annotation is:

> **Bifunctional 2-C-methyl-D-erythritol 4-phosphate cytidylyltransferase/2-C-methyl-D-erythritol 2,4-cyclodiphosphate synthase (IspD/IspF), catalyzing CDP-ME formation followed by ME-cPP formation in the MEP isoprenoid-biosynthesis pathway.**

This remains a sequence- and homology-based assignment rather than direct experimental characterization of the supplied protein.
