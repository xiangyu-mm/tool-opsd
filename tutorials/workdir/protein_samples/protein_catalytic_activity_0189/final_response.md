## Functional identification

The sequence encodes **arginine–tRNA ligase (ArgRS)**, an aminoacyl-tRNA synthetase.

### Catalytic reaction

\[
\text{L-arginine} + \text{tRNA}^{Arg} + \text{ATP}
\rightarrow
\text{L-arginyl-tRNA}^{Arg} + \text{AMP} + \text{diphosphate}
\]

The enzyme specifically attaches **L-arginine to the 3′ end of tRNA\(^\text{Arg}\)**, producing arginyl-tRNA for use during protein synthesis. ATP is cleaved to **AMP and pyrophosphate**, typically through an arginyl-adenylate intermediate.
