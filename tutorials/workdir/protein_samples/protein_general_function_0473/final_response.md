### Functional summary

This 393-aa protein is very likely a **bacterial flavohemoprotein (flavohemoglobin)** functioning as a **nitric oxide dioxygenase**.

- **Best evidence:** 73.5% amino-acid identity over 99.7% of the sequence to the reviewed *Pseudomonas aeruginosa* flavohemoprotein Q9I0H4.
- **Domain organization:**
  - N-terminal **globin/heme-binding domain**: approximately residues 27–131
  - Central **FAD-binding reductase domain**: approximately residues 159–253
  - C-terminal **NAD(P)-binding domain**: approximately residues 264–366
- **Expected cofactors:** one **heme b** and one **FAD** per subunit.
- **Likely reaction:** aerobic conversion of nitric oxide to nitrate using molecular oxygen and reducing equivalents from NADH or NADPH:

\[
2\,NO + 2\,O_2 + NAD(P)H \rightarrow 2\,NO_3^- + NAD(P)^+ + H^+
\]

- **Biological role:** detoxification of nitric oxide and protection against **nitrosative stress**.
- **Likely localization:** cytoplasm, consistent with close homologs; the sequence shows no evident secretion signal or membrane-spanning region.
- **Sequence properties:** predicted molecular mass ~43.2 kDa, theoretical pI ~6.0, mildly acidic overall.

Overall, the sequence architecture and close homologs provide **strong support** for a cytoplasmic flavohemoprotein involved in bacterial NO detoxification. The exact NADH-versus-NADPH preference and activity of this particular sequence remain inferred rather than experimentally demonstrated.
