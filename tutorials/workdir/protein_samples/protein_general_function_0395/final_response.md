### Summary

The 86-amino-acid sequence is an **exact sequence match** to reviewed UniProt protein **B2FTA6**, annotated as **MinE**, a bacterial cell-division topological specificity factor. A Pfam scan also detected the **MinE family domain (PF03776)** at residues 1–79.

MinE’s annotated role is to help position cell division correctly: it prevents the MinC/MinD division-inhibitory system from blocking septum formation at internal sites while allowing inhibition at the cell poles. This helps restrict septum formation to the proper division site. The reference annotation associates it with **cell division** and **regulation of division septum assembly**.
