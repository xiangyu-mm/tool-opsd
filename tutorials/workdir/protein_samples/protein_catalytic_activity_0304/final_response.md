## Functional identification

The 339-aa protein is **PtlH**, an ATPase component of the **Bordetella pertussis type IV secretion system**. The sequence is an exact match to curated PtlH proteins, including UniProt **Q7VSX3**.

It contains a characteristic P-loop NTP-binding motif:

```text
LVAGQTGSGKTT
```

consistent with an ATP-driven motor protein.

## Catalyzed reaction

The direct catalytic reaction is:

\[
\mathrm{ATP + H_2O \rightarrow ADP + P_i + H^+}
\]

The hydrolysis energy is coupled to protein export:

\[
\mathrm{ATP + H_2O + protein_{cytoplasmic}
\rightarrow ADP + P_i + protein_{exported}}
\]

Thus, PtlH functions as a **protein-exporting ATPase/molecular motor**, supplying energy for secretion of assembled pertussis toxin through the Ptl type IV secretion apparatus. It does **not** appear to chemically modify the toxin substrate; rather, it powers its translocation.

**Classification:** EC **7.4.2.8**, protein-exporting ATPase activity.  
**Localization:** Cytoplasmic-side, peripheral inner-membrane component of the secretion system.
