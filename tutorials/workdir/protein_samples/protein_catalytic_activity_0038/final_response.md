### Catalyzed reaction

\[
\text{L-asparagine} + \text{tRNA}^{\mathrm{Asn}} + \text{ATP}
\rightarrow
\text{L-asparaginyl-tRNA}^{\mathrm{Asn}} + \text{AMP} + \text{PP}_{i} + \text{H}^{+}
\]

In words, AsnRS attaches **L-asparagine to the 3′ terminal adenosine of tRNA\(^\mathrm{Asn}\)**, producing **Asn-tRNA\(^\mathrm{Asn}\)** for delivery to the ribosome during protein synthesis.

The reaction proceeds through the usual aminoacyl-tRNA synthetase mechanism:

1. **Activation:** asparagine reacts with ATP to form an enzyme-bound asparaginyl-AMP intermediate and pyrophosphate.
2. **Transfer:** the activated asparaginyl group is transferred to the terminal ribose hydroxyl of tRNA\(^\mathrm{Asn}\), releasing AMP.

This protein is a **cytoplasmic bacterial asparagine--tRNA ligase** that catalyzes ATP-dependent aminoacylation of tRNA\(^\mathrm{Asn}\), forming Asn-tRNA\(^\mathrm{Asn}\) for incorporation of asparagine into proteins.
