### Functional identification

The 431-residue protein is a **zinc-dependent 5′-methylthioadenosine/S-adenosylhomocysteine deaminase** (**MTA/SAH deaminase**), belonging to the **amidohydrolase family**.

This assignment is strongly supported: the sequence is an exact, full-length match to reviewed UniProt entry **Q24UA2** from *Desulfitobacterium hafniense*.

### Catalytic activity

The enzyme hydrolytically deaminates adenine-containing nucleosides, replacing the exocyclic amino group with a carbonyl group and releasing ammonium:

1. **S-adenosyl-L-homocysteine deamination**  
\[
\text{S-adenosyl-L-homocysteine} + H_2O + H^+
\rightarrow
\text{S-inosyl-L-homocysteine} + NH_4^+
\]

- EC: **3.5.4.31**
- Rhea: **RHEA:20716**

2. **5′-Methylthioadenosine deamination**  
\[
\text{5′-methylthioadenosine} + H_2O + H^+
\rightarrow
\text{5′-methylthioinosine} + NH_4^+
\]

- EC: **3.5.4.28**
- Rhea: **RHEA:25025**

The matched annotation also reports activity toward **adenosine**, producing **inosine and ammonium**.

### Cofactor and structural evidence

- Requires/binds **one Zn²⁺ ion per subunit**.
- Pfam domains:
  - **Amidohydro_1**, residues approximately 57–402
  - **Amidohydro_3**, residues approximately 144–402
- The sequence contains the expected conserved amidohydrolase-family architecture and annotated active-site/binding positions.

**Summary:** This protein is a Zn²⁺-dependent purine nucleoside deaminase that primarily converts 5′-methylthioadenosine and S-adenosyl-L-homocysteine into their corresponding inosine derivatives, releasing ammonium.
