### Functional summary

This 179-amino-acid sequence is **ATP synthase subunit delta**, a component of the **peripheral stalk** of the bacterial F-type ATP synthase. The sequence exactly matches reviewed UniProt entry **B2A3G5** (100% identity and coverage), annotated for *Natranaerobius thermophilus*. Its role is structural: the peripheral stalk links the membrane-associated F₀ region to the catalytic F₁ region and helps couple their activity during ATP synthesis. It is not itself annotated as the ATP-producing catalytic subunit.
