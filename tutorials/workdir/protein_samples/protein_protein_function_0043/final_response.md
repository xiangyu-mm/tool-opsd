## Molecular function

uS5 is a **structural ribosomal protein**, not an enzyme. Its functions include:

- Binding and organizing rRNA within the 30S subunit
- Stabilizing the relationship between the 30S body and head
- Contributing to **translational accuracy**
- Interacting with other small-subunit proteins, particularly **uS4 and uS8**
- Working functionally with **uS4 and uS12** in decoding fidelity

No catalytic reaction or specific cofactor was identified, which is expected for a structural ribosomal protein.

This is a small, highly basic bacterial **30S ribosomal protein uS5**.
