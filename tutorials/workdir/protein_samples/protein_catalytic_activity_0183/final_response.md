## Catalyzed reaction

The enzyme hydrolyzes the phosphodiester bond of a glycerophosphodiester:

\[
\text{sn-glycero-3-phosphodiester} + H_2O
\rightarrow
\text{sn-glycerol-3-phosphate} + \text{alcohol} + H^+
\]

Examples include:

- **Glycerophosphocholine + H₂O → glycerol-3-phosphate + choline**
- **Glycerophosphoethanolamine + H₂O → glycerol-3-phosphate + ethanolamine**
- **Glycerophosphoglycerol + H₂O → glycerol-3-phosphate + glycerol**

## Summary

This protein is a **GDPD-family phosphodiesterase involved in glycerophospholipid breakdown**, releasing **glycerol-3-phosphate** and the corresponding alcohol head group. Homologous plant GDPD proteins are reported to use **Mg²⁺**, but the strongest evidence here is the exact sequence match and curated EC/Rhea annotation; the precise substrate preference of this particular protein should be experimentally confirmed.
