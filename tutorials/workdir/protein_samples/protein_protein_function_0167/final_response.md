## Likely function

This 399-aa protein is most likely **CoaBC, the bifunctional enzyme in coenzyme A (CoA) biosynthesis**. The assignment is supported by close, reviewed CoaBC homologs with high sequence coverage (about 98–99%) and two matching Pfam domains: an N-terminal flavoprotein domain and a C-terminal DFP domain.

CoaBC carries out two consecutive reactions:

1. **Phosphopantothenate–cysteine ligase:** joins cysteine to 4′-phosphopantothenate, using CTP, to form 4-phosphopantothenoylcysteine.
2. **Phosphopantothenoylcysteine decarboxylase:** converts that intermediate to 4′-phosphopantetheine, releasing CO₂.
