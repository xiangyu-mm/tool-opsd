## Functional summary

The 478-amino-acid sequence is an **exact match** to the reviewed *Streptomyces coelicolor* protein **Obg GTPase** (UniProt **P95722**).

Obg is an essential bacterial GTPase that binds **GTP and GDP** and may also bind the alarmone **(p)ppGpp**. It is implicated in **cell-cycle control, stress response, ribosome biogenesis**, and—among differentiating bacteria—**morphogenesis and sporulation**. The available annotation supports these roles but does not establish a specific molecular mechanism for all of them.

**Cellular processes:** In *S. coelicolor*, Obg has a regulatory role in sporulation. Overexpression was reported to suppress sporulation and reduce actinorhodin production, without reducing growth rate; the mechanism behind these effects is not resolved by the available evidence.

**Subcellular location:** Annotated in the **cytoplasm** and at the **cell membrane as a peripheral membrane protein**. The membrane association was reported to be lost after salt washing.

These conclusions are supported by an exact sequence match to the reviewed reference annotation; the broader process assignments are annotations, not experiments performed on the supplied sequence.
