## Functional identification

The protein is **threonyl-tRNA synthetase** (**ThrRS; EC 6.1.1.3**), an aminoacyl-tRNA synthetase that charges tRNA\(^\mathrm{Thr}\) with L-threonine.

The assignment is highly reliable: the 640-residue sequence is an **exact full-length sequence match** to the reviewed *Halobacterium salinarum* threonine--tRNA ligase B0R6G6. Its domain architecture is also consistent with ThrRS, including TGS, tRNA_SAD, tRNA-synt_2b catalytic, and HGTP_anticodon domains.

## Catalyzed reaction

\[
\mathrm{L\!-\!threonine + tRNA^{Thr} + ATP}
\rightarrow
\mathrm{L\!-\!threonyl\!-\!tRNA^{Thr} + AMP + PP_i + H^+}
\]

The reaction occurs in two steps:

1. **Activation of threonine**
   \[
   \mathrm{L\!-\!threonine + ATP \rightarrow threonyl\!-\!AMP + PP_i}
   \]

2. **Transfer to tRNA**
   \[
   \mathrm{threonyl\!-\!AMP + tRNA^{Thr}
   \rightarrow L\!-\!threonyl\!-\!tRNA^{Thr} + AMP}
   \]

The product, threonyl-tRNA\(^\mathrm{Thr}\), supplies threonine for protein synthesis.
