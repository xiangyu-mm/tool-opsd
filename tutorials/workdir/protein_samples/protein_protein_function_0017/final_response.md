## Likely cellular roles

Myocilin is a secreted, nonenzymatic signaling and matrix-associated glycoprotein. Homolog annotations support roles in:

- **Cell–matrix adhesion**
- Cell spreading and focal-contact formation
- **Actin cytoskeleton and stress-fiber organization**
- Cell migration
- Regulation of **Rho**, Wnt-associated, PTK2/FAK–PI3K, and ERBB2/ERBB3 signaling
- Neurite outgrowth and peripheral myelination
- Regulation of trabecular-meshwork extracellular-matrix behavior and aqueous humor outflow

The protein is reported to form **N-terminal homodimers and higher oligomers**, although the exact oligomeric state of this sequence cannot be established from sequence alone.
