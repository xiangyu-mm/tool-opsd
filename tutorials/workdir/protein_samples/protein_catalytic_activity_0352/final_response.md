## Functional identification

The 355-residue protein is **GcpE/IspG**, also called **4-hydroxy-3-methylbut-2-en-1-yl diphosphate synthase**.

The assignment is highly confident because the sequence is an **exact full-length match** to a reviewed UniProt GcpE/IspG protein. It also contains the characteristic **GcpE and GcpE_C Pfam domains**.

### Catalytic reaction

GcpE catalyzes the reductive conversion of:

**2-C-methyl-D-erythritol 2,4-cyclodiphosphate (MEcPP)**  
→ **(E)-4-hydroxy-3-methylbut-2-en-1-yl diphosphate (HMBPP)**

A corresponding electron-transfer representation is:

\[
\text{MEcPP} + \text{reduced ferredoxin/flavodoxin}
\rightarrow
\text{HMBPP} + \text{oxidized ferredoxin/flavodoxin} + \mathrm{H_2O} + 2\mathrm{H^+}
\]

The exact proton/water convention depends on reaction direction and database balancing, but chemically this is an **iron–sulfur-dependent reductive ring-opening/rearrangement of MEcPP**.

- **EC number:** EC 1.17.7.3  
- **Cofactor:** one **[4Fe–4S] cluster**  
- **Electron donor:** reduced ferredoxin or flavodoxin  
- **Rhea reaction:** RHEA:43604  

### Biological role

This is the penultimate enzyme of the microbial **methylerythritol 4-phosphate (MEP/DOXP) pathway**, producing HMBPP, which is subsequently converted to the universal isoprenoid precursors **IPP and DMAPP**.
