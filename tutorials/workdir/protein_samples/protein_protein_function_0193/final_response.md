### Functional summary

This 55-amino-acid protein is most likely an **archaeal SecG subunit of the SecYEG preprotein translocase**.

- **Localization/topology:** Small **single-pass plasma-membrane protein**, with a C-terminal hydrophobic transmembrane helix and a short, positively charged N-terminal region.
- **Biological process:** **Sec-dependent protein export**—part of the membrane complex that translocates newly synthesized proteins across the archaeal membrane.
- **Likely specific role:** The SecG/β subunit does not form the main translocation channel; it likely helps **assemble or stabilize the SecYEG complex**.
- **Supporting evidence:** The sequence is an **exact match** to curated *Methanosarcina mazei* SecG (UniProt Q8PX50), with close homologs in other *Methanosarcina* species. It also matches the **Sec61_beta Pfam domain**.

**Confidence: high** for SecG-like identity and membrane localization, based on the exact curated sequence match. The precise molecular contribution of this very small subunit remains less certain.
