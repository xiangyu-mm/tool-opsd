### Likely function

This 199-aa protein is most likely a **Ham1p-like dITP/XTP pyrophosphatase** (**EC 3.6.1.66**), a small Mg²⁺-dependent nucleotide-pool sanitizing enzyme.

It likely hydrolyzes noncanonical purine nucleoside triphosphates:

- **dITP → dIMP + pyrophosphate**
- **XTP → XMP + pyrophosphate**
- potentially **ITP → IMP + pyrophosphate**

### Evidence

- Contains a **Ham1p_like domain** spanning nearly the full sequence: Pfam **PF01725**.
- Shows approximately **62–63% identity** over **95–97% of its length** to reviewed bacterial dITP/XTP pyrophosphatases.
- Close homologs are annotated as **Mg²⁺-dependent**, cytosolic, and commonly homodimeric.
- The protein lacks obvious features suggesting membrane localization or a secretory signal.

### Associated biological process

The associated process is **purine nucleoside triphosphate catabolism / nucleotide-pool quality control** (**GO:0009146**). By removing oxidized or otherwise noncanonical purine nucleotides, the enzyme helps prevent their incorporation into DNA or RNA and thereby reduces mutagenic genome damage.

**Overall annotation:**  
> **Probable cytosolic Ham1-family dITP/XTP pyrophosphatase involved in sanitizing the cellular purine nucleotide pool.**

The exact substrate preference should be considered homology-based until experimentally tested for this specific sequence.
