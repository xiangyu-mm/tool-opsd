### Molecular function

EcfA1 is an ATP-binding and ATP-hydrolyzing component of an **ECF transporter complex**. It supplies the energy required for uptake of substrates through associated membrane proteins. The complex generally contains:

- Two ATP-binding A components, such as EcfA1/EcfA2
- Two transmembrane T components
- Two membrane-embedded substrate-specific S components

ECF transporters commonly mediate uptake of micronutrients such as vitamins, although the precise substrate cannot be determined from this sequence alone.
