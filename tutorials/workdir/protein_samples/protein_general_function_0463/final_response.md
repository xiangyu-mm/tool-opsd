### Most likely function

The strongest homologs are reviewed *Oryza sativa* proteins:

- **Q6K7H2, UMP-CMP kinase 4:** 75.5% identity, E-value ~1 × 10⁻¹⁰⁴

These homologs are annotated as **UMP–CMP kinases**, which catalyze reactions of the general form:

\[
\text{ATP} + \text{UMP/CMP} \rightarrow \text{ADP} + \text{UDP/CDP}
\]

Thus, the query protein is most likely a **plant pyrimidine nucleoside-monophosphate kinase**, participating in UDP/CDP and broader pyrimidine nucleotide biosynthesis. It may also accept dCMP, as reported for the homologs.

### Localization and assembly

- Homologs are annotated in both the **cytoplasm and nucleus**.

- These properties are inferred from homologs rather than directly demonstrated for the query sequence.

### Confidence and limitations

**Functional confidence: moderate to high for an adenylate-kinase-family phosphotransferase; moderate for exact UMP/CMP specificity.** The conserved kinase domain and very strong homology support the assignment, but exact substrate preference and catalytic kinetics require biochemical validation. The lysine/serine-rich C-terminal extension may confer plant- or isoform-specific regulation.
