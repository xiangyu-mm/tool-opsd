## Functional summary

The sequence is an **exact, full-length match** to reviewed UniProt protein **MpaB (W6R4H8)** from *Penicillium roqueforti*. MpaB is an **endoplasmic-reticulum-associated oxygenase** involved in mycophenolic acid biosynthesis.

It catalyzes **oxidative cleavage of the C19–C20 double bond** in farnesyl-DHMP (FDHMP), forming **FDHMP-3C** through a **mycophenolic aldehyde** intermediate. The mapped reaction is:

**4-farnesyl-3,5-dihydroxy-6-methylphthalide + AH₂ + 2 O₂ → FDHMP-3C + acetone + A + H₂O + H⁺**

Here **AH₂/A** is the generic reduced/oxidized electron-donor pair in the reaction record; the protein report does not identify it more specifically. This is a pathway-annotated reaction, not a claim that MpaB directly synthesizes mycophenolic acid itself.

**Evidence caveat:** The sequence match is exact, but the W6R4H8 entry describes the specific catalytic assignment as “by similarity.” Closely related MpaB annotations support the same reaction.
