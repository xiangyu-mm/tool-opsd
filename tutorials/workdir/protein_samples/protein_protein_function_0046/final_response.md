## Functional identification

The sequence is an **exact match to *Caenorhabditis elegans* UNC-29 (UniProt P48181)**, a **beta-type, non-alpha subunit of a neuronal nicotinic acetylcholine receptor (nAChR)**.

## Evidence from the sequence

- **Length:** 493 amino acids  
- **Predicted molecular mass:** ~56.5 kDa  
- **Pfam domains:**
  - **Neur_chan_LBD**, residues ~30–230: extracellular ligand-binding-domain fold
  - **Neur_chan_memb**, residues ~237–462: nicotinic-receptor membrane domain
- **Topology:** characteristic pentameric ligand-gated ion-channel architecture:
  - N-terminal extracellular region
  - Four membrane-spanning helices in the C-terminal half
  - Large intracellular loop between membrane helices 3 and 4
  - Short extracellular C-terminal tail

The strong N-terminal hydrophobic segment is consistent with a signal peptide/signal-anchor used during receptor assembly and membrane insertion. The later hydrophobic regions form the canonical four-transmembrane channel domain.

## Likely molecular function

UNC-29 is a **structural and functional component of an acetylcholine-gated cation channel**. It is a **non-alpha subunit**, so it is primarily expected to contribute to:

- Receptor assembly and surface expression
- Channel gating and conductance properties
- Modulation of agonist sensitivity
- Formation of the ion-conducting pore together with other nAChR subunits

In *C. elegans*, the annotated UNC-29-containing receptor includes:

- **UNC-29** and **LEV-1** as non-alpha subunits
- **UNC-38, UNC-63, and LEV-8** as alpha subunits

The channel is predicted to conduct monovalent cations and can contribute to calcium influx, producing postsynaptic depolarization.

## Cellular localization

The protein is expected to localize to the:

- **Plasma membrane**
- **Postsynaptic membrane**
- **Neuronal projections and synapses**
- Particularly, nerve-cord synapses and neuromuscular junctions in *C. elegans*

## Biological processes

UNC-29 is likely involved in:

- **Cholinergic synaptic transmission**
- **Neuromuscular excitation**
- **Cation and calcium ion transport**
- **Regulation of membrane potential**
- **Neuronal signaling**
- **Locomotion**

Its receptor is also annotated as influencing sensitivity to compounds such as **nicotine and levamisole**, consistent with a nematode nicotinic acetylcholine receptor.

## Overall conclusion

This is not an enzyme or soluble binding protein. It is a **multi-pass neuronal ligand-gated ion-channel subunit**, specifically the *C. elegans* **UNC-29 beta-type nicotinic acetylcholine receptor subunit**. It forms part of a pentameric postsynaptic receptor that responds to acetylcholine by opening a cation-permeable channel, thereby supporting cholinergic neuromuscular transmission and locomotion.
