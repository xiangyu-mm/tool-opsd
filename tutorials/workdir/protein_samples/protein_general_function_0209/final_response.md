## Functional summary

This sequence is very likely an **HIV-1 envelope glycoprotein precursor, gp160 (Env)**. It matches reviewed HIV-1 Env references at **82.9% sequence identity**, with the full query aligned. Pfam also detects the characteristic **GP120** and **GP41** domains.

Env is made as a precursor and, in the reference proteins, is processed by host furin-like proteases into **gp120** and **gp41**. In the mature envelope spike, these form associated subunits that assemble into trimers. **Gp120** mediates attachment to host **CD4** and subsequent engagement of a chemokine coreceptor such as **CCR5 or CXCR4**; **gp41** anchors Env in the membrane and drives viral–host membrane fusion. The sequence is consistent with a signal peptide, a large extracellular region, a single membrane-spanning segment, and a cytoplasmic tail.

**Sequence-derived properties:** 768 amino acids; estimated molecular mass **86.5 kDa**; theoretical pI **8.65**. These are estimates for the unprocessed sequence and do not account for glycosylation or other modifications.
