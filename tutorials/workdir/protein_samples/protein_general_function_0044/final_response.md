## Functional summary

The 242-residue protein is most consistent with **UbiE, a bacterial ubiquinone/menaquinone biosynthesis C-methyltransferase**.

### Evidence

- **Strong homology:** 67.8% amino-acid identity across the full sequence to reviewed UbiE protein **Q9X3X2** from *Zymomonas mobilis*; E-value \(6.2 \times 10^{-114}\).
- **Domain support:** Pfam detects the characteristic **Ubie_methyltran domain (PF01209)** spanning approximately residues 16–241, along with several related methyltransferase signatures.
- **Expected reaction:** SAM-dependent methyl transfer:

\[
\text{quinone precursor} + \text{S-adenosyl-L-methionine}
\rightarrow
\text{methylated quinone} + \text{S-adenosyl-L-homocysteine}
\]

The homolog-supported activities correspond to:

- **EC 2.1.1.163:** demethylmenaquinone methyltransferase
- **EC 2.1.1.201:** 2-methoxy-6-polyprenyl-1,4-benzoquinol methyltransferase

Thus, the protein likely participates in **menaquinone and/or ubiquinone biosynthesis**, depending on the organism’s metabolic pathways.
