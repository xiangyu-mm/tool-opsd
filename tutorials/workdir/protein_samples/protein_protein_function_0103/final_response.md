## Functional assignment

The protein is most likely a **bacterial glutaminase** (**EC 3.5.1.2**).

### Evidence

- **Length:** 304 amino acids; predicted mass ~32.7 kDa.
- **Domain:** Strong match to the **Glutaminase Pfam domain PF04960** across nearly the full sequence.
- **Homology:** The sequence is an **exact full-length match** to the reviewed *Chromobacterium violaceum* glutaminase Q7NQH9. Other reviewed actinobacterial glutaminases show ~62–63% identity over essentially the complete sequence.
- **Catalytic reaction:**

\[
\text{L-glutamine} + \mathrm{H_2O}
\rightarrow
\text{L-glutamate} + \mathrm{NH_4^+}
\]

- **Likely oligomeric state:** Homotetramer, based on the characterized homologs.

## Probable biological process

The protein likely participates in **L-glutamine catabolism** by hydrolyzing glutamine to glutamate and ammonia. This can provide:

- **Glutamate**, which feeds central nitrogen metabolism and biosynthetic reactions.
- **Ammonium**, which can be reassimilated or used as a nitrogen source.

Thus, the concise assignment is:

> **A soluble bacterial glutaminase that catalyzes glutamine deamidation and participates in L-glutamine catabolism and nitrogen metabolism.**

The assignment is high confidence because it is supported by an exact reviewed sequence match, catalytic annotation, reaction mapping, GO process annotation, and a matching full-length glutaminase domain.
