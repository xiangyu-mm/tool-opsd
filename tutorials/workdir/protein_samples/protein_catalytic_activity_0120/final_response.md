## Functional summary

This 237-aa sequence is an **exact full-length match** to reviewed *Aspergillus aculeatus* endoglucanase-1 (UniProt **P22669**). The enzyme is annotated as **EC 3.2.1.4**, an endo-β-1,4-glucanase, and the sequence contains a **Glyco_hydro_12 (GH12)** domain. The reference protein is annotated as secreted.

**Reaction:** the enzyme uses water to hydrolyze internal **β-(1→4)-D-glucosidic bonds** in cellulose and related β-glucans, including lichenin:

**cellulose chain + H₂O → shorter glucan chains / oligosaccharides**

Because cleavage occurs at internal bonds, it breaks down glucan polymers rather than acting only at their ends. The exact sequence match supports this assignment strongly; the specific substrate range beyond the annotated cellulose and related β-glucans should not be inferred more broadly from homologs alone.
