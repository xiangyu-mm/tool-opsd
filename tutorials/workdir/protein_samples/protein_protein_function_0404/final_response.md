## Functional prediction

This protein is most likely **MCH1, a fungal vacuolar membrane transporter** belonging to the **major facilitator superfamily (MFS)**.

### Evidence

- **486 amino acids** and strongly hydrophobic, with numerous membrane-spanning segments, indicating a **multipass integral membrane protein**.
- Contains an **MFS_1 domain** spanning approximately residues **35–421**.
- The sequence is an **exact match to reviewed *Yarrowia lipolytica* MCH1 (UniProt Q6CGU8)**.
- Closely related fungal proteins from *Debaryomyces hansenii* and *Candida albicans* are also annotated as **probable transporters** localized to the **fungal vacuole membrane**.
- The protein’s size and architecture are consistent with an MFS-type transporter containing the characteristic repeated transmembrane-helical arrangement.

### Predicted function

**MCH1 is a probable vacuolar membrane transporter that facilitates transmembrane movement of an as-yet-unspecified small molecule or metabolite in fungi.**

The available evidence supports transporter activity and vacuolar localization, but **does not establish the transported substrate, transport direction, or energy-coupling mechanism**.
