## Functional summary

The 244-amino-acid protein is almost certainly **ZapD, a bacterial cell-division protein**.

### Evidence

- It is an **exact full-length sequence match** to reviewed *Shewanella oneidensis* ZapD (UniProt **Q8EJQ0**).
- It contains the characteristic **ZapD domain** (Pfam **PF07072**, residues approximately 18–222).
- Closely related ZapD proteins from other *Shewanella* strains share the same annotation and function.
- No catalytic reaction or enzyme-associated motif was identified, supporting a **structural/regulatory rather than enzymatic role**.

### Likely biological processes

ZapD likely participates in:

1. **FtsZ-ring assembly**  
   It binds FtsZ and promotes bundling and organization of FtsZ protofilaments into the cytokinetic Z-ring.

2. **FtsZ-dependent bacterial cytokinesis**  
   By stabilizing or organizing the Z-ring, ZapD helps coordinate constriction at the cell midpoint.

3. **Cell septum assembly**  
   Its activity contributes to formation of the septal division machinery and proper placement of the division site.

4. **Spatial regulation of cell division**  
   ZapD is a cytoplasmic protein that localizes to mid-cell in an FtsZ-dependent manner.

### Additional sequence properties

- Length: **244 aa**
- Estimated molecular mass: **28.7 kDa**
- Theoretical pI: **7.12**
- No predicted membrane-spanning segment or secretion signal was reported, consistent with a **soluble cytoplasmic protein**.

**Overall:** This protein is best characterized as a **nonenzymatic FtsZ-associated cell-division factor that promotes Z-ring organization and bacterial septum formation**, with high confidence based on the exact sequence match and curated annotation.
