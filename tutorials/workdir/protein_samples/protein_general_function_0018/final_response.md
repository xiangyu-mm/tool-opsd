### Functional summary

This 302-amino-acid protein is **NadA (quinolinate synthase A)**, a soluble enzyme in the archaeal **de novo NAD⁺ biosynthesis pathway**.

- **Function:** Catalyzes formation of quinolinate from iminoaspartate/iminosuccinate and dihydroxyacetone phosphate.  
  **EC:** 2.5.1.72; **Rhea:** 25888
- **Cofactor:** Binds one **[4Fe–4S] iron–sulfur cluster** per subunit.
- **Domain:** Complete **NadA domain (Pfam PF02445)**, spanning approximately residues 8–300.
- **Localization:** Expected to be **cytoplasmic**.
- **Sequence properties:** 302 aa; predicted molecular mass **34.1 kDa**; theoretical pI **6.48**; mildly hydrophilic overall.
- **Evidence strength:** The sequence is an **exact full-length match** to reviewed *Thermococcus kodakarensis* NadA (UniProt Q5JFX9), with additional close matches from other archaea.

Overall, this is a highly confident identification of a **[4Fe–4S]-dependent quinolinate synthase involved in NAD⁺ production**. The available evidence does not establish its oligomeric state.
