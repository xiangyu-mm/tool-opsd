## Functional summary

This is **pallidipin**, a secreted triabin-family lipocalin-like protein from a blood-feeding insect. It is reported to inhibit **collagen-induced platelet aggregation**. Related triabin proteins bind and sequester inflammatory eicosanoids, particularly thromboxane/prostaglandin-related molecules, suggesting that pallidipin may act through a similar **eicosanoid-scavenging mechanism**.

Overall, the sequence supports a small secreted extracellular protein involved in **anti-hemostatic and host-defense modulation**, rather than an enzyme. The exact sequence match provides strong confidence in the functional assignment, although individual ligand specificities and mechanisms should be confirmed experimentally.
