## Probable function

This is most likely **RibBA**, a bacterial bifunctional enzyme involved in **riboflavin (vitamin B₂) biosynthesis**.

The 407-residue protein contains two strongly supported domains:

- **DHBP synthase domain**: residues approximately 11–201  
  - Pfam: **DHBP_synthase (PF00926)**
  - Catalyzes conversion of **D-ribulose 5-phosphate** to **3,4-dihydroxy-2-butanone 4-phosphate (DHBP)**, releasing formate.
  - EC **4.1.99.12**

- **GTP cyclohydrolase II domain**: residues approximately 212–376  
  - Pfam: **GTP_cyclohydro2 (PF00925)**
  - Catalyzes conversion of **GTP** to a riboflavin-pathway pyrimidine precursor, with formate and phosphate/pyrophosphate products.
  - EC **3.5.4.25**

Curated RibBA homologs share approximately **65–68% amino-acid identity across nearly the entire sequence**, providing strong support for this annotation.

## Biological process

The protein participates in the early steps of **riboflavin biosynthesis**. Its two activities generate independent precursors that are subsequently assembled into riboflavin and its active cofactors:

- **FMN — flavin mononucleotide**
- **FAD — flavin adenine dinucleotide**

These flavin cofactors are required by many oxidoreductases and other metabolic enzymes.
