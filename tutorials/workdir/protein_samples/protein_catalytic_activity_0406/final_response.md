The sequence exactly matches reviewed UniProt entry **Q6A8L2**, the **HisH glutaminase subunit** of imidazole glycerol phosphate synthase (IGPS).

**Direct reaction catalyzed by HisH:**

\[
\text{L-glutamine} + \mathrm{H_2O} \rightarrow \text{L-glutamate} + \mathrm{NH_4^+}
\]

HisH supplies the resulting ammonia to the HisF subunit. Together, the IGPS complex uses glutamine and **PRFAR** to produce **imidazole glycerol phosphate (IGP)** and **AICAR**, with glutamate also formed. Thus, the direct catalytic activity of this protein is glutamine hydrolysis; the IGP/AICAR-forming reaction is the coupled activity of the enzyme complex.
