## Functional summary

This 192-aa protein is most likely a **Ham1-like dITP/XTP pyrophosphatase** (EC 3.6.1.66). It contains the Ham1p-like Pfam domain (PF01725), and aligns across nearly its full length to reviewed bacterial dITP/XTP pyrophosphatases at 53–60% identity. The functional assignment is therefore well supported by homology, but has **not been experimentally verified for this specific sequence**.

The enzyme hydrolyzes noncanonical purine nucleoside triphosphates to their monophosphates, with homolog annotations indicating preference for **dITP and XTP**, and activity on **ITP** as well. This “housekeeping” activity is thought to help clear abnormal nucleotides from the pool and reduce their incorporation into DNA or RNA.

**Reactions:**
- XTP + H₂O → XMP + diphosphate (pyrophosphate, PPi) + H⁺
- dITP + H₂O → dIMP + diphosphate (PPi) + H⁺
- ITP + H₂O → IMP + diphosphate (PPi) + H⁺

The corresponding reviewed homologs are annotated to use **Mg²⁺** (one ion per subunit) and to form a homodimer; these features are predicted for this sequence by homology, not directly established.
