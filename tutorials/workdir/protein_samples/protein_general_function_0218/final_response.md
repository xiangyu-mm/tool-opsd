### Functional summary

This 636-amino-acid protein is an **NAD-dependent DNA ligase** (**EC 6.5.1.2**), involved in sealing single-strand breaks or nicks in double-stranded DNA during **DNA replication and repair**.

The assignment is highly reliable: the sequence is an **exact full-length match** to reviewed UniProt protein **Q5UPZ0** from *Acanthamoeba polyphaga mimivirus*. A more distant viral homolog also supports the same function.

### Key attributes

- **Length:** 636 residues  
- **Predicted molecular mass:** ~72.1 kDa  
- **Theoretical pI:** 9.03; basic protein  
- **Predicted charge at pH 7:** approximately +11  
- **Hydropathy:** mildly hydrophilic overall; no obvious transmembrane region indicated  
- **DNA ligase adenylation domain:** residues ~91–290  
- **C-terminal BRCT domain:** residues ~561–627, consistent with DNA damage/repair-associated interactions  

### Catalytic activity

The enzyme uses **NAD⁺** as both cofactor and energy source to join:

- a DNA **5′-phosphate** terminus, and  
- a DNA **3′-hydroxyl** terminus,

forming a phosphodiester bond and producing AMP plus nicotinamide mononucleotide-derived product.

Overall, this is best classified as a **viral NAD-dependent DNA ligase with a C-terminal BRCT domain**, rather than an ATP-dependent DNA ligase.
