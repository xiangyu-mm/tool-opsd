## Functional identification

The 284-aa sequence is an **exact match** to reviewed UniProt entry **B2A499**, annotated as probable **endonuclease IV** from *Natranaerobius thermophilus* (EC **3.1.21.2**). This supports the identification more strongly than homology alone.

Endonuclease IV participates in DNA repair by incising DNA at **apurinic/apyrimidinic (AP) sites**—positions where a base is missing. It cleaves the DNA phosphodiester backbone at the lesion, yielding a **3′-hydroxyl end** and a **5′-terminal sugar-phosphate end**. The reference annotation reports binding of **three Zn²⁺ ions**.

**In brief:** this is an AP-site DNA-repair endonuclease; its annotated reaction is backbone cleavage at abasic DNA sites.
