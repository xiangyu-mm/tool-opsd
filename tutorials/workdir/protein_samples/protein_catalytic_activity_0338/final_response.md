## Functional identification

The protein is **argininosuccinate synthase** (**EC 6.3.4.5**), an ATP-dependent ligase involved in arginine production.

This assignment is highly confident because the 410-residue sequence is an **exact sequence match** to the reviewed UniProt argininosuccinate synthase **A6VVI2**. Additional reviewed homologs show approximately 81–82% identity across nearly the full sequence. The Pfam domains **Arginosuc_synth (PF00764)** and **Arginosuc_syn_C (PF20979)** independently support this identification.

### Catalyzed reaction

\[
\text{L-citrulline} + \text{L-aspartate} + \text{ATP}
\rightarrow
\text{L-argininosuccinate} + \text{AMP} + \text{diphosphate} + \text{H}^+
\]

The product is also called **2-(Nω-L-arginino)succinate**.

### Catalytic role

Argininosuccinate synthase joins the ureido group of **citrulline** to the amino-acid backbone of **aspartate**, using ATP energy. ATP is cleaved to **AMP and pyrophosphate**, indicating an adenylation-dependent ligation mechanism:

1. Citrulline is activated by ATP-dependent adenylation.
2. Aspartate attacks the activated intermediate.
3. Argininosuccinate is formed, with AMP and diphosphate released.

### Biological context

- **Pathway:** L-arginine biosynthesis; step 2 of 3 from ornithine and carbamoyl phosphate

- **Localization:** Cytoplasm

**Conclusion:** This is a canonical bacterial cytosolic **argininosuccinate synthase**, catalyzing ATP-dependent formation of argininosuccinate from citrulline and aspartate.
