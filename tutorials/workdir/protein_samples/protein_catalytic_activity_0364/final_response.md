## Functional identification

This is **aminomethyltransferase** (EC **2.1.2.10**), also called the **T protein** of the glycine cleavage system. The 372-amino-acid sequence is an exact match to the reviewed *Streptomyces coelicolor* UniProt protein **O86567**. Its GCV_T and GCV_T_C domain matches are consistent with this assignment.

## Catalytic activity

The enzyme transfers an aminomethyl group from the **lipoyl-lysine arm of the glycine-cleavage H protein** to tetrahydrofolate (THF). This forms 5,10-methylene-THF and releases ammonium, while leaving the lipoyl arm in its reduced form:

**N⁶-[(R)-S⁸-aminomethyldihydrolipoyl]-L-lysyl-[protein] + (6S)-5,6,7,8-tetrahydrofolate → N⁶-[(R)-dihydrolipoyl]-L-lysyl-[protein] + (6R)-5,10-methylene-5,6,7,8-tetrahydrofolate + NH₄⁺**

This is one step in glycine degradation by the glycine cleavage system; the aminomethyltransferase does **not** perform the entire glycine-cleavage process by itself. The exact sequence match and curated enzyme annotation provide strong support for the assignment.
