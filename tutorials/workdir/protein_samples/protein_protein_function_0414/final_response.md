## Likely function

This 669-aa protein is most likely a **bifunctional NAD(P)H-hydrate repair enzyme (Nnr)**. Its YjeF_N and Carb_kinase domains and significant similarity to curated Nnr proteins support this assignment.

The inferred activities are:
- **NAD(P)HX epimerase** (EC 5.1.99.6), interconverting the R and S forms of hydrated NADH/NADPH.
- **ADP-dependent NAD(P)H-hydrate dehydratase** (EC 4.2.1.136), converting the S form back to NADH or NADPH while ADP is converted to AMP and phosphate.

**Associated biological process:** metabolite repair, particularly nicotinamide nucleotide metabolism. This activity helps recover NAD(P)H lost to hydration.

**Evidence and caveat:** The assignment is supported by Pfam domains and reviewed homologs at about 31–33% sequence identity, but the exact protein has not been directly characterized in this analysis. Homologs bind K⁺; that cofactor assignment is inferred for this sequence. Localization is not established by the retrieved evidence.
