## Functional summary

This 487-amino-acid protein is most consistent with a **bifunctional NAD(P)H-hydrate repair enzyme (Nnr)** involved in **metabolite repair**.

- **Length:** 487 aa  
- **Predicted molecular mass:** ~51.4 kDa  
- **Theoretical pI:** 5.07

- **GRAVY:** 0.218, indicating a moderately hydrophilic protein  
- **Instability index:** 23.2, consistent with a predicted stable soluble protein  

### Functional assignment

The strongest homolog is the reviewed *Coxiella burnetii* Nnr protein **Q83CM5**:

- **47.9% sequence identity**
- **98.4% query coverage**
- **E-value:** 3.4 × 10⁻¹⁴²

Additional high-coverage homologs occur in *E. coli* and *Vibrio cholerae*. These homologs are annotated as **bifunctional NAD(P)H-hydrate repair enzymes**.

The likely activities are:

1. **NAD(P)HX epimerase activity**  
   Interconverts the R- and S-forms of NADHX and NADPHX.

2. **ADP-dependent NAD(P)H-hydrate dehydratase activity**  
   Repairs the S-form of NAD(P)HX using ADP:

   \[
   \text{NAD(P)HX} + \text{ADP}
   \rightarrow \text{NAD(P)H} + \text{AMP} + \text{phosphate}
   \]

These reactions restore functional NADH or NADPH from hydrated, damaged forms generated spontaneously or enzymatically.

### Domain architecture

The sequence contains two strong Pfam matches:

- **YjeF_N domain:** residues ~28–188  
- **Carbohydrate-kinase-like domain:** residues ~243–477  

This two-domain architecture is characteristic of bacterial Nnr proteins and supports the bifunctional annotation.

### Cofactor and cellular role

- Reviewed homologs bind **one potassium ion per subunit**.
- The protein is associated with **nicotinamide-nucleotide metabolism** and **metabolite repair**.
- Its exact cellular localization and oligomeric state were not established by this sequence analysis.

### Confidence and limitations

The functional assignment is supported by highly significant, nearly full-length similarity to reviewed Nnr proteins and matching domain organization. However, the query protein itself has not been experimentally characterized here, so the exact substrate preference, kinetic properties, potassium dependence, and reaction direction should be treated as **homology-based predictions** rather than direct experimental findings.
