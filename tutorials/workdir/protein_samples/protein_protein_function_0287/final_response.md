## Functional identification

The 101-aa sequence is **bacterial ribosomal protein uS14**, supported by an exact full-length match to reviewed *Pseudoalteromonas atlantica* UniProt entry Q15X60 and a matching Ribosomal_S14 domain (PF00253).

- **Molecular function:** Binds 16S rRNA and acts as a structural component of the ribosome. It contributes to assembly of the 30S ribosomal subunit; the reference annotation also suggests a role in shaping 16S rRNA at the A site.
- **Subcellular localization:** The **30S small ribosomal subunit** in the bacterial ribosome.
- **Biological process:** **Translation**, including assembly and function of the small ribosomal subunit.

The strongly basic sequence is consistent with an RNA-binding ribosomal protein, but the identification rests on the exact sequence match and domain evidence—not basic composition alone. **DNA-binding or chromatin-associated roles are not supported** by the retrieved evidence.
