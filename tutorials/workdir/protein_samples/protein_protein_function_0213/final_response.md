## Functional identification

The 539-amino-acid sequence is an **exact match** (100% identity and coverage) to reviewed *Bacillus subtilis* strain 168 protein **DhbE** (UniProt **P40871**), also called **2,3-dihydroxybenzoate-AMP ligase**.

DhbE participates in **bacillibactin biosynthesis**, a pathway that produces a catecholic siderophore. It activates **2,3-dihydroxybenzoate (DHB)** using ATP, forming a DHB–adenylate intermediate. This activation prepares DHB for incorporation into the growing siderophore.

## Localization and biological processes

- **Subcellular localization:** Cytoplasm, according to the matching UniProt annotation.
- **Biological process:** Siderophore biosynthesis, specifically bacillibactin biosynthesis.
- **Molecular function:** 2,3-dihydroxybenzoate–aryl-carrier-protein ligase activity (GO:0008668).
