## Catalytic reaction

\[
\text{Acyl-phosphate} + \text{sn-glycerol-3-phosphate}
\rightarrow
\text{1-acyl-sn-glycero-3-phosphate} + \text{phosphate}
\]

The product, **1-acyl-sn-glycero-3-phosphate**, is also called **lysophosphatidic acid (LPA)**. This reaction introduces the first fatty-acyl chain into the glycerol-phosphate backbone and contributes to **phospholipid biosynthesis**.

Importantly, this enzyme uses an **acyl-phosphate** as its fatty-acyl donor. The curated annotation specifically distinguishes it from acyltransferases that use **acyl-CoA** or **acyl-ACP**.
