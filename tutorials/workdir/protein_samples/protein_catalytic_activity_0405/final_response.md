## Functional identification

This 405-amino-acid sequence is an **exact sequence match** to reviewed *Porphyromonas gingivalis* RibBA (UniProt **B2RIG7**; 100% identity and coverage). RibBA is a **bifunctional enzyme in riboflavin biosynthesis**, with annotations **EC 3.5.4.25** and **EC 4.1.99.12**.

It catalyzes two reactions:

1. **D-ribulose-5-phosphate cleavage**  
   D-ribulose 5-phosphate → 3,4-dihydroxy-2-butanone 4-phosphate + formate  
   This produces a precursor for riboflavin synthesis.

2. **GTP conversion to a pyrimidine precursor**  
   The curated annotation describes conversion of GTP to **DARP** (2,5-diamino-6-ribosylamino-4(3H)-pyrimidinone 5′-phosphate), with formate and pyrophosphate released. The linked Rhea equation represents the pyrimidine product in an alternative chemical form and lists phosphate products.

The reference annotation reports binding of **Mg²⁺ or Mn²⁺** and **Zn²⁺**. These functional assignments are supported by the exact match to the reviewed reference protein.
