## Functional summary

This 768-aa sequence is strongly supported as an **ABCB6-like ATP-binding cassette (ABC) transporter**. Its N-terminal region contains membrane-spanning domains, while its C-terminal region contains an ABC ATPase/nucleotide-binding domain. It is not best understood as an enzyme that chemically modifies porphyrins: it uses ATP hydrolysis to move them across membranes.

**Best-supported coupled reaction:**

\[
\text{porphyrin}_{\text{in}}+\text{ATP}+\mathrm{H_2O}
\rightarrow
\text{porphyrin}_{\text{out}}+\text{ADP}+\mathrm{P_i}+\mathrm{H^+}
\]

The substrate is translocated, not chemically converted. Close reviewed ABCB6 homologs are annotated for transport of **heme and other porphyrins**, with database-linked examples including heme b, protoporphyrin IX, and coproporphyrin III. The precise substrate range and transport direction may depend on cellular context; transport from the cytoplasm toward the cell exterior or vesicle lumen is supported, while mitochondrial porphyrin import is a proposed additional role.
