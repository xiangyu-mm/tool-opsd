### Summary

The sequence is **768 amino acids** and is an exact match to the **N-terminal portion of human ASXL3** (UniProt Q9C0F0); the reference protein is 2,248 aa, so this sequence appears to be a fragment rather than the full-length protein.

ASXL3 is a nuclear, non-catalytic component of the **PR-DUB complex**, an epigenetic regulator that removes monoubiquitin from histone H2A at Lys119 (H2AK119ub1). The complex influences gene expression, including genes involved in development and cell signaling. UniProt also describes ASXL3 as a putative Polycomb-group protein.

Sequence analysis identifies **HARE-HTH** (residues 10–80) and **ASXH** (240–360) domains. The reference full-length protein has a C-terminal PHD domain, but that domain is not present in this fragment. The sequence is acidic overall (predicted pI 4.73), hydrophilic (GRAVY −0.64), and has a high predicted instability index; these are sequence-based estimates, not experimental measurements.

**Evidence caveat:** The functional description comes from the curated full-length ASXL3 reference record, not direct experiments on this isolated sequence. The fragment’s exact activity cannot be established from sequence alone.
