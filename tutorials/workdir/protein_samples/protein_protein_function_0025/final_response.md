## Functional prediction

The protein is **elongation factor Ts (EF-Ts)**, the bacterial guanine-nucleotide exchange factor for **EF-Tu**.

### Evidence

- **Exact sequence match:** 100% identity and 100% coverage to reviewed *Pseudomonas putida* EF-Ts protein A5W849.
- **Domain match:** Strong **EF_TS domain** signature (Pfam PF00889.26; residues ~79–265; i-Evalue 1.8 × 10⁻⁵⁴).
- **Size:** 287 amino acids, approximately 30.4 kDa, consistent with bacterial EF-Ts.
- **Cellular localization:** Cytoplasmic.
- **Sequence architecture:** Highly charged, largely soluble and helix-rich in composition; no apparent transmembrane segment or secretion signal.
- **Conserved functional region:** Residues 80–83 are annotated as contributing to Mg²⁺ displacement from EF-Tu.

### Biological role

EF-Ts binds the **EF-Tu·GDP** complex and promotes GDP release, allowing GTP binding:

\[
\mathrm{EF\text{-}Tu\cdot GDP + GTP \rightarrow EF\text{-}Tu\cdot GTP + GDP}
\]

The regenerated EF-Tu·GTP then delivers aminoacyl-tRNA to the ribosome during translation elongation. EF-Ts remains associated with the EF-Tu–GTP/aminoacyl-tRNA complex until GTP hydrolysis.

### Conclusion

**Predicted function:** bacterial **translation elongation factor Ts**, a cytoplasmic, non-enzymatic nucleotide-exchange factor that recycles EF-Tu from its GDP-bound to GTP-bound state. No independent catalytic reaction or required cofactor was identified.
