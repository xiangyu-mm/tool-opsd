## Functional summary

The 160-residue protein is **NHP2/NPH2, an H/ACA ribonucleoprotein complex subunit**. The identification is very strong: the sequence is an **exact 100% match** to the reviewed *Drosophila melanogaster* protein UniProt **Q9V3U2**.

### Key sequence features

- **Length:** 160 amino acids  
- **Estimated molecular mass:** ~17.7 kDa  
- **Theoretical pI:** ~8.25  
- **Predicted domain:** **Ribosomal_L7Ae domain** (PF01248; residues 52–145)  
- The protein is relatively basic, consistent with interaction with RNA.
- No transmembrane segment or secretion signal is apparent, supporting a soluble intracellular role.

### Molecular function

NHP2 is an **RNA-associated structural and assembly component** of the **box H/ACA small nucleolar RNP (snoRNP)** complex. It works together with proteins such as:

- **Nop60B**, the catalytic pseudouridine synthase
- **Gar1**
- **Nop10**
- H/ACA-type snoRNAs

NHP2 contributes to recognition and organization of the snoRNA–protein complex. It is **not itself expected to be the catalytic pseudouridine synthase**; the retrieved annotation did not provide an enzymatic reaction for NHP2.

### Biological processes

The protein is likely involved in:

1. **snoRNA-guided rRNA pseudouridylation**  
   H/ACA snoRNPs guide conversion of selected uridines in precursor rRNA into pseudouridine.

2. **snRNA pseudouridine synthesis**  
   Related H/ACA complexes can also modify small nuclear RNAs.

3. **Ribosome biogenesis**  
   Pseudouridylated rRNA contributes to proper rRNA folding, maturation, and ribosome function.

4. **RNA–protein complex assembly**  
   The L7Ae-like domain supports its role in binding or organizing structured RNA within the H/ACA complex.

In *Drosophila*, this pathway is also associated with germline development and oogenesis, although that organism-specific developmental annotation should be treated as context-dependent rather than a universal function of the protein.

### Cellular location

The expected localization is:

- **Nucleus**
- Especially the **nucleolus**, where rRNA processing and ribosome assembly occur

### Overall conclusion

This is a small, basic, soluble **H/ACA snoRNP RNA-binding/assembly protein**, most likely NHP2. Its primary role is to support snoRNA-guided pseudouridylation of rRNA and snRNA and thereby promote ribosome biogenesis. The functional assignment has **very high confidence** because the sequence exactly matches a curated reference protein, and its predicted L7Ae domain is consistent with the annotated role.
