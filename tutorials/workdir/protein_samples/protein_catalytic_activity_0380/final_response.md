The 234-amino-acid protein is **BioD, ATP-dependent dethiobiotin synthetase** (**EC 6.3.3.3**).

This assignment is highly reliable: the sequence is an **exact full-length match** to a reviewed Swiss-Prot BioD protein, with 100% identity and coverage. Its Pfam profile is also consistent with the BioD/CbiA enzyme family.

### Catalytic activity

BioD catalyzes an ATP-dependent carboxylation and cyclization step in **biotin biosynthesis**. It inserts carbon dioxide between the two amino groups of **7,8-diaminopelargonic acid (DAPA)**, forming the ureido ring of **dethiobiotin**.

**Overall reaction:**

\[
\text{7,8-diaminopelargonic acid} + \mathrm{CO_2} + \mathrm{ATP}
\rightarrow
\text{dethiobiotin} + \mathrm{ADP} + \mathrm{P_i} + 3\mathrm{H^+}
\]

More explicitly:

\[
(7R,8S)\text{-7,8-diammoniononanoate}+\mathrm{CO_2}+\mathrm{ATP}
\rightarrow
(4R,5S)\text{-dethiobiotin}+\mathrm{ADP}+\mathrm{phosphate}+3\mathrm{H^+}
\]

The reaction is unusual because ATP is used to drive **CO₂ insertion between the N7 and N8 nitrogen atoms**, followed by formation of the cyclic ureido group.
