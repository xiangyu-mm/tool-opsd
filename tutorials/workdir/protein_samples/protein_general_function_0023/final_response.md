### Function and localization
TGFBI is a **secreted extracellular-matrix adhesion protein**. It is associated with:

- Cell–matrix adhesion
- Collagen interactions
- Integrin-mediated adhesion
- Basement membranes, microfibrils, elastic fibers, and the extracellular matrix
- The cell surface and, in some contexts, extracellular vesicles/exosomes

The annotated protein binds **type I, II, and IV collagens** and may function as an extracellular-matrix structural or adhesion component.
