### Functional summary

This **352-amino-acid** sequence exactly matches the reviewed **Hendra virus matrix (M) protein UniProt O89341** (100% identity and coverage). It is a **paramyxovirus structural protein**, not a recognized enzyme.

- **Function:** The matrix protein helps assemble and bud new virus particles. It forms a shell along the inner face of the host-cell plasma membrane and is annotated as a structural constituent of the virion. The reference also describes it as a homomultimer.
- **Subcellular localization:** It is associated with the **virion**, and is found in the infected host cell’s **cytoplasm and nucleus**. During budding, it associates with the **inner surface of the plasma membrane**. The annotation notes interaction with host ANP32B as promoting nuclear localization.
- **Biological process:** Its primary annotated process is **virion assembly** (GO:0019068), including particle budding.
- **Domains:** Pfam detects paramyxovirus matrix-protein N- and C-terminal domains (PF00661, residues 18–184; PF23765, residues 187–345).
- **Catalytic activity/cofactors:** No catalytic reaction or cofactor was retrieved; the available evidence supports a structural/assembly role.

The sequence match makes the protein identification especially strong. However, some specific functional details in the Hendra reference are annotated “by similarity,” so they should be understood as database-supported annotation rather than all being direct experimental findings for this exact sequence. The reference annotations do not establish every possible role during infection.
