### Functional summary

The 768-aa sequence is most likely a **bifunctional aspartokinase/homoserine dehydrogenase**, which catalyzes two steps in the aspartate-family amino-acid biosynthesis pathway.

1. **Aspartokinase (EC 2.7.2.4):**  
   **L-aspartate + ATP ⇌ 4-phospho-L-aspartate + ADP**

2. **Homoserine dehydrogenase (EC 1.1.1.3):**  
   **L-aspartate 4-semialdehyde + NAD(P)H + H⁺ ⇌ L-homoserine + NAD(P)⁺**

The reaction database also lists the homoserine-dehydrogenase reaction in the reverse direction. Close reviewed homologs support both activities, with **42.7–46.5% identity across essentially the full query sequence**. This is strong sequence-based support, but not a direct assay of this protein; its specific NADH versus NADPH preference remains unresolved.
