## Protein assessment

### Primary characteristics
- **Length:** 503 amino acids  
- **Predicted molecular mass:** ~56.2 kDa  
- **Theoretical pI:** ~8.97; moderately basic overall  
- **Hydropathy:** one strongly hydrophobic **N-terminal segment (~residues 2–22)**  
- **Domain:** cytochrome P450 domain, approximately residues **35–491**  
- **Conserved feature:** heme axial-ligand region near residue **446**, characteristic of CYP enzymes  

### Functional identification
The sequence is an **exact sequence match** to reviewed UniProt entry **A0A125QZE2** from *Salvia miltiorrhiza*. It is therefore confidently identified as:

> **11-hydroxysugiol 20-monooxygenase**, a plant microsomal cytochrome P450 enzyme.

The enzyme is annotated as **EC 1.14.14.67** and participates in diterpenoid/tanshinone biosynthesis.

### Catalytic activity
The primary reported reaction is:

\[
\text{11-hydroxysugiol} + O_2 + \text{NADPH-derived reducing equivalents}
\rightarrow \text{11,20-dihydroxysugiol}
\]

It can also oxidize **11-hydroxyferruginol** to **11,20-dihydroxyferruginol**. As a cytochrome P450, it uses:

- **Heme** as the prosthetic group
- Molecular oxygen
- Electrons supplied indirectly by a plant NADPH–cytochrome P450 reductase system

### Localization and architecture
The N-terminal hydrophobic sequence is best interpreted as a **membrane anchor/transmembrane helix**, rather than a cleavable secretory signal peptide. This is typical of **microsomal plant P450s**, which are generally anchored to the endoplasmic-reticulum membrane through their N termini while the catalytic domain remains on the cytosolic side.

Although the sequence contains many Lys/Arg residues and is strongly basic in several regions, this does not indicate a separate nucleic-acid-binding mature protein. The overall architecture is that of a membrane-tethered P450 catalytic domain.

### Confidence and caveats
- **Functional identity:** very high confidence, because the sequence is an exact match to a reviewed reference protein.
- **Cofactor and P450 classification:** strongly supported by the Pfam P450 domain and conserved heme-binding region.
- **Exact substrate range:** the experimentally annotated substrates are 11-hydroxysugiol and 11-hydroxyferruginol; additional diterpenoid substrates should not be assumed solely from sequence similarity.

**Bottom line:** This is a **heme-dependent, ER-membrane-associated plant cytochrome P450 monooxygenase involved in tanshinone-related diterpenoid biosynthesis**, specifically catalyzing 20-hydroxylation of 11-hydroxysugiol.
