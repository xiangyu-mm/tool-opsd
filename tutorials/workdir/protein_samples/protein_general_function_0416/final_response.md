The sequence is an **exact match to the reviewed Solanum lycopersicum protein Q2MI45**, annotated as **NAD(P)H-quinone oxidoreductase subunit 1, chloroplastic**. It is therefore best assigned as a membrane subunit of the **chloroplast NADH dehydrogenase-like (NDH) complex**, located in the **thylakoid membrane**.

Functionally, it contributes to the membrane arm of the NDH complex, which transfers reducing equivalents toward plastoquinone and couples electron transport to **proton translocation**. The protein itself is not expected to bind FMN or Fe-S clusters; those functions are supplied by other NDH subunits. Its primary role is likely structural and/or in forming the membrane electron-transfer/proton-translocation pathway.
