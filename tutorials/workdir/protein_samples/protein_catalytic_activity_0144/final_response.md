## Functional identification

The 205-residue protein is **N-(5′-phosphoribosyl)anthranilate isomerase** (**PRAI**, EC **5.3.1.24**), a tryptophan-biosynthesis enzyme.

Evidence:

- Exact full-length sequence match to reviewed UniProt protein **B7J4T0**
- PRAI domain **PF00697** detected across approximately residues 6–200
- Curated EC and Rhea annotations support the same catalytic activity

## Catalytic reaction

\[
\text{N-(5-phospho-}\beta\text{-D-ribosyl)anthranilate}
\rightleftharpoons
\text{1-(2-carboxyphenylamino)-1-deoxy-D-ribulose 5-phosphate}
\]

This is an **intramolecular isomerization/rearrangement**: the phosphoribosyl anthranilate substrate is converted into the corresponding ribulose-5-phosphate intermediate. There is no net oxidation or reduction.

The reaction is **step 3 of 5 in the microbial L-tryptophan biosynthetic pathway from chorismate**. The enzyme is therefore best annotated as:

> **Phosphoribosylanthranilate isomerase**, catalyzing the rearrangement of N-(5′-phosphoribosyl)anthranilate to 1-(2-carboxyphenylamino)-1-deoxy-D-ribulose 5-phosphate.
