## Functional assignment

The 401-aa protein is **strongly predicted to be RibBA**, a bifunctional enzyme in **riboflavin (vitamin B₂) biosynthesis**. Its sequence has both expected domains—DHBP synthase (PF00926) and GTP cyclohydrolase II (PF00925)—and aligns across nearly its full length to reviewed RibBA proteins at about 52–55% identity. This is a homology-supported assignment, not an experimental characterization of this exact sequence.

RibBA carries out two reactions:

1. **DHBP synthase activity (EC 4.1.99.12):**  
   **D-ribulose 5-phosphate → 3,4-dihydroxy-2-butanone 4-phosphate (DHBP) + formate**  
   (Rhea: RHEA:18457)

2. **GTP cyclohydrolase II activity (EC 3.5.4.25):**  
   **GTP → 2,5-diamino-6-ribosylamino-4(3H)-pyrimidinone 5′-phosphate (DARP) + formate + pyrophosphate**  
   The reference reaction mapping also represents the products as the corresponding pyrimidine phosphate, formate, and phosphate species (Rhea: RHEA:23704).

**In short:** this is likely a two-activity RibBA enzyme that supplies intermediates for riboflavin synthesis. Reviewed homologs are annotated as binding divalent metal ions (Mg²⁺ or Mn²⁺) and Zn²⁺, but those cofactor assignments are transferred from homolog evidence, not verified for this sequence.
