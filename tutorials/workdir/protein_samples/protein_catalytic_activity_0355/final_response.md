## Biological function

SLC25A39 transports **reduced glutathione (GSH)** into mitochondria. Mitochondrial glutathione supports redox control and is important for the stability and function of mitochondrial iron–sulfur cluster proteins.

The protein may also bind a **[2Fe–2S] cluster**, which participates in regulation of SLC25A39 stability and degradation. This is a regulatory ligand interaction, not evidence of enzymatic catalysis.
