## Functional assignment

The 203-residue protein is most consistent with a **Ham1p-like dITP/XTP pyrophosphatase** (**EC 3.6.1.66**).

It belongs to the **Ham1p-like domain family** (Pfam PF01725), and close reviewed homologs show approximately **49–61% sequence identity over ~95–97% of the sequence**, strongly supporting this assignment.

## Catalytic reaction

The enzyme hydrolyzes noncanonical purine nucleoside triphosphates to their corresponding monophosphates:

- **dITP + H₂O → dIMP + PPᵢ + H⁺**
- **ITP + H₂O → IMP + PPᵢ + H⁺**
- **XTP + H₂O → XMP + PPᵢ + H⁺**

where PPᵢ is inorganic diphosphate (pyrophosphate).

The strongest functional description is therefore:

> **Mg²⁺-dependent purine nucleoside triphosphate pyrophosphatase, with preference for dITP, ITP, and XTP.**

This is a nucleotide “house-cleaning” enzyme that removes aberrant purine triphosphates from the cellular nucleotide pool, helping prevent their incorporation into DNA or RNA.

**Cofactor:** approximately one **Mg²⁺ per subunit**, based on the close curated homologs.

**Confidence:** **moderate to high**, based on the Ham1p-like domain and strong full-length homology; the exact substrate preference for this specific sequence remains inferred rather than directly experimentally measured.
