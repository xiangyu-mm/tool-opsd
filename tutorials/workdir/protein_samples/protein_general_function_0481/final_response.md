### Likely enzymatic activities

The protein appears to combine two normally separate pathway functions:

1. **IspD: MEP cytidylyltransferase**  
   Converts 2-C-methyl-D-erythritol 4-phosphate and CTP into 4-CDP-2-C-methyl-D-erythritol.

   **EC:** 2.7.7.60

2. **IspF: ME-cPP synthase**  
   Converts phosphorylated CDP-methylerythritol to 2-C-methyl-D-erythritol 2,4-cyclic diphosphate, releasing CMP.

   **EC:** 4.6.1.12

Together, these reactions correspond to steps **2 and 4** of the MEP pathway, which produces the precursors IPP and DMAPP for bacterial terpenoid/isoprenoid biosynthesis.
