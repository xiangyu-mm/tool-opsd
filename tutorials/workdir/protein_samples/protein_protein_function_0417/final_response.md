## Functional summary

This is **HIV-1 envelope glycoprotein gp160 (Env)**, supported by near-full-length matches to reviewed HIV-1 gp160 proteins (75.5–79.8% identity; 100% query coverage for the closest hit).

gp160 is a precursor that is cleaved by host furin-like proteases into **gp120** and **gp41**. gp120 mediates viral attachment to **CD4** and enables engagement of a chemokine coreceptor such as **CCR5 or CXCR4**. gp41 is the membrane-fusion subunit. Together, Env proteins form the viral surface spikes that promote entry into target cells.

**Localization:** Env is produced and processed through the host endoplasmic reticulum and Golgi, then occurs in the **virion envelope**. During entry, it is also associated with **host-cell plasma and endosomal membranes**.

**Associated biological processes:** Env precursor processing; virion attachment to host cells; clathrin-dependent viral endocytosis; and fusion of viral and host membranes to deliver viral contents into the cell.

These assignments are strongly supported by homology to reviewed HIV-1 Env proteins. The sequence supports identification as HIV-1 Env, but does not by itself establish the exact strain or CCR5/CXCR4 tropism.
