### Functional summary

This 470-amino-acid sequence is **influenza A virus neuraminidase (NA)**. It is an **exact full-length match** to reviewed UniProt entry **Q07579**, neuraminidase from the equine H3N8 strain A/Equine/Miami/1/1963.

Neuraminidase is a **sialidase (EC 3.2.1.18)**: it cleaves terminal sialic acid residues from viral and cellular glycoconjugates. This activity helps influenza virions detach and spread, including by removing sialic acids from viral hemagglutinin and host-cell surfaces. The reference annotation reports **Ca²⁺** as a cofactor.
