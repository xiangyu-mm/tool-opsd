### Probable function

This 554-aa protein is most likely a **bifunctional RibBA enzyme involved in riboflavin (vitamin B2) biosynthesis**.

The sequence contains two strong Pfam matches:

- **DHBP synthase domain**: residues ~11–204  
- **GTP cyclohydrolase II domain**: residues ~217–381  

It also shows strong similarity to the reviewed *Synechocystis* RibBA protein P74104 (**72.3% identity over 95% of the sequence; E-value 8.2 × 10⁻²⁷⁶**).

### Predicted enzymatic activities

The protein likely performs the two early riboflavin-biosynthesis reactions:

1. **3,4-Dihydroxy-2-butanone 4-phosphate synthase activity**  
   Converts **D-ribulose 5-phosphate** to 3,4-dihydroxy-2-butanone 4-phosphate and formate.  
   - Associated EC number: **4.1.99.12**
   - Rhea: **RHEA:18457**

2. **GTP cyclohydrolase II activity**  
   Converts **GTP** into a ribitylamino-pyrimidine precursor, with formate and phosphate/pyrophosphate products.  
   - Associated EC number: **3.5.4.25**
   - Rhea: **RHEA:23704**

The C-terminal region after approximately residue 380 is not assigned a separate Pfam domain and may represent an accessory or lineage-specific extension.

### Biological process

The protein participates in the **riboflavin biosynthetic process**, supplying two precursors that are subsequently combined and modified to produce riboflavin, the precursor of the flavin cofactors **FMN and FAD**.

### Functional summary

**Probable annotation:** RibBA, bifunctional riboflavin-biosynthesis protein containing DHBP synthase and GTP cyclohydrolase II activities. It likely catalyzes the first steps of both branches of riboflavin precursor formation, thereby contributing to cellular vitamin B2, FMN, and FAD biosynthesis.
