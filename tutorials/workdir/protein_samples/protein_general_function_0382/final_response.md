## Functional summary

This 362-amino-acid protein is **aminomethyltransferase (GcvT; EC 2.1.2.10)**, the T-protein component of the **glycine cleavage system**. The sequence exactly matches reviewed *Porphyromonas gingivalis* aminomethyltransferase references, providing strong support for the identification.

In the glycine cleavage reaction, the enzyme transfers an aminomethyl group from lipoyl-bound aminomethyl-dihydrolipoamide to tetrahydrofolate, producing **5,10-methylene-tetrahydrofolate, dihydrolipoamide, and ammonium**. This contributes to glycine degradation.
