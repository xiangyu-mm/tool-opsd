**This protein is uracil phosphoribosyltransferase (UPRT; EC 2.4.2.9).** The 215-amino-acid sequence is an exact full-length match to a reviewed UniProt reference annotated with this activity.

It catalyzes the phosphoribosyl transfer:

**Uracil + PRPP ⇌ UMP + diphosphate (PPi)**

In this reaction, the enzyme attaches the ribose-phosphate group from 5-phospho-α-D-ribose 1-diphosphate (PRPP) to uracil, forming uridine monophosphate (UMP). The curated annotation reports Mg²⁺ associated with PRPP as a cofactor. This reaction is consistent with a role in pyrimidine nucleotide salvage.
