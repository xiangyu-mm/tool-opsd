This 715-amino-acid sequence is very likely a **fatty acid oxidation complex subunit alpha**, based on near-full-length matches to reviewed bacterial homologs: approximately **80% sequence identity** and **99.6% query coverage** to *Shigella* and *E. coli* proteins.

The homolog annotations place the protein in the **cytoplasm**. This is a homology-based expectation for the query, not a direct localization measurement.

**Evidence caveat:** These conclusions rely on strong sequence similarity to reviewed homologs; the query itself was not experimentally characterized here. Exact substrate specificity and activity should therefore be treated as likely rather than confirmed.
