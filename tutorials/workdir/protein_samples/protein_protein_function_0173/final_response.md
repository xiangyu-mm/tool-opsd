## Functional summary

The 246-amino-acid sequence is an **exact match** to reviewed UniProt protein **A4J708** from *Desulforamulus reducens*. It is **HisA**, also called **ProFAR isomerase** (EC **5.3.1.16**).

HisA catalyzes the reversible isomerization of **ProFAR**—1-(5-phospho-β-D-ribosyl)-5-[(5-phospho-β-D-ribosylamino)methylideneamino]imidazole-4-carboxamide—to **PRFAR**, its isomer. This is annotated as **step 4 of 9 in L-histidine biosynthesis**.

- **Subcellular localization:** Cytoplasm, according to the reference protein annotation.
- **Biological processes:** Strongest support is for **L-histidine biosynthesis**. The reference also lists **L-tryptophan biosynthesis** as a GO process annotation; that secondary association should be treated cautiously rather than as evidence of an additional catalytic role.
- **Sequence/domain evidence:** A Pfam His_biosynth domain (PF00977) was detected across most of the sequence, consistent with the HisA assignment.

The protein knowledge-base result gives an exact sequence match, but the functional and localization descriptions are reference annotations, not new experimental measurements on a separately identified sample.
