### Functional summary

The 84-residue sequence is an **N-terminally secreted plant peptide**. Its first ~20 residues are strongly hydrophobic and form a signal peptide, indicating targeting to the secretory pathway and release into the **apoplast/extracellular space**.

Most importantly, the sequence is an **exact full-length match** to the reviewed *Arabidopsis thaliana* protein **PAMP-induced secreted peptide 2 (UniProt F4JRC5)**:

- **100% sequence identity and coverage**
- Secreted/apoplastic localization
- Functions as an endogenous immune elicitor
- **Amplifies flg22-triggered innate immune responses** to bacterial flagellin
- Contributes to response to bacteria
- Acts as a **negative regulator of root growth**, with links to root development and auxin response

Thus, this protein is best classified as a **small secreted plant immune-signaling peptide**, involved in coordinating antibacterial defense and growth regulation rather than as a conventional enzyme or globular protein. Its short mature region and lack of detectable Pfam domains are consistent with a processed signaling peptide.
