### Functional assignment

The 229-residue protein is most consistent with **UbiE**, a **S-adenosyl-L-methionine (SAM)-dependent quinone C-methyltransferase**.

Evidence includes:

- Strong similarity to reviewed UbiE proteins: **47.8–59.7% identity** over approximately **96–98% of the sequence**.
- A near-full-length **UbiE methyltransferase domain**: Pfam **Ubie_methyltran (PF01209)**.
- Homologs are annotated with **EC 2.1.1.163** and **Rhea:42640**.

### Principal reaction

\[
\text{2-demethylmenaquinol} + \text{SAM}
\rightarrow
\text{menaquinol} + \text{S-adenosyl-L-homocysteine} + \mathrm{H^+}
\]

In words, the enzyme transfers a methyl group from SAM to **demethylmenaquinol**, producing **menaquinol**, a component of the bacterial electron-transport quinone pool.

### Possible additional activity

Some close UbiE homologs also catalyze the corresponding methylation step in ubiquinone biosynthesis:

\[
\text{2-polyprenyl-6-methoxy-1,4-benzoquinol} + \text{SAM}
\rightarrow
\text{2-polyprenyl-3-methyl-6-methoxy-1,4-benzoquinol}
+ \text{S-adenosyl-L-homocysteine} + \mathrm{H^+}
\]

This is associated with **EC 2.1.1.201**, but for this sequence the demethylmenaquinol methyltransferase assignment is the stronger and most conservative conclusion.

**Summary:** The protein is most likely a **UbiE quinone C-methyltransferase involved primarily in menaquinone biosynthesis**, using SAM as the methyl donor.
