## Likely biochemical role

Acireductone dioxygenase oxidatively cleaves **acireductone** in an oxygen-dependent reaction. Its product depends on the bound metal:

- **Fe²⁺-dependent ARD:** produces **2-keto-4-methylthiobutyrate (KMTB)** and formate. KMTB can be converted back to methionine.
- **Ni²⁺-dependent ARD:** produces methylthiopropionate, carbon monoxide, and formate, representing a shunt that does not directly regenerate methionine.

The protein is predicted to bind approximately **one divalent metal ion per monomer**, and characterized homologs are generally monomeric.

**S-methyl-5-thioribose-1-phosphate → acireductone → KMTB → methionine**
