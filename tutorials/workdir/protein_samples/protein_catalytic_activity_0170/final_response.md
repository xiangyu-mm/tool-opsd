## Functional identification

The 199-aa protein is most likely a **Ham1p-like dITP/XTP pyrophosphatase** (**EC 3.6.1.66**).

This assignment is strongly supported by:

- A **Ham1p-like domain** spanning nearly the entire sequence: Pfam **PF01725**
- Close matches to reviewed dITP/XTP pyrophosphatases, with **65.8–71.6% identity** and essentially complete sequence coverage
- Homolog annotations consistently assigning the same catalytic activity

## Catalytic activity

The enzyme hydrolyzes noncanonical purine nucleoside triphosphates, cleaving the terminal phosphoanhydride bond:

\[
\mathrm{XTP + H_2O \rightarrow XMP + PP_i}
\]

\[
\mathrm{dITP + H_2O \rightarrow dIMP + PP_i}
\]

\[
\mathrm{ITP + H_2O \rightarrow IMP + PP_i}
\]

where:

- **XTP** = xanthosine triphosphate  
- **dITP** = deoxyinosine triphosphate  
- **ITP** = inosine triphosphate  
- **PPᵢ** = inorganic diphosphate/pyrophosphate  

The strongest functional interpretation is that it preferentially removes **dITP, ITP, and XTP** from the nucleotide pool. This “house-cleaning” activity helps prevent incorporation of abnormal purine nucleotides into DNA or RNA.

## Additional inferred properties

- **Cofactor:** Mg²⁺, inferred from close reviewed homologs  
- **Quaternary structure:** likely a homodimer, also inferred from homologs  
- **Cellular role:** cytosolic nucleotide-pool sanitization  
- **Confidence:** high for the enzyme family and general reaction; the exact relative substrate preference should ideally be confirmed experimentally for this specific sequence.
