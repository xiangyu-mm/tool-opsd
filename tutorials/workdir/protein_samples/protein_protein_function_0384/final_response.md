### Likely cellular functions

DnaK is an ATP-dependent chaperone that:

- Binds unfolded or partially folded proteins.
- Prevents inappropriate protein aggregation.
- Promotes protein folding and refolding.
- Assists recovery from heat and other proteotoxic stresses.
- Participates in protein quality control, commonly together with **DnaJ/Hsp40** cochaperones and **GrpE**, which stimulates nucleotide exchange.

The ATPase cycle controls alternating high- and low-affinity states for substrate binding and release.
