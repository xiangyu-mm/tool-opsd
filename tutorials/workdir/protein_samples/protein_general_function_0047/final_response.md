### Functional overview

This 636-aa protein is very strongly identified as **FtsH**, an ATP-dependent membrane-anchored zinc metalloprotease involved in bacterial protein quality control.

- **Topology:** Two N-terminal transmembrane helices, approximately residues 8–28 and 101–121, separated by a periplasmic loop. The remaining ~500 residues are predicted to face the cytoplasm.
- **Domain architecture:**
  - FtsH-specific N-terminal region: residues ~9–94
  - **AAA+ ATPase domain:** residues ~192–390
  - **M41 zinc metalloprotease domain:** residues ~406–594
  - C-terminal low-complexity/disordered tail
- **ATPase motifs:** The sequence contains a Walker A/P-loop motif, `GPPGSGKT`, and a compatible downstream ATPase architecture, supporting ATP-dependent mechanical protein processing.
- **Protease motif:** A conserved HExxH-like zinc metalloprotease motif, `HESGH`, is present in the protease region.
- **Localization:** Inner membrane, with the ATPase and protease domains exposed to the cytoplasm.
- **Cofactor:** Predicted to bind one **Zn²⁺ ion per subunit**.
- **Assembly:** FtsH proteins generally form **homohexameric protease complexes**.
- **Function:** Uses ATP hydrolysis to extract, unfold, and processively degrade selected membrane and cytosolic proteins, contributing to membrane-protein quality control and general proteostasis.
- **Likely GO activities:** ATP binding, ATP hydrolysis, ATP-dependent peptidase activity, zinc ion binding, metalloendopeptidase activity, proteolysis, and protein catabolic process.
- **Sequence properties:** Approximately 69.8 kDa; predicted pI ~4.93 and net acidic charge at neutral pH.

**Conclusion:** The sequence encodes a canonical bacterial **FtsH membrane AAA+ protease**, rather than an ABC transporter. The assignment is supported by highly similar reviewed FtsH homologs, conserved AAA and M41 protease domains, the two-pass membrane topology, and the characteristic ATPase and zinc-metalloprotease motifs.
