### Functional identification

The protein is **phosphoglycerate kinase (PGK)**, **EC 2.7.2.3**.

Evidence is exceptionally strong:

- 392 amino acids, approximately 41.1 kDa
- Exact sequence match to reviewed UniProt phosphoglycerate kinase **B8GP44**
- Full-length alignment: **100% identity and coverage**
- Contains the characteristic **PGK domain** (Pfam **PF00162**)
- Annotated as a cytosolic, monomeric enzyme

### Catalytic reaction

\[
\text{3-phosphoglycerate} + \text{ATP}
\rightleftharpoons
\text{1,3-bisphosphoglycerate} + \text{ADP}
\]

More specifically, the enzyme transfers the terminal phosphate of ATP to the 3-hydroxyl group of **(2R)-3-phosphoglycerate**, producing **1,3-bisphosphoglycerate**.

This is a reversible phosphoryl-transfer reaction:

- In **glycolysis**, it generates ATP from 1,3-bisphosphoglycerate and ADP.
- In the reverse direction during **gluconeogenesis**, it consumes ATP to produce 1,3-bisphosphoglycerate.
