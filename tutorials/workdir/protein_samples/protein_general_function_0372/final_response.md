This 91-amino-acid sequence is an **exact sequence match** to reviewed UniProt entry **Q47WN6**, the bacterial **SdhE FAD assembly factor** from *Colwellia psychrerythraea*. The sequence also contains a Pfam **Sdh5 domain** (PF03937; residues 16–88).
