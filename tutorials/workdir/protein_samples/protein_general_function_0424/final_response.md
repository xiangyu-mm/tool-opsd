### Functional summary

This is a **129-amino-acid mini-ribonuclease III-like protein**. The sequence exactly matches reviewed UniProt protein **Q8RIL0** from *Fusobacterium nucleatum*, so the family-level identification is strongly supported.

- **Likely role:** RNA cleavage/processing. The reference annotation describes a possible role in RNA processing and includes RNase III activity; related proteins are associated with rRNA processing.
- **Domain:** A Pfam **Ribonuclease_3 domain (PF00636)** was detected at residues **17–111**, strongly supporting the RNase III-family assignment.
- **Localization:** Related homologs are annotated as cytoplasmic; this is supportive evidence, not a direct localization measurement for this sequence.
- **Sequence-derived properties:** Estimated molecular mass **15.0 kDa**; theoretical pI **8.81**. These are computational estimates for the unmodified sequence.

**Caveat:** The exact-match reference uses cautious wording (“might be” involved in RNA processing). The sequence and domain support an RNase III-like function, but they do not by themselves establish its precise RNA substrates, cleavage specificity, or physiological role.
