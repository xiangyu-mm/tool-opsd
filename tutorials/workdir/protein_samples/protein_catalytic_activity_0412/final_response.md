## Functional assignment

The 92-residue protein is most likely a **pyrimidine/purine nucleoside phosphorylase** of the **Ppnp family**.

### Supporting evidence

- **Length:** 92 amino acids; predicted mass ≈ **10.1 kDa**
- **Domain:** Strong match to the **Ppnp Pfam domain (PF06865)** spanning nearly the entire sequence
- **Homology:** Up to **63.4% identity over the full sequence** to reviewed nucleoside phosphorylases, including UniProt A9FFI1
- Related homologs are annotated with **EC 2.4.2.1 and EC 2.4.2.2**

## Catalytic reaction

The enzyme catalyzes reversible phosphorolysis of nucleosides:

\[
\text{nucleoside} + \text{phosphate}
\rightleftharpoons
\text{free nucleobase} + \alpha\text{-D-ribose-1-phosphate}
\]

Representative reactions include:

\[
\text{guanosine} + P_i
\rightleftharpoons
\text{guanine} + \alpha\text{-D-ribose-1-phosphate}
\]

\[
\text{adenosine} + P_i
\rightleftharpoons
\text{adenine} + \alpha\text{-D-ribose-1-phosphate}
\]

\[
\text{inosine} + P_i
\rightleftharpoons
\text{hypoxanthine} + \alpha\text{-D-ribose-1-phosphate}
\]

The homologs are reported to accept multiple substrates, including **uridine, cytidine, thymidine, adenosine, guanosine, inosine, and xanthosine**.

### Functional significance

This reaction cleaves the **N-glycosidic bond** of a nucleoside while transferring the ribose moiety to inorganic phosphate. In the reverse direction, it supports nucleoside synthesis from a free base and ribose-1-phosphate.

### Confidence and caveat

The assignment is **strongly supported by full-length homology and domain evidence**, but the exact substrate range and catalytic activity have not been experimentally demonstrated for this particular sequence. No specific metal or organic cofactor is indicated by the available annotation.
