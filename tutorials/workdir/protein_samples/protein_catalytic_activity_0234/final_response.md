- **dITP + H₂O → dIMP + PPᵢ + H⁺**

- **XTP + H₂O → XMP + PPᵢ + H⁺**

## Biological role

By removing oxidized or otherwise noncanonical purine nucleotides from the cellular nucleotide pool, the enzyme helps prevent their incorporation into DNA or RNA, thereby reducing potential replication errors and chromosomal damage.
