## Functional summary

The 449-amino-acid sequence is an **exact, full-length match** to reviewed *Candida albicans* **beta-tubulin** (UniProt **P10875**). This gives high confidence in the protein identification.

Beta-tubulin pairs with alpha-tubulin to form heterodimers, the building blocks of **microtubules**. Microtubules make up part of the cytoskeleton and support cellular organization and processes such as spindle assembly. The reference annotation places the protein in the **cytoplasm and cytoskeleton** and reports **GTP binding** and **Mg²⁺** as a cofactor. The reference also annotates GTPase activity; the sequence analysis did not retrieve a specific catalytic reaction.
