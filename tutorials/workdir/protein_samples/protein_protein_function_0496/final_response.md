## Identification and functional summary

This 768-amino-acid sequence is **HIV-1 envelope glycoprotein precursor gp160 (Env)**. The identification is strongly supported by near-full-length matches to reviewed HIV-1 gp160 proteins: **75.6–78.0% identity**, about **99–100% query coverage**, and **E-value 0**. Pfam also detects the hallmark **gp120** and **gp41** domains.

Env is a viral surface protein that helps HIV-1 enter host cells. The precursor is cleaved by host furin or a furin-like protease during passage through the trans-Golgi, producing the surface subunit **gp120** and transmembrane subunit **gp41**. These associate as a trimeric Env spike. gp120 mediates attachment to **CD4** and, after that interaction, enables engagement of a chemokine coreceptor such as **CCR5 or CXCR4**. gp41 then drives fusion of the viral membrane with a host-cell membrane, allowing the viral core to enter the cell.

## Architecture and localization

- **N-terminus:** A hydrophobic signal peptide is consistent with entry into the host secretory pathway.
- **gp120 ectodomain:** Pfam **GP120 (PF00516)** spans residues **33–511**. This cysteine-rich, glycosylated region includes the receptor-binding surface. The sequence contains many potential N-linked glycosylation sequons; these are sequence-based predictions, not confirmation of occupancy.
- **Cleavage and gp41:** The sequence contains a furin-like cleavage region followed by Pfam **GP41 (PF00517)** at residues **530–720**. gp41 includes the fusion-associated region and membrane anchor.
- **C-terminus:** A membrane-spanning segment anchors Env, leaving the C-terminal tail on the intracellular side of the host membrane or virion envelope.

Env is routed through the **endoplasmic reticulum and Golgi** for assembly, glycosylation, and cleavage. Mature Env is found in the **virion envelope** and can be present at the **host-cell plasma membrane** and **host endosomal membranes** during viral entry.

## Biological processes

The annotations and sequence architecture support roles in:

- HIV attachment to host cells and receptor engagement
- Viral entry through membrane fusion at the plasma membrane or in endosomes
- Viral protein processing and formation of the mature Env spike
- Viral particle interaction with host-cell membranes

**Confidence and limits:** The gp160 identification and gp120/gp41 architecture are high-confidence sequence matches. Functional and localization descriptions are supported by reviewed homolog annotations, but this analysis does not establish the exact strain, coreceptor preference, glycosylation state, or experimental behavior of this specific sequence. No enzymatic or catalytic role is indicated.

Sequence-derived estimates for the unmodified precursor are approximately **86.4 kDa** and theoretical **pI 8.75**; these do not account for glycosylation or processing.
