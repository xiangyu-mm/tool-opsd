## Functional prediction

This sequence is a **maize 22 kDa α-zein seed storage protein**, most likely **22 kDa alpha-zein 4 (UniProt O48966)**.

### Evidence from the sequence

- **Length:** 266 amino acids; calculated molecular mass ~28.9 kDa for the unprocessed precursor.
- **N-terminal signal peptide:** residues ~1–20 are strongly hydrophobic (`MATKILSLLALLALFASATNA`), targeting the protein to the **endoplasmic reticulum**.
- **Mature-protein composition:** the remainder is highly enriched in **glutamine, leucine, alanine, and proline**, with repeated zein-like motifs.
- **Domain match:** Pfam **Zein domain PF01559** spans residues 23–266.
- **Homology:** exact sequence match to reviewed *Zea mays* alpha-zein O48966; related alpha-zeins show ~95% identity.
- **Functional annotation:** **nutrient reservoir activity** (GO:0045735).

### Biological role

α-Zeins are **prolamin storage proteins deposited in maize endosperm protein bodies**. They provide nitrogen and amino acids during seed germination and contribute to the formation of the protein matrix surrounding starch granules.

The N-terminal signal peptide indicates entry into the secretory pathway and subsequent storage in the endomembrane system; this is **not evidence for a cell-surface or extracellular structural protein**. The sequence lacks features expected for an enzyme, receptor, or multipass membrane protein.

**Confidence: very high**, because the query is an exact full-length match to a curated alpha-zein sequence.
