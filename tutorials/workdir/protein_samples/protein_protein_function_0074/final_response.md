### Functional prediction

This protein is **F-type ATP synthase subunit b (atpF)**, a structural component of the bacterial **F₁F₀ ATP synthase peripheral stalk**.

### Evidence

- **Length:** 161 aa; predicted mass ~17.8 kDa.
- **N-terminus:** strongly hydrophobic segment consistent with a **single transmembrane helix** anchoring the protein in the bacterial inner membrane.
- **C-terminal region:** highly charged and rich in helix-favoring residues, characteristic of an extended **α-helical stalk domain**.
- **Domains:** Pfam matches to **ATP-synt_B (PF00430)** and **Mt_ATP-synt_B (PF05405)**.
- **Homology:** the sequence is an **exact match** to reviewed UniProt protein **B0ULY4**, annotated as ATP synthase subunit b.

### Biological role

Subunit b does **not** form the proton-conducting pore and is not catalytic. Instead, it anchors in the F₀ membrane sector and extends into the cytoplasmic side to form the **peripheral stalk**, linking and stabilizing the F₁ catalytic head relative to F₀ during rotary ATP synthesis. It commonly functions as a **b₂ homodimer**, together with the delta subunit, to resist rotational torque.

**Summary:** a single-pass inner-membrane **ATP synthase peripheral-stalk protein**, providing mechanical coupling and structural support between the F₁ and F₀ sectors.
