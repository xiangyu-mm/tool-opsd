### Functional identification

The 504-aa sequence encodes a **BCCT-family, multispass membrane transporter**, specifically an **L-carnitine/γ-butyrobetaine antiporter**.

### Transport reaction

It catalyzes substrate exchange rather than a covalent chemical transformation:

\[
\text{γ-butyrobetaine}_{in} + \text{L-carnitine}_{out}
\rightleftharpoons
\text{γ-butyrobetaine}_{out} + \text{L-carnitine}_{in}
\]

Equivalently, γ-butyrobetaine is transported inward while L-carnitine is transported outward, with the direction reversible depending on substrate gradients.

- **Rhea:** RHEA:29427  
- **γ-Butyrobetaine:** 4-(trimethylammonio)butanoate  
- **Chemical transformation:** none; this is coupled antiport

### Evidence

- Exact sequence match to reviewed UniProt **P59334**
- Strongly hydrophobic 504-aa protein with approximately **12 transmembrane helices**
- Contains a **BCCT domain (PF02028)**
- Homologs are annotated as inner-membrane L-carnitine/γ-butyrobetaine antiporters
- Reported oligomeric state: **homotrimer**
- No catalytic cofactor is required or supported

**Conclusion:** This protein is not a soluble metabolic enzyme. It is a bacterial inner-membrane transporter that exchanges L-carnitine and γ-butyrobetaine, participating in carnitine metabolism and osmoprotective transport.
