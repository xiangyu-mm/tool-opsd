## Catalyzed reaction

The principal reaction is oxidative cleavage of the ether bond in **S-dichlorprop**:

\[
\text{(S)-2-(2,4-dichlorophenoxy)propanoate}
+ \text{2-oxoglutarate}
+ O_2
\rightarrow
\text{2,4-dichlorophenol}
+ \text{pyruvate}
+ \text{succinate}
+ CO_2
\]

Thus, the enzyme uses molecular oxygen and 2-oxoglutarate to cleave the herbicide’s **aryl–alkyl ether bond**. The 2-oxoglutarate cosubstrate is oxidatively decarboxylated to succinate and CO₂, while the propionate side chain is released as pyruvate.

It also catalyzes the analogous reaction with **S-mecoprop**:

\[
\text{(S)-2-(4-chloro-2-methylphenoxy)propanoate}
+ \text{2-oxoglutarate}
+ O_2
\rightarrow
\text{2-methyl-4-chlorophenol}
+ \text{pyruvate}
+ \text{succinate}
+ CO_2
\]

Related phenoxyacetate substrates, including **2,4-dichlorophenoxyacetate (2,4-D)**, may also be accepted:

## Concise summary

This protein is an **iron- and 2-oxoglutarate-dependent dioxygenase that initiates bacterial degradation of phenoxy herbicides by oxidative ether-bond cleavage**. Its best-supported substrates are **S-dichlorprop and S-mecoprop**, producing the corresponding chlorophenol, pyruvate, succinate, and CO₂.
