## Functional identification

The 344-aa sequence is **fructose-1,6-bisphosphatase class 1** (EC **3.1.3.11**). It is an **exact sequence match** to reviewed UniProt protein **A4SM33** from *Aeromonas salmonicida* strain A449, supporting this identification with high confidence.

**Catalyzed reaction:**

\[
\beta\text{-D-fructose 1,6-bisphosphate} + \mathrm{H_2O}
\rightarrow
\beta\text{-D-fructose 6-phosphate} + \mathrm{P_i}
\]

In other words, the enzyme hydrolyzes the phosphate at carbon 1 of fructose-1,6-bisphosphate, producing fructose-6-phosphate and inorganic phosphate. This reaction participates in **gluconeogenesis** and carbohydrate biosynthesis; it provides the phosphatase step rather than simply reversing the kinase reaction.
