## Chemical reaction

\[
\text{UDP-N-acetylmuramoyl-L-alanyl-D-glutamate}
+ \text{meso-2,6-diaminopimelate}
+ \text{ATP}
\]

\[
\rightarrow
\text{UDP-N-acetylmuramoyl-L-alanyl-D-glutamyl-meso-2,6-diaminopimelate}
+ \text{ADP}
+ \text{phosphate}
+ \text{H}^{+}
\]

In words, MurE adds **meso-diaminopimelic acid (meso-DAP)** to the UDP-linked MurNAc-dipeptide precursor, producing the **UDP-MurNAc tripeptide**. This is the third-peptide-residue addition step in the formation of the bacterial peptidoglycan stem peptide.

The reaction is ATP-dependent and requires **Mg²⁺**, which coordinates ATP and supports substrate activation. The resulting UDP-MurNAc tripeptide is subsequently used in later peptidoglycan assembly reactions.
