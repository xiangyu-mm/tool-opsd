## Molecular function

ClpY is the ATPase/chaperone component of the **ClpQ–ClpY**, or **HslUV**, protease complex.

Its main activities are:

- **ATP binding and hydrolysis**
- **Substrate protein unfolding**
- **Delivery of unfolded proteins to the ClpQ protease**
- **Activation and assembly of the proteolytic complex**
- General **chaperone/protein quality-control activity**

The sequence contains characteristic **AAA+ ATPase domains**, including Pfam matches to:

- **AAA domain**
- **AAA_2 domain**
- **ClpB_D2-small-like region**

The prominent P-loop-like sequence **MIGPTGVGKTE** is consistent with an ATP-binding Walker A region. Thus, this protein belongs to the **AAA+ unfoldase family**, rather than the ABC, FtsK, or SpoIIIE ATPase families.

Importantly, ClpY is not itself the principal proteolytic chamber. The protease component is **ClpQ/HslV**; ClpY uses ATP hydrolysis to recognize, unfold, and translocate substrate proteins into that chamber.

## Complex architecture

The curated homolog annotation describes:

- A **homohexameric ClpY ring**
- ClpY rings capping one or both sides of a **double-ring ClpQ protease**
- ATP-dependent assembly and activation of the complex

This arrangement is analogous in overall logic to other AAA+ protease systems: the ATPase recognizes and mechanically unfolds substrates, while the protease core degrades them.
