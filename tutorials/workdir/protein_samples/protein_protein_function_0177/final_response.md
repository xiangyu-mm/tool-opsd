### Predicted role: Ribosome-binding factor A (RbfA)

The 135-amino-acid sequence is an **exact, full-length match** to reviewed *Vibrio campbellii* RbfA (UniProt A7MZI4). It also contains the RBFA domain (PF02033; residues 7–110).

RbfA is a cytoplasmic ribosome-biogenesis factor involved in **late maturation of the 30S ribosomal small subunit**. It associates with free 30S subunits and supports efficient processing of 16S rRNA; it may interact with the 5′-terminal helix of 16S rRNA. The associated biological-process annotation is small-subunit rRNA maturation (GO:0030490).

**Overall:** The sequence strongly supports a role in bacterial 30S ribosome assembly and 16S rRNA maturation.
