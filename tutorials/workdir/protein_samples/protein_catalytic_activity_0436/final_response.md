## Functional summary

This 139-amino-acid protein is **aspartate 1-decarboxylase** (EC **4.1.1.11**). The sequence exactly matches a reviewed *Mycobacterium bovis* reference protein across its full length, supporting a high-confidence functional assignment.

It catalyzes the decarboxylation of **L-aspartate**, producing **β-alanine** and carbon dioxide:

**L-aspartate + H⁺ → β-alanine + CO₂**

The curated annotation describes the reaction as **pyruvoyl-dependent**, with a covalently bound pyruvoyl group. The enzyme’s pathway role is supplying β-alanine for pantothenate (vitamin B₅) biosynthesis.
