## Catalyzed reaction

The principal predicted reaction is:

\[
\text{L-kynurenine} + \mathrm{H_2O}
\rightarrow
\text{anthranilate} + \text{L-alanine} + \mathrm{H^+}
\]

In words, kynureninase cleaves the kynurenine carbon skeleton, producing **anthranilic acid/anthranilate** and **L-alanine**. The reaction is a PLP-dependent carbon–carbon cleavage/β-elimination-type reaction.

The enzyme may also act on 3-hydroxy-L-kynurenine:

\[
\text{3-hydroxy-L-kynurenine} + \mathrm{H_2O}
\rightarrow
\text{3-hydroxyanthranilate} + \text{L-alanine} + \mathrm{H^+}
\]

## Biological context

This activity is associated with:

- **L-tryptophan degradation**
- **L-kynurenine catabolism**
- **Quinolinate and NAD⁺ biosynthesis**, when the substrate is 3-hydroxykynurenine or kynurenine in the relevant pathway

## Confidence and limitation

The assignment is supported strongly by sequence homology, domain architecture, and agreement among multiple reviewed kynureninase homologs. However, the specific substrate preference and catalytic activity have **not been experimentally demonstrated for this exact sequence**, so the annotation should be regarded as a **high-confidence homology-based prediction**, rather than a direct biochemical characterization.
