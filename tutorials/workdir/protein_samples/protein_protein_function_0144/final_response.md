## Functional identification

The 532-amino-acid sequence is an **exact sequence match to reviewed bovine flavin-containing monooxygenase 3 (FMO3; UniProt Q8HYJ9)**. This provides strong evidence for a specific FMO3 assignment rather than only a family-level prediction.

FMO3 is an **FAD-dependent oxidoreductase** that uses NADPH and molecular oxygen to oxidize nitrogen- and sulfur-containing substrates:

\[
\text{substrate} + \mathrm{NADPH} + \mathrm{O_2}
\rightarrow \text{oxygenated product} + \mathrm{NADP^+} + \mathrm{H_2O}
\]

A principal reaction is:

\[
\text{trimethylamine} \rightarrow \text{trimethylamine N-oxide (TMAO)}
\]

## Cellular and physiological roles

The protein is involved in:

- **Hepatic xenobiotic metabolism**, including oxidation of drugs and other foreign compounds.
- **Dietary metabolite processing**, especially conversion of gut microbiota-derived trimethylamine to TMAO.
- Oxidation of additional substrates such as **albendazole, N,N-dimethylaniline, hypotaurine, and nicotine**, although the exact substrate range depends on biological context.
- Regulation of TMAO concentration, which can influence **platelet responsiveness and thrombus formation**.

Thus, this protein links gut microbial metabolism and dietary precursor processing to host hepatic metabolism and cardiovascular-related physiology.
