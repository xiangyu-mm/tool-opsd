## Functional summary

The 473-amino-acid sequence is an **exact full-length match** to reviewed *Cryptococcus deneoformans* **pre-mRNA-splicing factor PRP46** (UniProt P0CS48). This strongly supports a role in **pre-mRNA splicing through the spliceosome**, including **mRNA cis-splicing** (GO:0000398; GO:0045292).

PRP46 is also annotated as **required for cell-cycle progression at the G2/M transition**. It is reported in the **nucleus and cytoplasm**, and associated with the **Prp19 complex** and spliceosomal complexes, including the catalytic-step-2 spliceosome.

The sequence contains multiple predicted **WD40 repeats**, consistent with a role in protein interactions; no catalytic reaction was retrieved, so the evidence supports a spliceosomal factor rather than an enzyme with an identified reaction. These functional assignments are strongly supported by the exact sequence match, though the annotations themselves are from the reference protein.
