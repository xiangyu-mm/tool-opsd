- 100% sequence identity and coverage
- Strong full-length Pfam **YbeY domain** match (PF02130)
- Predicted molecular mass: **16.76 kDa**

## Likely molecular function

YbeY is a small, zinc-dependent **RNA-processing endoribonuclease**. Its principal roles are:

1. **Maturation of the 3′ terminus of bacterial 16S rRNA**
2. **Late-stage quality control of 70S ribosomes**
3. Cleavage or processing of single-stranded RNA substrates during ribosome biogenesis and surveillance

Curated homologs are annotated as binding **one Zn²⁺ ion**, with zinc-associated residues located in the C-terminal portion of the protein. The sequence contains the conserved YbeY fold and catalytic region expected for this function.

## Biological processes

The protein is most likely involved in:

- **Ribosome biogenesis**
- **16S rRNA maturation**
- **rRNA processing**
- **Quality control of assembled 70S ribosomes**
- Maintenance of accurate bacterial translation

Loss of YbeY in bacteria commonly disrupts 16S rRNA maturation and ribosome function.
