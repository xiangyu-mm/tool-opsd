## Functional summary

This 512-aa protein is **most likely a UDP-N-acetylmuramoyl-L-alanyl-D-glutamate–meso-diaminopimelate ligase** (MurE; EC 6.3.2.13), an ATP-dependent enzyme in bacterial peptidoglycan biosynthesis. It is predicted to add meso-diaminopimelate to UDP-MurNAc-L-Ala-D-Glu, helping build the bacterial cell wall.

This assignment is supported by strong, nearly full-length similarity to reviewed MurE proteins: about **40–41% sequence identity**, **97.7–99.0% query coverage**, and E-values around **10⁻¹²⁰**. The inferred reaction uses ATP and is associated with **Mg²⁺**. The homolog annotations place the enzyme in the cytoplasm, but that localization and the reaction have not been experimentally verified for this particular sequence.
