### Functional estimate

This 309-aa protein is a **bifunctional HPr kinase/phosphorylase (HPrK/P)** of the bacterial phosphoenolpyruvate:sugar phosphotransferase system (PTS).

**Strong evidence:**

- It is an **exact sequence match** to the reviewed HPrK/P protein UniProt **A6TVI5**.
- It contains both characteristic domains:
  - **Hpr_kinase_N**: residues 4–127
  - **Hpr_kinase_C**: residues 130–298
- It is predicted to be a compact, soluble enzyme of approximately **35.1 kDa**.

### Molecular activity

HPrK/P reversibly controls the phosphorylation state of the PTS phosphocarrier protein **HPr**:

1. **Kinase reaction**  
   HPr-Ser + ATP → phospho-HPr-Ser + ADP

2. **Phosphorylase reaction**  
   phospho-HPr-Ser + inorganic phosphate → HPr-Ser + pyrophosphate

The enzyme can also use pyrophosphate for the phosphorylation reaction. The matched annotation indicates a requirement for **Mg²⁺** and a **homohexameric** assembly.

### Biological process

The protein is likely involved in:

- **Carbon catabolite repression**
- Regulation of **PTS-dependent carbohydrate uptake**
- Control of **inducer exclusion**
- Metabolic sensing of available carbon sources such as glucose or fructose

By changing HPr phosphorylation, HPrK/P modulates PTS signaling and helps the bacterium prioritize preferred carbon substrates.

### Overall summary

**Predicted function:** HPr serine kinase/phosphorylase, also called HPrK/P.  
**Primary biological role:** A metabolic sensor/regulator that links carbon availability to PTS sugar transport and carbon catabolite repression.  
**Confidence:** **Very high**, because the sequence is an exact match to a curated HPrK/P reference and contains the expected two-domain architecture.
