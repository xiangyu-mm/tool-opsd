### Catalytic activity

CTP synthase catalyzes the ATP-dependent conversion of UTP to CTP:

\[
\text{UTP} + \text{glutamine} + \text{ATP}
\rightarrow
\text{CTP} + \text{glutamate} + \text{ADP} + \text{phosphate}
\]

It may also use free ammonia as the nitrogen source:

\[
\text{UTP} + \text{NH}_4^+ + \text{ATP}
\rightarrow
\text{CTP} + \text{ADP} + \text{phosphate}
\]

The glutaminase domain generates ammonia from glutamine, while the synthetase domain incorporates the nitrogen into UTP.
