## Functional summary

**This is ClpX, an ATP-dependent component of the Clp protease system.** The 439-aa sequence is an exact, full-length match to reviewed UniProt entry **B4S620**, annotated as ClpX from *Prosthecochloris aestuarii*. This makes the identification highly confident.

ClpX uses ATP to select and unfold protein substrates and deliver them to the ClpP protease for degradation. It forms a **hexameric ring** that associates with ClpP; without ClpP, ClpX can also perform chaperone functions. The annotation supports ATP binding and ATP hydrolysis, although the protein lookup did not return a specific catalytic reaction equation.
