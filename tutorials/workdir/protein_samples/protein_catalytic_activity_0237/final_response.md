### Functional identification

This 279-residue protein is **hydroxymethylpyrimidine/phosphomethylpyrimidine kinase**, a bifunctional enzyme in **thiamine diphosphate (ThDP, vitamin B1) biosynthesis**.

The assignment is highly reliable because the sequence is an **exact full-length match** to reviewed UniProt protein **Q9ZBL1** from *Mycobacterium leprae* and has strong homologs in *M. tuberculosis* and *M. bovis*. It also contains the expected **Phos_pyr_kin** domain.

### Catalytic activity

The enzyme transfers phosphate groups from ATP to the hydroxymethyl group of the pyrimidine moiety of thiamine:

1. **Hydroxymethylpyrimidine phosphorylation**

\[
\text{HMP} + \text{ATP}
\rightarrow
\text{HMP-P} + \text{ADP} + \mathrm{H^+}
\]

More explicitly:

\[
\text{4-amino-5-hydroxymethyl-2-methylpyrimidine}
+
\text{ATP}
\rightarrow
\text{4-amino-2-methyl-5-phosphooxymethylpyrimidine}
+
\text{ADP}
\]

This activity corresponds to **hydroxymethylpyrimidine kinase**, EC **2.7.1.49**.

2. **Phosphomethylpyrimidine phosphorylation**

\[
\text{HMP-P} + \text{ATP}
\rightarrow
\text{HMP-PP} + \text{ADP}
\]

More explicitly:

\[
\text{4-amino-2-methyl-5-phosphooxymethylpyrimidine}
+
\text{ATP}
\rightarrow
\text{4-amino-2-methyl-5-diphosphooxymethylpyrimidine}
+
\text{ADP}
\]

This activity corresponds to **phosphomethylpyrimidine kinase**, EC **2.7.4.7**.

### Biological role

The product HMP-PP is the diphosphomethylpyrimidine component that is subsequently incorporated into **thiamine diphosphate**, an essential cofactor for enzymes such as pyruvate dehydrogenase, transketolase, and 2-oxoglutarate dehydrogenase.
