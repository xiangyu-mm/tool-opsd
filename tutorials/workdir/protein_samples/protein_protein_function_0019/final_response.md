## Function

uL1 is a structural and functional component of the **bacterial 50S ribosomal subunit**. Its principal functions are:

1. **23S rRNA binding**  
   It binds directly to 23S rRNA and helps organize the large ribosomal subunit.

2. **Ribosome structural support**  
   It contributes to the architecture of the L1 stalk, a mobile region of the ribosome.

3. **Participation in translation**  
   The L1 stalk interacts with tRNA during ribosomal translocation and is involved particularly in **release of deacylated tRNA from the E site**.

4. **Possible translational regulation**  
   Bacterial L1 proteins can act as RNA-binding translational repressors. In the matched reference protein, uL1 is annotated as regulating translation of the **L11 operon** through binding to its mRNA.
