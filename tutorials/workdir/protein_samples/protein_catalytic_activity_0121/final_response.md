## Catalytic activity

LpxD is an acyltransferase in the **lipid A biosynthesis pathway**. It transfers a second 3-hydroxyacyl group from acyl carrier protein (ACP) to the amino group of UDP-3-O-acylglucosamine.

The general reaction is:

\[
\text{UDP-3-O-[(3R)-3-hydroxyacyl]-}\alpha\text{-D-glucosamine}
+
\text{(3R)-hydroxyacyl-ACP}
\]

\[
\longrightarrow
\text{UDP-2-N,3-O-bis[(3R)-3-hydroxyacyl]-}\alpha\text{-D-glucosamine}
+
\text{holo-ACP}
+
\mathrm{H^+}
\]

For the annotated myristoyl-specific activity:

\[
\text{UDP-3-O-(3-hydroxytetradecanoyl)glucosamine}
+
\text{3-hydroxytetradecanoyl-ACP}
\]

\[
\longrightarrow
\text{UDP-2-N,3-O-bis(3-hydroxytetradecanoyl)glucosamine}
+
\text{holo-ACP}
+
\mathrm{H^+}
\]

Thus, the enzyme forms the **bis-acylated UDP-glucosamine intermediate**, an early precursor of **lipid A**, the membrane-anchoring component of bacterial lipopolysaccharide.
