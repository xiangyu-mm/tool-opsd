### Functional annotation

The 357-aa protein is **histidinol-phosphate aminotransferase** (**HisC**, EC **2.6.1.9**), a **pyridoxal 5′-phosphate (PLP)-dependent aminotransferase**.

### Catalyzed reaction

\[
\text{L-histidinol phosphate} + \text{2-oxoglutarate}
\rightleftharpoons
\text{3-(imidazol-4-yl)-2-oxopropyl phosphate} + \text{L-glutamate}
\]

In this transamination reaction, the amino group of L-histidinol phosphate is transferred to 2-oxoglutarate, producing L-glutamate and the corresponding keto-acid intermediate.
