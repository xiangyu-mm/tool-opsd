## Functional summary

The 503-residue protein is most likely **HldE**, a bacterial **bifunctional enzyme involved in ADP-L-glycero-β-D-manno-heptose biosynthesis**.

It likely combines two enzymatic activities:

1. **Heptose-7-phosphate kinase**  
   Phosphorylates D-glycero-D-manno-heptose 7-phosphate at C1:

   `heptose-7-phosphate + ATP → heptose-1,7-bisphosphate + ADP`

   **EC 2.7.1.167**

2. **Heptose-1-phosphate adenylyltransferase**  
   Transfers AMP/ADP-derived nucleotide from ATP to heptose-1-phosphate:

   `heptose-1-phosphate + ATP → ADP-heptose + PPi`

   **EC 2.7.7.70**
