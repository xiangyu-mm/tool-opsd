## Functional assignment

The 430-residue protein is most consistent with a **pyridoxal 5′-phosphate (PLP)-dependent kynureninase**, **EC 3.7.1.3**.

This assignment is supported by:

- Strong similarity to curated bacterial kynureninases: approximately **46–49% sequence identity** over most of the protein.
- Presence of an **Aminotran_5 PLP-dependent enzyme domain**.
- Presence of a C-terminal **KYNU_C kynureninase domain**.
- Homolog annotations identifying a catalytic PLP-linked lysine and kynureninase activity.
