**Probable function:** This is a **Ham1-family dITP/XTP pyrophosphatase**, likely a small, Mg²⁺-dependent enzyme that hydrolyzes noncanonical purine nucleoside triphosphates to their monophosphate forms and pyrophosphate.

**Evidence:** The 199-aa sequence contains a Ham1p-like domain (PF01725) and is 81.7% identical across 96% of its length to a reviewed *Caulobacter vibrioides* dITP/XTP pyrophosphatase. That homolog acts on XTP, dITP, and ITP; the specific substrate preference of this query protein is inferred from the close homology rather than directly tested.

**Likely biological process:** It probably helps sanitize the nucleotide pool by removing aberrant purine triphosphates, reducing their incorporation into DNA or RNA and the resulting risk of damage. The homolog is annotated as cytosolic and homodimeric, but those details remain predictions for this sequence.

In short, the strongest interpretation is a **nucleotide-pool “house-cleaning” pyrophosphatase**, not a structural protein.
