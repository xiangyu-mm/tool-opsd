## Functional identification

The protein is **tRNA dimethylallyltransferase (MiaA)**, also called **tRNA isopentenyltransferase**.

The assignment is highly confident because the sequence is an **exact 322-residue sequence match** to reviewed UniProt protein **O30762**, annotated as a *Pseudomonas putida* MiaA enzyme. It also contains the characteristic **IPPT domain** (PF01715).

## Catalytic reaction

\[
\text{tRNA-A37} + \text{dimethylallyl diphosphate}
\rightarrow
\text{tRNA-i}^{6}\text{A37} + \text{diphosphate}
\]

More specifically, MiaA transfers a **dimethylallyl group** from dimethylallyl diphosphate (DMAPP) to the exocyclic N6 amino group of **adenosine at position 37** in selected tRNAs. The product is **N6-(dimethylallyl)adenosine**, abbreviated **i⁶A37**.

The modified tRNAs generally decode codons beginning with **uridine**.

## Summary

This enzyme catalyzes an **RNA prenylation/tRNA-modification reaction**, installing a dimethylallyl group onto A37 of specific tRNAs to form **i⁶A37**. The functional assignment is based on exact sequence identity to a curated Swiss-Prot MiaA protein, rather than sequence similarity alone.
