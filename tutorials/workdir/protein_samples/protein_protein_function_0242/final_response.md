## Functional summary

The 448-aa sequence is an **exact match** to reviewed rat **MAP2K5** (UniProt **Q62862**), also called **MEK5** or dual-specificity MAP kinase kinase 5.

- **Function:** A protein kinase in the **ERK5/MAPK7 signaling pathway**. The reference annotation describes MAP2K5 as helping assemble a signaling complex involving MAP3K2/3 and MAPK7. Its annotations include ATP binding and MAP kinase kinase activity.
- **Biological processes:** Most directly associated with the **ERK5 and MAPK cascades** and responses to growth-factor signaling. The reference also links the pathway to stress-induced apoptosis protection, neuronal survival, heart development, and angiogenesis; some of these roles are annotated as inferred “by similarity.” A specific rat annotation additionally reports regulation of apoptosis in cardiomyocytes.
- **Cellular location:** Primarily **cytoplasm/cytosol**. Membrane localization is reported for an alternatively spliced isoform; a spindle association also appears in GO annotations.

**Confidence and caveat:** Protein identification is high confidence because the query matches the rat reference sequence across its full length at 100% identity and coverage. The process and localization details are reference annotations, not predictions made from sequence alone, and some biological roles rely on similarity-based evidence.
