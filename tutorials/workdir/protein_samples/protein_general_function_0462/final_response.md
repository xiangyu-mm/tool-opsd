### Catalytic function

The enzyme phosphorylates NAD⁺ using ATP:

\[
\mathrm{NAD^+ + ATP \rightarrow NADP^+ + ADP + H^+}
\]

Specifically, it adds a phosphate to the **2′-hydroxyl group of the adenosine moiety of NAD⁺**, producing NADP⁺. This supports cellular NADP⁺ biosynthesis and maintenance of the NAD⁺/NADP⁺ balance.
