## Functional summary

This protein is **most likely a glycoside hydrolase family 47 (GH47) α-1,2-mannosidase** (**EC 3.2.1.113**), involved in trimming high-mannose N-glycans, likely in an ER-associated processing context.

The representative homolog-supported reaction is:

\[
\mathrm{Man_9GlcNAc_2 + 4H_2O \rightarrow Man_5GlcNAc_2 + 4\,\beta\text{-D-mannose}}
\]

GH47 enzymes can also process certain Man₈GlcNAc₂ forms to Man₅GlcNAc₂. The sequence has a strong **Glyco_hydro_47 domain** at residues 294–764 and an N-terminal **Rad10-like domain** at residues 80–199. Five reviewed homologs show about 55% identity over roughly 62% of the query and support the α-1,2-mannosidase assignment.
