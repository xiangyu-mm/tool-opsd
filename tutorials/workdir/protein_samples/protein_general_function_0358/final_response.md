### Functional summary

This protein is a component of the **50S ribosomal subunit stalk**. bL12 forms dimers that associate with ribosomal protein L10, producing the L10–L12 stalk complex. The stalk recruits and interacts with **GTP-bound translation factors**, helping coordinate factor binding during protein synthesis.

The assignment is highly reliable: the sequence exactly matches curated full-length bL12 proteins from *Neisseria* species, with 100% identity and complete coverage. It is therefore best classified as a **structural and translation-factor-interacting ribosomal protein**, not as an enzyme.
