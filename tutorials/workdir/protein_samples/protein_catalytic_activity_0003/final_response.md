## Functional identification

The 308-residue protein is **porphobilinogen deaminase**, also called **hydroxymethylbilane synthase** (**EC 2.5.1.61**).

The identification is highly reliable: the sequence is an **exact match to a reviewed Swiss-Prot porphobilinogen deaminase**, with additional strong homologous matches. It also contains the characteristic **Porphobil_deam** and **Porphobil_deamC** domains.

## Catalyzed reaction

\[
\mathbf{4\ porphobilinogen + H_2O \rightarrow hydroxymethylbilane + 4\ NH_4^+}
\]

The enzyme joins four molecules of the monopyrrole **porphobilinogen** into the linear tetrapyrrole **hydroxymethylbilane**, releasing four ammonium ions. This is an early step in **heme/porphyrin biosynthesis**.

- **Rhea:** RHEA:13185  
- **GO activity:** hydroxymethylbilane synthase activity  
- **Cofactor:** one covalently bound **dipyrromethane** prosthetic group  

In short, this protein is a cytosolic porphobilinogen deaminase that catalyzes the second committed step of bacterial tetrapyrrole biosynthesis.
