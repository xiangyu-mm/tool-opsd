## Functional summary

The 565-amino-acid sequence is an **exact, full-length match** to reviewed UniProt **O14335**, DNA-binding protein **Scr1** from *Schizosaccharomyces pombe*. This exact match makes the reference annotation substantially more reliable than a function inferred only from a distant homolog.

Scr1 is a **nuclear transcriptional repressor** involved in **carbon catabolite repression**—repressing transcription of genes including *inv1*. Curated annotations also support sequence-specific DNA binding and negative regulation of RNA polymerase II transcription.

- Much of the remaining sequence is rich in low-complexity, serine/threonine-, proline-, and glutamine-rich regions. These are sequence observations, not evidence for a specific regulatory mechanism or modification.
- Calculated properties: **59.7 kDa**, theoretical **pI 10.21**, and **GRAVY −0.57**. The instability index is **66.08**, a sequence-based estimate rather than an experimental measurement.

**Limitations:** The values above are sequence-derived predictions; they do not account for protein processing, post-translational modifications, or binding partners. The protein analysis does not establish specific DNA targets beyond those stated in the reference annotation.
