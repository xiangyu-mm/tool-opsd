GlmU performs the final two steps in making **UDP-N-acetylglucosamine (UDP-GlcNAc)**:

UDP-GlcNAc supports bacterial cell-envelope construction, including **peptidoglycan biosynthesis** and, in annotated bacterial homologs, **lipid A biosynthesis**.
