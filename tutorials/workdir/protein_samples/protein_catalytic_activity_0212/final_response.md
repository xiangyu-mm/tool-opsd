## Functional identification

The 432-aa sequence is an **exact sequence match** to reviewed *Candida tropicalis* methionine aminopeptidase 2 (MetAP2; UniProt C5M8M4). This supports the enzyme identification directly, rather than by homology alone.

**Catalytic activity:** MetAP2 is a metal-dependent aminopeptidase that hydrolyzes the peptide bond after an N-terminal methionine, removing the initiator methionine from nascent proteins. Cleavage is favored when the second residue is small and uncharged.

**Overall reaction:**

\[
\text{Met–peptide} + \mathrm{H_2O}
\rightarrow \text{methionine} + \text{peptide lacking N-terminal Met}
\]

The reference annotation gives EC **3.4.11.18**. The enzyme can use divalent metals including Fe²⁺, Zn²⁺, Mn²⁺, or Co²⁺; the physiological cofactor is reported as uncertain, with Fe²⁺ considered likely.
