## Protein summary

The 395-aa sequence is an **exact match** to reviewed *Arabidopsis thaliana* **BUBR1** (UniProt **O22806**; 100% identity and coverage). This makes the protein identification highly confident.

- **Likely role:** Mitotic spindle assembly checkpoint component. BUBR1 helps delay anaphase until chromosomes are properly attached to the spindle.
- **Possible mechanism:** The reference annotation suggests BUBR1 may inhibit APC/C by preventing CDC20 binding; this mechanism is qualified as “by similarity,” so it should be treated as proposed rather than established specifically from this sequence analysis.
- **Interactions noted for the reference:** BUBR1 is part of the mitotic checkpoint complex and is annotated as interacting with CDC20, BUB3.1, MAD2, and APC/C.
- **Domain:** Pfam detected a **Mad3_BUB1_I** domain at residues 65–186.
- **Localization:** The reference is associated with kinetochores/centromeres, spindle microtubules, cytoplasm, and nucleus; it accumulates at unattached kinetochores during checkpoint activation.
- **Pathway/GO:** Annotated in mitotic spindle assembly checkpoint signaling (**GO:0007094**). Other reported cellular-component annotations include kinetochore, cytoplasm, nucleus, and spindle microtubule.

**Bottom line:** This is Arabidopsis BUBR1, a spindle-checkpoint protein associated with kinetochores and mitotic checkpoint signaling. The identification is direct from the exact sequence match; details of APC/C inhibition are based on the reference annotation and carry more uncertainty.
