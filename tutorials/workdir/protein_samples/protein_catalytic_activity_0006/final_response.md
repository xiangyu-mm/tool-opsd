### Functional identification

The 217-amino-acid sequence is an **exact match** to reviewed UniProt entry **Q5KW28**, a *Geobacillus kaustophilus* **tRNA (guanine-N7)-methyltransferase** (EC **2.1.1.33**). This supports the identification more strongly than a similarity-based prediction.

### Catalyzed reaction

The enzyme transfers a methyl group from **S-adenosyl-L-methionine (SAM)** to guanosine at position 46 of tRNA, forming **N7-methylguanosine at position 46 (m⁷G46)** and **S-adenosyl-L-homocysteine (SAH)**:

**tRNA guanosine-46 + SAM → tRNA m⁷G46 + SAH**

The reaction is recorded as **Rhea: RHEA:42708**. Closely related reviewed *Geobacillus* homologs have the same annotation. **SAM is the methyl donor** in the curated reaction.

**In short:** this enzyme modifies tRNA by N7-methylating guanosine 46.
