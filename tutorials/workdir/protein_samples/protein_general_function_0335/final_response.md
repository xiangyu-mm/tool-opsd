## Functional overview

This 476-aa sequence is very likely **GlmU**, a bifunctional bacterial enzyme that catalyzes the final two steps of **UDP-N-acetylglucosamine (UDP-GlcNAc) biosynthesis**. The assignment is strongly supported by a reviewed *Acidovorax* GlmU homolog with **79.4% identity across 99.6% of the query**.

- **N-terminal activity:** transfers UMP from UTP to N-acetylglucosamine-1-phosphate, producing UDP-GlcNAc (EC 2.7.7.23).
- **C-terminal activity:** transfers an acetyl group from acetyl-CoA to glucosamine-1-phosphate, producing N-acetylglucosamine-1-phosphate (EC 2.3.1.157).

UDP-GlcNAc supplies precursors for bacterial cell-envelope components, including peptidoglycan and, in relevant bacteria, lipid A. Close-homolog annotations also indicate **cytoplasmic localization** and **Mg²⁺ binding**; these are homology-based expectations, not direct experimental results for this sequence.
