### Likely function

UbiE catalyzes methylation steps in the biosynthesis of **menaquinone and ubiquinone**, using SAM as the methyl donor:

- Demethylmenaquinone → menaquinone
- 2-polyprenyl-6-methoxy-1,4-benzoquinol → its methylated ubiquinone-pathway product

The corresponding reported activities are:

- **EC 2.1.1.163:** demethylmenaquinone methyltransferase
- **EC 2.1.1.201:** 2-methoxy-6-polyprenyl-1,4-benzoquinol methyltransferase

The generalized reaction is:

**Quinone substrate + SAM → methylated quinone product + S-adenosyl-L-homocysteine**

### Biological context

The protein is therefore likely involved in:

- **Menaquinone biosynthesis**
- **Ubiquinone biosynthesis**
- **Quinol/quinone metabolism**
- Supporting bacterial respiratory electron transport
