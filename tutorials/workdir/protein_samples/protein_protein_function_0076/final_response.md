- dITP → dIMP + pyrophosphate

## Biological process

This protein is likely involved in **nucleotide-pool sanitization**. It removes aberrant or noncanonical purine nucleotides before they can be incorporated into DNA or RNA, thereby reducing the risk of mismatches, replication errors, and genome damage.
