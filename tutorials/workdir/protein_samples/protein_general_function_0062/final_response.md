## Functional assignment

The sequence is an **exact 100% identity and full-length match** to reviewed UniProt protein **Q4L977** from *Staphylococcus haemolyticus*. It is annotated as:

> **4,4′-diaponeurosporenoate glycosyltransferase**

This enzyme catalyzes the glucose esterification of 4,4′-diaponeurosporenic acid to produce **glycosyl-4,4′-diaponeurosporenoate**, an intermediate in **staphyloxanthin biosynthesis**.

Related reviewed *Staphylococcus aureus* proteins show approximately **45.5% identity over essentially the full sequence** and carry the same annotation.

## Localization and functional interpretation

The protein is most likely a **cell-membrane-associated, multi-pass membrane glycosyltransferase**. It is **not best interpreted as a conventional transporter or receptor**: the N-terminal glycosyltransferase domain provides strong evidence for catalytic activity, while the hydrophobic C-terminal portion likely anchors the enzyme in the membrane and positions it near its lipid-associated substrate.
