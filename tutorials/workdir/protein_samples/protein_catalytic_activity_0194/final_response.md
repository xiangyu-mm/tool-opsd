## Functional summary

The 768-aa sequence is strongly identified as **Rotavirus A VP3**, based on a match to reviewed UniProt protein Q6WNV6 with **88.2% identity and 100% query coverage**. VP3 is a multifunctional viral enzyme; its main role is to cap viral mRNAs, and the curated homolog annotation also reports 2′-5′ oligoadenylate phosphodiesterase activity.

**RNA capping reactions:**

- **Guanylyl transfer:** transfers GMP from GTP onto the 5′-diphosphate end of an RNA, forming the unusual 5′–5′ triphosphate-linked cap and releasing pyrophosphate:  
  *RNA 5′-diphosphate + GTP → Gppp-RNA + pyrophosphate.*

- **Cap methylation:** transfers a methyl group from **S-adenosyl-L-methionine (SAM)** to the cap guanosine’s N7 position, yielding the methylated cap and S-adenosyl-L-homocysteine (SAH).

**Additional reported activity:** VP3 hydrolyzes **2′-5′-linked oligoadenylates (2-5A)** produced by host OAS. The curated reaction annotation gives the trimer breakdown as AMP, AMP, and ATP. This activity is associated with preventing activation of host RNase L.

**Evidence caveat:** These activities are supported here by close, reviewed VP3 homolog annotations—not direct experiments on the supplied sequence itself. The match strongly supports the VP3 assignment, but the specific activity evidence is annotation transfer.
