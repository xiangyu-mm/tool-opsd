## Functional summary

The 297-aa sequence is an **exact full-length match** to curated *Bacillus anthracis* FtsX (UniProt Q81X30), making **cell division protein FtsX** the strong assignment.

FtsX is a **multi-pass cell-membrane protein** and the membrane component of the FtsEX complex; it interacts with the FtsE ATPase. Its best-supported biological roles are:

- **Cell division**, including asymmetric division and septum assembly.
- **Initiation or positive regulation of sporulation** in *Bacillus*; annotations place FtsX upstream of sporulation phosphorelay components.

Pfam detected the FtsX extracellular-domain family region (residues 58–164) and the FtsX family region (175–293). These support the family assignment. The sequence analysis establishes a multi-pass membrane protein, but does not provide a validated transmembrane-helix count or experimentally established topology. FtsX should be understood as part of an ATPase-associated division/signaling system, **not assigned a specific transported substrate** from this evidence.

The sporulation role is supported by *Bacillus* annotations; its importance may vary in other bacterial species. The annotation also reports an approximately 33.2-kDa predicted molecular mass.
