## Functional assignment

The 193-residue protein is most consistent with a **Ham1p-like dITP/XTP pyrophosphatase**, **EC 3.6.1.66**.

Evidence:

- Contains a near-full-length **Ham1p_like domain** (Pfam PF01725).
- Shows approximately **58% sequence identity over 97% coverage** to reviewed bacterial dITP/XTP pyrophosphatases.
- Homologs are annotated as **Mg²⁺-dependent** nucleotide pyrophosphatases.

## Catalytic activity

The enzyme hydrolyzes noncanonical purine nucleoside triphosphates, removing pyrophosphate and producing the corresponding nucleoside monophosphate:

- **XTP + H₂O → XMP + diphosphate + H⁺**
- **dITP + H₂O → dIMP + diphosphate + H⁺**
- **ITP + H₂O → IMP + diphosphate + H⁺**

The primary physiological role is likely **nucleotide-pool sanitization**: preventing incorporation of aberrant purine nucleotides into DNA or RNA.

The sequence strongly supports this enzyme family and reaction class. However, the precise substrate preference—whether XTP, dITP, or ITP is preferred—remains inferred from homologs rather than directly demonstrated for this exact sequence.
