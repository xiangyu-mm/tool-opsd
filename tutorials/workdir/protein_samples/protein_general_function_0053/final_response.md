## Functional summary

This 370-amino-acid protein is **3-dehydroquinate synthase (DHQS)**, enzyme commission **EC 4.2.3.4**. The sequence exactly matches reviewed UniProt protein **B2I9G0** from *Xylella fastidiosa*, supporting a high-confidence identification.

DHQS catalyzes the conversion of **3-deoxy-D-arabino-heptulosonate 7-phosphate (DAHP) to 3-dehydroquinate**, releasing phosphate. This is a step in **chorismate biosynthesis** and the broader pathway for aromatic amino acid precursors. The reference annotation reports **NAD⁺** and one divalent metal ion per subunit, which may be **Co²⁺ or Zn²⁺**. It is annotated as cytoplasmic.

**Sequence-derived properties:** predicted molecular mass **39.4 kDa**, theoretical pI **6.42**, instability index **29.8**, and GRAVY **0.069**. These are estimates and do not account for processing or modifications.

Pfam scans detected **DHQ_synthase (PF01761)** and **DHQS_C (PF24621)** domains, consistent with the enzyme assignment; an **Fe-ADH_2 (PF13685)** hit was also reported.
