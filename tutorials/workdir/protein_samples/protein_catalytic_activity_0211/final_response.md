## Functional assignment

The 564-residue protein is a **60 kDa lysophospholipase/transacylase**, corresponding to the reviewed rat protein **UniProt O88202** with an exact full-length sequence match.

### Principal catalytic reaction

It hydrolyzes lysophospholipids, especially **1-acyl-sn-glycero-3-phosphocholine**:

\[
\text{1-acyl-GPC} + H_2O
\rightarrow
\text{sn-glycerol-3-phosphocholine} + \text{free fatty acid} + H^+
\]

This is **phosphatidylcholine lysophospholipase A1 activity** (EC **3.1.1.47**, Rhea **RHEA:15177**).

### Additional catalytic activities

The enzyme is multifunctional and also supports:

- Hydrolysis of 2-acyl phosphatidylcholine and lysophosphatidylethanolamine.
- **PAF acetylhydrolase activity**, removing acetate from platelet-activating factor.
- **Phospholipid transacylation**, transferring acyl groups between lysophospholipids, glycerol, and acylglycerols.
- **L-asparaginase activity**:

\[
\text{L-asparagine} + H_2O
\rightarrow
\text{L-aspartate} + NH_4^+
\]

This corresponds to EC **3.5.1.1** and Rhea **RHEA:21016**.

### Sequence support

The sequence contains conserved **Asparaginase** and **Asparaginase_C** domains, consistent with the asparaginase reaction, and matches curated lysophospholipase homologs at very high or exact identity. Thus, the strongest overall annotation is:

> **A multifunctional lysophospholipase/transacylase that deacylates phospholipids and transfers acyl groups between lysophospholipid substrates, with additional L-asparaginase activity.**
