### Functional identification

The 432-aa protein is **gamma-glutamyl phosphate reductase** (also called **glutamate-5-semialdehyde dehydrogenase**), **EC 1.2.1.41**.

This assignment is highly reliable: the sequence is an **exact full-length match** to a reviewed Swiss-Prot protein annotated as gamma-glutamyl phosphate reductase. It also contains an **Aldedh domain** (PF00171), consistent with its aldehyde dehydrogenase-like chemistry.

### Catalyzed reaction

\[
\text{L-glutamate 5-phosphate} + \text{NADPH} + \mathrm{H^+}
\rightarrow
\text{L-glutamate 5-semialdehyde} + \text{phosphate} + \text{NADP}^+
\]

The product, **L-glutamate 5-semialdehyde**, spontaneously cyclizes to form **1-pyrroline-5-carboxylate**, an intermediate in **L-proline biosynthesis**.
