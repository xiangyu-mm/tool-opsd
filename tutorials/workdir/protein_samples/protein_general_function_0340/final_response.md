## Brief report

- **Identification:** The 326-aa sequence is an **exact match** to reviewed *Salmonella arizonae* ZipA (UniProt **A9MIF9**).
- **Primary function:** ZipA is a bacterial cell-division protein. It anchors the **FtsZ Z ring** to the inner membrane, helps stabilize and cross-link FtsZ protofilaments, and supports recruitment of other proteins to the septal ring.
- **Sequence features:** The N-terminus contains a strongly hydrophobic segment consistent with a **single-pass membrane anchor**. A proline/glutamine-rich, low-complexity region lies before a C-terminal **ZipA_C domain** (Pfam PF04354; residues 192–317).
- **Calculated properties:** Approximate molecular mass **36.1 kDa**; theoretical pI **5.26**.

These functional and localization annotations come from the exact reviewed reference match. The sequence supports a membrane anchor, but does not by itself establish whether that segment is cleaved.
