## Functional summary

The sequence is an **M17-family manganese-dependent aminopeptidase**, most consistent with a probable cytosolic aminopeptidase. It catalyzes **hydrolysis of peptide bonds at the N-terminus**, releasing a free amino acid:

**Peptide–Xaa + H₂O → shortened peptide + Xaa**

The enzyme removes unsubstituted N-terminal residues from peptides; leucine is a preferred substrate in related reaction annotations, but the available evidence does not establish a single exclusive substrate.

**Evidence:** The sequence exactly matches reviewed UniProt protein A4SSA7 (100% identity and coverage), annotated with EC 3.4.11.1 and EC 3.4.11.10. Pfam detects M17 peptidase domains. The reference annotation reports two Mn²⁺ ions per subunit and cytoplasmic localization. The reported biological context is intracellular protein processing and turnover.

This is strong sequence-to-reference evidence, but it is not a new experimental assay of the provided sequence; specific substrate preference and physiological targets remain uncertain.
