## Functional identification

This 211-residue protein is almost certainly a **tRNA (guanine-N7)-methyltransferase**, commonly called **TrmB/TrmB-like tRNA m⁷G46 methyltransferase**.

### Evidence

- **Exact sequence match** to reviewed UniProt protein **Q97FU9** from *Clostridium acetobutylicum*.
- Strong full-length homologs from *Clostridium tetani* and *C. kluyveri* are also annotated as tRNA m⁷G46 methyltransferases:
  - 68.2% identity over the complete sequence
  - 64.8% identity over essentially the complete sequence
- Pfam detects a strong **Methyltransf_4 domain** spanning approximately residues **38–204**:
  - PF02390, i-Evalue **6.1 × 10⁻⁴⁰**
- Additional overlapping methyltransferase-family profiles support a compact RNA methyltransferase fold.

### Likely biochemical function

The enzyme catalyzes:

\[
\text{tRNA guanine}_{46} + \text{SAM}
\rightarrow
\text{tRNA m}^7\text{G}_{46} + \text{SAH}
\]

It installs **7-methylguanosine at position 46 of tRNA**, a conserved modification that can influence tRNA folding, stability, and decoding efficiency.

### Cellular localization and architecture

- The protein is likely a **soluble cytosolic bacterial enzyme**.
- No transmembrane region or signal peptide is evident.
- Its basic character—predicted pI approximately **9.0**—is compatible with interaction with negatively charged tRNA.
- The sequence is compact, with the catalytic methyltransferase domain occupying most of the protein.
- The exact oligomeric state cannot be established from the sequence alone.

### Summary

**Most probable annotation:**  
**tRNA guanine-N7 methyltransferase; catalyzes formation of m⁷G46 in tRNA.**

The identification is high confidence because it is supported by an exact curated sequence match, conserved full-length homologs, and a highly significant methyltransferase domain.
