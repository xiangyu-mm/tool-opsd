## Catalytic role

The enzyme promotes ionization of farnesyl diphosphate, generating a reactive farnesyl carbocation. This intermediate undergoes intramolecular cyclization and rearrangement to form the bicyclic sesquiterpene **delta(6)-protoilludene**, with release of diphosphate.

Thus, the enzyme performs a **Mg²⁺-dependent carbon–carbon bond-forming terpene cyclization**, rather than an oxidation, reduction, hydrolysis, or transfer reaction.

## Summary

**This protein is delta(6)-protoilludene synthase, a fungal sesquiterpene cyclase that converts farnesyl diphosphate into delta(6)-protoilludene.** It is likely an early committed enzyme in protoilludene-derived secondary-metabolite pathways, including melleolide biosynthesis.
