## Functional identification

The 501-residue protein is most consistent with **RAD18**, a **RING-type E3 ubiquitin-protein ligase** involved in DNA-damage tolerance and postreplication repair.

### Evidence from the sequence

- **Strong homology to human RAD18 (UniProt Q9NS91):** 49.2% identity over 98.6% of the sequence.
- **RING-type C3HC4 zinc-finger domain:** approximately residues 25–64. This is the characteristic catalytic/recruitment module of RING E3 ligases.
- **UBZ4 zinc-binding region:** approximately residues 201–228, supporting interaction with ubiquitinated or DNA-associated factors.
- **SAP domain:** approximately residues 255–289, consistent with nucleic-acid/chromatin association.
- The protein is basic and contains low-complexity regions, fitting a nuclear DNA-repair factor rather than a soluble metabolic enzyme.

## Catalytic activity

The protein acts as an **E3 ubiquitin-protein ligase**, commonly assigned **EC 2.3.2.27**.

RAD18 does not function primarily by forming a stable catalytic ubiquitin intermediate itself. Instead, its RING domain brings an ubiquitin-loaded E2 enzyme—especially **UBE2B/RAD6**—into proximity with the target substrate and promotes ubiquitin transfer.

### Chemical reaction

The overall ubiquitin-transfer reaction is:

\[
\text{E2–S–ubiquitin} + \text{PCNA-Lys-NH}_2
\rightarrow
\text{E2–SH} + \text{PCNA-Lys-NH-CO–ubiquitin}
\]

More explicitly:

- The E2 enzyme carries ubiquitin as a **thioester** on its catalytic cysteine.
- RAD18 positions the E2–ubiquitin complex and the substrate.
- The ε-amino group of **PCNA Lys164** attacks the ubiquitin carbonyl.
- Ubiquitin becomes attached to PCNA through an **isopeptide bond**.
- The E2 catalytic cysteine is regenerated.

The physiological reaction is therefore:

> **Transfer of ubiquitin from an E2 catalytic cysteine to Lys164 of PCNA, producing monoubiquitinated PCNA.**

Monoubiquitinated PCNA promotes recruitment of translesion DNA polymerases and enables bypass of replication-blocking DNA lesions.

## Cofactors and partners

- **E2 partner:** UBE2B/RAD6.
- **Substrate:** PCNA, primarily **Lys164**.
- **Metal ions:** The RING and UBZ regions coordinate structural zinc; these zinc ions support domain folding and molecular recognition rather than serving as a metabolic catalytic cofactor.
- **E1 enzyme:** Required upstream to activate ubiquitin, although it is not part of the RAD18 catalytic complex itself.

## Overall summary

This sequence encodes a **RAD18-like RING E3 ubiquitin ligase**, not a conventional metabolic enzyme. Its catalytic role is to facilitate the **E2-dependent covalent transfer of ubiquitin onto PCNA Lys164**, generating monoubiquitinated PCNA during DNA-damage tolerance and postreplication repair.
