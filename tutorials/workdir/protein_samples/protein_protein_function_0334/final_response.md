## Biological process

The enzyme catalyzes the **final step of lipid IVA biosynthesis**, adding a phosphate group to the 4′ position of the lipid A disaccharide precursor. This contributes to:

- **Lipid A biosynthesis**
- **Lipopolysaccharide core-region biosynthesis**
- **Gram-negative bacterial outer-membrane construction**

The protein is associated with the bacterial plasma membrane, consistent with its role in modifying a membrane-associated lipid substrate. It is likely a membrane-associated enzyme rather than a multi-pass membrane transporter.
