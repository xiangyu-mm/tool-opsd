### Biological pathway and processes

The protein participates in the **universal L-histidine degradation pathway**, catalyzing its third and final step:

1. Histidine is converted through urocanate and imidazolone intermediates.
2. Imidazolonepropionase converts imidazolone-5-propanoate to N-formimidoyl-L-glutamate.
3. Downstream reactions ultimately produce **L-glutamate**.
