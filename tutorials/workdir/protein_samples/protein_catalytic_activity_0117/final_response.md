## Functional identification

This 351-amino-acid sequence is an **exact, full-length match** to the reviewed *Corynebacterium diphtheriae* protein **phenylalanine–tRNA ligase alpha subunit** (UniProt **Q6NHH2**). It is annotated as **EC 6.1.1.20**, phenylalanyl-tRNA synthetase activity.

## Catalyzed reaction

**tRNA(Phe) + L-phenylalanine + ATP → L-phenylalanyl-tRNA(Phe) + AMP + diphosphate + H⁺**

The enzyme charges phenylalanine-specific tRNA with **L-phenylalanine**, producing phenylalanyl-tRNA(Phe), which is used to incorporate phenylalanine during protein synthesis. The reference annotation also reports ATP and tRNA binding and magnesium-ion binding.

**Evidence and confidence:** The function and reaction are supported by a reviewed UniProt reference with an exact sequence match and a mapped Rhea reaction (RHEA:19413), rather than by sequence similarity alone. The protein analysis identifies this sequence as the **alpha subunit**; it does not independently establish the composition or assembly state of the full ligase complex.
