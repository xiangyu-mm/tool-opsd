### Likely function

The 87-aa sequence is an **exact match to Arabidopsis thaliana DEVIL 11 (DVL11; UniProt Q8S8S3)**. Its DVL family domain (PF08137) is detected at residues 61–79.

DVL11 is annotated as a small regulatory polypeptide involved in **plant growth and development**, particularly:

- **Negative regulation of cell proliferation**, including a proposed role in restricting polar growth in lateral organs.
- **Shoot-system development**.
- Potential coordination of **trichome socket-cell recruitment and differentiation**.

The reference annotations place it at the **plasma membrane**, with a nucleus annotation also reported. The trichome and lateral-organ roles are described as probable regulatory functions, so they should be treated as proposed rather than definitive mechanisms. These process assignments are based on the exact Arabidopsis sequence match and its annotations—not on functional experiments with the supplied sequence itself.
