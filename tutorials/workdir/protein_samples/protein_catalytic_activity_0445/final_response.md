## Functional identification

The 322-residue protein is a **DHH-family manganese-dependent inorganic pyrophosphatase**, also called an inorganic diphosphate phosphatase.

This assignment is highly confident because:

- The sequence is an **exact sequence match** to reviewed UniProt protein **O29502** from *Archaeoglobus fulgidus*.
- It contains the characteristic tandem **DHH domain** and **DHHA2 domain**:
  - DHH: residues ~5–157
  - DHHA2: residues ~195–318
- Curated homologs are annotated with **EC 3.6.1.1**, inorganic diphosphatase activity.
- The enzyme is reported to bind **two Mn²⁺ ions per subunit**.

## Catalyzed reaction

\[
\mathrm{PP_i + H_2O \rightarrow 2P_i}
\]

More explicitly, depending on protonation state:

\[
\mathrm{diphosphate + H_2O \rightarrow 2\ phosphate + H^+}
\]

Thus, the enzyme hydrolyzes **inorganic pyrophosphate (PPi)** into two orthophosphate molecules. This reaction removes PPi generated during biosynthetic processes and can help drive otherwise reversible reactions forward.
