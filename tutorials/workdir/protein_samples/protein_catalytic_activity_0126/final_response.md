## Functional identification

The 414-aa protein is a **phthiocerol/phthiodiolone dimycocerosyl transferase**, also known as a **PapA-family acyltransferase**.

- **EC:** 2.3.1.282  
- **Family/domain:** PapA_C domain, residues approximately 200–385  
- **Evidence strength:** Very high—the sequence is an exact 100% identity and 100% coverage match to reviewed UniProt entry **A0PQ31** from *Mycobacterium ulcerans*.

## Catalytic reaction

The enzyme performs the final acylation step in **phthiocerol dimycocerosate (PDIM) biosynthesis**. It transfers mycocerosyl groups from the acyl carrier protein domain of mycocerosic acid synthase (Mas) onto hydroxyl groups of phthiocerol-related alcohols:

\[
\text{mycocerosyl-Mas} + \text{phthiocerol}
\rightarrow
\text{dimycocerosyl phthiocerol} + \text{holo-Mas}
\]

Analogous reactions occur with **phthiodiolone** and **phenolphthiocerol**, producing their corresponding dimycocerosate esters. In other words, it is an **acyltransferase/ester synthase**, not a hydrolase or oxidoreductase.
