## Identification

The sequence is an **exact full-length match to human EWSR1/EWS**, UniProt **Q01844**. It encodes a 656-amino-acid, approximately 68.5-kDa protein.

## Functional summary

**EWSR1 is a multifunctional RNA-binding protein and transcriptional coregulator.** Its main roles include:

- Binding single-stranded RNA, including RNA containing an **AGGUAA** consensus sequence.
- Participating in regulation of DNA-templated transcription, potentially acting as a transcriptional repressor or coregulator.
- Interacting with components of the transcription machinery, including **RNA polymerase II subunit POLR2C**.
- Associating with other RNA- and protein-binding factors such as **SF1, calmodulin, TDRD3, and PTK2B/FAK2**.
- Contributing to nuclear RNA-processing and ribonucleoprotein-associated processes.

## Cellular processes

EWSR1 may participate in:

1. **RNA binding and RNA metabolism**
2. **Transcriptional regulation**
3. **RNA polymerase II-associated regulatory complexes**
4. **Nuclear body and ribonucleoprotein organization**
5. **Signal-dependent redistribution between nuclear and cytoplasmic compartments**

EWSR1 is also medically important because chromosomal rearrangements can generate **EWSR1 fusion proteins**. These fusions may place the EWSR1 low-complexity regulatory region next to a DNA-binding or transcription-factor domain, causing abnormal gene regulation and contributing to several cancers. This disease role applies to fusion proteins and should not be interpreted as the normal function of intact EWSR1.

## Subcellular localization

The primary localization is **nuclear**, including:

- Nucleoplasm
- Cajal bodies
- Other nuclear ribonucleoprotein-associated compartments

Curated annotations also report EWSR1 in the **cytoplasm** and, under particular conditions, at the **plasma membrane**. Following PTK2B/FAK2 activation, it can redistribute from the cytoplasm toward **ribosome-associated compartments**.

## Bottom line

This sequence encodes **EWSR1/EWS**, a largely disordered, glycine/arginine-rich **RNA-binding nuclear regulatory protein**. Its RRM supports direct RNA binding, while its low-complexity regions and zinc-ribbon module facilitate interactions with transcriptional and ribonucleoprotein complexes. It is primarily nuclear but can shuttle or redistribute to cytoplasmic and ribosome-associated compartments in response to cellular signaling.
