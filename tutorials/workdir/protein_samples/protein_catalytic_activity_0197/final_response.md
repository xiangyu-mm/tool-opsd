The sequence is most consistent with **ArgJ**, a bifunctional enzyme in the cyclic pathway of arginine biosynthesis. This assignment is supported by near-full-length matches (~49% identity) to reviewed ArgJ proteins; it is **homology-based, not a direct assay of this sequence**.

Its key reaction is the transfer of an acetyl group from N²-acetyl-L-ornithine to L-glutamate:

**N²-acetyl-L-ornithine + L-glutamate ⇌ N-acetyl-L-glutamate + L-ornithine**  
(Rhea: RHEA:15349; EC 2.3.1.35)

ArgJ homologs are also annotated to catalyze:

**L-glutamate + acetyl-CoA ⇌ N-acetyl-L-glutamate + CoA + H⁺**  
(Rhea: RHEA:24292; EC 2.3.1.1)

Together, these activities support arginine biosynthesis via the cyclic pathway.
