## Functional summary

This is very likely an **HIV-1 envelope glycoprotein precursor, gp160 (Env)**. The sequence is 768 amino acids long and matches reviewed HIV-1 gp160 references at about **72–73% identity**, with full query coverage. Pfam detects the characteristic **gp120 domain** (residues 33–510) and **gp41 domain** (529–720).

Gp160 is processed in the host cell’s secretory pathway: glycosylation is completed and cellular furin-like proteases cleave it into **gp120 and gp41**. In the reference annotation, gp120 binds CD4 and then a chemokine coreceptor such as CCR5 or CXCR4, while gp41 drives viral–host membrane fusion. Env forms trimers and is associated with viral and host-cell/endosomal membranes.
