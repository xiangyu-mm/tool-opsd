It likely performs two consecutive reactions:
1. **Phosphopantothenate–cysteine ligase** activity joins cysteine to 4′-phosphopantothenate, forming phosphopantothenoylcysteine.
2. **Phosphopantothenoylcysteine decarboxylase** activity converts that intermediate to 4′-phosphopantetheine.
