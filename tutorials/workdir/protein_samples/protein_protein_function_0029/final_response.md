### Functional summary

The 64-residue protein is **50S ribosomal protein bL33**.

- **Evidence:** Its sequence exactly matches curated cyanobacterial bL33 proteins, including UniProt Q8YNV9, with 100% sequence identity and coverage. A strong **Ribosomal_L33 Pfam domain** spans residues 9–64.
- **Function:** Structural component of the bacterial **50S large ribosomal subunit**, contributing to ribosome assembly and function during protein synthesis.
- **Subcellular localization:** **Cytoplasm**, as part of the ribosome.
- **Biological process:** **Translation** / bacterial protein biosynthesis.
- **Sequence features:** Very small, highly basic protein (~7.4 kDa; predicted pI ~10.2) with clustered cysteines. This cysteine-rich region is characteristic of the metal-binding ribosomal bL33 fold and should not be interpreted as a eukaryotic transcription-factor zinc finger.

**Conclusion:** This is a high-confidence bacterial/cyanobacterial **50S ribosomal protein bL33**, involved in the cytoplasmic ribosome and translation.
