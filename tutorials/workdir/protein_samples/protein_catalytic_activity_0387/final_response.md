## Functional assignment

The 197-residue protein is most likely a **Ham1p-like dITP/XTP pyrophosphatase** (**EC 3.6.1.66**).

Evidence:

- Pfam **Ham1p_like (PF01725)** spans nearly the entire sequence.
- It has **61.6% identity over 100% of the sequence** to the reviewed dITP/XTP pyrophosphatase Q6MF40.
- Other reviewed homologs show approximately 48%–49% full-length identity.
- The inferred protein is about **22.3 kDa** and is likely a soluble enzyme.

## Catalytic reaction

This enzyme hydrolyzes the terminal phosphoanhydride bond of noncanonical purine nucleoside triphosphates:

\[
\mathrm{XTP + H_2O \rightarrow XMP + PP_i + H^+}
\]

\[
\mathrm{dITP + H_2O \rightarrow dIMP + PP_i + H^+}
\]

It can also act on ITP:

\[
\mathrm{ITP + H_2O \rightarrow IMP + PP_i + H^+}
\]

Here, **XTP** is xanthosine triphosphate, **dITP** is deoxyinosine triphosphate, **ITP** is inosine triphosphate, and \(PP_i\) is inorganic diphosphate/pyrophosphate.

The enzyme therefore converts potentially mutagenic or otherwise noncanonical nucleotide triphosphates into their corresponding **monophosphates**, preventing their incorporation into DNA or RNA. It is best viewed as a **nucleotide-pool sanitization enzyme**.

## Cofactor and likely organization

- **Mg²⁺:** curated homologs bind approximately one Mg²⁺ ion per subunit, consistent with metal-assisted phosphate-group hydrolysis.
- **Oligomerization:** homologs are annotated as **homodimers**, although this has not been experimentally established for the supplied sequence.

## Confidence

**Moderate-to-high confidence** for the protein family and broad catalytic activity, based on the near-full-length match to reviewed homologs and the characteristic Ham1p-like domain. The exact substrate preference among XTP, dITP, and ITP should be regarded as inferred from homologs rather than directly demonstrated for this particular sequence.
