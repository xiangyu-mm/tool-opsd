### Protein identification and summary

The 184-aa sequence is an **exact match to *Caenorhabditis elegans* LET-60/Ras protein** (UniProt **P22981**).

- **Protein family:** Ras-family small GTPase  
- **Function:** Molecular switch in cell signaling; cycles between inactive **GDP-bound** and active **GTP-bound** states.
- **Catalytic activity:** GTP hydrolysis  
  **GTP + H₂O → GDP + phosphate + H⁺**  
  EC **3.6.5.2**, Rhea **RHEA:19669**
- **Regulation:** Guanine-nucleotide exchange factors activate it; GTPase-activating proteins stimulate return to the GDP-bound state.
- **Localization:** Plasma/cell membrane via a C-terminal lipid anchor. The terminal **Cys181** is annotated as S-farnesylated; residues 182–184 are removed during maturation.
- **Biological role:** LET-60 regulates developmental signaling, notably the switch between vulval and hypodermal cell fates during *C. elegans* vulval induction.
