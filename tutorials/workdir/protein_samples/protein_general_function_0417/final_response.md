## Functional summary

The 502-residue protein is most likely **MurE**, an ATP-dependent enzyme in bacterial peptidoglycan biosynthesis:

**UDP-N-acetylmuramoyl-L-alanyl-D-glutamate—meso-2,6-diaminopimelate ligase**  
**EC 6.3.2.13**

It catalyzes the ATP-dependent addition of **meso-diaminopimelate (meso-DAP)** to UDP-MurNAc-L-Ala-D-Glu, producing the corresponding UDP-MurNAc tripeptide precursor used for bacterial cell-wall assembly.
