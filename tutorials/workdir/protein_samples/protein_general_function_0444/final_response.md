## Functional summary

This 370-amino-acid protein is **CbiD, cobalt-precorrin-5B C(1)-methyltransferase** (**EC 2.1.1.195**), an enzyme in the **anaerobic biosynthesis of vitamin B12 (adenosylcobalamin)**.

It catalyzes:

\[
\text{Cobalt-precorrin-5B} + \text{SAM}
\rightarrow
\text{Cobalt-precorrin-6A} + \text{SAH}
\]

The enzyme transfers a methyl group from **S-adenosyl-L-methionine (SAM)** to the C-1 position of cobalt-precorrin-5B. This is reported as **step 6 of 10** in the anaerobic cobalamin biosynthetic pathway.
