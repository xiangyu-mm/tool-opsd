## Functional assignment

The 371-residue protein is **aminomethyltransferase (T-protein)** of the **glycine cleavage system**, **EC 2.1.2.10**.

This assignment is strongly supported: the sequence is an **exact full-length match** to reviewed UniProt entry **C6D8W9**, with additional close homologs showing the same annotation. It also contains the characteristic **GCV_T** and **GCV_T_C** domains.

## Catalytic reaction

The enzyme transfers the aminomethyl group carried on the lipoylated H-protein to tetrahydrofolate:

\[
\text{aminomethyl-dihydrolipoyl-H protein}
+ \text{tetrahydrofolate}
\rightarrow
\text{dihydrolipoyl-H protein}
+ \text{5,10-methylene-THF}
+ \mathrm{NH_4^+}
\]

In the full glycine-cleavage pathway, this is the step that converts the aminomethyl group derived from glycine into **5,10-methylene-tetrahydrofolate**, a one-carbon metabolic intermediate.

### Functional summary

**Aminomethyltransferase catalyzes the transfer of the glycine-derived aminomethyl group from the lipoylated H-protein of the glycine cleavage complex to tetrahydrofolate, producing 5,10-methylene-tetrahydrofolate, ammonia/ammonium, and reduced H-protein.**

The protein is therefore a component of the cytosolic **glycine cleavage complex**, rather than an enzyme that independently decarboxylates glycine.
