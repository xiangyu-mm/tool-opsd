## Functional summary

This 768-residue protein is very likely **Ccr4**, the catalytic deadenylase subunit of the **CCR4–NOT mRNA regulation complex**.

### Main characteristics

- **Length:** 768 amino acids  
- **Predicted mass:** ~86.6 kDa  
- **Theoretical pI:** ~7.06  
- **Overall composition:** strongly acidic/hydrophilic and low-complexity, with extensive **glutamine-, asparagine-, proline-, glycine-, and serine-rich regions**, especially toward the N-terminus  
- **Likely architecture:**
  - N-terminal low-complexity region, likely intrinsically disordered and involved in regulatory or protein–protein interactions
  - Internal **LRR-like region** around residues 392–448
  - C-terminal **Exo_endo_phos domain** around residues 541–760, consistent with the catalytic region of Ccr4-family proteins
- **No obvious signal peptide or long transmembrane segment** is apparent from the sequence.

### Homology evidence

The sequence showed extremely strong similarity to reviewed fungal Ccr4 proteins:

- **Candida glabrata Ccr4, UniProt Q6FRT2:** reported as 100% identical across the query alignment
- **Saccharomyces cerevisiae Ccr4, UniProt P31384:** approximately 59% identity over essentially the full length
- Additional fungal homologs gave similar functional annotations.

### Likely function

Ccr4 is a catalytic component of the **CCR4–NOT complex**, which regulates gene expression and mRNA turnover. Its principal inferred activity is:

- **3′→5′ ribonuclease/deadenylase activity**
- Preferential shortening of **poly(A) tails**
- Promotion of deadenylation-dependent mRNA decay
- Possible additional roles in transcriptional regulation and transcription elongation

The protein is therefore expected to participate in post-transcriptional mRNA control, rather than being a general metabolic enzyme.

### Localization and complex association

Based on homolog annotations, the protein is expected to localize mainly to:

- **Cytoplasm**
- **Nucleus**
- Potentially **P-bodies**

It is predicted to associate with the **CCR4–NOT core complex**, a large multiprotein complex containing Ccr4, Caf1, Not1–Not5, Caf40, and other subunits.

### Confidence and caveats

The functional assignment is **highly supported by full-length homology to reviewed Ccr4 proteins and by the conserved C-terminal nuclease-associated domain**. However, the exact catalytic activity, substrate preference, localization, and complex composition have not been experimentally tested for this particular sequence. Thus, poly(A)-specific deadenylase activity should be regarded as a strong homology-based inference rather than a direct assay result.
