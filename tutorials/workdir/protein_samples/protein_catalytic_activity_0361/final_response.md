### Functional identification

The 102-residue protein is the **β subunit of urease** (**Urease_beta, Pfam PF00699**). Its sequence is an exact match to curated *Bordetella* urease β-subunit proteins, including UniProt P0A4R2/P0A4R3.

### Catalytic reaction

The complete urease complex catalyzes the nickel-dependent hydrolysis of urea:

\[
\mathrm{urea + 2H_2O + H^+ \rightarrow HCO_3^- + 2NH_4^+}
\]

Equivalent products are often written as **CO₂ and NH₃**, depending on pH.

- **EC:** 3.5.1.5  
- **Rhea:** RHEA:20557  
- **Pathway:** Urea degradation / nitrogen metabolism  

Importantly, this small β subunit is **not the principal catalytic subunit**. It is an accessory/structural component of the multimeric urease complex, supporting assembly and activity of the nickel-containing catalytic urease enzyme.
