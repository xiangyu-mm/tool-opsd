## Functional summary

This 481-amino-acid protein is very likely **MurD**, the bacterial UDP-N-acetylmuramoyl-L-alanyl-D-glutamate–meso-diaminopimelate ligase (**EC 6.3.2.13**). The sequence matches reviewed *Clostridium* MurD proteins across nearly its full length (about 60–63% identity and 99.6–100% coverage), and it contains all three expected Mur-ligase domains.

MurD uses ATP to add **meso-diaminopimelate (meso-DAP)** to UDP-MurNAc-L-Ala-D-Glu, producing the next peptidoglycan precursor. The homolog annotations indicate a **Mg²⁺** requirement. This step supports bacterial **cell-wall peptidoglycan biosynthesis**, and thereby contributes to processes such as cell division and maintenance of cell shape.

**Likely location:** cytoplasm/cytosol, where soluble peptidoglycan precursor synthesis occurs. The localization is based on the close homolog annotations, not a direct localization experiment on this sequence.

**Confidence and caveat:** The MurD assignment is strongly supported by full-length homology and domain composition, but the catalytic function, substrate specificity, and localization are inferred from reviewed homologs rather than demonstrated experimentally for the query protein. The available result does not establish its oligomeric state.
