### Biological role

ND1 forms part of the membrane arm of **NADH:ubiquinone oxidoreductase, or Complex I**. Complex I transfers electrons from NADH toward ubiquinone and couples this process to proton translocation across the inner mitochondrial membrane. ND1 is primarily a membrane-embedded structural and functional component of the complex and is important for complex I assembly and catalytic activity.

The sequence itself is dominated by long hydrophobic stretches with very few charged residues, consistent with multiple membrane-spanning helices. It is therefore not expected to function as a soluble NADH-binding enzyme independently; its activity depends on its incorporation into the full Complex I assembly.
