### Functional identification

The 180-residue protein is **alkyl hydroperoxide reductase AhpD** (**EC 1.11.1.28**), a small bacterial redox enzyme/accessory reductase.

The assignment is very strong: the sequence is an **exact 100% sequence match** to reviewed UniProt protein Q0BXT2, with additional AhpD homologs sharing 61–68% identity. A Pfam scan also detects the characteristic **CMD domain** near residues 99–176.

### Catalytic role

AhpD regenerates the reduced, active form of the peroxiredoxin **AhpC** after AhpC has been oxidized during peroxide reduction. It functions as a redox relay:

1. Reduced lipoyl groups donate reducing equivalents to AhpD.
2. AhpD reduces the oxidized catalytic cysteine of AhpC.
3. Reduced AhpC can then reduce alkyl hydroperoxides and related peroxide substrates.

The annotated catalytic region includes:

- **Cys134**: forms a cysteine-sulfenic-acid intermediate.
- **Residue 131**: acts as a proton donor.
- AhpD can form an interchain disulfide with AhpC during the redox cycle.

### Overall coupled reaction

The Rhea-mapped overall reaction is:

\[
\text{dihydrolipoyl-[lipoyl-carrier]} + \text{hydroperoxide}
\rightarrow
\text{lipoyl-[lipoyl-carrier]} + \text{alcohol} + \mathrm{H_2O}
\]

For an alkyl hydroperoxide, this can be represented generically as:

\[
\mathrm{ROOH + 2e^- + 2H^+ \rightarrow ROH + H_2O}
\]

The electrons are supplied indirectly by reduced lipoyl groups through AhpD and AhpC.

### Cofactor and pathway interpretation

- **No classical flavin, heme, or metal cofactor is indicated.**
- The redox input is provided by **reduced lipoyl groups**.
- The protein participates in **peroxide detoxification, cellular redox homeostasis, and oxidative-stress response**.

### Concise summary

This sequence encodes **AhpD, a lipoyl-dependent alkyl hydroperoxide reductase/peroxiredoxin reductase**. It restores the reduced active-site cysteine of AhpC by transferring reducing equivalents from reduced lipoyl carriers, thereby enabling AhpC-mediated conversion of hydroperoxides into the corresponding alcohols and water.
