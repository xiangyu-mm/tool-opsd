## Catalytic reaction

MurE adds **meso-2,6-diaminopimelate (meso-DAP)** to the UDP-linked peptidoglycan precursor UDP-MurNAc-L-Ala-D-Glu:

\[
\text{UDP-MurNAc-L-Ala-D-Glu}
+ \text{meso-DAP}
+ \text{ATP}
\rightarrow
\text{UDP-MurNAc-L-Ala-D-Glu-meso-DAP}
+ \text{ADP}
+ \text{P}_{i}
+ \text{H}^{+}
\]

In expanded form, the product is:

**UDP-N-acetylmuramoyl-L-alanyl-γ-D-glutamyl-meso-2,6-diaminopimelate**

This is the fourth cytoplasmic precursor in the construction of the bacterial peptidoglycan stem peptide. MurE therefore contributes the DAP residue that later becomes part of the peptide cross-linking network in many Gram-positive bacteria and Gram-negative organisms.

## Catalytic role

MurE is an ATP-dependent peptide ligase. ATP is used to activate the terminal carboxyl group of the UDP-MurNAc-linked D-glutamate substrate, allowing formation of a new amide bond with the amino group of meso-DAP. Mg²⁺ supports ATP binding and phosphate-group handling during catalysis.

A conserved carboxylated lysine is reported in close MurE homologs, but its modification should be considered a homolog-based expectation rather than experimentally demonstrated for this exact sequence.

**Conclusion:** This protein is a strongly supported **MurE meso-DAP-adding ligase**, an essential cytosolic enzyme in bacterial cell-wall peptidoglycan biosynthesis.
