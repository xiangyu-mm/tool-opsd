## Functional summary

The 768-aa sequence is an **exact match to a segment of *Caenorhabditis elegans* UBR-1** (UniProt **P91133**), a reviewed **E3 ubiquitin-protein ligase**. The reference protein is 2,058 aa long, so the submitted sequence represents only part of it.

UBR-1 is annotated as a component of the **N-end rule pathway**: it recognizes proteins with destabilizing N-terminal residues and promotes their ubiquitination and degradation. This pathway assignment is annotated **by similarity**. The sequence’s UBR-family Pfam hits (including zf-UBR and ARM_UBR domains) are consistent with that identification.

### Likely biological processes
- **Protein ubiquitination and ubiquitin-dependent degradation**, including the N-end rule pathway.
- In *C. elegans*, UBR-1 is reported to work with CED-3 in LIN-28 cleavage/degradation, contributing to **seam-cell fate patterning during larval development**.
- It is also reported to negatively regulate glutamate metabolism through GOT-1.2; glutamate-level modulation is linked to **backward locomotion**.

### Cellular location
The full-length UBR-1 annotation includes **cytoplasm and membrane**, and describes it as a **single-pass membrane protein** associated with a ubiquitin-ligase complex. These are annotations for full-length UBR-1, not localization measurements of the submitted fragment itself.

**Caveat:** The identification is strong because of the exact sequence match, but the sequence is partial. The pathway role is supported by UniProt annotation; the specialized developmental and behavioral roles are *C. elegans*-specific and should not be generalized beyond that organism without additional evidence.
