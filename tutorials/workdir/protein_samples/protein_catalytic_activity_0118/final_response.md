## Functional identification

The 542-aa sequence is an **exact full-length match** to reviewed *Agrobacterium tumefaciens* phosphoglucomutase **UniProt P39671** (100% identity and coverage; E-value 0). This supports a high-confidence identification as **phosphoglucomutase (EC 5.4.2.2)**.

## Catalytic activity

**Net reversible reaction** (Rhea **RHEA:23536**):

\[
\alpha\text{-D-glucose 1-phosphate}
\;\rightleftharpoons\;
\alpha\text{-D-glucose 6-phosphate}
\]

The enzyme shifts the phosphate group within the glucose molecule, from carbon 1 to carbon 6 or vice versa. The reaction proceeds through **glucose 1,6-bisphosphate**: an enzyme-bound phosphoserine first donates a phosphate to glucose 1-phosphate, then receives a phosphate back as glucose 6-phosphate is formed. Thus, there is **no net phosphate consumption**.

The reviewed reference annotation reports **Mg²⁺** as a cofactor, with one Mg²⁺ per subunit. This enzyme supports glucose metabolism and, in *A. tumefaciens*, is annotated as required for capsular-polysaccharide synthesis and normal lipopolysaccharide.

**Evidence basis:** exact sequence match to the reviewed *A. tumefaciens* protein; closely related reviewed phosphoglucomutase annotations also describe the reversible reaction and glucose 1,6-bisphosphate intermediate. The intermediate detail is supported by those related annotations.
