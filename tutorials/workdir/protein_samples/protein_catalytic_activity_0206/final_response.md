## Functional annotation

The 492-aa protein is most consistent with a **bifunctional NAD(P)H-hydrate repair enzyme, Nnr**.

### Evidence

- Strong homology to reviewed Nnr proteins:
  - ~32–34% sequence identity
  - ~87% sequence coverage
  - E-values around \(10^{-49}\)–\(10^{-51}\)
- Two characteristic domains:
  - **YjeF N-terminal domain**: residues approximately 34–182; associated with NAD(P)HX epimerase activity
  - **Carbohydrate-kinase-like C-terminal domain**: residues approximately 243–466; associated with ADP-dependent NAD(P)HX dehydration
- Predicted size: approximately **51.8 kDa**
- No transmembrane segment or obvious signal peptide is apparent, supporting a **soluble intracellular enzyme**.
- Homologs bind approximately **one K⁺ ion per subunit**.

## Catalytic activities

### 1. NAD(P)HX epimerization

The enzyme interconverts the two stereoisomers of hydrated nicotinamide cofactors:

\[
(6R)\text{-NADHX} \rightleftharpoons (6S)\text{-NADHX}
\]

and

\[
(6R)\text{-NADPHX} \rightleftharpoons (6S)\text{-NADPHX}
\]

This activity corresponds to **NAD(P)HX epimerase**, associated with EC **5.1.99.6**.

### 2. ADP-dependent NAD(P)HX dehydration

The enzyme then repairs the S-epimer by converting it back to the functional reduced cofactor:

\[
(6S)\text{-NADHX} + \text{ADP}
\rightarrow
\text{NADH} + \text{AMP} + \text{P}_{i} + \text{H}^{+}
\]

and analogously:

\[
(6S)\text{-NADPHX} + \text{ADP}
\rightarrow
\text{NADPH} + \text{AMP} + \text{P}_{i} + \text{H}^{+}
\]

This is **ADP-dependent NAD(P)H-hydrate dehydratase** activity, associated with EC **4.2.1.136**.

## Biological role

NADH and NADPH can undergo hydration at the nicotinamide ring, forming inactive **NADHX/NADPHX** derivatives. This enzyme repairs those damaged cofactors by:

1. Epimerizing either hydrated stereoisomer to the S-form.
2. Using ADP to drive dehydration of the S-form back to NADH or NADPH.

Thus, the protein participates in **NAD(P)H metabolite repair and nicotinamide nucleotide metabolism**.

## Conclusion

**Most likely annotation:** bifunctional NAD(P)H-hydrate repair enzyme Nnr  
**Activities:** NAD(P)HX epimerase plus ADP-dependent NAD(P)HX dehydratase  
**Cofactor:** likely one bound K⁺ per subunit  
**Localization:** probably soluble and intracellular  
**Confidence:** high for the enzyme family and overall reaction based on domain architecture and homologs; exact activity for this particular sequence remains homology-based rather than experimentally confirmed.
