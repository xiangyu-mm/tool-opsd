## Main function

VPS53B is a component of the **Golgi-associated retrograde protein (GARP) complex**. It participates in:

- Retrograde transport from early and late endosomes to the late Golgi/trans-Golgi network
- Tethering of endosome-derived vesicles at the Golgi
- Membrane fusion and coordination of SNARE-complex assembly

It is best interpreted as a **vesicle-trafficking/tethering factor**, rather than as an enzyme.
