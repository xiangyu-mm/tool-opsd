### Predicted biochemical role

TsaD works with **TsaB and TsaE** to modify adenosine at position 37 of selected tRNAs. It transfers a threonylcarbamoyl group from threonylcarbamoyl-AMP to the N⁶ amino group of A37:

\[
\text{TC-AMP} + \text{tRNA-A37}
\rightarrow
\text{tRNA-t}^6\text{A37} + \text{AMP}
\]

This modification improves the accuracy and efficiency of decoding codons beginning with adenine.
