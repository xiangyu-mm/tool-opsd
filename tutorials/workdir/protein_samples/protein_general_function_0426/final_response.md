### Protein overview

The 227-amino-acid sequence is most likely **phosphatidylethanolamine N-methyltransferase (PEMT)**, a multi-pass membrane enzyme involved in phosphatidylcholine production.

- **Function:** Uses S-adenosylmethionine (SAM) to methylate phosphatidylethanolamine (PE) in three successive steps, yielding phosphatidylcholine (PC) through PMME and PDME intermediates.
- **Localization:** Likely associated with the endoplasmic-reticulum membrane; related PEMT proteins are also reported in mitochondrial membranes.
- **Sequence evidence:** It is strongly similar to reviewed human PEMT (UniProt Q9UBM1; 61.6% identity over 83.7% of the query), and contains a PEMT Pfam domain (PF04191.19, residues 120–220). Its hydrophobic stretches are consistent with a multi-pass membrane protein.
- **Estimated properties:** 227 aa; molecular mass about 25.2 kDa; theoretical pI 7.55.

The functional assignment is supported by homology and the matching domain, rather than direct experimental characterization of this exact sequence.
