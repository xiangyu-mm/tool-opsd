## Functional identification

This 328-residue protein is **3-dehydroquinate synthase (DHQS)**, EC **1.4.1.24**, an NAD-dependent enzyme in the **shikimate pathway**.

The assignment is highly reliable because the sequence is an **exact full-length match** to reviewed UniProt entry **Q2FQ51**. Additional reviewed homologs show approximately 58% identity across the full sequence and have the same annotation.

### Catalytic reaction

The enzyme catalyzes oxidative deamination followed by intramolecular cyclization:

\[
\text{2-amino-2,3,7-trideoxy-D-lyxo-hept-6-ulosonate}
+ \mathrm{NAD^+}
+ \mathrm{H_2O}
\rightarrow
\text{3-dehydroquinate}
+ \mathrm{NH_4^+}
+ \mathrm{NADH}
+ \mathrm{H^+}
\]

This reaction is represented by **Rhea RHEA:25956**. The substrate is also described in the annotation as **2-amino-3,7-dideoxy-D-threo-hept-6-ulosonic acid**, reflecting an alternative stereochemical/tautomeric naming convention for the same reaction entry.

### Functional interpretation

DHQS converts an amino-containing precursor into **3-dehydroquinate**, removing the amino group as ammonium while reducing NAD⁺ to NADH. 3-Dehydroquinate then enters the canonical shikimate pathway, ultimately contributing to biosynthesis of aromatic amino acids such as phenylalanine, tyrosine, and tryptophan.
