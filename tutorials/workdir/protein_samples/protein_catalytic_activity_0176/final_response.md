## Catalytic reaction

The enzyme synthesizes **5-phospho-α-D-ribose 1-diphosphate**, usually abbreviated **PRPP**, from ribose-5-phosphate and ATP:

\[
\text{D-ribose-5-phosphate} + \text{ATP}
\rightarrow
\text{5-phospho-α-D-ribose-1-diphosphate} + \text{AMP} + \text{H}^{+}
\]

In words, the enzyme transfers a **pyrophosphoryl group from ATP to the anomeric 1-hydroxyl group of ribose-5-phosphate**. ATP is therefore converted to AMP rather than ADP.

Mg²⁺ ions coordinate the nucleotide phosphate groups and help stabilize the highly charged transition state and products.

## Biological role

PRPP is a central activated ribose-phosphate donor used in:

- de novo purine biosynthesis,
- de novo pyrimidine biosynthesis,
- purine and pyrimidine salvage pathways,
- biosynthesis of other ribonucleotide-derived metabolites.
