## Biochemical role

ArgJ participates in the **cyclic pathway of L-arginine biosynthesis** by transferring acetyl groups among pathway intermediates. Its reported activities include:

1. Formation of **N-acetylglutamate** from glutamate using acetyl-CoA.
2. Transacetylation between **N-acetylornithine and glutamate**, producing ornithine and regenerating N-acetylglutamate.

Thus, the protein helps initiate and recycle the N-acetylated intermediates required for production of **ornithine and ultimately L-arginine**.
