## Functional identification

The 565-aa sequence is an **exact match** to reviewed UniProt **Q6GZT8**, annotated as the **large subunit (R1) of Frog virus 3 ribonucleoside-diphosphate reductase**. The enzyme is classified as **EC 1.17.4.1**. The annotation describes an R1/R2 holoenzyme that supplies deoxyribonucleotides for viral DNA synthesis.

## Catalyzed reaction

In the physiological reductive direction, the enzyme converts ribonucleoside diphosphates into the corresponding 2′-deoxyribonucleoside diphosphates:

**ribonucleoside 5′-diphosphate + reduced thioredoxin → 2′-deoxyribonucleoside 5′-diphosphate + oxidized thioredoxin + H₂O**

Thus, it performs the key conversion needed to generate DNA building blocks from ribonucleotide precursors. The database reaction (Rhea **23252**) is listed in the reverse direction, with deoxyribonucleotide and oxidized thioredoxin as reactants.

The sequence contains matches to the **Ribonuc_red_lgC** Pfam domain (PF02867). Its exact-match reference annotates active-site positions 256, 258, and 260, including a cysteine radical intermediate at position 258. The protein is the large subunit; full activity is associated with the RNR holoenzyme and its small subunit.
