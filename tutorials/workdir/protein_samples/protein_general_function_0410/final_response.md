### Functional summary
This 177-amino-acid sequence is an **ATP synthase subunit delta**. It exactly matches the reviewed *Shewanella woodyi* UniProt protein B1KQ37, and the OSCP domain (PF00213) spans nearly the full sequence.

Subunit delta is part of the **peripheral stalk** of F-type ATP synthase, which links the membrane-embedded F₀ portion to the catalytic F₁ portion. It helps couple their activity, likely by transmitting conformational changes; the reference annotation also notes a possible role in proton conduction. This is a structural component, not itself the ATP-making catalytic subunit.
