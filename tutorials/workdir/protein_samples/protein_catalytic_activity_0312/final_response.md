## Functional identification
The 400-aa sequence is an **exact match (100% identity and coverage)** to reviewed UniProt protein **FtsW (B6ELH6)** from *Aliivibrio salmonicida*. It contains the **FTSW_RODA_SPOVE** family domain (PF01098; residues 29–383) and is annotated as a **probable peptidoglycan glycosyltransferase/polymerase** (**EC 2.4.99.28**).

FtsW is a multipass inner-membrane protein associated with the division septum. It extends peptidoglycan glycan strands during cell-wall synthesis and cell division.

## Catalyzed reaction

**Growing peptidoglycan chainₙ + lipid-linked disaccharide–pentapeptide precursor → growing chainₙ₊₁ + undecaprenyl diphosphate + H⁺**
