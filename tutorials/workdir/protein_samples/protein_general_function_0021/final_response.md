## Functional summary

This 436-residue protein is **proteasome-activating nucleotidase (PAN)**, an archaeal **AAA+ ATPase** that prepares substrates for degradation by the 20S proteasome. The identification is highly secure: the sequence is an **exact full-length match** to reviewed UniProt entry **Q8TX03** from *Methanopyrus kandleri*.

### Molecular function

PAN performs the mechanical steps required for proteasomal degradation:

1. Recognizes and binds protein substrates.
2. Uses ATP hydrolysis to unfold them.
3. Translocates unfolded substrates into the 20S proteasome core.
4. Uses its extreme C-terminal tail to dock into pockets between proteasome α-subunits, inducing gate opening.

The associated reaction is:

**ATP + H₂O → ADP + phosphate + H⁺**  
Rhea: **RHEA:13065**; EC assignment: **3.6.1.-**

ATP binding promotes association with the proteasome, while ATP hydrolysis powers substrate unfolding and translocation. No specific non-ATP cofactor is reported.
