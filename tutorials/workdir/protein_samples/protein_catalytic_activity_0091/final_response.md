The sequence is an **exact match** to reviewed *Thermotoga maritima* proline–tRNA ligase (ProRS; UniProt **Q9WYY4**), EC **6.1.1.15**. This is a direct sequence match to a curated annotation, not merely a function inferred from a distant homolog.

**Net reaction:**

\[
\text{L-proline} + \text{tRNA}^{\text{Pro}} + \text{ATP}
\rightarrow
\text{L-prolyl-tRNA}^{\text{Pro}} + \text{AMP} + \text{diphosphate (PPi)}
\]

The enzyme first uses ATP to activate proline as **prolyl-AMP**, then transfers proline to the acceptor end of tRNA(Pro). Its annotation also describes editing of alanine errors: hydrolysis of Ala-AMP before transfer and deacylation of mischarged Ala-tRNA(Pro). **ATP is a reaction substrate**; the retrieved annotation does not specify an additional cofactor.
