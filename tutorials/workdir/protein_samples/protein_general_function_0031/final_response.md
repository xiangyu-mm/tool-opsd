## Functional summary

This 264-residue protein is an **ABC-transporter ATP-binding protein, CysA**, forming the nucleotide-binding/energy-coupling subunit of a bacterial **sulfate/thiosulfate import system**.

### Key features

- **Length:** 264 amino acids  
- **Predicted molecular mass:** ~29.1 kDa  
- **Theoretical pI:** ~6.32  
- **Protein class:** ABC transporter nucleotide-binding domain  
- **Pfam domain:** ABC_tran (PF00005), residues approximately 18–164  
- **Strong sequence evidence:** Exact sequence match to reviewed UniProt **Q93DX8**, annotated as sulfate/thiosulfate import ATP-binding protein CysA.

### Catalytic and structural motifs

The sequence contains the characteristic motifs of ABC ATPases:

- **Walker A/P-loop:** `GPSGCGKTT`, supporting ATP binding
- **ABC transporter signature:** `LSGGQ`, characteristic of ABC transporter nucleotide-binding subunits
- Additional conserved regions consistent with ATP hydrolysis and coupling to the membrane transporter

These motifs identify the protein as an **ATP-driven transport motor**, rather than a soluble metabolic enzyme.

### Biological function

CysA hydrolyzes ATP to provide energy for importing:

- **Sulfate**
- **Thiosulfate**

The associated reactions are represented as:

`Sulfate(out) + ATP + H₂O → sulfate(in) + ADP + phosphate`

and the analogous thiosulfate transport reaction.

The transporter complex is expected to contain:

- Two CysA ATP-binding subunits
- Two membrane-spanning components, CysT and CysW
- One periplasmic solute-binding protein, CysP

CysA itself is a **peripheral component of the inner membrane transporter complex**, not the transmembrane channel.

### Annotation summary

- **EC classification:** 7.3.2.3, ABC-type transport system ATPase
- **GO terms include:**
  - ATP binding
  - ATP hydrolysis
  - ABC transporter complex
  - ABC-type sulfate transporter activity
  - ABC-type thiosulfate transporter activity

### Overall conclusion

The sequence is confidently identified as the **CysA ATP-binding subunit of a bacterial ABC sulfate/thiosulfate importer**. Its primary role is to bind and hydrolyze ATP, mechanically driving substrate translocation through the associated CysT/CysW membrane components. No separate cofactor requirement or independent catalytic metabolic function is indicated by the available annotations.
