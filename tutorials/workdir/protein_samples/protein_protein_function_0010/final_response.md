## Function
Vif helps HIV-1 evade the host’s innate antiviral defense. It recruits host proteins—including **CBF-β, CUL5, RBX2, and elongin B/C**—to assemble a cullin-5 E3 ubiquitin ligase complex. Vif directs this complex toward the host restriction factors **APOBEC3G and APOBEC3F**, promoting their ubiquitination and degradation by the proteasome. This reduces the APOBEC-mediated damage these proteins can cause to viral replication. Reference annotations also report that Vif may inhibit APOBEC3G’s deaminase activity through a degradation-independent mechanism.

Reviewed annotations additionally report roles in viral particle/core morphology and infected-cell G2 cell-cycle arrest; these are secondary annotations, not conclusions established independently from this sequence analysis.
