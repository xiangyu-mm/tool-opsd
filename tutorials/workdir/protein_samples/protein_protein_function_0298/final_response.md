## Likely function

This 403-residue bacterial protein is most likely a **bifunctional IspD/IspF enzyme** in the **methylerythritol phosphate (MEP; non-mevalonate) pathway** of isoprenoid biosynthesis.

### Evidence

- Strong homology to curated Swiss-Prot IspD/IspF fusion proteins:
  - ~45–46% amino-acid identity
  - ~98–99% sequence coverage
  - E-values around \(10^{-94}\) to \(10^{-96}\)
- Matching Pfam domains:
  - **IspD domain**: residues ~4–228
  - **YgbB/IspF-associated domain**: residues ~241–396
  - **NTP-transferase-related region**: residues ~5–128

### Predicted enzymatic activities

The protein likely performs two sequential MEP-pathway reactions:

1. **IspD — MEP cytidylyltransferase**  
   \[
   \text{MEP} + \text{CTP} \rightarrow \text{CDP-ME} + \text{PP}_i
   \]
   EC **2.7.7.60**, Rhea **RHEA:13429**

2. **IspF — ME-cPP synthase**  
   \[
   \text{CDP-ME2P} \rightarrow \text{ME-cPP} + \text{CMP}
   \]
   EC **4.6.1.12**, Rhea **RHEA:23864**

A **divalent metal cation** is associated with the activity of curated homologs.

### Associated biological process

The enzyme participates in the **MEP pathway for isopentenyl diphosphate (IPP) biosynthesis**, which supplies precursors for:

- bacterial and plastidial isoprenoid synthesis
- terpenoid biosynthesis
- downstream production of molecules such as quinones, carotenoids, and other terpenoids

### Additional properties

- Length: **403 aa**
- Predicted molecular mass: **~43.4 kDa**
- Predicted pI: **~5.2**
- The architecture is consistent with an **IspD–IspF fusion protein**, rather than two separate enzymes.

**Overall assignment:** a bacterial bifunctional **2-C-methyl-D-erythritol 4-phosphate cytidylyltransferase/ME-cPP synthase**, supporting the non-mevalonate isoprenoid biosynthetic pathway. This assignment is strongly supported by sequence homology and domain architecture, but remains computational rather than experimentally demonstrated for this exact sequence.
