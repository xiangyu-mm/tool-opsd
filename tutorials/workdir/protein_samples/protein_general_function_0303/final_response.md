### Functional summary

The 509-amino-acid sequence is an **exact, full-length match** to reviewed mouse mitochondrial **dihydrolipoyl dehydrogenase (DLD; UniProt O08749)**. This supports a high-confidence identification.

DLD is a **FAD-dependent oxidoreductase (EC 1.8.1.4)**. It reoxidizes protein-bound dihydrolipoamide, using NAD⁺ as the electron acceptor:

**dihydrolipoyl-protein + NAD⁺ → lipoyl-protein + NADH + H⁺**

It serves as the **E3 component** of the pyruvate, 2-oxoglutarate, and branched-chain α-ketoacid dehydrogenase complexes, and also participates in the glycine cleavage system. The reference annotation describes it as a **homodimer**, with one FAD cofactor per subunit, and places it mainly in the **mitochondrial matrix**.
