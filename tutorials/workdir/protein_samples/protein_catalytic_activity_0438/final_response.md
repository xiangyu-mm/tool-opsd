## Functional identification

The 347-residue protein is **uroporphyrinogen decarboxylase (UROD)**, **EC 4.1.1.37**.

Evidence is very strong: the sequence is an **exact sequence match** to a reviewed UROD protein, and it contains the characteristic **URO-D domain (Pfam PF01208)** spanning nearly the entire sequence.

## Catalytic reaction

UROD catalyzes the stepwise decarboxylation of the four acetate side chains of uroporphyrinogen III:

\[
\text{Uroporphyrinogen III} + 4H^+
\rightarrow
\text{Coproporphyrinogen III} + 4CO_2
\]

In chemical terms, the four acetate substituents on uroporphyrinogen III are converted into methyl groups, releasing four molecules of carbon dioxide.

## Biological role

- **Pathway:** Heme/protoporphyrin IX biosynthesis  
- **Pathway position:** Fourth and final step from 5-aminolevulinate to coproporphyrinogen III  
- **Likely localization:** Cytosol  
- **Likely oligomeric state:** Homodimer  
- **Cofactor:** No separately required cofactor is indicated by the curated annotations  

**Summary:** This protein is a cytosolic uroporphyrinogen decarboxylase that converts uroporphyrinogen III into coproporphyrinogen III by removing four carboxyl groups as CO₂.
