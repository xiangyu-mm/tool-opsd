**Identity:** This 393-amino-acid sequence is an exact, full-length match to reviewed rat **MAP2K1 (MEK1; UniProt Q01986)**. It is also highly similar to mouse and human MAP2K1.

**Function:** MAP2K1 is a dual-specificity protein kinase in the **RAS–RAF–MEK–ERK pathway**. It phosphorylates and activates ERK1/MAPK3 and ERK2/MAPK1 on both threonine and tyrosine residues in their activation-loop Thr-Glu-Tyr motif.

**Characteristics and regulation:** The sequence is consistent with a soluble, intracellular kinase—not a membrane-spanning protein. In the pathway, RAF proteins activate MEK1; phosphorylation at Ser-218 and Ser-222 is associated with MEK1 activation, while ERK-mediated feedback phosphorylation at Thr-292 contributes to downregulation. Reported locations include cytoplasm/cytosol, nucleus, centrosome and membrane-associated contexts.
