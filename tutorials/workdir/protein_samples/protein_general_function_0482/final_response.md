### Catalytic role

LpxD transfers a 3-hydroxyacyl group from **3-hydroxyacyl-ACP** to the amino group of **UDP-3-O-acylglucosamine**:

\[
\text{UDP-3-O-acylglucosamine}
+
\text{3-hydroxyacyl-ACP}
\rightarrow
\text{UDP-2-N,3-O-bis(3-hydroxyacyl)glucosamine}
+
\text{ACP}
\]

This is an early and essential step in formation of **lipid A**, the membrane-anchoring component of lipopolysaccharide in Gram-negative bacteria.
