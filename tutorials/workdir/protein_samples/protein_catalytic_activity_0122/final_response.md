## Functional annotation

The protein is most likely a **1-acyl-sn-glycerol-3-phosphate acyltransferase (AGPAT/LPAAT)**, probably an **AGPAT1-like membrane enzyme**.

### Catalyzed reaction

\[
\text{1-acyl-sn-glycerol-3-phosphate} + \text{acyl-CoA}
\rightarrow
\text{1,2-diacyl-sn-glycerol-3-phosphate} + \text{CoA}
\]

In biological terms, it converts **lysophosphatidic acid (LPA)** into **phosphatidic acid (PA)** by adding an acyl group to the **sn-2 position** of LPA.

- **EC:** 2.3.1.51  
- **Rhea:** RHEA:19709  
- **Acyl donor:** acyl-CoA  
- **Product:** phosphatidic acid  
- **Cofactor:** No additional metal or redox cofactor is indicated; the activated acyl-CoA substrate supplies the acyl group.

## Likely biological role

The enzyme participates in **glycerolipid and phospholipid biosynthesis**, specifically the step producing phosphatidic acid from LPA. Phosphatidic acid is subsequently used in pathways including **CDP-diacylglycerol and membrane phospholipid synthesis**.

**Confidence:** Moderate-to-high based on strong sequence homology and a matching acyltransferase domain. The precise acyl-chain preference cannot be determined reliably from this sequence alone.
