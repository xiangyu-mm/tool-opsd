## Functional assignment

The 329-residue protein is most likely **prostaglandin reductase 1 (PTGR1)-like**, an **NAD(P)H-dependent oxidoreductase**.

### Evidence

- Length: **329 aa**, approximately **35.9 kDa**
- Strong similarity to reviewed mammalian PTGR1 proteins:
  - **61–62% sequence identity**
  - Approximately **99% alignment coverage**
  - E-values around **10⁻¹⁴²**
- Contains alcohol-dehydrogenase-like regions, including **ADH_N_2** and **zinc-dependent alcohol dehydrogenase-like** domains.
- Homologs are annotated as cytosolic enzymes with **15-oxoprostaglandin 13-reductase** and **2-alkenal reductase** activities.

## Primary catalytic reaction

The principal reaction is reduction of the 13,14 double bond of 15-oxo-prostaglandins:

\[
\text{15-oxo-prostaglandin} + \text{NADPH} + \mathrm{H^+}
\rightarrow
\text{13,14-dihydro-15-oxo-prostaglandin} + \text{NADP}^+
\]

For example:

\[
\text{15-oxo-PGE}_2 + \text{NADPH} + \mathrm{H^+}
\rightarrow
\text{13,14-dihydro-15-oxo-PGE}_2 + \text{NADP}^+
\]

The same activity can apply to 15-oxo-PGE1, 15-oxo-PGF1α, 15-oxo-PGF2α, and related lipid mediators.

## Additional likely activities

Based on the homolog annotations, the enzyme may also:

- Reduce **15-oxo-lipoxin A4**
- Reduce **α,β-unsaturated aldehydes and ketones**, such as medium-chain alkenals and 4-hydroxy-2-nonenal:

\[
\text{α,β-unsaturated carbonyl} + \text{NADPH} + \mathrm{H^+}
\rightarrow
\text{saturated carbonyl} + \text{NADP}^+
\]

- Oxidize leukotriene B4 at C12 with lower efficiency:

\[
\text{Leukotriene B4} + \text{NADP}^+
\rightleftharpoons
\text{12-oxo-leukotriene B4} + \text{NADPH} + \mathrm{H^+}
\]

## Summary

This sequence is best assigned as a **cytosolic NADPH-dependent prostaglandin/lipid aldehyde reductase**, most closely related to **PTGR1**. Its primary biochemical role is expected to be the **reductive inactivation of 15-oxo-prostaglandins by saturating their 13,14 double bond**. The assignment is supported by strong full-length homology and conserved domain architecture, but exact substrate preferences should ideally be confirmed experimentally for this particular sequence.
