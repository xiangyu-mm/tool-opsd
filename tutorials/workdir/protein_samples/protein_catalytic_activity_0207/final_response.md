## Functional identification

The 446-residue protein is the **glycine dehydrogenase (decarboxylating) P-component subunit 1** of the **glycine cleavage system**.

**Evidence:**

- Exact sequence match to reviewed UniProt protein **B6YT11** from *Thermococcus onnurineus*.
- Strong match to the **GDC-P domain** (Pfam **PF02347**; residues 4–442).
- Curated annotation: **EC 1.4.4.2**, glycine dehydrogenase (decarboxylating).
- The archaeal P protein is a heterodimer; this sequence represents its **subunit 1**, rather than necessarily the complete standalone P component.

## Catalytic reaction

The enzyme catalyzes the oxidative decarboxylation of glycine as part of the glycine cleavage reaction:

\[
\text{H-protein-lipoyl-lysine} + \text{glycine} + H^+
\rightarrow
\text{aminomethyl-dihydrolipoyl-H-protein} + CO_2
\]

In words, the PLP-dependent P component:

1. Binds glycine through its **pyridoxal-5′-phosphate (PLP)** cofactor.
2. Removes the carboxyl group as **CO₂**.
3. Transfers the remaining **aminomethyl group** to the lipoamide cofactor attached to the glycine-cleavage H protein.

The resulting aminomethyl-lipoamide intermediate is subsequently processed by the other glycine-cleavage components.

**Conclusion:** This sequence encodes a highly confident **PLP-dependent glycine decarboxylase P-component subunit involved in glycine degradation**, EC **1.4.4.2**; the associated Rhea reaction is **RHEA:24304**.
