## Functional identification

This sequence is **granzyme B**, a secreted **trypsin-like serine endopeptidase**. It is an exact sequence match to the reviewed rat granzyme B protein **UniProt P18291**.

### Evidence from the sequence

- **Length:** 248 aa  
- **Signal peptide:** residues 1–18, consistent with secretion into cytolytic granules  
- **Activation propeptide:** residues 19–20  
- **Mature protease:** begins at residue 21  
- **Domain:** Peptidase S1/Trypsin domain, approximately residues 21–241  
- **Catalytic triad:**  
  - His65  
  - Asp109  
  - Ser204  
- **Predicted molecular mass:** ~27.3 kDa  
- **Theoretical pI:** ~9.6  

The catalytic triad is characteristic of chymotrypsin-like serine proteases. No metal ion or other catalytic cofactor is required; catalysis relies on the serine-protease charge-relay system.

## Catalytic activity

Granzyme B hydrolyzes peptide bonds, with strong preference for cleavage **after an aspartate residue** at the P1 position:

\[
\text{Peptide–Asp|Xaa–Peptide} + H_2O
\rightarrow
\text{Peptide–Asp–OH} + \text{H}_2\text{N–Xaa–Peptide}
\]

In general protease notation:

\[
\mathrm{R{-}CO{-}NH{-}R' + H_2O \rightarrow R{-}COOH + H_2N{-}R'}
\]

Thus, the enzyme is an **Asp-specific serine protease**, classified under **EC 3.4.21.79**.

## Biological role

Granzyme B is stored in cytotoxic T-cell and natural-killer-cell granules. Following delivery into a target cell by perforin, it cleaves intracellular protein substrates involved in cell death, including:

- **Caspase-3 and caspase-9**, promoting apoptosis
- **Gasdermin E**, which can promote pyroptotic cell death

The sequence strongly supports granzyme B identity and Asp-directed proteolysis. The exact cellular targets depend on biological context and should not be inferred solely from the sequence.

**Summary:** This protein is a secreted, zymogen-processed **granzyme B serine protease** that catalyzes hydrolysis of peptide bonds, preferentially cleaving on the C-terminal side of aspartate residues.
