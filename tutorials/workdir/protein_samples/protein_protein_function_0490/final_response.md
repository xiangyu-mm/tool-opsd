## Functional summary

The 307-aa sequence is an **exact full-length match** to reviewed *Drosophila melanogaster* **EBP2** (UniProt Q9V9Z9), also called probable rRNA-processing protein EBP2. Its strong Ebp2-domain match (PF05890) supports this identification.

- **Function:** EBP2 is annotated as an RNA-binding protein required for processing 27S precursor rRNA.
- **Localization:** Primarily the **nucleus and nucleolus**; it is also associated with the preribosome large-subunit precursor. A nuclear-periphery annotation is reported as well.
- **Biological processes:** **rRNA processing** and **large ribosomal subunit biogenesis**.

The exact sequence match makes the *Drosophila* reference annotation directly relevant. These annotations support the functional summary, but do not by themselves establish a more detailed molecular mechanism.
