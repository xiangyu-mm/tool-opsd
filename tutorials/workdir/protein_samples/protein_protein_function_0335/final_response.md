## Likely function

TatA is a small membrane component that helps form the protein-conducting channel of the Tat machinery. The Tat system transports fully folded proteins across the bacterial inner membrane when those substrates contain an N-terminal **twin-arginine signal motif**, typically `RR`.

TatA functions within Tat protein complexes, including TatABC-associated complexes and TatA-only assemblies. It is not itself the transported substrate.
