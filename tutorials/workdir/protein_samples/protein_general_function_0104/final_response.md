## Functional summary

This is very likely a **bacterial FtsH-family ATP-dependent zinc metalloprotease**, involved in protein quality control, particularly the turnover of membrane-associated proteins. The identification is strongly supported by **70.7% sequence identity over 95.3% coverage** to reviewed *Bacillus subtilis* FtsH (UniProt P37476), as well as matching FtsH, AAA+, and M41 peptidase domains.

- **Architecture:** 680 amino acids, approximately **75.3 kDa**. Two hydrophobic segments near the N-terminus are consistent with membrane anchoring; the large downstream catalytic region is expected to face the cytoplasm.
- **ATPase module:** An AAA+ domain (Pfam positions 199–330) contains the P-loop motif **LVGPPGTGKT**, consistent with ATP binding and hydrolysis.
- **Protease module:** A Peptidase_M41 domain (positions 412–601) and the sequence motif **HESGH** support zinc metalloprotease activity.
- **Likely role:** FtsH uses ATP-driven unfolding/translocation coupled to proteolysis; its homologs degrade membrane and cytoplasmic proteins and contribute to membrane-protein quality control. Homologs are annotated as homohexamers, but that oligomeric state is inferred—not directly established—for this sequence.
- **Additional properties:** The calculated theoretical pI is **4.71**. The strongly acidic, Ser/Asp/Glu-rich C-terminal segment may be a flexible regulatory tail, but disorder or a specific regulatory role was not directly assessed.

Overall, the sequence is best described as a **membrane-anchored AAA+ protease, likely FtsH**, with strong homology-based support for its function and localization.
