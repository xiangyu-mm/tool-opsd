## Molecular function

HSP60 uses ATP-dependent conformational changes to assist the folding and refolding of proteins, especially mitochondrial proteins that are newly imported, unfolded, or damaged by stress.

Representative reaction:

**ATP + H₂O + unfolded polypeptide → ADP + phosphate + folded polypeptide**

It is therefore best classified as an:

- **ATP-dependent protein-folding chaperone**
- **Protein-refolding factor**
- **Molecular chaperone**, rather than a conventional metabolic enzyme
