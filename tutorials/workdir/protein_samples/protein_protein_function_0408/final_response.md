## Functional assignment

**Probable protein:** 4-hydroxy-3-methylbut-2-enyl diphosphate reductase (**IspH**, EC 1.17.7.4)

**Confidence:** Very high. The sequence is an exact 100% sequence match to the reviewed Swiss-Prot protein **A5GIF7** and has the characteristic **LYTB domain** found in IspH proteins.

### Molecular function

IspH catalyzes the final reaction of the methylerythritol phosphate (**MEP/DOXP**) pathway:

\[
\text{HMBPP} \rightarrow \text{IPP} + \text{DMAPP}
\]

More specifically, it converts **1-hydroxy-2-methyl-2-(E)-butenyl 4-diphosphate (HMBPP)** into a mixture of:

- **Isopentenyl diphosphate (IPP)**
- **Dimethylallyl diphosphate (DMAPP)**

The enzyme contains one oxygen-sensitive **[4Fe–4S] cluster per subunit**, which is essential for catalysis and participates in the reductive rearrangement of HMBPP.

### Biological process

This protein participates in:

- **Methylerythritol phosphate/DOXP isoprenoid biosynthesis**
- **IPP and DMAPP biosynthesis**
- **Terpenoid and isoprenoid precursor production**

IPP and DMAPP are subsequently used to synthesize essential terpenoid compounds, including quinones, carotenoids, prenylated metabolites, and other isoprenoid products.
