### Structural and mechanistic clues

The sequence is highly similar to ArgJ proteins that undergo **autolytic processing near residue 180**. A conserved catalytic nucleophile is located at approximately the cleavage boundary, consistent with the characteristic ArgJ catalytic mechanism. Processed ArgJ proteins are commonly described as an **alpha/beta-chain complex**, forming a heterotetramer of two alpha and two beta chains.
