## Predicted localization

**High confidence: secreted/extracellular, exported through the mycobacterial ESX-1/type VII secretion system.**

### Evidence

- The 526-residue sequence is an **exact sequence match** to curated *Mycolicibacterium smegmatis* EspB (UniProt **L8FKX3**).
- EspB is annotated as an **ESX-1-secreted protein** localized to the **extracellular region**.
- The sequence contains EspB-associated domains:
  - **EspB_PE**: residues 12–84
  - **EspB_PPE**: residues 135–282
- It lacks evidence for a conventional N-terminal Sec/Tat signal peptide or transmembrane helix, consistent with export by the specialized **type VII secretion pathway** rather than classical secretion.
- The C-terminal half is highly glycine/proline-rich and low-complexity, a feature consistent with a specialized mycobacterial secretion-associated protein.

## Functional summary

This protein is **EspB**, an ESX-1 secretion-associated protein of mycobacteria. It is likely exported from the bacterial cytoplasm to the **cell envelope/extracellular environment**, where it may participate in host interaction and, depending on the species, DNA-conjugation or virulence-related processes. EspB homologs can undergo processing and oligomerization after secretion, but those detailed properties are best regarded as homolog-based rather than certain for every strain.

**Overall prediction:**  
**Extracellular / secreted via ESX-1 (type VII secretion), not a membrane protein or conventionally secreted Sec/Tat protein.**
