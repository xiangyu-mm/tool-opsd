## Functional assignment

The 411-residue protein is most consistent with **RlmN**, an **[4Fe–4S]-dependent radical-SAM dual-specificity RNA methyltransferase**.

Supporting evidence:

- Strong similarity to reviewed RlmN proteins: approximately **42–49% identity over 89–94% of the sequence**.
- Detected **RlmN_N** and **Radical_SAM** domains.
- Contains the cysteine-rich region characteristic of binding a radical-SAM **[4Fe–4S] cluster**.
- Homologs are annotated as **EC 2.1.1.192**.

## Catalytic activity

The enzyme methylates the **C2 position of adenine** in RNA:

1. **23S rRNA substrate**

\[
\text{Adenosine}_{2503}\text{ in 23S rRNA}
\rightarrow
\text{2-methyladenosine}_{2503}\text{ (m}^2\text{A2503)}
\]

2. **tRNA substrate**

\[
\text{Adenosine}_{37}\text{ in tRNA}
\rightarrow
\text{2-methyladenosine}_{37}\text{ (m}^2\text{A37)}
\]

In both cases, **S-adenosyl-L-methionine (SAM)** supplies the methyl group and participates in radical-SAM chemistry. The [4Fe–4S] cluster activates SAM to generate **5′-deoxyadenosyl radical**, while a second SAM molecule can serve as the methyl donor. The reaction produces **5′-deoxyadenosine**, **L-methionine**, and, in the detailed two-SAM bookkeeping, **S-adenosyl-L-homocysteine**.

A simplified representation is:

\[
\text{RNA-A} + 2\,\text{SAM}
\rightarrow
\text{RNA-m}^{2}\text{A} + \text{5′-deoxyadenosine}
+ \text{L-methionine} + \text{SAH}
\]

where RNA-A is either A2503 in 23S rRNA or A37 in tRNA.

## Summary and confidence

**Predicted function:** dual-specificity RlmN RNA methyltransferase  
**Reaction:** C2 methylation of A2503 in 23S rRNA and A37 in tRNA  
**Cofactor:** one [4Fe–4S] radical-SAM cluster  
**Methyl donor/radical precursor:** SAM  
**Confidence:** **moderate to high**, based on domain architecture and strong homology; the exact dual substrate specificity is inferred from homolog annotations rather than experimentally demonstrated for this particular sequence.
