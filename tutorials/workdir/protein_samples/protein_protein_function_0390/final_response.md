## Functional identification

The 152-amino-acid protein is **FabZ, 3-hydroxyacyl-[acyl-carrier-protein] dehydratase**.

This assignment is **high confidence**: the sequence is an exact match to the reviewed UniProt FabZ protein Q9CJK9, with additional strong matches to FabZ proteins from related bacteria.

### Molecular function

FabZ catalyzes dehydration during bacterial fatty-acid synthesis:

\[
\text{β-hydroxyacyl-ACP} \rightarrow \text{enoyl-ACP} + H_2O
\]

It acts on short-chain β-hydroxyacyl-ACP substrates and on longer saturated or unsaturated β-hydroxyacyl-ACP intermediates.

## Summary

This is a small, soluble bacterial **FabZ dehydratase** that participates in the type II fatty-acid synthase pathway. It converts β-hydroxyacyl-ACP intermediates into enoyl-ACPs and likely contributes both to general fatty-acid production and, in Gram-negative organisms, to lipid A biosynthesis.
