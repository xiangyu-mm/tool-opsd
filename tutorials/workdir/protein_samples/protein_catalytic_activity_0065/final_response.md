## Functional identification

The 193-residue protein is **xanthine phosphoribosyltransferase (XPRT)**, **EC 2.4.2.22**.

The sequence is an exact match to reviewed *Streptococcus pneumoniae* XPRT proteins, including UniProt accessions **Q04IV9** and **Q8DNL1** (100% sequence identity and coverage).

## Catalytic reaction

In the physiologically relevant forward direction, XPRT catalyzes the salvage of xanthine:

\[
\text{xanthine} + \text{5-phospho-}\alpha\text{-D-ribose 1-diphosphate (PRPP)}
\rightarrow
\text{xanthosine 5′-monophosphate (XMP)} + \text{diphosphate (PP}_{i}\text{)}
\]

This is a **phosphoribosyl-transfer reaction**: the enzyme transfers the phosphoribosyl group from PRPP to xanthine, producing XMP. XMP can then be used in guanine/nucleotide biosynthesis for RNA and DNA production.

**Reaction database mapping:** Rhea **RHEA:10800**. The reaction is chemically reversible, although the forward direction represents xanthine salvage.
