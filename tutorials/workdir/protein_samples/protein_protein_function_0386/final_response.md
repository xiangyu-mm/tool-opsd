## Enzymatic function

SULT2A1 transfers a sulfate group from **3′-phosphoadenosine-5′-phosphosulfate (PAPS)** to hydroxyl-containing acceptors.

General reaction:

\[
\mathrm{PAPS + R{-}OH \rightarrow PAP + R{-}O{-}SO_3^-}
\]

Its reported substrates include:

- Steroid hormones and hydroxysteroids
- Dehydroepiandrosterone (DHEA)
- Pregnenolone
- Androsterone
- Cholesterol and related sterols
- Bile acids
- Alcohols and phenols
- Various xenobiotic compounds

The sulfate conjugation generally increases substrate water solubility and promotes transport and renal or biliary excretion. However, sulfation can also bioactivate certain compounds, particularly some aromatic or carcinogenic xenobiotics.
