## Functional identification

This 310-residue protein is **protoheme IX farnesyltransferase**—also called **heme O synthase**—a member of the **UbiA intramembrane prenyltransferase family**.

The assignment is highly reliable because the sequence is an **exact match** to reviewed UniProt protein **A0RZ28** and contains the characteristic UbiA domain spanning approximately residues 24–291.

### Sequence features

- Length: **310 aa**
- Strongly hydrophobic, consistent with an integral membrane enzyme
- Approximately **nine transmembrane helices**
- Pfam domain: **UbiA, PF01040**
- Cellular location: **cell/plasma membrane**
- EC number: **2.5.1.141**

## Catalytic reaction

The enzyme catalyzes the transfer of a farnesyl group from farnesyl diphosphate to protoheme IX:

\[
\text{heme B} + \text{(2E,6E)-farnesyl diphosphate} + H_2O
\rightarrow
\text{heme O} + \text{diphosphate}
\]

Chemically, it replaces the **C2 vinyl substituent of protoheme IX (heme B)** with a **hydroxyethyl-farnesyl side chain**, producing **heme O**.

- Rhea: **RHEA:28070**
- Pathway: **heme O biosynthesis**
- Biological role: supplies heme O for subsequent heme-copper respiratory oxidase assembly, such as cytochrome *c* oxidase biosynthesis.

### Summary

The sequence encodes a **multi-pass membrane-bound prenyltransferase**, not a soluble transporter or generic membrane protein. Its specific catalytic activity is **farnesylation of heme B to form heme O**, using **farnesyl diphosphate** as the prenyl donor.
