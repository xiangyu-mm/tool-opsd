### Catalyzed reaction

\[
\text{1-acyl-sn-glycerol-3-phosphate} + \text{acyl-CoA}
\rightarrow
\text{1,2-diacyl-sn-glycerol-3-phosphate} + \text{CoA}
\]

In biochemical terms, the enzyme acylates the **sn-2 hydroxyl group of lysophosphatidic acid (LPA)** using an acyl-CoA donor, producing **phosphatidic acid (PA)**.

### Functional summary

This is an **ER-associated membrane glycerolipid biosynthetic enzyme** that catalyzes the second acylation step of the de novo phosphatidic-acid pathway:

**LPA + acyl-CoA → phosphatidic acid + CoA**

The sequence strongly supports AGPAT activity, but the exact preferred acyl-chain lengths and degree of unsaturation cannot be determined confidently from sequence homology alone.
