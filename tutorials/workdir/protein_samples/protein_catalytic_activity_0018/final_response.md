### Functional summary

The 279-aa sequence exactly matches the reviewed *Streptococcus equi* subsp. *equi* protein **C0M8J5**, annotated as an **undecaprenyl-diphosphatase** (EC **3.6.1.27**). It is a multipass membrane protein.

Its annotated catalytic reaction is:

**Undecaprenyl diphosphate + H₂O → undecaprenyl phosphate + inorganic phosphate + H⁺**

Thus, the enzyme removes one phosphate from undecaprenyl diphosphate (UPP). The annotation also reports that the protein confers bacitracin resistance. The functional assignment is strongly supported by the exact sequence match; the reaction is the curated annotation, not a mechanism inferred from sequence alone.
