## Functional overview

The 405-amino-acid protein is **PurT, formate-dependent phosphoribosylglycinamide formyltransferase** (**EC 6.3.1.21**), an enzyme in bacterial **de novo purine/IMP biosynthesis**.

### Key evidence

- The sequence is an **exact 100% identity match** to reviewed UniProt protein **A9BNI6** from *Delftia acidovorans*.
- Strong homologs from other bacteria show the same annotation and approximately 85–86% sequence identity.
- Conserved domain architecture supports the identification:
  - **RS_preATP-grasp-like domain:** residues ~14–109
  - **ATP-grasp domain:** residues ~122–312
  - **PurT C-terminal domain:** residues ~332–400

### Catalytic reaction

PurT catalyzes the ATP-dependent formylation of GAR:

\[
\text{GAR} + \text{formate} + \text{ATP}
\rightarrow
\text{FGAR} + \text{ADP} + \text{phosphate}
\]

where GAR is 5-phosphoribosylglycinamide and FGAR is 5-phosphoribosyl-N-formylglycinamide. This is the formate-dependent route to the first formylated intermediate of purine biosynthesis. The reaction is recorded as **Rhea:RHEA:24829**.
