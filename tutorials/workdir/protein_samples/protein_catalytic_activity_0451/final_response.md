### Functional summary

The 768-amino-acid sequence is an **alanine–tRNA ligase** (alanyl-tRNA synthetase; **EC 6.1.1.7**). It matches the reviewed *Nostoc punctiforme* enzyme B2J876 with **100% identity across the query sequence**, supporting the functional assignment.

The enzyme attaches alanine to its cognate tRNA in two steps: ATP first activates L-alanine to form an enzyme-bound alanyl-AMP intermediate, then alanine is transferred to tRNA(Ala).

**Overall reaction:**

\[
\text{L-alanine} + \text{tRNA}^{\text{Ala}} + \text{ATP}
\rightarrow
\text{L-alanyl-tRNA}^{\text{Ala}} + \text{AMP} + \text{diphosphate}
\]

This supplies correctly charged tRNA for protein synthesis. The reference annotation also reports an editing function that removes serine or glycine mistakenly attached to tRNA(Ala), and binding of one Zn²⁺ per subunit. Those details are annotations of the matching reference protein, rather than independent experimental measurements on the supplied sample.

**Sequence-derived properties:** 768 aa; estimated molecular mass ~85.7 kDa. The sequence tool’s evidence grade was *moderate homology support*, although the reported exact match to the reference strongly supports the assignment.
