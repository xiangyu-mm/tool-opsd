### Protein summary

The sequence is an **exact match to *Caenorhabditis elegans* PEX10** (UniProt **C0HKD7**), a peroxisome biogenesis protein.

- **Length:** 314 amino acids  
- **Calculated properties:** about 36.0 kDa; theoretical pI 9.01  
- **Domains/motifs:** a Pex2/Pex12-family region at residues 18–221 and a C-terminal RING-type zinc-finger region around residues 254–300  
- **Localization:** annotated as a multipass peroxisomal membrane protein  
- **Main role:** PEX10 is part of the **PEX2–PEX10–PEX12 retrotranslocation channel**. It helps export the PEX5 matrix-protein import receptor from the peroxisome so PEX5 can be recycled, and has an annotated E3 ubiquitin-ligase role in that process. When recycling is impaired, PEX5 ubiquitination can contribute to its degradation.
