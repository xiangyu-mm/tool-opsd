## Functional identification

The sequence is **archaeal ribosomal protein eS27** (also called **small ribosomal subunit protein eS27**).

### Evidence

- **Exact sequence match** to reviewed UniProt protein **Q4J9B1** from *Sulfolobus acidocaldarius*:
  - 100% sequence identity
  - 100% coverage
- Strong Pfam match to **Ribosomal_S27e (PF01667)**.
- The protein is only **66 amino acids long**, highly basic, and contains a conserved **Cys-rich zinc-binding motif**, characteristic of archaeal eS27 proteins.

### Function

eS27 is a small structural component of the **30S small ribosomal subunit**. It contributes to ribosome architecture and interacts with small-subunit rRNA. Its conserved cysteines are annotated as mediating **zinc-ion binding**, likely stabilizing the protein structure.

### Subcellular localization

The protein is expected to be located in the **cytoplasm**, as part of the **cytosolic 30S ribosomal subunit**. It is not predicted to be a membrane or secreted protein.

### Biological processes

- **Translation** — direct, strongly supported assignment.
- **Ribosome assembly/function** — as a structural constituent of the small subunit.
- **rRNA processing** — reported for a close archaeal homolog, but this is a less direct annotation than its ribosomal structural role.

### Summary

This sequence encodes a **small, zinc-binding archaeal ribosomal protein eS27**, a component of the cytoplasmic **30S ribosomal subunit** involved primarily in **translation**. The identification is high confidence because the sequence exactly matches a curated archaeal eS27 protein.
