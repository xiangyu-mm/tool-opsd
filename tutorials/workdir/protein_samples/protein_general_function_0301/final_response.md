## Functional overview

This 346-amino-acid protein is **very likely a pyrophosphate-dependent phosphofructokinase (PPi-PFK; EC 2.7.1.90)**. The assignment is supported by a Pfam PFK domain (PF00365, residues 5–301) and a full-length match to reviewed UniProt Q9KH71 at **88.2% identity and 100% coverage**.

PPi-PFK catalyzes the reversible reaction:

**Fructose 6-phosphate + pyrophosphate (PPi) ⇌ fructose 1,6-bisphosphate + phosphate**

This enzyme is associated with glycolysis and can also support the reverse gluconeogenic direction. The homolog annotation reports **Mg²⁺** as a cofactor and cytoplasmic localization. Those functional details are inferred from close homologs, not experimentally verified for the supplied sequence.
