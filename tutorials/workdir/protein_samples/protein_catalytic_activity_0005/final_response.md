## Functional identification

This 534-aa sequence is very likely a **CTP synthase (CTPS)**, an ATP-dependent amidoligase in pyrimidine nucleotide biosynthesis (EC 6.3.4.2). The assignment is supported by close reviewed Swiss-Prot homologs: about **60% sequence identity across more than 98% of the sequence**, plus the characteristic domain arrangement—an N-terminal CTP-synthase domain and a C-terminal glutamine amidotransferase domain.

## Catalytic activity and reaction

CTP synthase converts **UTP to CTP**, using ATP to drive the reaction. When glutamine supplies the nitrogen, the enzyme also hydrolyzes glutamine to glutamate and transfers the resulting ammonia to UTP:

**UTP + L-glutamine + ATP + H₂O → CTP + L-glutamate + ADP + phosphate + 2 H⁺**

It can also use free ammonium:

**UTP + NH₄⁺ + ATP → CTP + ADP + phosphate + 2 H⁺**

Thus, the enzyme couples glutamine hydrolysis (its glutaminase activity) to ATP-dependent amination of UTP. Its role is to supply CTP for nucleotide metabolism and RNA/DNA synthesis.
