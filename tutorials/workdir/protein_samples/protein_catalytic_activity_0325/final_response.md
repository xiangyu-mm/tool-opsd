### Functional assignment

The 512-aa protein is most consistent with **bifunctional NAD(P)H-hydrate repair enzyme Nnr**.

Evidence includes:

- Strong similarity to reviewed Nnr proteins: **40–49% identity over ~91–95% of the sequence**.
- Two characteristic domains:
  - **YjeF_N domain**: residues ~38–197
  - **C-terminal carbohydrate-kinase-like domain**: residues ~257–491
- Homologs carry EC assignments **5.1.99.6** and **4.2.1.136** and are annotated as potassium-dependent enzymes.

### Catalytic activities

The enzyme repairs hydrated NADH/NADPH intermediates, collectively called **NAD(P)HX**.

1. **NAD(P)HX epimerase**

It interconverts the R and S epimers:

\[
(6R)\text{-NADHX} \rightleftharpoons (6S)\text{-NADHX}
\]

and

\[
(6R)\text{-NADPHX} \rightleftharpoons (6S)\text{-NADPHX}
\]

2. **ADP-dependent NAD(P)H-hydrate dehydratase**

It converts the S epimer back to the functional reduced dinucleotide:

\[
(6S)\text{-NADHX} + ADP
\rightarrow NADH + AMP + P_i + H^+
\]

and

\[
(6S)\text{-NADPHX} + ADP
\rightarrow NADPH + AMP + P_i + H^+
\]

### Summary

This protein is likely an **NAD(P)H-hydrate repair enzyme** that prevents accumulation of damaged NADH/NADPH formed by spontaneous or enzyme-mediated hydration. It first epimerizes NAD(P)HX when necessary and then uses **ADP-dependent dehydration** to regenerate NADH or NADPH. The assignment is strongly supported by sequence architecture and curated homologs, but remains a homology-based prediction rather than direct biochemical characterization of this exact sequence.
