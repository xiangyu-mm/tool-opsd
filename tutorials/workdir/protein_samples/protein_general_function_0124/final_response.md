## Functional summary

The 148-amino-acid sequence is an **exact full-length match** to the reviewed *Arabidopsis thaliana* mitochondrial small-subunit ribosomal protein **uS7m** (UniProt **P92557**; 100% identity and coverage). The match and the Ribosomal_S7 domain (PF00177; residues 9–140) strongly support this identification.

uS7m is a **structural, rRNA-binding component of the mitochondrial small ribosomal subunit**. The reference annotation describes it as binding 18S rRNA and helping nucleate assembly of the small-subunit head; it is associated with ribosome function and translation. These functional details are based on the matched reference protein’s annotation.

**Sequence-derived characteristics:** 148 residues; estimated molecular mass **17.2 kDa**; theoretical pI **10.54**; predicted charge at pH 7 approximately **+15.8**. These estimates assume an unmodified protein.

No catalytic reaction or cofactor was reported. The evidence supports a **ribosomal structural role**, not an enzymatic one.
