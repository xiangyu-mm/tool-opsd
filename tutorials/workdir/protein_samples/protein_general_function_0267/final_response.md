## Functional summary

This 177-aa protein is most likely a **Ham1p-like dITP/XTP pyrophosphatase** (**EC 3.6.1.66**), a small bacterial nucleotide “house-cleaning” enzyme.

It likely hydrolyzes abnormal purine nucleoside triphosphates:

- **dITP + H₂O → dIMP + PPi**

By removing these noncanonical nucleotides, the enzyme may help prevent their incorporation into DNA or RNA and reduce mutation risk.
