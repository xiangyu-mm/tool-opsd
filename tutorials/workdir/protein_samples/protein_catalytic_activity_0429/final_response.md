## Functional identification

The 240-residue protein is **phosphoribosylaminoimidazole-succinocarboxamide synthetase**, commonly called **SAICAR synthetase** (**EC 6.3.2.6**).

This assignment is highly secure because:

- The sequence is an **exact 100% identity, 100% coverage match** to reviewed UniProt protein **C0R444**.
- It contains the characteristic **SAICAR_synt domain** (**Pfam PF01259**, residues 7–234).
- Matching reviewed proteins carry the same EC and Rhea reaction annotations.

## Catalyzed reaction

\[
\text{5-amino-1-(5-phospho-D-ribosyl)imidazole-4-carboxylate}
+ \text{L-aspartate} + \text{ATP}
\]

\[
\rightarrow
\text{SAICAR}
+ \text{ADP} + \text{P}_{i} + 2\text{H}^{+}
\]

More explicitly, the product is:

**(2S)-2-[5-amino-1-(5-phospho-β-D-ribosyl)imidazole-4-carboxamido]succinate**, also known as **SAICAR**.

This is an **ATP-dependent ligation reaction**: ATP supplies energy to join the carboxylated purine intermediate to the amino group of L-aspartate.

## Biological role

The enzyme performs the **first of two steps converting CAIR toward AICAR** in the **de novo IMP/purine biosynthesis pathway**:

\[
\text{CAIR} \rightarrow \text{SAICAR} \rightarrow \text{AICAR}
\]

The predicted protein is a compact, soluble cytosolic enzyme of approximately **27.7 kDa**. No additional cofactor requirement was supported by the retrieved annotations.
