### Functional summary

This 679-amino-acid protein is **EXO84, an exocyst complex component**. The assignment is highly reliable because the sequence is an **exact match to reviewed UniProt Q4HYZ2** from *Gibberella zeae*.

- **Size:** 76.5 kDa; predicted pI 9.54; strongly basic overall charge.
- **Architecture:** Low-complexity/disordered N-terminal regions, a coiled-coil region, and conserved EXO84-associated domains:
  - VPS51_Exo84_N: residues 129–213
  - PH_EXO84: residues 238–412
  - Exo84_C: residues 455–654
- **Primary function:** Structural component of the **exocyst**, an evolutionarily conserved vesicle-tethering complex.
- **Cellular role:** Helps assemble and polarize the exocyst at the plasma membrane, enabling tethering of secretory vesicles during exocytosis and Golgi-to-plasma-membrane trafficking.
- **Localization:** Cytoplasmic/secretory vesicles and the cell periphery; polarization is associated with actin cables in fungal annotations.
- **Biochemical nature:** No catalytic reaction, cofactor, or enzyme motif was identified. It is therefore best interpreted as a **nonenzymatic trafficking/scaffolding protein**, rather than a metabolic enzyme.
- **Additional annotation:** Possible involvement in spliceosome assembly is reported by similarity, but this is less firmly supported than its exocyst function.

Overall, the sequence represents a basic, partially disordered, interaction-rich **fungal EXO84 vesicle-trafficking scaffold**.
