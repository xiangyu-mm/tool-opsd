## Functional summary

This sequence is **HIV-1 envelope glycoprotein Env (gp160)**, supported by a strong match to reviewed HIV-1 Env references: **82.1% sequence identity across 100% of the query** to a subtype C reference. The sequence is most consistent with an HIV-1 Env protein; the match alone does not establish its exact isolate or subtype.

Env is made as a precursor that is processed by host furin-like proteases into **gp120** and **gp41**. In the mature viral spike, gp120 binds host **CD4** and promotes engagement of a chemokine coreceptor such as **CCR5 or CXCR4**. Gp41 is a class I fusion protein that helps drive fusion of viral and host-cell membranes, enabling viral entry. The reference annotation describes mature Env as a **trimer of gp120–gp41 pairs**.
