## Functional summary

The 255-amino-acid protein is **uracil-DNA glycosylase (UNG)** from **human herpesvirus 2**, matching UniProt **P28275 exactly**. This identification is supported by 100% sequence identity and full-length coverage.

### Molecular function
- **Enzyme:** Uracil-DNA glycosylase  
- **EC number:** **3.2.2.27**
- Removes uracil bases from DNA caused by:
  - Misincorporation of dUMP during DNA replication
  - Cytosine deamination
- The reaction initiates base-excision repair by cleaving the damaged base–sugar bond and releasing free uracil, leaving an abasic site for downstream repair enzymes.
