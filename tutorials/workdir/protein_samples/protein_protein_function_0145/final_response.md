## Molecular function

NAD kinase phosphorylates NAD⁺ using ATP:

\[
\mathrm{NAD^+ + ATP \rightarrow NADP^+ + ADP + H^+}
\]

The enzyme specifically phosphorylates the **2′-hydroxyl group of the adenosine moiety of NAD⁺**. It is annotated as binding:

- ATP  
- NAD⁺  
- A divalent metal cation, likely required for phosphate transfer  

## Cellular role

This enzyme maintains the intracellular balance between **NAD⁺ and NADP⁺** and is directly involved in **NADP⁺ biosynthesis**. NADP⁺ and NADPH support many cellular processes, including:

- Reductive biosynthesis  
- Antioxidant and redox-defense systems  
- Fatty-acid and other anabolic pathways  
- Maintenance of cellular redox balance  

The specific downstream pathways depend on the organism, but the central role of this protein is to supply the NADP⁺/NADPH pool.
