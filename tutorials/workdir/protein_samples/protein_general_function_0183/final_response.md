## Functional summary

- **Function:** Incorporated into nucleosomes, where it contributes to chromatin organization and regulation of DNA accessibility, transcription, chromosome stability, and heterochromatin/euchromatin state.
- **Localization:** Nuclear/chromosomal.
- **Sequence features:** Lysine-rich N-terminal tail, consistent with histone-DNA interaction and regulation by post-translational modifications.
- **Domains:** Histone domain **PF00125** and C-terminal H2A histone domain **PF16211**.
- **Estimated molecular mass:** 15.29 kDa.
- **Theoretical pI:** 10.35; predicted charge at pH 7: approximately **+15.8**, reflecting its strongly basic character.

Overall, this is a canonical fungal **H2A.Z chromatin histone**, not an enzyme or membrane protein.
