## Functional identification

The 353-residue protein is **MnmA**, also called **tRNA-specific 2-thiouridylase** or **tRNA-uridine 2-sulfurtransferase**.

The sequence is an **exact match** to the reviewed *Clostridium botulinum* MnmA protein UniProt **B1ILH4**:

## Molecular function

MnmA catalyzes sulfur insertion at the wobble uridine of tRNA:

\[
\text{uridine}_{34}\text{ in tRNA} \rightarrow \text{2-thiouridine}_{34}\text{ (s}^2\text{U34)}
\]

This is an ATP-dependent sulfur-transfer reaction involving a protein-bound sulfur intermediate. The resulting **s²U34 modification** changes the chemical properties of the tRNA anticodon.

## Cellular processes

The protein is involved in:

- **tRNA maturation and post-transcriptional modification**
- **Wobble-base modification**
- **Accurate and efficient decoding during translation**
- Maintenance of translation fidelity, particularly for codons decoded by tRNAs carrying the U34 modification

Thus, MnmA supports normal protein synthesis by chemically modifying selected tRNAs before or during their use by the ribosome.

## Subcellular location

The curated reference annotation places MnmA in the:

- **Cytoplasm**

This is consistent with its role in modifying cytoplasmic tRNAs in bacteria. No transmembrane segment or secretion signal is apparent from the sequence, supporting a soluble intracellular protein.

## Confidence and limitations

The functional assignment is **very strong** because the query sequence exactly matches a reviewed MnmA sequence, rather than relying only on remote homology. The Pfam results also identify regions compatible with tRNA-modifying enzyme architecture, although those domain calls should be treated as supporting evidence rather than the primary basis for annotation.

**Summary:** This is a soluble bacterial **MnmA tRNA thiouridylase** that modifies wobble uridine U34 to s²U34 in the cytoplasm, thereby contributing to tRNA maturation and accurate translation.
