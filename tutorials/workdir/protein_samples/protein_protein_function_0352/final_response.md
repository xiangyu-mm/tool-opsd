## Functional summary

The 157-amino-acid protein is **lipoprotein signal peptidase II (LspA; EC 3.4.23.36)**, a small bacterial membrane protease.

### Evidence

- The sequence is an **exact match** to reviewed UniProt protein **Q17VS8** from *Helicobacter acinonychis*.
- It has strong similarity to reviewed *Helicobacter pylori* LspA proteins:
  - ~90% identity over the full length
  - E-value ~10⁻⁹⁴
- It contains the characteristic **Peptidase_A8 domain (PF01252)** across most of the sequence.
- Its high hydrophobicity indicates a compact, integral membrane protein.

### Function

LspA cleaves the signal peptide from **bacterial prolipoproteins** after they have been translocated across the membrane. This produces mature bacterial lipoproteins, which can then be anchored to the membrane through their lipid-modified N-terminal cysteine.

This is a membrane-embedded proteolytic reaction and is distinct from the larger, soluble/peripheral bacterial signal peptidase I family.

### Subcellular localization

- **Bacterial inner/cytoplasmic membrane**
- **Multi-pass integral membrane protein**
- The hydrophobic sequence composition is consistent with several transmembrane helices surrounding the Peptidase_A8 catalytic region.

### Biological processes and pathways

The protein is involved in:

- **Lipoprotein biosynthesis**
- **Protein maturation/modification**
- **Signal-peptide cleavage**
- **Proteolysis**, specifically processing of prolipoproteins
- GO annotations include:
  - **GO:0006508 — proteolysis**
  - **GO:0004190 — aspartic-type endopeptidase activity**
  - **GO:0005886 — plasma membrane** in bacterial annotation systems

### Confidence

**Very high.** This assignment is supported not merely by general hydrophobicity or remote homology, but by an exact full-length match to a reviewed LspA sequence and a highly significant Peptidase_A8 domain match.
