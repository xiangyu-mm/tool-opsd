## Likely cellular role

The protein probably functions as a **membrane transport component involved in fungal pheromone-response signaling or mating-related physiology**. Its membrane domain could transport a small molecule, ion, or metabolite, or contribute to membrane homeostasis during pheromone-induced cellular remodeling.

The sequence is **more consistent with a transporter than with a conventional receptor**:

- It has a compact C-terminal multipass membrane region.
- It lacks an obvious large extracellular ligand-binding domain.
- It does not resemble a typical seven-transmembrane G-protein-coupled pheromone receptor.
- The long low-complexity N-terminal tail is more suggestive of regulation or protein interactions than direct ligand recognition.
