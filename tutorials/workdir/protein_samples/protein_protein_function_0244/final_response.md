## Functional summary

The 182-amino-acid sequence is an **exact sequence match** to reviewed rat **receptor activity-modifying protein 2 (RAMP2; UniProt Q9JHJ1)**. The analysis also identified the RAMP domain (Pfam PF04901) at residues 76–179.

RAMP2 is a membrane accessory protein that alters the function and trafficking of certain G-protein-coupled receptors (GPCRs). In particular, it helps transport **CALCRL** to the plasma membrane and partners with CALCRL to form an **adrenomedullin receptor complex**. With **CALCR**, it can contribute to calcitonin- and amylin-receptor complexes.

**Likely biological processes** include adrenomedullin and amylin receptor signaling, GPCR signaling that activates adenylate cyclase, and—among broader annotations—angiogenesis and basement-membrane assembly.

**Cellular location:** primarily a single-pass type I membrane protein associated with the cell membrane and plasma membrane, including the cell surface and signaling-receptor complexes. Additional annotations include cytoplasm; human RAMP2 is also annotated at clathrin-coated pits and lysosomes.

These functional and localization assignments are based on the exact match to curated rat RAMP2 and supporting homolog annotations. Not every listed process or compartment is necessarily active in every cell type.
