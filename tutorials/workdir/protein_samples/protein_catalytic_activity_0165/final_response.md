**Likely function:** a bifunctional NAD(P)H-hydrate repair enzyme, Nnr. The sequence is 46.6% identical to a reviewed Nnr protein across 96.5% of the query, so this assignment is supported by strong full-length homology, but has not been directly confirmed experimentally for this sequence.

It is annotated to catalyze two linked activities:

1. **Epimerization:** converts the R form of the damaged cofactor hydrate to the S form:
   - (6R)-NADHX ⇌ (6S)-NADHX
   - (6R)-NADPHX ⇌ (6S)-NADPHX

2. **ADP-dependent dehydration:** repairs the S form, regenerating the normal reduced cofactor:
   - (6S)-NADHX + ADP → NADH + AMP + phosphate + H⁺
   - (6S)-NADPHX + ADP → NADPH + AMP + phosphate + H⁺

Together, these activities help recover NADH and NADPH damaged by hydration. A reviewed homolog is annotated as binding K⁺, but that cofactor assignment is inferred for this query rather than directly established.
