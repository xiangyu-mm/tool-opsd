## Functional identification

The 407-residue protein is **aminoacylase-1 (ACY1)**, a **zinc-dependent M20/M28 metallohydrolase**.

This assignment is highly reliable: the sequence is an **exact full-length match** to curated porcine aminoacylase-1 (**UniProt P37111**), with 100% identity and coverage. It also contains the characteristic **Peptidase_M28, Peptidase_M20, and M20_dimer** domains.

### Catalytic activity

**EC 3.5.1.14 — aminoacylase activity**

The enzyme hydrolyzes the N-acyl bond of N-acylated amino acids:

\[
\text{N-acyl-L-amino acid} + H_2O
\rightarrow
\text{L-amino acid} + \text{carboxylate}
\]

Examples include:

\[
\text{N-acetyl-L-methionine} + H_2O
\rightarrow
\text{L-methionine} + \text{acetate}
\]

and

\[
\text{N-formyl-L-methionine} + H_2O
\rightarrow
\text{L-methionine} + \text{formate}
\]

Thus, the protein functions as an **N-acetylamino-acid hydrolase and N-formylamino-acid deformylase**, releasing free amino acids from their N-acyl derivatives.

### Biological role

ACY1 participates in degradation of N-acylated peptides and amino acids. In one proposed sequential pathway, **aminopeptidase activity first releases N-acylamino acids from N-acylated peptides**, after which ACY1 removes the N-acyl group to produce a free amino acid and acetate or formate.

**Overall conclusion:** This sequence encodes a **binuclear zinc aminoacylase-1 that catalyzes hydrolytic deacylation of N-acetylated and N-formylated amino acids**.
