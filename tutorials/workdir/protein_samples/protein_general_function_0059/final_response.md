## Most likely function

The protein is most consistent with **RlmN, a dual-specificity radical-SAM RNA methyltransferase**. Close reviewed homologs show approximately **46% sequence identity over 90–96% of the sequence**, supporting this assignment.

RlmN catalyzes C2 methylation of:

1. **A2503 in 23S rRNA**, producing m²A2503  
2. **A37 in tRNA**, producing m²A37  

The corresponding enzyme classification is **EC 2.1.1.192**.

## Cofactor and localization

- Expected cofactor: one **[4Fe–4S] cluster**, coordinated by three cysteines and an exchangeable SAM molecule
- Substrate/cofactor: **S-adenosyl-L-methionine (SAM)**
- Likely localization: **cytoplasm**, as expected for a bacterial RNA-modifying enzyme
- Likely molecular partners: rRNA and tRNA; oligomeric state cannot be determined reliably from this sequence alone

## Confidence and caveat

The domain architecture, radical-SAM cysteine motif, and strong similarity to reviewed RlmN proteins provide **moderate-to-strong homology-based support** for the annotation. However, the exact RNA substrates and catalytic activity remain predictions until experimentally verified for this particular sequence.
