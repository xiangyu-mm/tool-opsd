### Functional summary

The 508-residue protein is most consistent with **MurE**, the bacterial **UDP-N-acetylmuramoyl-L-alanyl-D-glutamate—meso-diaminopimelate ligase** (**EC 6.3.2.13**).

- **Predicted size:** 54.6 kDa; theoretical pI 6.32  
- **Family/domain architecture:** Contains the characteristic three-part Mur ligase architecture:
  - Mur_ligase domain, PF01225
  - Mur_ligase_M domain, PF08245
  - Mur_ligase_C domain, PF02875
- **Reaction:**  
  UDP-MurNAc-L-Ala-D-Glu + meso-DAP + ATP → UDP-MurNAc-L-Ala-γ-D-Glu-meso-DAP + ADP + phosphate
- **Cofactor:** Likely requires **Mg²⁺** and binds ATP.
- **Cellular role:** Cytosolic enzyme in **peptidoglycan biosynthesis**, contributing to bacterial cell-wall construction, cell division, and maintenance of cell shape.
- **Evidence strength:** The assignment is supported by approximately **45–46% sequence identity over 93–97% of the sequence** to reviewed MurE proteins, with highly significant E-values, as well as the matching domain organization.

Overall, this is very likely a **soluble bacterial MurE ligase involved in incorporating meso-diaminopimelate into the peptidoglycan precursor pathway**. Exact substrate specificity should still be regarded as inferred rather than experimentally confirmed for this particular sequence.
