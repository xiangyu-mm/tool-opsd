### Functional identification

The 404-residue protein is strongly supported as a **bifunctional IspD/IspF enzyme** in the bacterial methylerythritol phosphate (MEP) pathway for isoprenoid biosynthesis.

This assignment is supported by:

- 70.6–86.7% identity to reviewed IspD/IspF homologs
- Near-complete alignment coverage
- An N-terminal **IspD domain** (Pfam PF01128)
- A C-terminal **YgbB/IspF-related domain** (Pfam PF02542)

### Catalytic activities

The protein catalyzes two sequential reactions:

1. **IspD activity — MEP cytidylyltransferase**  
   
   \[
   \text{2-C-methyl-D-erythritol 4-phosphate} + \text{CTP} + H^+
   \rightarrow
   \text{4-CDP-2-C-methyl-D-erythritol} + \text{diphosphate}
   \]

   - EC: **2.7.7.60**
   - Rhea: **RHEA:13429**
   - This is step 2 of the MEP pathway.

2. **IspF activity — ME-cPP synthase**  
   
   \[
   \text{4-CDP-2-C-methyl-D-erythritol 2-phosphate}
   \rightarrow
   \text{2-C-methyl-D-erythritol 2,4-cyclic diphosphate} + \text{CMP}
   \]

   - EC: **4.6.1.12**
   - Rhea: **RHEA:23864**
   - This is step 4 of the MEP pathway.

### Cofactor

The homologous enzyme annotation indicates dependence on a **divalent metal cation**, but the exact metal ion cannot be determined reliably from this sequence analysis alone.

### Summary

This protein is best described as a **bifunctional 2-C-methyl-D-erythritol 4-phosphate cytidylyltransferase/2-C-methyl-D-erythritol 2,4-cyclodiphosphate synthase**, catalyzing two reactions in bacterial isoprenoid precursor biosynthesis.
