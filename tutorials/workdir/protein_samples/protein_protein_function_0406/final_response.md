## Biological role

TpPycTM is part of a two-component **Pycsar antiphage system**:

1. The adjacent PycC pyrimidine/uridylate cyclase produces cyclic-nucleotide second messengers, probably **cUMP** for this system.
2. The cyclic nucleotide activates TpPycTM.
3. The effector disrupts **bacterial inner-membrane integrity**, leading to bacterial death.
4. This aborts phage replication and protects the population from bacteriophage infection.

### Biological processes associated with the protein

- Antiphage defense
- Pyrimidine cyclase signaling
- Abortive phage infection
- Membrane-integrity disruption
- Programmed bacterial cell death during phage attack

**Conclusion:** This is not a conventional transporter or housekeeping membrane protein. It is a small, multi-pass inner-membrane **suicide/effector protein in a cyclic-nucleotide-triggered bacterial antiphage defense system**.
