## Functional identification

The 334-residue protein is **glyoxylate reductase**, corresponding exactly to reviewed *Pyrococcus horikoshii* protein **UniProt O58320**. The sequence is an exact hash match, providing stronger evidence than ordinary homology-based annotation.

- **EC number:** EC 1.1.1.26  
- **Likely cellular location:** Cytosol  
- **Oligomeric state:** Homodimer  
- **Cofactor:** NADH and/or NADPH  
- **Protein size:** ~38.0 kDa  

## Catalytic reaction

The enzyme catalyzes the reversible NAD(P)H-dependent reduction of glyoxylate to glycolate:

\[
\text{glyoxylate} + \text{NAD(P)H} + \mathrm{H^+}
\rightleftharpoons
\text{glycolate} + \text{NAD(P)^+}
\]

The reaction is also represented in the oxidative direction as:

\[
\text{glycolate} + \text{NAD}^+
\rightarrow
\text{glyoxylate} + \text{NADH} + \mathrm{H^+}
\]

This is a **two-electron redox reaction** in which the aldehyde group of glyoxylate is reduced to the primary alcohol of glycolate.

## Sequence support

The assignment is supported by:

- Exact sequence identity to reviewed glyoxylate reductase O58320.
- Highly significant matches to homologs from *Pyrococcus abyssi* and *Pyrococcus furiosus*.
- Pfam domains characteristic of **2-hydroxyacid dehydrogenases**, including:
  - `2-Hacid_dh`
  - `2-Hacid_dh_C`
  - NAD-binding-associated regions
- Curated annotations for NAD binding, glyoxylate reductase activity, and hydroxypyruvate reductase activity.

The protein may also reduce **hydroxypyruvate to glycerate** using NAD(P)H, but the best-supported primary assignment is **glyoxylate reductase**.
