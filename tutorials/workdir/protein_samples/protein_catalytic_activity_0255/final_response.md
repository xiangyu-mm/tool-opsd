## Functional identification

The protein is **phosphatidylserine decarboxylase proenzyme** (**EC 4.1.1.65**), also known as **Psd**.

The sequence is an **exact 100% identity and coverage match** to reviewed UniProt protein **Q2NW88**, providing very strong evidence for this assignment.

## Catalytic reaction

\[
\text{Phosphatidylserine} + H^+
\rightarrow
\text{Phosphatidylethanolamine} + CO_2
\]

More specifically:

\[
\text{1,2-diacyl-sn-glycero-3-phospho-L-serine}
\rightarrow
\text{1,2-diacyl-sn-glycero-3-phosphoethanolamine} + CO_2
\]

Thus, the enzyme **decarboxylates phosphatidylserine to produce phosphatidylethanolamine**, an essential membrane phospholipid.

The enzyme uses a **covalently bound pyruvoyl group** as its catalytic cofactor. The precursor is generally processed to generate the active pyruvoyl-containing enzyme form.
