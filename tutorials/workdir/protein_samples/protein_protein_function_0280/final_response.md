## Functional summary

This is **succinyl-diaminopimelate desuccinylase** (EC **3.5.1.18**), a metal-dependent hydrolase in the bacterial **lysine biosynthesis pathway**.

It catalyzes removal of the succinyl group from **N-succinyl-L,L-diaminopimelate (SDAP)**, producing **LL-2,6-diaminopimelate (DAP)** and succinate. DAP is a precursor for lysine and an important component of bacterial cell-wall peptidoglycan.

The identification is especially strong: the sequence is an exact, full-length match to a reviewed UniProt reference protein, and it also has a highly significant **Peptidase_M20** domain match. Related reviewed homologs support the same function and pathway. The reference annotations report binding of **Zn²⁺ or Co²⁺**; these are annotation-based cofactor assignments, not measurements made on this sequence.

**Likely biological process:** the succinylase route of bacterial DAP/lysine biosynthesis, contributing to lysine production and cell-wall precursor synthesis.
