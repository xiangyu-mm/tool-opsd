### Functional summary

The sequence is **SecA**, the ATP-driven motor component of the bacterial Sec protein-translocation system. It couples ATP hydrolysis to the movement of precursor proteins through the SecYEG channel and across the inner membrane. This assignment is strongly supported by a **100% identity, full-query-coverage match** to reviewed SecA UniProt entry B3EJ06, as well as characteristic SecA domains.
