## Identification and functional summary

The **409-amino-acid sequence is an exact full-length match** to reviewed *Nicotiana tabacum* **Shaggy-related protein kinase NtK-1** (UniProt **Q40518**). This supports the identification more strongly than a similarity-only match.

- **Function:** An ATP-dependent **serine/threonine protein kinase** (EC 2.7.11.1), which transfers phosphate from ATP to protein serine or threonine residues. Its annotation suggests a role in relaying extracellular signals that regulate transcription during cell differentiation.
- **Subcellular localization:** Annotated in the **cytoplasm and nucleus**.
- **Biological processes:** The exact-match annotation associates it with **signal transduction** and **cell differentiation**.

Closely related Arabidopsis Shaggy-like kinases have annotations involving meristem organization and salt-stress responses, but those paralog-specific findings should **not** be taken as established roles for NtK-1 itself. The evidence supports the protein’s identity and kinase class; it does not establish its specific substrates or detailed signaling mechanism.
