## Functional prediction

This 199-amino-acid protein is a **small FMN-dependent NAD(P)H:quinone dehydrogenase**, most likely an **NAD(P)H dehydrogenase (quinone), EC 1.6.5.2**.

### Evidence

- **Exact sequence match** to reviewed UniProt protein **Q0A961** from *Alkalilimnicola ehrlichii*.
- Predicted **FMN_red** domain spanning approximately residues 3–142.
- Overlapping **flavodoxin-like domain** spanning approximately residues 6–136, consistent with a flavin-mediated electron-transfer fold.
- Homologous annotated proteins bind **one FMN cofactor per monomer**.
- The reference enzyme catalyzes reduction of quinones using either NADH or NADPH:

\[
\text{quinone} + \text{NAD(P)H} + H^+ \rightarrow \text{quinol} + \text{NAD(P)}^+
\]

### Structural interpretation

The protein is compact—approximately **21.2 kDa**—and lacks a long hydrophobic transmembrane segment. Its flavodoxin-like/FMΝ-binding architecture is consistent with a soluble or peripheral redox enzyme that transfers reducing equivalents from NAD(P)H to a quinone substrate through FMN.

### Functional summary

**High-confidence assignment:** an FMN-containing, non-electrogenic NAD(P)H dehydrogenase that reduces quinones to quinols. The exact quinone substrate and whether NADH or NADPH is preferred cannot be determined from this sequence alone. UniProt annotations associate related proteins with membranes, but the sequence itself does not strongly support an integral membrane protein.
