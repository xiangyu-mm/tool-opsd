## Functional assignment

The 309-residue protein is **ribokinase** (**EC 2.7.1.15**), a member of the **PfkB carbohydrate-kinase family**.

This assignment is highly confident because the sequence is an **exact full-length match** to reviewed *E. coli* ribokinases P0A9J6/P0A9J7. It also contains the expected Pfam domains:

- **PfkB domain:** residues 6–297  
- **Phos_pyr_kin domain:** residues 165–287  

The protein is predicted to be a soluble cytosolic enzyme, likely functioning as a homodimer.

## Catalytic activity

Ribokinase transfers the γ-phosphoryl group of ATP to the 5′ hydroxyl group of **D-ribose**, producing ribose-5-phosphate:

\[
\text{D-ribose} + \text{ATP}
\rightarrow
\text{D-ribose-5-phosphate} + \text{ADP} + \text{H}^{+}
\]

This reaction is recorded as **Rhea RHEA:13697**.

## Cofactor and mechanism

The reaction requires a divalent metal ion, primarily **Mg²⁺**. Magnesium coordinates ATP and helps stabilize the negative charge that develops during phosphoryl transfer. **Mn²⁺** may also support activity, based on the reference annotation.

The product, **D-ribose-5-phosphate**, enters pathways for:

- Nucleotide biosynthesis
- Histidine and tryptophan biosynthesis
- Pentose-phosphate metabolism
- D-ribose catabolism

## Concise summary

This sequence encodes a **cytosolic ribokinase** that uses **Mg²⁺-ATP to phosphorylate D-ribose at O-5**, yielding **D-ribose-5-phosphate and ADP**. The identification is supported by an exact sequence match to curated *E. coli* ribokinase and by the characteristic PfkB-family domain architecture.
