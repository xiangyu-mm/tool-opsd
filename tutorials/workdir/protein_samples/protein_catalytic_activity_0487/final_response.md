## Functional identification

The 394-aa protein is most consistent with **RlmN**, a **dual-specificity radical-SAM RNA methyltransferase** (**EC 2.1.1.192**).

### Catalytic activity

It methylates the **C2 position of adenine** in either:

- **A2503 of 23S rRNA**, producing 2-methyladenosine-2503; or
- **A37 of tRNA**, producing 2-methyladenosine-37.

A simplified reaction is:

\[
\text{RNA-adenosine} + \text{S-adenosyl-L-methionine}
\rightarrow
\text{RNA-2-methyladenosine} + \text{S-adenosyl-L-homocysteine}
\]

Because RlmN uses radical-SAM chemistry, the formal reaction annotation also includes a second SAM molecule, formation of **5′-deoxyadenosine**, and electron transfer through reduced ferredoxin:

\[
\text{RNA-A} + 2\,\text{SAM} + \text{reducing equivalents}
\rightarrow
\text{RNA-2-methyl-A} + 5′\text{-deoxyadenosine}
+ \text{methionine} + \text{SAH}
\]

### Supporting sequence evidence

- Strong similarity to reviewed RlmN proteins: approximately **64% identity over 93–95% of the sequence**.
- Contains a predicted **Radical_SAM domain** spanning residues approximately **142–321**.
- Contains a characteristic cysteine-rich **CxxxCxxC motif**, consistent with binding a catalytic **[4Fe–4S] cluster**.
- The predicted protein size and basic, soluble composition are compatible with a bacterial cytoplasmic RNA-modifying enzyme.

### Confidence

**Moderate-to-high assignment by homology.** The sequence strongly supports RlmN, but the exact substrate preference—23S rRNA A2503, tRNA A37, or both—has not been experimentally demonstrated for this particular sequence.
