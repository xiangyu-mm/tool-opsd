### Functional summary

The 434-aa sequence is an **exact full-length match** to reviewed *Bacillus anthracis* protein Q81K13, **UDP-N-acetylglucosamine 1-carboxyvinyltransferase 1** (MurA; EC 2.5.1.7). This supports a high-confidence functional assignment.

MurA catalyzes the transfer of an enolpyruvyl group from phosphoenolpyruvate (PEP) to UDP-N-acetylglucosamine, producing UDP-N-acetyl-3-O-(1-carboxyvinyl)-glucosamine and phosphate. This is an early step in **peptidoglycan biosynthesis**, so the enzyme contributes to bacterial cell-wall formation.

The reference protein is annotated as **cytoplasmic**. It is also associated with UDP-N-acetylgalactosamine biosynthesis in its GO annotations; the clearest supported role for this sequence is its function in peptidoglycan precursor synthesis.
