### Functional assignment

The sequence encodes **L-lactate dehydrogenase 3** (**EC 1.1.1.27**), a cytoplasmic, NAD-dependent oxidoreductase.

### Catalyzed reaction

\[
\text{(S)-lactate} + \text{NAD}^+
\rightleftharpoons
\text{pyruvate} + \text{NADH} + \text{H}^+
\]

In the physiologically relevant fermentation direction, it commonly reduces pyruvate:

\[
\text{pyruvate} + \text{NADH} + \text{H}^+
\rightarrow
\text{(S)-lactate} + \text{NAD}^+
\]

### Evidence

- The 316-residue sequence is an **exact full-length match** to reviewed UniProt L-lactate dehydrogenase 3 Q81XJ7.
- Closely related reviewed bacterial proteins show the same annotation and **EC 1.1.1.27**.
- The sequence contains the expected lactate dehydrogenase domains:
  - **Ldh_1_N**: PF00056
  - **Ldh_1_C**: PF02866
- The mapped reaction is **Rhea RHEA:23444**.

Thus, this enzyme promotes the reversible NAD⁺/NADH-dependent interconversion of **L-lactate and pyruvate**, supporting bacterial lactate fermentation.
