### Most likely localization
**Integral membrane protein, most likely localized to the plasma membrane/cell surface.**

### Functional summary
The 317-residue protein is a **seven-transmembrane serpentine receptor**, a nematode GPCR-like membrane receptor. Its sequence is highly hydrophobic and contains multiple membrane-spanning segments, consistent with an integral membrane topology rather than a soluble or secreted protein.

The identification is strong:

- It is an **exact sequence match** to *C. elegans* serpentine receptor class delta-44 (UniProt **O17823**).
- It has a highly significant **7TM_GPCR_Srd** Pfam domain match spanning approximately residues 6–293.
- Related *C. elegans* serpentine receptors are also annotated as multi-pass membrane proteins.

Thus, the protein likely functions as a **cell-surface signal receptor**, transmitting extracellular chemical or sensory signals across the membrane through its seven-transmembrane architecture. The exact ligand, signaling pathway, and tissue specificity cannot be established from this sequence alone.
