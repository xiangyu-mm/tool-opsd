**Identification:** The 344-aa sequence is an exact full-length match to reviewed UniProt protein **Q38WL5, MetN** from *Latilactobacillus sakei* subsp. *sakei*. This supports a strong identification as the ATP-binding subunit of the **MetNIQ ABC methionine-import system**.

- **Likely role:** MetN couples ATP hydrolysis to methionine uptake. The ABC transporter complex is annotated with MetN ATPase subunits, MetI membrane subunits, and the MetQ substrate-binding protein. MetN is peripheral to the membrane rather than itself a transmembrane transporter.
- **Sequence features:** It contains the Walker A/P-loop motif `GYSGAGKST` and an ABC transporter domain (PF00005; residues 21–169), consistent with ATP binding and hydrolysis. A C-terminal NIL domain is also detected (residues 267–340).

- **Limits:** This is a sequence-based annotation anchored by an exact reference match, not a new experimental assay. No specific non-ATP cofactor is established by the retrieved evidence.
