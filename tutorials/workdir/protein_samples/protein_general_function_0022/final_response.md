### Ligands and signaling

The receptor is annotated as responding to:

- **Chemerin/RARRES2**, a chemoattractant adipokine
- **TAFA1**, also called FAM19A1

Reported signaling characteristics include:

- Predominantly **β-arrestin-mediated signaling**
- Weaker or context-dependent activation of heterotrimeric G proteins
- Calcium mobilization
- MAPK/ERK activation
- ROCK/ERK and ROCK/STAT3 signaling in response to TAFA1

The receptor is also annotated in human as an **HIV-1 coreceptor**.

### Cellular localization and biological roles

Expected localization is the **plasma membrane**. The receptor is associated with:

- GPCR signaling
- Neuropeptide/adipokine signaling
- Regulation of inflammatory responses
- Glucose homeostasis
- Calcium signaling
- Neuronal projection-related functions

Ligand activation is associated with receptor internalization, consistent with the presence of a cytoplasmic regulatory tail.
