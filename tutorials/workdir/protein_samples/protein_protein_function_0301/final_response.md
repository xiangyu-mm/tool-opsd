## Functional assessment

**Most likely identity:** **Shu2 (Suppressor of hydroxyurea sensitivity protein 2)**, a fungal DNA-repair factor.

### Evidence

- **Length:** 251 amino acids; predicted molecular mass ~28.5 kDa.
- **Exact sequence match:** The sequence is identical across its full length to the reviewed *Kluyveromyces lactis* Shu2 protein, UniProt **B4UNA2**.
- **Domain:** Contains a near-full-length **SHU2 domain** (Pfam **PF29629**, residues 12–246).
- **Conserved homologs:** Related fungal Shu2 proteins show ~35–38% identity over nearly the entire sequence and are annotated as nuclear DNA-repair proteins.
- **Cellular localization:** Most likely **nuclear**, based on the exact reviewed ortholog and conserved fungal homologs.

### Likely biological role

Shu2 is a component of the fungal **Shu complex**, which supports **RAD51/RAD54-dependent homologous recombination repair**. It is involved particularly in error-free repair of DNA lesions arising during S phase, including lesions caused by methyl methanesulfonate and hydroxyurea-associated replication stress. Its role is likely to be **regulatory or structural within the recombination-repair machinery**, rather than direct DNA catalysis.

### Interpretation

This protein is **not likely to be a metabolic enzyme**. The sequence and domain evidence instead support a conserved, soluble DNA-repair accessory protein. The functional assignment is **high confidence**, because of the exact full-length match to a reviewed Shu2 protein; however, the precise molecular interactions and mechanism cannot be determined from sequence alone.
