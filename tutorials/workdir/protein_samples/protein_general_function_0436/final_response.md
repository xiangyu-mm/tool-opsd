### Functional summary

This 679-aa protein is most consistent with **FtsH, an ATP-dependent zinc metalloprotease** involved in membrane-protein quality control and regulated proteolysis.

- **Size/properties:** 73.5 kDa; predicted pI ~5.0; acidic overall charge at neutral pH.
- **Membrane topology:** Two strong N-terminal hydrophobic segments, consistent with the characteristic **two-pass membrane anchor** of FtsH. The large catalytic domain is predicted to face the cytoplasm. The first hydrophobic segment is therefore more likely a transmembrane anchor than a cleavable secretory signal peptide.
- **ATPase domain:** Contains an AAA+ ATPase module with the Walker A motif **GPPGTGKT** around residues 220–230.
- **Protease domain:** Contains a Peptidase_M41/FtsH domain with the characteristic zinc-binding **HEXXH-like motif** (`HEGGH`) and the downstream catalytic-region motifs expected for FtsH metalloproteases.
- **Architecture:** N-terminal membrane anchor → cytosolic AAA+ ATPase → zinc metalloprotease domain → proline/serine-rich C-terminal tail.
- **Likely activity:** ATP-powered unfolding/translocation and degradation of selected membrane and cytosolic proteins, including misfolded membrane proteins.
- **Assembly:** Homologous FtsH proteins form **hexameric complexes**, making a similar oligomeric state likely here.
- **Evidence strength:** Strong; reviewed FtsH homologs share approximately **58–70% sequence identity over 91–93% of the sequence**, and the domain organization is concordant.

**Overall:** a membrane-anchored, cytosolic-facing **FtsH AAA+ zinc protease**, probably functioning as a hexameric protein-quality-control protease. Exact substrate specificity and the role of the unusual C-terminal low-complexity tail cannot be determined from sequence alone.
