## Catalytic activity

PdxJ catalyzes the final ring-closure step in the de novo biosynthesis of vitamin B6. It combines two acyclic phosphorylated precursors:

- **1-deoxy-D-xylulose 5-phosphate (DXP)**
- **3-amino-2-oxopropyl phosphate**, also called **1-aminoacetone 3-phosphate (AAP)**

The products are **pyridoxine 5′-phosphate (PNP)** and inorganic phosphate.

### Overall reaction

\[
\text{3-amino-2-oxopropyl phosphate}
+

\rightarrow
\text{pyridoxine 5′-phosphate}

\text{phosphate}
+
2\mathrm{H_2O}
+
\mathrm{H^+}
\]

This is a complex **phospho-transfer/ring-closure reaction** that constructs the pyridine ring of pyridoxine 5′-phosphate.
