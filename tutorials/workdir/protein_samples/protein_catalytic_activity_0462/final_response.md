### Functional assignment

The sequence encodes **RlmN**, a **dual-specificity radical-SAM RNA methyltransferase** (**EC 2.1.1.192**).

Evidence is very strong: the 386-residue sequence is an exact full-length match to a reviewed RlmN protein, and it contains a **Radical_SAM domain** with the cysteine-rich region required to bind a catalytic **[4Fe–4S] cluster**.

### Catalytic reaction

RlmN methylates the **C2 position of adenine** in two RNA substrates:

1. **A2503 in 23S rRNA**  
   \[
   \text{23S rRNA-A2503} \rightarrow \text{2-methyladenosine-A2503}
   \]

2. **A37 in tRNA**  
   \[
   \text{tRNA-A37} \rightarrow \text{2-methyladenosine-A37}
   \]

The radical-SAM mechanism uses **S-adenosyl-L-methionine (SAM)** and a reducing system. The mapped net reaction produces **5′-deoxyadenosine**, **L-methionine**, and **S-adenosyl-L-homocysteine**, with the [4Fe–4S] cluster activating SAM to generate the 5′-deoxyadenosyl radical.

### Summary

**This is a cytoplasmic [4Fe–4S]-dependent radical-SAM enzyme that installs 2-methyladenosine in both 23S rRNA and tRNA, modifying rRNA A2503 and tRNA A37.**
