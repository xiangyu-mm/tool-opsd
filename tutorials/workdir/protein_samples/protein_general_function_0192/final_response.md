## Protein overview

The 194-amino-acid sequence is an **exact match** to reviewed UniProt protein Q1QCZ9 from *Psychrobacter cryohalolentis*, annotated as **RuvC crossover junction endodeoxyribonuclease**.

RuvC is a **Mg²⁺-dependent endonuclease** involved in DNA recombination and repair. Together with RuvA and RuvB, it resolves Holliday-junction DNA intermediates by making paired, symmetrical cuts in the junction strands. The matching reference annotation reports two Mg²⁺ ions bound per subunit.

**Sequence features:** predicted molecular mass ~21.3 kDa; theoretical pI ~8.33. A RuvC domain is detected at residues 4–155 (Pfam PF02075). The protein is annotated as cytoplasmic.

The functional assignment is especially strong because the sequence exactly matches a reviewed reference; the detailed cleavage behavior and cofactor annotation come from that reference and should not be mistaken for a new experiment on this sequence.
