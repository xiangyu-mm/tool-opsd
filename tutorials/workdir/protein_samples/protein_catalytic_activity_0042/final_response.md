## Functional summary

This 768-aa sequence is **glycine dehydrogenase (decarboxylating), the P protein of the glycine cleavage system** (EC **1.4.4.2**). The strongest sequence hit is a reviewed *Pectobacterium carotovorum* homolog with **100% identity and 100% query coverage**; the matching GDC-P domain also supports this assignment. The annotation is based on reference-protein evidence, not a direct activity assay of the supplied sequence.

The enzyme uses **pyridoxal 5′-phosphate (PLP)** to decarboxylate glycine and transfer the remaining aminomethyl group to the lipoyl arm of the glycine-cleavage H protein:

\[
\text{lipoyl-H protein} + \text{glycine} + \mathrm{H^+}
\rightarrow
\text{aminomethyl-dihydrolipoyl-H protein} + \mathrm{CO_2}
\]

Thus, it performs the glycine-cleavage system’s **P-protein step**: CO₂ is released, while the glycine-derived aminomethyl group is passed to H protein for subsequent processing. It is not, by itself, the entire glycine-cleavage system.
