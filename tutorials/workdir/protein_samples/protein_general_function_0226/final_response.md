### Likely function

The protein most likely catalyzes the sequential, **S-adenosyl-L-methionine-dependent methylation** of phosphatidylethanolamine:

\[
\text{PE} \rightarrow \text{PMME} \rightarrow \text{PDME} \rightarrow \text{phosphatidylcholine}
\]

where:

- PE = phosphatidylethanolamine  
- PMME = phosphatidylmonomethylethanolamine  
- PDME = phosphatidyldimethylethanolamine  

This reaction contributes to **phosphatidylcholine biosynthesis** and membrane lipid homeostasis.

### Expected localization

Based on the homologs, the protein is likely localized to an intracellular membrane, particularly:

- **Endoplasmic-reticulum membrane**
- Potentially also the **mitochondrial membrane**

Because the source organism is not specified, these localization assignments should be regarded as homology-based rather than definitive.

### Overall interpretation

This is a small, basic, hydrophobic membrane enzyme strongly resembling **PEMT family proteins**. Its sequence architecture and highly significant PEMT-domain match support a role in membrane phospholipid methylation, most likely phosphatidylethanolamine-to-phosphatidylcholine conversion. The exact number of transmembrane helices, topology, oligomeric state, and catalytic properties cannot be established reliably from this analysis alone.
