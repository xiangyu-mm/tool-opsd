## Molecular function

bL17 is a **structural component of the bacterial 50S ribosomal subunit**. It helps form and stabilize the large ribosomal subunit and contacts **ribosomal protein L32**.

The strongly basic character of the sequence is characteristic of ribosomal proteins:

- Predicted pI: **10.59**
- Estimated charge at pH 7: approximately **+13**
- The basic N-terminal region is well suited for interactions with negatively charged **ribosomal RNA**

This protein is not expected to be an enzyme and has no evidence for catalytic activity.

It may also contribute indirectly to ribosome assembly or stability, but the available annotations directly support its role as a ribosomal structural protein rather than assigning a specific assembly function.

- **Bacterial cytosol**

Its sequence contains no obvious long hydrophobic segment suggestive of a transmembrane helix or signal peptide, consistent with a soluble intracellular ribosomal protein.
