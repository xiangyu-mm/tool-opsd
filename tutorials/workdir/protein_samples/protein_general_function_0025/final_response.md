### Functional summary

This 203-amino-acid protein is most likely a **Ham1p-family dITP/XTP pyrophosphatase** (**EC 3.6.1.66**), a nucleotide “house-cleaning” enzyme that protects genome integrity.

- **Strong evidence:** 72.8% identity and ~99.5% coverage to the reviewed *Ruegeria pomeroyi* dITP/XTP pyrophosphatase Q5LWF7, with additional high-identity bacterial homologs.
- **Domain:** Ham1p-like domain (Pfam **PF01725**) spanning nearly the full sequence.
- **Likely activity:** Mg²⁺-dependent hydrolysis of noncanonical purine nucleotides:
  - dITP → dIMP + pyrophosphate
  - ITP → IMP + pyrophosphate
  - XTP → XMP + pyrophosphate
- **Biological role:** Removes abnormal purine nucleotides from the cellular pool, reducing their incorporation into DNA or RNA.
- **Likely properties:** Approximately **22.1 kDa**, predicted acidic (**pI ~5.39**), soluble/cytosolic, and probably homodimeric based on close homologs.

The functional assignment is **high confidence from sequence homology and domain conservation**, although the exact substrate preference, oligomeric state, and Mg²⁺ binding have not been experimentally tested for this particular sequence.
