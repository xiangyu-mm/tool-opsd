## Functional identification

The protein is a **CdaS-type diadenylate cyclase** (cyclic di-AMP synthase), an enzyme that produces the bacterial second messenger **cyclic di-AMP (c-di-AMP)**.

Evidence is very strong: the 201-residue sequence is an **exact full-length match** to the reviewed *Bacillus thuringiensis* CdaS protein (UniProt **D5TM67**). It also contains the characteristic:

- **CdaS_N domain**: residues 1–69  
- **DAC catalytic domain**: residues 78–194  

## Catalyzed reaction

\[
\boxed{2\ ATP \rightarrow 3',3'\text{-cyclic di-AMP} + 2\ PP_i}
\]

In words, the enzyme condenses two ATP molecules to form one molecule of **3′,3′-cyclic di-AMP**, releasing two molecules of pyrophosphate.

- **EC number:** 2.7.7.85  
- **Rhea reaction:** RHEA:35655  
- **Cofactor:** Mg²⁺, which coordinates ATP phosphate groups during catalysis  

## Functional summary

This is a small, likely soluble **diadenylate cyclase** involved in synthesis of c-di-AMP, a signaling nucleotide that regulates processes such as bacterial cell-wall homeostasis, growth, and sporulation. The matched CdaS annotation also reports slower activity with ADP and possible weak ADPase activity, but the principal catalytic function is **ATP-dependent c-di-AMP synthesis**.
