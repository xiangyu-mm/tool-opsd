## Functional identification

The 340-residue protein is an **ATP-dependent 6-phosphofructokinase** (**PFK; EC 2.7.1.11**).

The sequence is an **exact full-length match** to the reviewed *Lactococcus lactis* protein P0DOB6, with 100% sequence identity and coverage, providing strong evidence for this assignment.

## Catalyzed reaction

\[
\text{D-fructose 6-phosphate} + \text{ATP}
\rightarrow
\text{D-fructose 1,6-bisphosphate} + \text{ADP} + \text{H}^+
\]

The enzyme transfers the terminal phosphate of ATP to fructose 6-phosphate. This is the **first committed and regulatory step of glycolysis**.
