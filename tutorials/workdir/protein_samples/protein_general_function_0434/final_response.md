### Functional interpretation

This protein is a small, acidic, likely **coiled-coil cell-division factor**. ZapB participates in bacterial cytokinesis by interacting with **FtsZ** and helping organize or stabilize the FtsZ Z-ring at mid-cell. It is recruited to the divisome through an FtsA- or ZipA-dependent process and is associated with **cell septum assembly** and **FtsZ-dependent cytokinesis**.

The sequence’s high abundance of glutamate, aspartate, glutamine, and lysine, together with its hydrophilic character and Pfam assignment, is consistent with an extended helical/coiled-coil protein rather than a globular enzyme. There is no indication of a transmembrane segment or catalytic domain.

**Overall conclusion:** This sequence is confidently identified as **ZapB**, a small soluble bacterial divisome-associated protein involved in FtsZ-ring organization and cytokinesis.
