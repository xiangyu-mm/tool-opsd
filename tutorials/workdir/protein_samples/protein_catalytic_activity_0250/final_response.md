## Functional summary

The 432-amino-acid sequence is an **exact match** to reviewed rat testis-specific glyceraldehyde-3-phosphate dehydrogenase (GAPDH; UniProt **Q9ESV6**). The reference annotation assigns **EC 1.2.1.12**. Mouse and human testis-specific GAPDH are also strong full-length homologs.

GAPDH catalyzes the reversible oxidation and phosphorylation of glyceraldehyde-3-phosphate in glycolysis:

**D-glyceraldehyde 3-phosphate + inorganic phosphate + NAD⁺ ⇌ 1,3-bisphosphoglycerate + NADH + H⁺**

The product is also called **3-phospho-D-glyceroyl phosphate**. In glycolysis, this step captures oxidation energy in the high-energy phosphate bond of 1,3-bisphosphoglycerate while reducing NAD⁺ to NADH. The reaction is reversible and can run in the opposite direction during gluconeogenesis.

The sequence-level identification is an exact reference match; the retrieved UniProt functional wording describes the activity “by similarity,” so this supports the enzyme assignment but is not, by itself, a direct experimental assay of this particular sequence.
