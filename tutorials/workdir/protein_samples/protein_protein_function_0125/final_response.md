## Functional prediction

This 198-amino-acid protein is most likely a **Ham1p-like dITP/XTP pyrophosphatase**, corresponding to **EC 3.6.1.66**.

### Evidence

- **Pfam:** Strong match to the **Ham1p_like domain (PF01725)** across nearly the entire sequence, residues 4–191.
- **Homology:** Closest reviewed proteins are dITP/XTP pyrophosphatases with:
  - 46–53% sequence identity
  - 93–99% sequence coverage
  - Extremely significant similarity scores
- **Predicted size:** Approximately 21.4 kDa, consistent with this enzyme family.
- **Cellular role:** Likely a small soluble/cytosolic nucleotide-pool “sanitizing” enzyme.

### Likely biochemical activity

The enzyme hydrolyzes noncanonical purine nucleoside triphosphates:

- **dITP + H₂O → dIMP + diphosphate**
- **ITP + H₂O → IMP + diphosphate**
- **XTP + H₂O → XMP + diphosphate**

It likely uses **Mg²⁺** and may function as a **homodimer**, as reported for closely related characterized homologs.

## Likely biological pathway

The protein is probably part of **purine nucleotide-pool quality control**, also termed **nucleotide sanitization**. Its role would be to remove oxidized, deaminated, or otherwise aberrant purine triphosphates before they are incorporated into DNA or RNA.

This helps prevent:

- Misincorporation of hypoxanthine- or xanthine-containing nucleotides
- Chromosomal mutations
- DNA/RNA damage caused by noncanonical purine bases

**Family/domain:** Ham1p-like  
**Pathway:** Noncanonical purine nucleotide degradation and nucleotide-pool sanitization  
**Confidence:** High for family-level assignment; exact relative preference for dITP, ITP, or XTP would require biochemical testing.
