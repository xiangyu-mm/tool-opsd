## Catalytic activity

The enzyme introduces a **cis double bond at the Δ9 position** of saturated fatty acyl-CoA substrates.

The preferred reaction is:

\[
\text{stearoyl-CoA} + O_2 + 2H^+ + 2\,\text{reduced cytochrome }b_5
\rightarrow
\text{oleoyl-CoA} + 2H_2O + 2\,\text{oxidized cytochrome }b_5
\]

In systematic terms:

\[
\text{octadecanoyl-CoA} \rightarrow (9Z)\text{-octadecenoyl-CoA}

\[
