## Functional identification

The 500-residue protein is a **ProP-family proline/betaine transporter**, also called a **proline:proton symporter**. The sequence is an exact full-length match to reviewed *Escherichia coli* ProP proteins **UniProt P0C0L7/P0C0L8**, providing very strong identification.

### Functional role

- **Multi-pass inner-membrane transporter**, likely using the proton-motive force to drive uptake.
- Imports compatible osmolytes, especially:
  - **Proline**
  - **Glycine betaine**
  - Potentially stachydrine, pipecolic acid, ectoine, and taurine
- Functions both as:
  - An **osmolyte uptake system**
  - An **osmosensor/osmoregulatory component** that responds to increased external osmolarity or salinity

### Cellular processes

The protein is involved in:

- Amino-acid and compatible-solute transport
- Proline import
- Glycine betaine transport
- Cellular adaptation to hyperosmotic and high-salt stress
- Osmosensory signaling and early bacterial osmoregulatory responses

By accumulating compatible solutes, it helps maintain cellular water balance and protects macromolecules during osmotic stress.

### Subcellular location and architecture

- **Location:** bacterial cytoplasmic/inner membrane
- **Topology:** highly hydrophobic, multi-pass membrane protein
- **Family/domain evidence:** MFS-like transporter domains and an osmosensory C-terminal region
- **Length:** 500 amino acids
- **Estimated molecular mass:** approximately 54.8 kDa

Overall, this is a **proton-coupled osmoprotectant transporter that helps bacteria sense and survive hyperosmotic conditions**. The functional assignment is high confidence because the sequence exactly matches curated *E. coli* ProP reference proteins.
