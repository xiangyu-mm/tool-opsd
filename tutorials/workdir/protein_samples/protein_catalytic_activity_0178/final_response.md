## Functional assignment

The 350-residue protein is most consistent with a **pyrophosphate-dependent phosphofructokinase (PPi-PFK)**, also called **diphosphate–fructose-6-phosphate 1-phosphotransferase**.

It contains a highly significant **PFK domain (Pfam PF00365; residues 4–302)**. Its closest reviewed homologs are approximately **50% identical over ~98% of the sequence** and are annotated as PPi-dependent phosphofructokinases.

### Catalyzed reaction

\text{D-fructose-6-phosphate} + \text{PPi}
\rightleftharpoons
\text{D-fructose-1,6-bisphosphate} + \text{Pi} + \text{H}^+

- **EC number:** 2.7.1.90  
- **Rhea:** RHEA:13613  
- **Cofactor:** Mg²⁺  
- **Pathway:** Glycolysis, with potential participation in gluconeogenesis

Unlike conventional ATP-dependent phosphofructokinase, this enzyme uses **inorganic pyrophosphate (PPi)** as the phosphoryl donor. This makes the reaction comparatively reversible, allowing it to function in both the forward glycolytic direction and the reverse gluconeogenic direction.

**Conclusion:** The sequence strongly supports a **Mg²⁺-dependent PPi-phosphofructokinase**, although the assignment is based on sequence/domain and homolog evidence rather than direct biochemical characterization of this specific protein.
