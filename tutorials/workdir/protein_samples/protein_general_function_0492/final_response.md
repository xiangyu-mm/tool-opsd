## Functional summary

The 768-amino-acid sequence is most consistent with the **E1 (2-oxoglutarate dehydrogenase) component** of the mitochondrial 2-oxoglutarate dehydrogenase complex (**EC 1.2.4.2**). It matches a curated *Dictyostelium discoideum* reference across the full query sequence and has strong similarity to fungal homologs. Its Pfam domains—2-oxogl_dehyd_N, E1_dh, and Transket_pyr—also support this assignment.

E1 catalyzes the thiamine-diphosphate- and Mg²⁺-dependent decarboxylation of 2-oxoglutarate and transfers the resulting succinyl group to the complex’s lipoyl carrier. It functions in the **mitochondrial matrix** as part of the **tricarboxylic acid cycle**. These functional and localization details are supported by homolog annotations; they are not direct experimental results for the query sequence.

**Sequence-derived properties:** estimated molecular mass **85.7 kDa**, theoretical pI **6.43**, and GRAVY **−0.43**. Exact organism and any targeting-peptide processing are not established by this analysis.
