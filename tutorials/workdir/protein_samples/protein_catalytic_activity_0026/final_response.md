## Functional identification

This sequence is **2′-5′-oligoadenylate synthase 2 (OAS2)**. It exactly matches the reviewed *Mus musculus* OAS2 sequence (UniProt **E9Q9A9**); the human and rat OAS2 homologs support the same annotation. The enzyme is **EC 2.7.7.84**.

OAS2 is an interferon-induced antiviral enzyme activated by double-stranded RNA. It uses ATP to synthesize **2′–5′-linked oligoadenylates (2-5A)**. A representative trimer-forming reaction is:

**3 ATP → pppA(2′→5′)A(2′→5′)A + 2 PPᵢ**

Here, **pppA** denotes the oligomer’s 5′-triphosphate end, and PPᵢ is inorganic diphosphate. OAS2 can form longer 2-5A products as well. These products activate RNase L, which degrades cellular and viral RNA and thereby contributes to antiviral defense.
