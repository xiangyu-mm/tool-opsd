## Functional identification

The 705-residue protein is **phenylalanine ammonia-lyase (PAL)**, specifically an exact sequence match to the reviewed plant enzyme **PAL2 from *Lithospermum erythrorhizon*** (UniProt **O49836**).

### Biochemical role

PAL catalyzes the first committed step of phenylpropanoid metabolism:

\[
\text{L-phenylalanine} \rightarrow \text{trans-cinnamate} + \text{NH}_4^+
\]

- **EC number:** 4.3.1.24  
- **Rhea reaction:** RHEA:21384  
- **Pfam domain:** Lyase_aromatic, PF00221, residues approximately 52–525

This is a **non-oxidative deamination/lyase reaction**, not a typical PLP-dependent aminotransferase reaction.

### Catalytic chemistry

The sequence contains the characteristic PAL catalytic modification:

- A conserved serine is converted into **2,3-didehydroalanine**.
- This participates in an autocatalytically generated **MIO prosthetic group**, involving a Ser–Gly–Ala-derived cross-link.
- The reference enzyme also contains conserved active-site and substrate-binding residues consistent with PAL activity.

Thus, the sequence strongly supports a functional PAL enzyme rather than merely a generic aromatic amino-acid enzyme.

### Cellular and physiological processes

The enzyme is involved in:

- **Phenylpropanoid biosynthesis**
- **Cinnamic acid production**
- **L-phenylalanine catabolism**
- Production of downstream phenylpropanoid-derived compounds, including precursors for diverse plant natural products such as lignin-related, flavonoid-related, and other aromatic metabolites

The exact downstream products depend on the organism and the expression context of other phenylpropanoid enzymes.

### Subcellular location and organization

- **Likely location:** cytoplasm  
- **Quaternary structure:** homotetramer

The sequence is consistent with a soluble enzyme: it lacks an obvious signal peptide or long hydrophobic transmembrane segments, supporting the annotated cytoplasmic localization.

### Sequence properties

- Length: **705 amino acids**
- Predicted molecular mass: **~76.5 kDa**
- Theoretical pI: **~5.87**
- Predicted net charge at pH 7: mildly negative

## Overall summary

This protein is a **cytoplasmic homotetrameric plant phenylalanine ammonia-lyase**. It initiates phenylpropanoid metabolism by converting L-phenylalanine into trans-cinnamate and ammonium. Its exact sequence identity to a curated PAL2 reference, the characteristic Lyase_aromatic domain, and the conserved MIO-generating catalytic modification provide very strong support for this assignment.
