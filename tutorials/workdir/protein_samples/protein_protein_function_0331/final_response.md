### Functional role

CodY is a **DNA-binding transcriptional regulator** that coordinates gene expression with cellular nutrient status. It generally functions as a transcriptional repressor, particularly during nutrient-rich growth, and controls genes involved in:

- Adaptation to nutrient limitation and starvation
- Metabolic reprogramming as nutrients become depleted
- Regulation of genes whose products support survival under nutrient stress
- Negative regulation of DNA-templated transcription
