### Functional identification

The 411-residue protein is **gamma-glutamyl phosphate reductase** (**ProA**, EC **1.2.1.41**), a cytosolic enzyme in **L-proline biosynthesis**.

The assignment is highly reliable: the sequence is an **exact full-length match** to reviewed UniProt protein **Q7M8Z4**, with 100% identity and coverage. It also contains an **Aldedh domain** (Pfam PF00171; approximately residues 2–273), consistent with an aldehyde-forming dehydrogenase.

### Catalyzed reaction

\[
\text{L-glutamate 5-phosphate}
+ \text{NADPH}
+ \text{H}^+
\rightarrow
\text{L-glutamate 5-semialdehyde}
+ \text{NADP}^+
+ \text{P}_{i}
\]

The product, **L-glutamate 5-semialdehyde**, spontaneously cyclizes to form **1-pyrroline-5-carboxylate**, which is subsequently converted toward proline by the downstream enzyme ProC.

Thus, ProA performs an **NADPH-dependent reductive dephosphorylation of an acyl phosphate**, converting the γ-carboxyl-derived phosphate group of glutamate 5-phosphate into an aldehyde.
