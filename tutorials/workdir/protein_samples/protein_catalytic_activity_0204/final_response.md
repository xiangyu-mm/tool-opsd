## Catalytic activity

This enzyme catalyzes **calcium-dependent hydrolysis of the ester bond at the sn-2 position of glycerophospholipids**.

For a phosphatidylcholine substrate, the reaction is:

\[
\text{1,2-diacyl-sn-glycero-3-phosphocholine}
+ \mathrm{H_2O}
\rightarrow
\text{1-acyl-sn-glycero-3-phosphocholine}
+ \text{fatty acid}
+ \mathrm{H^+}
\]

In words, PLA₂ removes the **second fatty-acyl chain** from a membrane phospholipid, producing a **lysophospholipid** and a **free fatty acid**. The released fatty acid may include arachidonic acid, depending on substrate composition.

## Concise summary

This sequence encodes a **secreted, Ca²⁺-dependent venom PLA₂** that cleaves the **sn-2 acyl ester of phospholipids**. Its exact match to the curated A8E2V8 sequence provides strong evidence for the assignment, including the reaction **RHEA:15801**. In its venom context, the enzyme is also associated with platelet-aggregation inhibition and other membrane/lipid-mediated toxic effects.
