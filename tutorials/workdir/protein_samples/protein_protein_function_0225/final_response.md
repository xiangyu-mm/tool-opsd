## Functional identification

The 88-residue protein is **bacterial large ribosomal subunit protein bL27**.

## Function

bL27 is a **structural component of the bacterial 50S ribosomal subunit**. Its positively charged character promotes interaction with rRNA and helps organize the peptidyl-transferase-center region of the ribosome. It contributes primarily to ribosome assembly and structural integrity rather than acting as an enzyme.

## Subcellular localization

- **Cytosolic**
- Incorporated into the **large bacterial ribosomal subunit**, commonly called the **50S subunit**

There is no apparent signal peptide or transmembrane segment, supporting a non-secreted, non-membrane-associated localization.

## Biological processes

The protein is associated with:

- **Translation**
- **Ribosome assembly and function**
- **Structural organization of the cytosolic large ribosomal subunit**
- **Peptide-bond synthesis indirectly through maintenance of the ribosomal catalytic-center architecture**

### Summary

This sequence encodes a small, highly basic bacterial **50S ribosomal protein bL27**, a conserved rRNA-binding structural protein required for proper ribosome organization and protein translation.
