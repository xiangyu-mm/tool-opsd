## Catalytic reaction

MIPEP catalyzes hydrolytic cleavage of imported mitochondrial protein precursors:

\[
\text{mitochondrial intermediate precursor} + H_2O
\rightarrow
\text{mature mitochondrial protein} + \text{N-terminal octapeptide}
\]

It performs the **second processing step** for a subset of mitochondrial proteins. After the mitochondrial targeting presequence has been removed by mitochondrial processing peptidase, MIPEP removes an additional **eight-residue N-terminal peptide**, producing the mature protein.

Mechanistically, this is a **Zn²⁺-dependent peptide-bond hydrolysis** performed by a zinc metalloprotease.
