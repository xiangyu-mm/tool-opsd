## Molecular function

uL3 is a structural and RNA-binding component of the bacterial **50S ribosomal subunit**. Its main roles are to:

- Bind the **23S ribosomal RNA**, particularly near its 3′ region
- Help nucleate and stabilize assembly of the 50S subunit
- Contribute to the architecture of the mature ribosome
- Support accurate and efficient protein synthesis

It is not predicted to be an enzyme and there is no evidence for catalytic activity, transmembrane activity, secretion, or ligand-dependent metabolism.

## Subcellular localization

## Biological processes

The protein is primarily involved in:

1. **50S ribosomal subunit assembly**  
   uL3 binds 23S rRNA early during assembly and helps organize the large subunit.

2. **Ribosome biogenesis**  
   It contributes to maturation and structural stabilization of the bacterial large ribosomal subunit.

3. **Translation**  
   As a structural constituent of the 50S subunit, it participates indirectly in decoding mRNA and synthesizing proteins.

4. **rRNA binding**  
   Its basic residues and conserved ribosomal-L3 domain support interaction with 23S rRNA.

The reviewed annotation also indicates that uL3 forms a local ribosomal protein cluster with **L14 and L19**.
