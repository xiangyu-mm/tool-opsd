## Functional summary

The 451-aa sequence is an **exact full-length match** to reviewed human UniProt **A4D126**, the protein **ISPD** (D-ribitol-5-phosphate cytidylyltransferase).

- **Molecular function:** ISPD uses CTP and D-ribitol-5-phosphate to produce **CDP-ribitol**, a nucleotide sugar. The reviewed annotation also reports activity with other pentose-phosphate substrates.
- **Biological processes:** CDP-ribitol is used by **FKTN** in synthesis of a phosphorylated O-mannosyl glycan on **alpha-dystroglycan (DAG1)**. Thus ISPD participates in **protein O-linked mannosylation and glycoprotein biosynthesis**; this glycan is important for alpha-dystroglycan binding to extracellular proteins.
- **Cellular location:** Curated annotation places ISPD in the **cytoplasm/cytosol** (GO:0005829).
