## Functional assignment

The sequence is most consistent with **RlmN, a dual-specificity radical-SAM RNA methyltransferase**. It matches reviewed RlmN homologs at about **52–54% identity across nearly the full sequence**. The assignment is supported by homolog evidence; the activity has not been experimentally established for this exact sequence.

RlmN methylates the **C2 position of adenine**—forming **2-methyladenosine (m²A)**—at either of two reported sites:

- **A2503 in 23S rRNA**
- **A37 in tRNA**

In simplified form: **adenosine in RNA → 2-methyladenosine in RNA**. Curated homolog annotations report use of **S-adenosyl-L-methionine (SAM)** and a **[4Fe–4S] cluster**; the retrieved Rhea reaction equations also include a second SAM and reduced ferredoxin. These annotations support RlmN-family activity, but do not establish which RNA substrate this particular protein acts on in vivo.
