### Functional identification

The 435-residue protein is strongly supported as a **pyrophosphate-dependent phosphofructokinase (PPi-PFK)**, also called **diphosphate–fructose-6-phosphate 1-phosphotransferase**.

Evidence includes:

- A high-confidence **PFK domain** spanning approximately residues 24–339.
- 63–72% sequence identity to reviewed PPi-PFK homologs over roughly 94–95% of the sequence.
- Conserved catalytic and substrate-binding regions matching annotated PPi-PFKs.
- Predicted size of approximately **47.2 kDa**.

### Catalyzed reaction

\[
\beta\text{-D-fructose 6-phosphate} + \text{diphosphate (PPi)}
\rightleftharpoons
\beta\text{-D-fructose 1,6-bisphosphate} + \text{phosphate (Pi)} + H^+
\]

**EC number:** EC 2.7.1.90  
**Rhea reaction:** RHEA:13613

This enzyme transfers a phosphoryl group from inorganic pyrophosphate to the C1 hydroxyl of fructose 6-phosphate. Unlike ATP-dependent phosphofructokinase, it uses **PPi rather than ATP**, making the reaction reversible and potentially useful in both:

- **Glycolysis:** fructose 6-phosphate → fructose 1,6-bisphosphate
- **Gluconeogenesis:** fructose 1,6-bisphosphate → fructose 6-phosphate

The reaction generally requires a divalent metal ion, most commonly **Mg²⁺**; Co²⁺ or Mn²⁺ may also substitute in some homologs. The assignment is based on strong homology and curated annotations of close proteins; direct biochemical assay of this particular sequence was not provided.
