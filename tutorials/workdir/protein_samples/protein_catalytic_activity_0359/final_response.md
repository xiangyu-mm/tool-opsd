## Functional identification

The 465-residue protein is **cysteine--tRNA ligase (cysteinyl-tRNA synthetase, CysRS)**, **EC 6.1.1.16**.

This assignment is highly reliable: the sequence is an **exact full-length match** to reviewed UniProt entry **P41259** from *Helicobacter pylori*. The sequence also contains the characteristic class-I aminoacyl-tRNA synthetase motifs:

- **HIGH region:** approximately residues 29–39  
- **KMSKS region:** approximately residues 269–273  
- Pfam domains consistent with class-I tRNA synthetases

## Catalyzed reaction

\[
\text{L-cysteine} + \text{ATP} + \text{tRNA}^{\text{Cys}}
\rightarrow
\text{cysteinyl-tRNA}^{\text{Cys}} + \text{AMP} + \text{pyrophosphate}
\]

Equivalently:

\[
\mathrm{Cys + ATP \rightarrow Cys\!-\!AMP + PP_i}
\]

followed by:

\[
\mathrm{Cys\!-\!AMP + tRNA^{Cys} \rightarrow Cys\!-\!tRNA^{Cys} + AMP}
\]

Thus, the enzyme charges tRNA\(^\mathrm{Cys}\) with cysteine for incorporation into proteins during translation.

## Additional properties

- **Cofactor:** binds one **Zn²⁺ ion per subunit**
- **Localization:** cytoplasm
- **Subunit state:** monomer
- **Functional class:** class-I aminoacyl-tRNA synthetase
- **Reaction database identifiers:** EC 6.1.1.16; Rhea RHEA:17773

**Summary:** This protein is a zinc-binding cysteinyl-tRNA synthetase that activates L-cysteine with ATP and transfers the activated cysteine to tRNA\(^\mathrm{Cys}\), producing cysteinyl-tRNA\(^\mathrm{Cys}\), AMP, and pyrophosphate.
