### Identification
The 768-aa sequence is most consistent with **TECPR1 (tectonin beta-propeller repeat-containing protein 1)**. It matches a reviewed chicken TECPR1 reference at **100% identity across the query**, and human TECPR1 (UniProt **Q7Z6L1**) at **72.1% identity across the query**. The references are longer than the query, so this may be a shorter isoform or a partial sequence relative to those proteins.

### Likely function
TECPR1 is annotated as an **autophagy tethering factor** involved in autophagosome maturation and fusion with lysosomes. Human TECPR1 annotations describe association with the **ATG5–ATG12 conjugate** and binding to **phosphatidylinositol-3-phosphate (PI3P)** on autophagosomes. These features are consistent with a role in bringing autophagic compartments toward lysosomal degradation.

### Localization
Annotations for close TECPR1 homologs place the protein at **autophagosome and lysosomal membranes**, and human annotations also include the **early endosome membrane**. It is therefore likely associated with intracellular vesicular membranes rather than being a freely secreted protein.

### Biological processes
Likely associated processes include:

- **Autophagy and macroautophagy**
- **Autophagosome maturation and lysosomal fusion**
- **Selective antibacterial autophagy** (reported for human TECPR1)

### Sequence and evidence caveats
Pfam scanning detected **Pex24p** and **tectonin** regions, along with repeated **Hyd_WA** hits. These support a repeat-containing domain architecture, but do not independently establish the mechanism. The functional assignment is **homology-supported**, not a direct experimental characterization of this exact sequence; some chicken-reference annotations are explicitly “by similarity.” The sequence-derived estimates are about **86.8 kDa** and pI **5.34**.
