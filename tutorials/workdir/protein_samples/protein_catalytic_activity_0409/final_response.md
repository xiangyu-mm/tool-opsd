## Functional summary

The 515-aa sequence is most consistent with **bifunctional NAD(P)H-hydrate repair enzyme Nnr**. Its closest reviewed reference match is 54.8% identical across 99.8% of the query sequence; other near-full-length matches support the same annotation. This is strong homology evidence, but the function has not been experimentally demonstrated for this specific sequence.

Nnr repairs hydrated, damaged forms of NADH and NADPH (NAD(P)HX) through two activities:

- **Epimerization:** interconverts the two hydrate stereoisomers, for example  
  **(6R)-NADHX ⇌ (6S)-NADHX**  
  and likewise for NADPHX.
- **ADP-dependent dehydration:** restores the reduced cofactor from the (6S) hydrate:  
  **(6S)-NADHX + ADP → NADH + AMP + phosphate + H⁺**  
  The corresponding NADPHX reaction yields NADPH.

The reference annotations also report **K⁺** binding. The enzyme’s overall role is to recover functional NAD(P)H from hydrates formed through enzymatic or heat-dependent damage.
