## Identification

The sequence is **RABEPK** (Rab9 effector protein), also known as a **Rab9-binding protein with kelch-repeat motifs**.

- Length: **372 amino acids**
- Exact full-length match to reviewed rat RABEPK (**UniProt Q4V8F4**)
- Strong homologs in mouse and human
- Contains multiple **kelch-related β-propeller domains**
- No evidence of a signal peptide or transmembrane helix, supporting a soluble/peripheral membrane protein rather than an integral membrane protein

## Most probable subcellular location

**Endosomal membrane, with a cytoplasmic pool.**

RABEPK is recruited to endosomal membranes through interaction with **PIKFYVE** and phosphorylation-dependent regulation. It also binds **GTP-bound RAB9**.

## Functional summary

RABEPK is a **Rab9 effector involved in endosome-to-trans-Golgi-network trafficking**. Its kelch-repeat β-propeller architecture likely provides protein–protein interaction surfaces for vesicle-tethering or trafficking machinery. The protein is expected to be primarily **cytoplasmic when unbound** and **peripherally associated with endosomal membranes** when recruited.

**Conclusion:** the most probable localization is **the endosomal membrane**, rather than the cytosol alone or a secretory/integral membrane compartment.
