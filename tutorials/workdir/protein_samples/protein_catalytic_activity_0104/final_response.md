### Functional identification

The 325-residue protein is **homoserine O-succinyltransferase** (**EC 2.3.1.46**), an enzyme in the **de novo L-methionine biosynthesis pathway**.

### Catalyzed reaction

\[
\text{L-homoserine} + \text{succinyl-CoA}
\rightarrow
\text{O-succinyl-L-homoserine} + \text{CoA}
\]

The enzyme transfers a succinyl group from succinyl-CoA to the hydroxyl group of L-homoserine, producing **O-succinyl-L-homoserine**.
