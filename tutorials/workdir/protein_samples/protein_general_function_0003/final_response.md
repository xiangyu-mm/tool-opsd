## Molecular function

KDM4E is a **histone lysine demethylase** that removes methyl groups from methylated lysines on histone H3, particularly:

- **H3K9me2/H3K9me3**
- **H3K56 methylation**, according to the curated annotation

The reaction requires **Fe²⁺, 2-oxoglutarate, and molecular oxygen**, producing demethylated histone, **formaldehyde, succinate, and CO₂**.

Functionally, this enzyme participates in regulation of the **histone code and chromatin state**, and therefore can influence transcriptional regulation and genome organization.
