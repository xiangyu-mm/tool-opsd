## Protein assessment

**Most likely function:** **PLP-dependent kynureninase**  
**EC:** 3.7.1.3  
**Confidence:** Moderate to high based on strong full-length homology to reviewed kynureninases.

### Primary characteristics

- **Length:** 424 amino acids
- **Estimated molecular mass:** 48.4 kDa
- **Theoretical pI:** 9.09; relatively basic protein
- **Predicted character:** Soluble enzyme; no obvious membrane-spanning region or secretion signal was identified
- **Domain architecture:**
  - **Aminotran_5 domain**: residues approximately 41–384
  - **KYNU_C domain**: residues approximately 331–418
- **Likely oligomeric state:** Homodimer, inferred from characterized homologs

### Functional assignment

The sequence shows approximately **51–56% identity over 97–99% of its length** to reviewed bacterial kynureninases, with extremely strong alignment significance. These homologs catalyze PLP-dependent β-elimination/cleavage reactions:

\[
\text{L-kynurenine} + H_2O \rightarrow \text{anthranilate} + \text{L-alanine}
\]

and

\[
\text{3-hydroxy-L-kynurenine} + H_2O \rightarrow
\text{3-hydroxyanthranilate} + \text{L-alanine}
\]

The enzyme therefore likely participates in **tryptophan degradation**, **kynurenine utilization**, and potentially **quinolinate/NAD⁺ biosynthesis**.

### Cofactor and catalytic features

- Requires **pyridoxal 5′-phosphate (PLP)**.
- The Aminotran_5 family assignment is consistent with a PLP-dependent enzyme.
- Kynureninase homologs contain a conserved lysine that forms the PLP internal aldimine; the corresponding catalytic residue is likely present in this sequence, although the present analysis does not experimentally map it.

### Cellular localization and pathway

- Likely **cytoplasmic**, based on reviewed homolog annotations.
- Expected pathway roles:
  - L-kynurenine degradation
  - L-tryptophan catabolism
  - Quinolinic acid/NAD⁺ biosynthesis

### Overall conclusion

This is most consistent with a **bacterial kynureninase**, a soluble homodimeric **PLP-dependent enzyme of approximately 48 kDa**. The assignment is well supported by conserved domain architecture and near-full-length similarity to experimentally annotated kynureninases. Exact substrate preference between L-kynurenine and 3-hydroxy-L-kynurenine, catalytic kinetics, and oligomerization should still be confirmed experimentally.
