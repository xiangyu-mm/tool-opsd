### Function

TyrRS charges tRNA\(^\mathrm{Tyr}\) with tyrosine in two steps:

1. **L-tyrosine + ATP → tyrosyl-AMP + PPi**
2. **Tyrosyl-AMP + tRNA\(^\mathrm{Tyr}\) → tyrosyl-tRNA\(^\mathrm{Tyr}\) + AMP**

Overall reaction:

\[
\mathrm{L\text{-}tyrosine + ATP + tRNA^{Tyr}
\rightarrow tyrosyl\text{-}tRNA^{Tyr} + AMP + PP_i}
\]
