### Catalytic activity

MurE catalyzes the ATP-dependent addition of **meso-2,6-diaminopimelate (meso-DAP)** to the UDP-linked peptidoglycan precursor UDP-MurNAc-L-Ala-D-Glu.

**Reaction:**

\[
\text{UDP-MurNAc-L-Ala-D-Glu} + \text{meso-DAP} + \text{ATP}
\rightarrow
\text{UDP-MurNAc-L-Ala-}\gamma\text{-D-Glu-meso-DAP} + \text{ADP} + \text{P}_{i} + \text{H}^{+}
\]

In words, the enzyme builds the third amino-acid position of the bacterial peptidoglycan stem peptide. ATP activates the D-glutamate carboxyl group, after which the amino group of meso-DAP forms the new amide bond.

### Biological role

This is a cytosolic enzyme in the **peptidoglycan biosynthesis pathway**. Its product is a precursor for subsequent incorporation into the bacterial cell wall. The meso-DAP-containing stem is characteristic of many Gram-negative and some Gram-positive bacteria.
