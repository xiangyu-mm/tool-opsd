## Functional prediction

This 225-amino-acid protein is very likely a **Ham1p-like dITP/XTP pyrophosphatase** (EC 3.6.1.66), an enzyme that hydrolyzes noncanonical purine nucleoside triphosphates into their monophosphate forms and diphosphate.

**Evidence:** The sequence contains a Ham1p-like domain (Pfam PF01725, residues 7–218) and matches reviewed dITP/XTP pyrophosphatases at approximately 74% identity across 97% of the sequence. Those homologs are annotated to act on dITP, ITP, and XTP, with Mg²⁺ as a cofactor.

**Likely biological process:** Purine nucleoside triphosphate catabolism and nucleotide-pool “housekeeping.” By removing aberrant nucleotides, the enzyme may help prevent their incorporation into DNA or RNA. This role is a strong inference from homolog annotations; the sequence itself has not been experimentally tested here.

**Confidence:** High for the broad enzyme-family assignment; exact substrate preference and biological role in the source organism remain predictions.
