## Functional identification

This 250-amino-acid sequence is an **exact full-length match** to reviewed UniProt protein **Q8D382, UbiE**, a ubiquinone/menaquinone-biosynthesis C-methyltransferase from *Wigglesworthia glossinidia brevipalpis*. The sequence analysis also detected a UbiE methyltransferase domain.

UbiE transfers a **methyl group from S-adenosyl-L-methionine (SAM)** to quinone precursors. The annotation supports these reactions:

1. **Menaquinol formation**  
   2-demethylmenaquinol + SAM → menaquinol + S-adenosyl-L-homocysteine (SAH) + H⁺  
   **Rhea: RHEA:42640; EC 2.1.1.163**

2. **Ubiquinol-pathway methylation**  
   2-methoxy-6-(all-trans-polyprenyl)benzene-1,4-diol + SAM → 5-methoxy-2-methyl-3-(all-trans-polyprenyl)benzene-1,4-diol + SAH + H⁺  
   **Rhea: RHEA:28286; EC 2.1.1.201**

**In brief:** UbiE is a SAM-dependent C-methyltransferase involved in quinone cofactor biosynthesis, including menaquinone and ubiquinone pathways. The identity is especially well supported by the exact sequence match; the substrate and reaction assignments are based on the reviewed reference annotation, not a separate assay of the supplied protein.
