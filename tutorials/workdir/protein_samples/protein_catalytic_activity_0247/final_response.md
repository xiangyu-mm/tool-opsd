## Functional summary

The 388-aa sequence is most consistent with **CoaBC**, a bifunctional enzyme in coenzyme A biosynthesis. This assignment is supported by a reviewed *Bacillus subtilis* CoaBC homolog (59.5% identity, 99% query coverage) and matches to the enzyme’s Flavoprotein and DFP domains. The evidence is strong for a homology-based assignment, but the query protein itself has not been experimentally tested here.

CoaBC catalyzes two sequential reactions:

1. **Phosphopantothenate–cysteine ligase (EC 6.3.2.5; Rhea RHEA:19397)**  
   (R)-4′-phosphopantothenate + L-cysteine + CTP → N-[(R)-4-phosphopantothenoyl]-L-cysteine + CMP + diphosphate + H⁺

2. **Phosphopantothenoylcysteine decarboxylase (EC 4.1.1.36; Rhea RHEA:16793)**  
   N-[(R)-4-phosphopantothenoyl]-L-cysteine + H⁺ → (R)-4′-phosphopantetheine + CO₂

Together, these steps convert phosphopantothenate and cysteine into phosphopantetheine, an intermediate in CoA biosynthesis. The reviewed homolog is annotated as binding **FMN** and requiring **Mg²⁺**; those cofactor assignments are inferred for this sequence by homology.
