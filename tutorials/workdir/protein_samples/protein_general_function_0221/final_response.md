### Functional summary

The 219-residue protein is **RimP**, a bacterial **ribosome maturation factor** involved in assembly and maturation of the **30S small ribosomal subunit**.

The assignment is highly reliable: the sequence is an **exact 100% sequence match** to reviewed UniProt entry **A8M743** from *Salinispora arenicola*. Related reviewed RimP proteins from other actinomycetes also show strong similarity.

The sequence is notably rich in **arginine and glycine near the N-terminus**, followed by acidic and polar regions. This composition is consistent with a small, soluble bacterial ribosome-associated factor rather than a membrane or secreted protein.

No experimentally supported oligomeric state, regulatory mechanism, catalytic activity, or cofactor requirement was identified from the available annotations. Overall, this is best characterized as a **small cytosolic RimP assembly factor required during bacterial 30S ribosome biogenesis**.
