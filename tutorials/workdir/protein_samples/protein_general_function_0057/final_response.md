### Likely activity

Nnr repairs hydrated forms of NADH and NADPH, collectively called **NAD(P)HX**:

1. **NAD(P)HX epimerase activity**  
   Interconverts the R- and S-forms of NAD(P)HX.  
   **EC 5.1.99.6**

2. **ADP-dependent NAD(P)H-hydrate dehydratase activity**  
   Converts S-NAD(P)HX back to NAD(P)H or NADPH, using ADP, which is converted to AMP and inorganic phosphate.  
   **EC 4.2.1.136**

Representative reactions are:

- (6S)-NADHX + ADP → NADH + AMP + phosphate
