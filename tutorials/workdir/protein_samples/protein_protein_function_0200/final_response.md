## Functional identification

This assignment is highly confident because the sequence is an **exact full-length match** to the reviewed *Trichodesmium erythraeum* UniProt protein Q115B3. It also contains the characteristic **TsaD domain** (Pfam PF00814; residues approximately 23–318).

### Likely reaction

TsaD catalyzes the final transfer step in formation of **N⁶-threonylcarbamoyladenosine (t⁶A37)** in tRNA:

\[
\text{threonylcarbamoyl-AMP} + \text{A37 in tRNA}
\rightarrow \text{t⁶A37-tRNA} + \text{AMP}
\]

The corresponding enzyme classification is **EC 2.3.1.234**.

TsaD functions together with the bacterial t⁶A biosynthesis proteins **TsaE and TsaB**. The available annotation indicates that TsaD likely performs the direct catalytic transfer of the threonylcarbamoyl group.
