## Functional assignment

The 360-residue protein is most consistent with **RlmN, a radical-SAM dual-specificity RNA methyltransferase**.

### Evidence

- **Pfam domains:**  
  - RlmN_N domain, residues 5–63  
  - Radical_SAM domain, residues 115–277
- **Strong homology:** 40–43% identity and approximately 98% coverage to reviewed RlmN proteins.
- **Cofactor:** Likely one **[4Fe–4S] cluster**, coordinated by three cysteines.
- **Sequence properties:** approximately 40.95 kDa; predicted pI 8.12.
- **Functional annotation of homologs:** EC **2.1.1.192**, cytoplasmic RlmN activity.

### Catalytic reaction

RlmN methylates the **C2 position of adenine** in RNA:

\[
\text{Adenosine in 23S rRNA or tRNA}
+ \text{SAM}
\rightarrow
\text{2-methyladenosine in RNA}
\]

The annotated overall radical-SAM reaction uses two SAM molecules and reducing equivalents:

\[
\text{RNA-adenosine}
+ 2\,\text{SAM}
+ \text{reduced ferredoxin}
\rightarrow
\text{RNA-2-methyladenosine}
+ \text{5'-deoxyadenosine}
+ \text{S-adenosylhomocysteine}
+ \text{oxidized ferredoxin}
\]

The principal known substrates are:

- **A2503 of 23S rRNA**, producing **m²A2503**
- **A37 of tRNA**, producing **m²A37**

Mechanistically, RlmN uses radical-SAM chemistry and a transient **S-methylcysteine intermediate** to transfer the methyl group to RNA adenine.

**Conclusion:** This sequence encodes a probable **[4Fe–4S]-dependent RlmN RNA adenine C2 methyltransferase**, with moderate-to-strong homology-based support rather than direct experimental characterization of the submitted sequence.
