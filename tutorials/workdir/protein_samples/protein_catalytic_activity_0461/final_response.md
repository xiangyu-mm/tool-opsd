## Catalytic activity

PdxJ catalyzes the complex ring-closure step in bacterial **pyridoxine 5′-phosphate (PNP; vitamin B6 phosphate) biosynthesis**.

### Chemical reaction

\[
\text{3-amino-2-oxopropyl phosphate}
+
\text{1-deoxy-D-xylulose 5-phosphate}
\rightarrow
\text{pyridoxine 5′-phosphate}
+
\text{phosphate}
+
2\mathrm{H_2O}
+
\mathrm{H^+}
\]

The substrates are also described as:

- **3-amino-2-oxopropyl phosphate**, also known as 1-amino-acetone-3-phosphate
- **1-deoxy-D-xylulose 5-phosphate (DXP)**

The product, pyridoxine 5′-phosphate, is subsequently used to generate the biologically active vitamin B6 coenzyme forms, including PLP.
