**Catalytic reaction:** TGT replaces guanine at position 34—the anticodon wobble position—in specific tRNAs (tRNA-Asp, -Asn, -His, and -Tyr) with **preQ₁**, the queuine precursor:

**preQ₁ + guanosine(34) in tRNA → preQ₁-modified tRNA(34) + guanine**
