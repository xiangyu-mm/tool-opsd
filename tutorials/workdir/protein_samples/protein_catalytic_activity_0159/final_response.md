## Functional identification

The 407-residue protein is most consistent with **RibBA**, a bifunctional enzyme in **riboflavin (vitamin B₂) biosynthesis**. Its assignment is strongly supported by:

- 56–58% identity to reviewed RibBA homologs over ~97% of the sequence
- An N-terminal **DHBP synthase** domain, PF00926
- A C-terminal **GTP cyclohydrolase II** domain, PF00925

Thus, it combines the activities often encoded separately by **RibB** and **RibA**.

## Catalytic activities

### 1. 3,4-Dihydroxy-2-butanone-4-phosphate synthase  
**EC 4.1.99.12; Rhea RHEA:18457**

This N-terminal activity converts **D-ribulose 5-phosphate** into the four-carbon riboflavin precursor **3,4-dihydroxy-2-butanone 4-phosphate (DHBP)**, releasing formate:

\[
\text{D-ribulose-5-phosphate}
\rightarrow
\text{3,4-dihydroxy-2-butanone-4-phosphate}
+
\text{formate}
+
\mathrm{H^+}
\]

This reaction supplies the DHBP branch of riboflavin biosynthesis.

### 2. GTP cyclohydrolase II  
**EC 3.5.4.25; Rhea RHEA:23704**

The C-terminal activity initiates the second riboflavin-biosynthetic branch by converting **GTP** into the pyrimidine precursor commonly called **DARP**:

\[
\text{GTP} + 4\mathrm{H_2O}
\rightarrow
\text{DARP}
+
\text{formate}
+
2\text{ phosphate}
+
3\mathrm{H^+}
\]

DARP is also described as **2,5-diamino-6-ribosylamino-4(3H)-pyrimidinone 5′-phosphate** or **2,5-diamino-6-hydroxy-4-(5-phosphoribosylamino)pyrimidine**.

## Cofactors and localization

Curated RibBA homologs are annotated as binding:

- **Two divalent metal ions**, typically Mg²⁺ or Mn²⁺
- **One Zn²⁺ ion**

The protein is expected to be a **cytosolic** enzyme and functions in the early steps of riboflavin biosynthesis.

## Overall conclusion

**Likely function:** bifunctional riboflavin-biosynthesis enzyme RibBA  
**Main catalytic role:** formation of both early riboflavin precursors—DHBP from ribulose-5-phosphate and DARP from GTP.  

The assignment is supported by strong sequence homology, domain architecture, and reviewed homolog annotations, although the catalytic activities have not been experimentally demonstrated for this exact sequence.
