### Functional summary

The 221-amino-acid sequence is an **exact match** to reviewed *Kluyveromyces lactis* **SYF2**, a pre-mRNA-splicing factor (UniProt **Q6CPI4**). The reference annotation places it in the **nucleus** and associates it with pre-mRNA splicing. A Pfam scan also detects the **SYF2 domain (PF08231)** at residues 98–218.

A likely biological role is participation in **spliceosome assembly or maturation**. In the related yeast *Saccharomyces cerevisiae*, SYF2 is reported as part of the Prp19/NTC-associated complex, helping support spliceosome rearrangement or stability after U4 snRNA dissociation. Cell-cycle effects reported for defective SYF2 may occur indirectly through impaired splicing of genes such as **TUB1**; that detail is from a homolog and should not be treated as directly established for this exact protein.

**Bottom line:** this is very likely a nuclear spliceosome-associated protein involved in mRNA splicing, with a plausible secondary influence on cell-cycle progression through effects on transcript processing.
