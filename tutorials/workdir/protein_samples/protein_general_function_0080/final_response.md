### Functional summary

This 252-residue protein is **adenylate kinase (ADK; EC 2.7.4.3)**, an ATP-dependent phosphotransferase that maintains adenine nucleotide balance:

\[
\mathrm{AMP + ATP \rightleftharpoons 2\,ADP}
\]

### Evidence

- **Exact sequence match** to reviewed UniProt adenylate kinase A5E598.
- Strong homology to additional curated fungal adenylate kinases: approximately **79% identity over the full sequence**.
- Contains the characteristic adenylate kinase domain **ADK/PF00406** spanning residues approximately **42–228**.
- Contains the **adenylate kinase lid domain** (**ADK_lid/PF05191**, residues approximately 165–200).
- The N-terminal **P-loop-like ATP-binding region** around `VLVLIGAPGSGKGT` supports nucleotide binding and phosphotransferase activity.

### Properties

- Length: **252 aa**
- Predicted molecular mass: **28.21 kDa**
- Theoretical pI: **8.21**
- Mildly basic at neutral pH; predicted charge at pH 7 is approximately **+2**
- Hydrophilic overall profile; no transmembrane segment is evident
- Curated homologs are annotated as **monomeric**

### Cellular role and localization

The enzyme participates in **AMP, ADP, and ATP metabolism**, contributing to cellular energy homeostasis and adenine nucleotide biosynthesis. The matching curated fungal protein is annotated mainly to the **mitochondrial intermembrane space**, with additional cytosolic localization.

**Overall conclusion:** this is a canonical, likely monomeric adenylate kinase that reversibly converts AMP and ATP into two ADP molecules.
