### Functional identification

The 218-residue protein is **adenylate kinase** (**EC 2.7.4.3**), an AMP kinase involved in adenine-nucleotide and cellular energy metabolism.

The assignment is strongly supported by:

- **Exact full-length sequence match** to reviewed UniProt adenylate kinase C1D6J0.
- Conserved **ADK catalytic domain** and **ADK lid domain**.
- Consistent matches to multiple reviewed adenylate kinases.

### Catalyzed reaction

\[
\boxed{\mathrm{AMP + ATP \rightleftharpoons 2\,ADP}}
\]

Adenylate kinase transfers the terminal (γ) phosphate of ATP to AMP:

- **Substrates:** AMP and ATP  
- **Products:** two ADP molecules  
- **Reaction type:** reversible phosphotransfer  
- **Rhea:** RHEA:12973  

The reaction helps maintain the cellular balance among AMP, ADP, and ATP. The enzyme generally acts on Mg²⁺-complexed adenine nucleotides, although Mg²⁺ is not consumed in the net reaction.
