### Functional identification
The 164-aa sequence is an **exact match** to reviewed human **phospholipase A and acyltransferase 4 (PLAAT4; UniProt Q9UL19)**. Its annotation supports both phospholipase and acyltransferase activities.

### Main catalytic reaction
PLAAT4 acts **without requiring calcium** to hydrolyze an acyl ester bond in a glycerophospholipid:

**Glycerophospholipid + H₂O → lysophospholipid + fatty acid**

It can cleave the fatty acyl group at either the **sn-1 (PLA1)** or **sn-2 (PLA2)** position. For most tested substrates, PLA1 activity is reported to be stronger than PLA2 activity.

PLAAT4 also has acyltransferase activities: it can transfer a fatty acyl group to the hydroxyl of a lysophospholipid, or transfer an acyl group from a glycerophospholipid—particularly phosphatidylcholine—to the amine of phosphatidylethanolamine, producing **N-acylphosphatidylethanolamine (NAPE)**. These are annotation-supported activities; the exact preferred substrate can depend on context.
