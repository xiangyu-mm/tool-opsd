### Functional identification

The 247-residue protein is **pyrroloquinoline-quinone synthase** (**EC 1.3.3.11**), an enzyme in **pyrroloquinoline quinone (PQQ) biosynthesis**.

This assignment is highly reliable because the sequence is an **exact match** to the reviewed UniProt protein **B9JJ30** and closely matches other reviewed PQQ synthases. It also contains the characteristic **TENA_THI-4 domain** (PF03070).

### Catalytic activity

The enzyme performs **ring cyclization coupled to eight-electron oxidation** of a PQQ biosynthetic intermediate, producing PQQ:

\[
\text{PQQ precursor} + 3\,O_2
\rightarrow
\text{PQQ} + 2\,H_2O_2 + 2\,H_2O + H^+
\]

More explicitly, the substrate is a dioxo hexahydroquinoline carboxylate intermediate, commonly described as:

**3a-(2-amino-2-carboxyethyl)-4,5-dioxo-4,5,6,7,8,9-hexahydroquinoline-7,9-dicarboxylic acid**

which is converted into **pyrroloquinoline quinone (PQQ)**.
