## Functional identification

The protein is **tyrosyl-tRNA synthetase** (TyrRS; **EC 6.1.1.1**), a class I aminoacyl-tRNA synthetase.

Evidence includes:

- Exact full-length match to a reviewed Swiss-Prot tyrosyl-tRNA ligase sequence.
- Conserved class I synthetase **HIGH** and **KMSKS** motifs.
- Presence of the catalytic `tRNA-synt_1b` domain and a C-terminal RNA-binding region.
- Predicted cytoplasmic homodimeric enzyme.

## Catalyzed reaction

\[
\text{L-tyrosine} + \text{tRNA}^{\text{Tyr}} + \text{ATP}
\rightarrow
\text{L-tyrosyl-tRNA}^{\text{Tyr}} + \text{AMP} + \text{PP}_{i} + \text{H}^{+}
\]

The reaction occurs in two stages:

1. **Tyrosine activation**

\[
\text{L-tyrosine}+\text{ATP}
\rightarrow
\text{Tyr-AMP}+\text{PP}_{i}
\]

2. **Transfer to tRNA**

\[
\text{Tyr-AMP}+\text{tRNA}^{\text{Tyr}}
\rightarrow
\text{Tyr-tRNA}^{\text{Tyr}}+\text{AMP}
\]

Thus, the enzyme specifically charges tRNA\(^\text{Tyr}\) with tyrosine for use during protein synthesis. The assignment is high confidence because the sequence is an exact match to a curated enzyme annotation.
