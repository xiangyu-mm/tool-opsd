## Functional identification

The 316-amino-acid sequence is an **exact, full-length match** to reviewed UniProt entry **B0K8K4** from *Thermoanaerobacter pseudethanolicus*. It is annotated as **phospho-N-acetylmuramoyl-pentapeptide-transferase** (EC **2.7.8.13**), also known as **MraY**.

## Catalytic reaction

The enzyme transfers the MurNAc-pentapeptide group from UDP-MurNAc-pentapeptide to the membrane lipid carrier undecaprenyl phosphate:

**UDP-MurNAc-pentapeptide + undecaprenyl phosphate → undecaprenyl-diphospho-MurNAc-pentapeptide (lipid I) + UMP**

This is the first lipid-carrier step in bacterial peptidoglycan biosynthesis. The product, **lipid I**, is used in subsequent steps to build the cell-wall precursor. The annotation lists **Mg²⁺** as a cofactor and places the protein in the **cell membrane**. The reaction is referenced as **Rhea RHEA:28386**.

**Evidence note:** This assignment is supported by an exact sequence match to a reviewed UniProt entry, rather than by homology alone. Sequence-derived properties include a length of 316 aa and predicted molecular mass of about 34.8 kDa.
