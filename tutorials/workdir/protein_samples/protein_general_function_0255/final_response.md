## Functional summary

The sequence is **strongly supported as GlmU**, a bacterial bifunctional enzyme involved in making **UDP-N-acetylglucosamine (UDP-GlcNAc)**. The match to reviewed *Acinetobacter baumannii* GlmU proteins is full length, with about **83% sequence identity** and **100% coverage**. This supports the functional assignment, though it is homology-based rather than a direct assay of this exact sequence.

GlmU catalyzes two consecutive reactions:

1. **Acetylation:** glucosamine-1-phosphate + acetyl-CoA → N-acetylglucosamine-1-phosphate + CoA  
2. **Uridyl transfer:** N-acetylglucosamine-1-phosphate + UTP → UDP-GlcNAc + diphosphate

UDP-GlcNAc is a precursor for bacterial cell-envelope components, including peptidoglycan and lipid A.

**Bottom line:** this is very likely a bacterial **GlmU bifunctional enzyme** that produces UDP-GlcNAc through sequential acetyltransferase and uridyltransferase reactions.
