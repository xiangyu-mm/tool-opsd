## Functional role

MBP2C is a **plant cytoskeletal and RNA-associated regulatory protein**. Its reported activities include:

- Binding RNA
- Associating with microtubules and regulating cytoskeletal organization
- Influencing cell shape through cytoskeletal regulation
- Interacting with the Arabidopsis homeobox protein **STM**
- Altering STM RNA-binding behavior and preventing STM association with plasmodesmata

Thus, MBP2C likely functions as a scaffold or regulatory factor that links RNA/protein interactions with cytoskeletal organization and intercellular trafficking.

## Cellular processes

The annotated protein is involved in several plant processes:

- **Cortical microtubule and cytoskeleton organization**
- **Plasmodesmata-mediated cell-to-cell transport**
- Regulation of STM-dependent developmental signaling
- **Stomatal patterning**
- Response to **water deprivation/drought**
- Host defense against tobamoviruses, probably by restricting viral cell-to-cell movement

Its antiviral role appears to result from interfering with viral movement through the plant cytoskeletal/plasmodesmal transport system rather than from enzymatic antiviral activity.

## Subcellular location

The primary localization is:

- **Cytoplasm**
- **Microtubule cytoskeleton**
- Cytosolic puncta when associated with STM

A nuclear annotation is also present, but the strongest curated localization evidence supports a predominantly cytoplasmic, cytoskeleton-associated protein.

## Overall summary

This sequence encodes **Arabidopsis MBP2C**, a soluble, charged, non-enzymatic regulatory protein associated with the microtubule cytoskeleton. It binds RNA and interacts with developmental regulators such as STM, thereby influencing cytoskeletal organization, plasmodesmal intercellular transport, stomatal development, drought responses, and plant antiviral defense.
