## Functional summary

This sequence is an **exact full-length match to *Aspergillus niger* rhamnogalacturonase B**, UniProt **P87161**. The assignment is therefore strongly supported, not merely inferred from weak homology.

### Likely function

- **Enzyme:** Rhamnogalacturonase B  
- **EC number:** **3.2.1.171**
- **Protein family:** Glycoside hydrolase family 28, supported by a **Glyco_hydro_28 domain** spanning approximately residues **145–369**.
- **Substrate:** Rhamnogalacturonan regions of pectin.
- **Reaction:** Cleavage of **α-D-galacturonosyl-(1→2)-α-L-rhamnosyl linkages** in the rhamnogalacturonan backbone, producing shorter pectic oligosaccharides.

In practical terms, this is a **secreted fungal pectin-degrading enzyme** involved in breaking down the complex “hairy” regions of plant cell-wall pectin.

### Cellular location and trafficking

The protein is expected to be:

1. Synthesized into the **endoplasmic reticulum** through its N-terminal signal peptide.
2. Processed through the **Golgi/secretory pathway**, where it may receive N-linked glycans.
3. Exported to the **extracellular region**, likely the fungal cell exterior or culture medium.

The mature secreted protein would begin near residue **22** after signal-peptide cleavage. The curated reference contains N-linked glycosylation sites around residues **145, 239, 254, and 321**, as well as several disulfide bonds, features consistent with a secreted, oxidizing-environment enzyme.

### Biological processes

The most likely biological role is participation in:

- **Pectin catabolism**
- **Plant cell-wall polysaccharide degradation**
- **Extracellular breakdown of rhamnogalacturonan**
- Potentially, fungal utilization of plant-derived carbohydrates during growth on plant biomass

It likely acts together with other secreted pectinolytic enzymes, such as polygalacturonases, pectin lyases, and pectin methylesterases, which collectively dismantle pectin into metabolizable sugars and oligosaccharides.
