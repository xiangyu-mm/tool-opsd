## Functional assessment

**Most likely enzyme:** **arylacetonitrilase / nitrilase**  
**Enzyme class:** nitrile hydrolase, EC **3.5.5.1 / 3.5.5.5**  
**Confidence:** **High**

### Evidence

- The 327-residue sequence is an **exact sequence match** to the reviewed *Neurospora crassa* arylacetonitrilase UniProt entry V5IPE4.
- It contains the characteristic **CN hydrolase domain** (**Pfam PF00795**, residues approximately 6–292).
- Conserved catalytic-region residues correspond to the annotated nitrilase active-site positions, including the catalytic nucleophile near residue 167.
- Closely related reviewed fungal proteins are experimentally annotated as arylacetonitrilases.

### Catalytic reaction

The enzyme hydrolyzes nitriles to the corresponding carboxylate and ammonium:

\[
\mathrm{R{-}C{\equiv}N + 2H_2O \rightarrow R{-}COO^- + NH_4^+}
\]

For the preferred substrate **phenylacetonitrile**:

\[
\mathrm{C_6H_5CH_2CN + 2H_2O
\rightarrow C_6H_5CH_2COO^- + NH_4^+}
\]

Thus, the products are **phenylacetate** and **ammonium**.

### Likely substrate range

The strongest annotation supports preference for:

- **Phenylacetonitrile**
- **(R,S)-Mandelonitrile**
- **2-Phenylpropionitrile**

Related homologs can also act on other substituted aryl nitriles and some dinitriles, but the exact substrate breadth of this sequence should be confirmed experimentally.

### Cofactors and localization

No metal ion, organic cofactor, or prosthetic group is required by the available annotation. The sequence appears to encode a typical soluble nitrilase-domain enzyme; no transmembrane segment or secretion signal is evident from the supplied sequence.

### Summary

This protein is best annotated as a **fungal arylacetonitrilase**, a member of the **nitrilase superfamily**. It catalyzes direct hydrolysis of aryl-substituted nitriles, especially phenylacetonitrile, producing the corresponding carboxylate and ammonium.
