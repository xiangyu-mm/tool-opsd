## Functional identification

The sequence is an **exact match** to the reviewed *Arabidopsis thaliana* protein **OHP1** (UniProt **O81208**; 110 amino acids; ~12.0 kDa). This provides high-confidence identification.

OHP1 is a **light-harvesting complex-like chloroplast protein**. It is not an enzyme; it is a small membrane-associated component involved in photosynthetic-complex assembly, maintenance, and photoprotection.

## Functional role

OHP1 is implicated in:

- **Photosystem II biogenesis and repair**
- Assembly of early PSII reaction-center-like complexes
- Functional maintenance of both **photosystem I and photosystem II**
- Formation of a complex with **OHP2 and HCF244**
- Recruitment of ribosomes to **psbA mRNA**, which encodes the PSII D1/PsbA core protein
- Binding or delivery of chlorophylls and carotenoids through an OHP1/OHP2 complex
- Protection of the photosynthetic apparatus during **high-light stress**

Thus, OHP1 likely acts as a small pigment-associated assembly/photoprotection factor rather than as a catalytic protein.

## Sequence and topology

The sequence supports this interpretation:

- The N-terminal region is rich in **Ser/Thr and Arg**, consistent with a chloroplast targeting peptide.
- The C-terminal portion contains a strongly hydrophobic membrane-spanning region, consistent with a **single-pass thylakoid membrane protein**.
- No catalytic motifs or large soluble domains are evident.
- The protein is basic overall, with a predicted pI of approximately **9.57**.

## Subcellular location

OHP1 is localized to the:

**Chloroplast → thylakoid membrane**

It is associated with both PSI and PSII, with particularly important roles in PSII assembly and repair.

### Concise summary

This 110-residue protein is chloroplast **OHP1**, a small single-pass thylakoid membrane protein involved in pigment handling, photosystem organization, PSII assembly, and repair. In cooperation with OHP2 and HCF244, it helps assemble and maintain PSII and may protect photosynthetic complexes under high-light conditions.
