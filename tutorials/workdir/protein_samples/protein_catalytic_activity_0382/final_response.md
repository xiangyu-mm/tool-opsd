## Catalytic reaction

The enzyme transfers a terminal segment of an **α-1,4-linked glucan chain** to the C6 hydroxyl group of a glucose residue in the same or another glucan chain, creating an **α-1,6-glycosidic branch**:

\[
\text{α-1,4-glucan donor} + \text{α-1,4-glucan acceptor}
\rightarrow
\text{shortened α-1,4-glucan} + \text{branched α-1,4/α-1,6-glucan}
\]

In glycogen biosynthesis, this converts relatively linear α-1,4-glucan chains into the highly branched glycogen polymer. The branching increases glycogen solubility and generates additional chain ends for glycogen synthesis and breakdown.

**Summary:** This is a glycogen branching transglycosylase that forms α-1,6 branches by transferring α-1,4-glucan segments; the assignment is supported by an exact match to the reviewed human GBE1 sequence and curated EC 2.4.1.18 annotation.
