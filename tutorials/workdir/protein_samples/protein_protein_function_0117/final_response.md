## Functional prediction

The protein is a **transketolase (EC 2.2.1.1)**, a thiamine-diphosphate-dependent enzyme of approximately **72.8 kDa**.

This assignment is highly reliable because the sequence is an **exact full-length match** to reviewed *Scheffersomyces stipitis* transketolase UniProt **P34736**. It also contains the expected transketolase domain architecture:

## Catalytic role

Transketolase transfers a **two-carbon ketol group** from a ketose phosphate to an aldose phosphate through a thiamine-pyrophosphate-dependent catalytic intermediate.

The curated reference reaction is:

\[
\text{Sedoheptulose-7-phosphate} + \text{Glyceraldehyde-3-phosphate}
\rightleftharpoons
\text{Ribose-5-phosphate} + \text{Xylulose-5-phosphate}
\]

This corresponds to **Rhea RHEA:10508**.

## Biological pathway

The protein participates in the **nonoxidative pentose phosphate pathway**, also called the pentose-phosphate shunt. Its role is to rearrange carbon skeletons among sugar phosphates, linking:

- Pentose phosphate intermediates
- Glycolytic intermediates
- Production and utilization of ribose-5-phosphate
- Carbohydrate degradation and biosynthetic carbon redistribution
