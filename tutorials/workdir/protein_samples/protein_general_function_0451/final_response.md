### Likely biological role

This protein is **microcin N**, a small antibacterial **bacteriocin** produced by *E. coli*. Its curated annotation indicates that it:

- Acts against ***E. coli* and Salmonella**.
- Is not active against some tested organisms, including **Listeria** and **Campylobacter**.
- Functions as a **channel-forming antimicrobial peptide/protein**, likely disrupting target-cell membranes.
- Is secreted and acts in the extracellular environment.
- Is probably neutralized in the producer organism by the immunity protein **McnI**.

The related bacteriocin microcin E492 also forms membrane channels, supporting the broader channel-forming bacteriocin interpretation, although oligomerization and cation selectivity reported for microcin E492 should not be assumed definitively for microcin N.

### Interpretation

Because the sequence exactly matches a reviewed UniProt entry, this is not merely a weak homology-based prediction: the protein can be confidently annotated as **microcin N**. The sequence is unusually short and alanine/glycine-rich, consistent with a compact bacteriocin rather than a conventional metabolic enzyme. No catalytic residues, cofactor-binding motifs, or enzyme-like domains are evident.

**Overall conclusion:** this sequence encodes a small secreted antibacterial microcin, specifically *E. coli* **microcin N**, whose principal function is likely membrane channel formation and killing of susceptible enteric bacteria.
