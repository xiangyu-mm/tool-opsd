### Molecular role

The beta subunit contributes to substrate and nucleotide recognition, including binding succinate and determining nucleotide specificity. The complete succinate–CoA ligase complex catalyzes:

\[
\text{succinyl-CoA} + \text{ADP} + P_i
\rightleftharpoons
\text{succinate} + \text{CoA} + \text{ATP}
\]

The beta subunit is **not an independent complete enzyme**: the alpha subunit provides major CoA- and phosphate-binding functions.
