## Functional identification

The sequence encodes a **Src-family non-receptor protein tyrosine kinase**, most closely matching **Fgr**. It is 527 residues long and has the characteristic architecture:

- N-terminal regulatory region
- **SH3 domain**: approximately residues 80–134
- **SH2 domain**: approximately residues 142–224
- C-terminal **protein kinase domain**: approximately residues 263–506

It matches reviewed human Fgr with **90% sequence identity over the full length**.

## Catalytic reaction

The enzyme transfers the terminal γ-phosphate of ATP to a tyrosine residue on a protein substrate:

\[
\text{L-tyrosyl-[protein]} + \text{ATP}
\rightarrow
\text{O-phospho-L-tyrosyl-[protein]} + \text{ADP} + \text{H}^{+}
\]

Thus, it catalyzes **protein tyrosine phosphorylation** and is classified as **EC 2.7.10.2**, non-membrane-spanning protein tyrosine kinase activity.

## Catalytic evidence

The kinase domain contains conserved motifs expected for an active eukaryotic protein kinase, including:

- **VAIK-region lysine**, involved in ATP positioning
- **HRDLAARN** catalytic-loop motif
- **DFG** motif, involved in Mg²⁺/ATP coordination and kinase activation

The exact physiological protein substrates cannot be established from sequence alone, but the strong full-length homology indicates an **Fgr-like Src-family kinase** involved in phosphorylation-dependent cellular signaling.
