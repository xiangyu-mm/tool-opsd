### Functional summary

This 471-amino-acid sequence is an **exact match** to reviewed *Debaryomyces hansenii* UniProt protein **Q6BVY9**, mitochondrial import inner membrane translocase subunit **TIM50**.

TIM50 is a component of the **TIM23 complex** in the mitochondrial inner membrane. The complex imports proteins carrying mitochondrial targeting sequences; TIM50 is annotated as helping recognize or direct incoming preproteins to the TIM23 channel, and may help transfer them from the TOM complex. The reference annotation also describes TIM50 as a single-pass membrane protein. These functional details are annotated as “by similarity,” so they should be read as reference-based evidence rather than direct experiments on this sequence.

**Properties:** estimated mass **54.2 kDa**; theoretical pI **6.11**. Pfam detects an **NIF domain** at residues **190–337**. The sequence-derived estimates do not account for processing or modifications.
