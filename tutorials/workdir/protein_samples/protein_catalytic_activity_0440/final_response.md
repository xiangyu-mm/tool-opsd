### Functional identification

The 174-residue protein is most consistent with a **Ham1p-like dITP/XTP pyrophosphatase** (**EC 3.6.1.66**). It contains a strong **Ham1p-like domain** (Pfam PF01725), and close reviewed homologs share approximately **47–54% sequence identity** over most of the sequence.

### Catalytic activity

The enzyme hydrolyzes noncanonical purine nucleoside triphosphates, cleaving the terminal pyrophosphate:

- **XTP + H₂O → XMP + pyrophosphate + H⁺**
- **dITP + H₂O → dIMP + pyrophosphate + H⁺**
- **ITP + H₂O → IMP + pyrophosphate + H⁺**

Its strongest predicted substrate preference is for **xanthosine triphosphate (XTP)** and **deoxyinosine triphosphate (dITP)**, with ITP also included in the annotated substrate range. The reaction is **Mg²⁺-dependent**; homologs are annotated to bind one Mg²⁺ ion per subunit.

### Biological role

This is likely a nucleotide-“house-cleaning” enzyme. By removing abnormal purine triphosphates from the nucleotide pool, it helps prevent their incorporation into DNA or RNA and thereby reduces potential mutagenic damage.
