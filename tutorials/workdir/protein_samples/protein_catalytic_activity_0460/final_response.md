### Functional assignment

The sequence is best identified as **phosphoenolpyruvate carboxylase (PEPC)**, **EC 4.1.1.31**.

Evidence:

- **768 aa** protein, approximately **85.96 kDa**
- Strong match to the **PEPcase domain (Pfam PF00311)** across nearly the entire sequence
- **100% sequence identity** over the full query length to reviewed *Pseudomonas aeruginosa* PEPC **UniProt Q9HXV3**
- Conserved annotation as a **Mg²⁺-dependent phosphoenolpyruvate carboxylase**

### Catalytic reaction

The physiological carboxylation reaction is:

\[
\text{Phosphoenolpyruvate} + \text{HCO}_3^- \rightarrow \text{Oxaloacetate} + \text{P}_i
\]

Thus, PEPC fixes bicarbonate onto phosphoenolpyruvate, producing the four-carbon metabolite **oxaloacetate** and inorganic phosphate. The reverse reaction is represented in Rhea as:

\[
\text{Oxaloacetate} + \text{phosphate} \rightarrow \text{phosphoenolpyruvate} + \text{hydrogencarbonate}
\]

### Biological role

The generated oxaloacetate can:

- Replenish tricarboxylic-acid-cycle intermediates (**anaplerosis**)
- Support oxaloacetate metabolism
- Contribute to carbon-fixation pathways

The enzyme is expected to be **cytosolic** and to require **Mg²⁺** for catalysis and/or substrate binding.

### Confidence and caveat

The functional assignment is **high confidence**, based on the exact match to a reviewed PEPC homolog and the matching Pfam domain and EC/Rhea annotation. However, the query is 768 aa whereas the referenced *P. aeruginosa* PEPC is 878 aa, so it may represent a shorter homolog or sequence fragment. The catalytic function is nevertheless strongly supported by the available sequence evidence.
