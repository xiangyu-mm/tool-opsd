- Functional evidence: **moderate homology support**

## Catalyzed reaction

The enzyme catalyzes **reversible phosphorolysis of nucleosides**:

\[
\text{nucleoside} + \text{phosphate}
\rightleftharpoons
\text{free nucleobase} + \alpha\text{-D-ribose-1-phosphate}
\]

Representative reactions include:

\[
\text{guanosine} + P_i
\rightleftharpoons
\text{guanine} + \alpha\text{-D-ribose-1-phosphate}
\]

\[
\text{adenosine} + P_i
\rightleftharpoons
\text{adenine} + \alpha\text{-D-ribose-1-phosphate}
\]

Related homologs are annotated with **EC 2.4.2.1** and **EC 2.4.2.2**, and can act on several purine and pyrimidine nucleosides, including guanosine, adenosine, inosine, uridine, cytidine, and thymidine.

## Functional summary

This protein likely participates in **nucleoside salvage and degradation**, cleaving the N-glycosidic bond of nucleosides by using inorganic phosphate rather than water. The products are a free base and ribose-1-phosphate; the reaction can also proceed in reverse to synthesize nucleosides from bases and ribose-1-phosphate.

The exact substrate range of this particular sequence cannot be established solely from homology, but the available evidence supports annotation as a **broad-specificity nucleoside phosphorylase**.
