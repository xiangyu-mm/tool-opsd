### Functional summary

This 633-residue, ~69.9 kDa protein is strongly predicted to be a **fused CysN/CysC (NodQ-like) sulfate-activating enzyme** involved in sulfur metabolism.

- **N-terminal region (~residues 20–430):** CysN-like sulfate adenylyltransferase-associated **P-loop GTPase module**, with GTP_EFTU/MMR_HSR1-related domains.
- **C-terminal region (~residues 461–609):** **adenosine-5′-phosphosulfate kinase (APS kinase)** domain.

Its likely sequential activities are:

1. **Sulfate activation:** sulfate → adenosine 5′-phosphosulfate (APS), associated with ATP sulfurylase activity.
2. **APS phosphorylation:** APS + ATP → 3′-phosphoadenosine-5′-phosphosulfate (PAPS).

The closest reviewed homolog, *Pseudomonas aeruginosa* CysN/CysC (UniProt **O50274**), shares **63% identity across the full sequence** and has the same bifunctional annotation. In that system, the CysN/CysC protein associates with the smaller **CysD** subunit to form the ATP sulfurylase complex; GTP hydrolysis by the CysN module is coupled to sulfate activation.
