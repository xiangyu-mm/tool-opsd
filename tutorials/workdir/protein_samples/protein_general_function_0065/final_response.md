### Functional summary

The 212-aa sequence is an **exact match** to the reviewed *Nitrobacter hamburgensis* protein **BioD** (UniProt **Q1QJL1**), so its identification as **ATP-dependent dethiobiotin synthetase** is strongly supported.

BioD catalyzes a step in **biotin biosynthesis**, using ATP and CO₂ to convert 7,8-diaminopelargonic acid (DAPA) into dethiobiotin by forming a ureido ring. The reaction is annotated as **EC 6.3.3.3** (Rhea: **15805**) and uses **Mg²⁺** as a cofactor. The reference annotation places the protein in the **cytoplasm**.

Other characteristics: predicted molecular mass **22.9 kDa**, theoretical pI **5.84**, and a Pfam **AAA_26** domain match spanning residues 4–191. These sequence-derived values are estimates; the functional assignment is especially confident because the sequence exactly matches the reviewed reference.
