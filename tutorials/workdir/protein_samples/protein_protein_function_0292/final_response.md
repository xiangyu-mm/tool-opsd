### Functional summary

This 79-amino-acid sequence is **NAD(P)H-quinone oxidoreductase subunit L (NdhL)**, a small, highly hydrophobic membrane protein. The strongest evidence is an **exact sequence match** to reviewed UniProt protein B0JWY5 from *Microcystis aeruginosa*, with additional strong matches to NdhL proteins from other cyanobacteria. A Pfam scan also identifies the **NdhL family domain (PF10716)** across most of the sequence.

NdhL is a component of **NDH-1**, a membrane complex involved in electron transfer to quinones and coupled proton translocation. In cyanobacteria, NDH-1 also contributes to inorganic carbon concentration. The sequence evidence supports identifying this protein as an NDH-1 subunit; it does not establish a distinct catalytic role for NdhL itself.

**Likely localization:** thylakoid membrane, as a multipass membrane-complex subunit.  
**Sequence properties:** 79 residues; strongly hydrophobic (GRAVY 0.74), consistent with membrane embedding.

The NDH-1 functions above come from annotations of the matched reference proteins, not from a direct functional assay of this sequence.
