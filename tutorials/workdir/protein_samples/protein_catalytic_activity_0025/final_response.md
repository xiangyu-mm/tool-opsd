Its sequence matches reviewed bacterial Nnr proteins across most of their length (32.9–36.2% identity; up to 98.8% query coverage), supporting the assignment, though not proving the function experimentally for this exact protein.

It is thought to repair hydrated, damaged forms of NADH and NADPH (NAD(P)HX) in two steps:

- **Epimerization:** converts the (6R) form to the (6S) form. **EC 5.1.99.6**; Rhea references include RHEA:32215 and RHEA:32227.
- **Dehydration and repair:** converts the (6S) form back to NADH or NADPH, coupled to ADP conversion to AMP:  
  **(6S)-NADHX + ADP → NADH + AMP + phosphate + H⁺**  
  The analogous reaction applies to NADPHX. **EC 4.2.1.136**; Rhea references include RHEA:32223 and RHEA:32235.
