## Summary

This is **FOXO1 (forkhead box protein O1)**. The 624-amino-acid sequence is an **exact full-length match** to reviewed bovine FOXO1 (UniProt **E1BPQ1**).

FOXO1 is a sequence-specific transcription factor that binds insulin-response and related forkhead DNA elements. It regulates metabolic and stress-response programs, including glucose homeostasis. Its activity and localization are regulated: insulin/AKT signaling can promote its export from the nucleus, while stress-associated signals can favor nuclear accumulation. It shuttles between the **nucleus and cytoplasm**.

The sequence contains a **Forkhead DNA-binding domain** (Pfam PF00250; residues 132–213), plus FOXO-associated KIX-binding and transactivation domains. It also has low-complexity/disordered regions, especially outside the DNA-binding domain.
