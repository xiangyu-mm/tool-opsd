### Functional identification

The sequence is **Ssu72**, an RNA polymerase II C-terminal-domain (CTD) phosphatase. This assignment is highly reliable because the sequence is an **exact match** to the reviewed *Aspergillus oryzae* Ssu72 protein (UniProt **Q2UPU5**), with 100% identity and coverage. Pfam analysis independently identifies the Ssu72 domain (**PF04722**) across residues 28–261.

### Catalytic activity

Ssu72 catalyzes **protein serine/threonine phosphatase activity**, with the best-supported physiological substrate being **Ser-5 within the YSPTSPS heptad repeats of the largest RNA polymerase II subunit**.

The reaction is:

\[
\text{RNA polymerase II CTD–O-phospho-Ser} + H_2O
\rightarrow
\text{RNA polymerase II CTD–Ser} + P_i
\]

where \(P_i\) is inorganic phosphate.

In more general form:

\[
\text{protein-O-phosphoserine/threonine} + H_2O
\rightarrow
\text{protein-OH} + \text{phosphate}
\]

The annotation corresponds to **EC 3.1.3.16**, protein-serine/threonine phosphatase activity. Ssu72 is reported to act **processively**, removing CTD Ser-5 phosphorylation during transcription termination and RNA 3′-end processing.

### Biological role

Ssu72 is a **nuclear** component of the **cleavage and polyadenylation factor complex**. By dephosphorylating the RNA polymerase II CTD, it contributes to:

- transcription termination,
- pre-mRNA 3′-end formation,
- mRNA cleavage and polyadenylation,
- processing of some noncoding RNAs such as snoRNAs.

**Summary:** This is a cysteine-dependent Ssu72 protein phosphatase that hydrolyzes the phosphate ester on RNA polymerase II CTD Ser-5 residues, converting phospho-Ser to Ser and releasing inorganic phosphate.
