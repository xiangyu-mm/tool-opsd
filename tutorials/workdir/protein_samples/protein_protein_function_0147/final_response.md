## Biochemical role

Pantothenate synthetase catalyzes the final step of bacterial pantothenate/vitamin B5 biosynthesis:

\[
(R)\text{-pantoate} + \beta\text{-alanine} + ATP
\rightarrow (R)\text{-pantothenate} + AMP + PP_i
\]

The reaction proceeds through a **pantoyl-adenylate intermediate**. Pantothenate is subsequently used to produce **coenzyme A** and **4′-phosphopantetheine**, so this enzyme supports central cofactor production and related metabolic processes.
