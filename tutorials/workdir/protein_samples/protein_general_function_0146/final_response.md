## Functional summary

This 768-amino-acid sequence is **delta-1-pyrroline-5-carboxylate synthase (P5CS; ALDH18A1)**, a bifunctional enzyme in amino-acid metabolism. The identification is strongly supported by a **100% full-query match to reviewed mouse P5CS (UniProt Q9Z110)** and **95.7% identity to reviewed human P5CS (P54886)**.

P5CS performs two consecutive steps converting glutamate to glutamate 5-semialdehyde:

1. **Glutamate + ATP → glutamyl 5-phosphate + ADP**  
   Glutamate 5-kinase activity (EC 2.7.2.11).
2. **Glutamyl 5-phosphate + NADPH → glutamate 5-semialdehyde + phosphate + NADP⁺**  
   Glutamate-5-semialdehyde dehydrogenase activity (EC 1.2.1.41).

This intermediate feeds **proline, ornithine, and arginine biosynthesis**.
