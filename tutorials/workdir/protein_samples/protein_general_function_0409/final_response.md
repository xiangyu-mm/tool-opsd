## Functional overview

The 589-aa sequence is an **exact sequence match** to the reviewed *Azoarcus* sp. BH72 protein **isocitrate dehydrogenase kinase/phosphatase** (UniProt A1K4K7). This supports a high-confidence assignment as a bifunctional regulator of isocitrate dehydrogenase (IDH):

- **Kinase activity:** transfers phosphate from ATP to a serine residue on IDH.
- **Phosphatase activity:** removes that phosphate, reversing the modification.
- **Role:** regulates IDH activity and carbon-flow balance between the TCA cycle and glyoxylate shunt, in response to carbon source.

The reference annotation places it in the **cytoplasm** and lists ATP- and AMP-binding annotations. Its sequence-derived molecular mass is approximately **68.2 kDa**, with a theoretical pI of **5.80**.

These functional details come from the exact-match reviewed reference annotation; the physical properties are sequence-based estimates and may differ for a processed or modified protein.
