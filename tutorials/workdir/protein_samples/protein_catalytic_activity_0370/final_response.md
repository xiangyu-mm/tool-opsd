## Catalytic activity

The enzyme hydrolyzes noncanonical purine nucleoside triphosphates, removing the diphosphate group and producing the corresponding nucleoside monophosphate:

\[
\mathrm{dITP + H_2O \rightarrow dIMP + PP_i}
\]

\[
\mathrm{ITP + H_2O \rightarrow IMP + PP_i}
\]

\[
\mathrm{XTP + H_2O \rightarrow XMP + PP_i}
\]

where:

- **dITP** = deoxyinosine triphosphate  
- **ITP** = inosine triphosphate  
- **XTP** = xanthosine triphosphate  
- **dIMP, IMP, XMP** = the corresponding monophosphates  
- **PPᵢ** = inorganic pyrophosphate  

Mg²⁺ likely coordinates the nucleotide phosphates and supports hydrolytic cleavage of the phosphoanhydride bond.

## Biological role

This is likely a **nucleotide-pool sanitizing enzyme**. By degrading aberrant purine triphosphates such as dITP, ITP, and XTP, it reduces their incorporation into DNA or RNA and thereby helps prevent replication or transcription errors.
