## Functional identification

The 209-amino-acid protein is **eukaryotic translation initiation factor 4E (eIF4E)**.

This assignment is highly confident because the sequence is an **exact sequence match** to the reviewed *Candida glabrata* UniProt protein **Q9P974**. It also contains the characteristic **IF4E domain, Pfam PF01652, spanning residues 34–204**.

### Main function

eIF4E is the mRNA cap-binding component of the **eIF4F translation-initiation complex**. It:

- Recognizes the **7-methylguanosine (m⁷G) cap** at the 5′ end of eukaryotic mRNAs.
- Helps recruit the translation machinery and ribosome to capped mRNAs.
- Promotes formation of the translation preinitiation complex.
- Functions together with eIF4A and eIF4G in cap-dependent translation initiation.

The direct role of eIF4E is cap recognition and recruitment; RNA unwinding is primarily carried out by the associated eIF4A helicase.

### Likely biological processes

The protein is expected to participate in:

- **Cap-dependent translational initiation**
- **Regulation of translation initiation**
- **Formation of the eIF4F complex**
- **mRNA recruitment to ribosomes**
- Potentially **mRNA surveillance/nonsense-mediated decay**
- In fungi, regulation of translation during changing environmental or cellular conditions

The latter processes are based on annotations from closely related yeast eIF4E proteins and should be considered broader biological associations rather than independently demonstrated properties of this sequence.

### Subcellular localization

The most likely locations are:

- **Cytoplasm**, where cap-dependent translation occurs
- **Nucleus**, consistent with yeast eIF4E annotations and shuttling between nuclear and cytoplasmic compartments
- Potential association with **cytoplasmic stress granules** under stress conditions

The sequence has no obvious long hydrophobic segment or N-terminal signal peptide, supporting a soluble intracellular protein rather than a membrane or secreted protein.

### Sequence properties

- Length: **209 residues**
- Predicted molecular mass: **23.9 kDa**
- Theoretical pI: **5.12**
- Predicted net charge at pH 7: approximately **−9**
- Hydrophilic, acidic protein overall
- IF4E domain: **residues 34–204**

## Summary

This sequence encodes a small, soluble fungal **eIF4E cap-binding translation-initiation factor**. It binds the 5′ m⁷G cap of mRNAs and functions in the eIF4F complex to recruit capped transcripts to the ribosome. Its primary location is likely the **cytoplasm**, with additional nuclear and stress-granule association possible.
