## Functional assignment

The sequence is most consistent with a **plant allene oxide cyclase (AOC)**, EC **5.3.99.6**.

Evidence:

- Length: **256 aa**
- Contains an **Allene_ox_cyc domain** (Pfam PF06351.17; residues ~82–254)
- Matches curated plant AOCs at approximately **71–73% sequence identity**
- Homologs are annotated in **jasmonate biosynthesis** and catalyze formation of OPDA

## Catalyzed reaction

The enzyme promotes intramolecular cyclization of a fatty-acid-derived allene oxide:

\[
\text{(9Z,13S,15Z)-12,13-epoxyoctadeca-9,11,15-trienoate}
\rightarrow
\text{(9S,13S,15Z)-12-oxophyto-10,15-dienoate}
\]

The product is **12-oxo-phytodienoic acid (OPDA)**, a precursor of jasmonic acid.

In biochemical terms, AOC converts a **12,13-allene oxide** into the cyclopentenone OPDA skeleton. This is a cyclization/rearrangement reaction and does not require an externally supplied redox cofactor such as NAD(P)H or FAD.

## Biological role

The predicted enzyme likely functions in the plastidial branch of **jasmonate biosynthesis**, contributing to production of OPDA during plant development and stress or wound responses.

This assignment is strongly supported by the domain and homolog comparisons, although the exact activity of this specific sequence remains a sequence-based inference rather than a direct enzymatic assay.
