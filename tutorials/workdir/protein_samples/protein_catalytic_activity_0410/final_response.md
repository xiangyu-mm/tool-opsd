## Functional identification

The protein is **ornithine carbamoyltransferase (OTC)**, EC **2.1.3.3**. The assignment is highly confident because the 313-residue sequence is an **exact full-length match** to the reviewed UniProt OTC protein Q7NGR7.

## Catalytic reaction

\[
\text{Carbamoyl phosphate} + \text{L-ornithine}
\rightleftharpoons
\text{L-citrulline} + \text{phosphate} + \mathrm{H^+}
\]

OTC transfers the **carbamoyl group** from carbamoyl phosphate to the **Nε amino group of L-ornithine**, producing L-citrulline.

## Functional context

- **Pathway:** L-arginine biosynthesis  
- **Pathway position:** First of three steps from L-ornithine and carbamoyl phosphate to L-arginine  
- **Localization:** Cytoplasm  
- **Oligomeric state:** Typically a homohexamer  
- **Cofactor:** No separate metal or organic cofactor is indicated for the transfer reaction  
- **Supporting domains:** OTCace_N and OTCace Pfam domains

Thus, this enzyme catalyzes the ATP-independent **carbamoylation of ornithine to form citrulline**, a central step in arginine biosynthesis.
