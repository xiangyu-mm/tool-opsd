### Functional overview

This 445-amino-acid sequence is an **exact sequence match** to reviewed *Bacillus subtilis* BraB (UniProt **O34545**), a branched-chain amino-acid permease. It is therefore best identified as a **multi-pass membrane transporter**, not a respiratory-chain component.

BraB is annotated as participating in uptake of **isoleucine and valine, and probably leucine**. In *B. subtilis*, it also contributes—along with BcaP and BrnQ—to activation of the amino-acid-responsive regulator CodY. The strong Pfam **Branch_AA_trans** match (PF05525; residues 8–432) supports this assignment.

**Sequence attributes:** 445 aa; estimated molecular mass **46.6 kDa**; theoretical pI **9.41**; GRAVY **1.03**, consistent with a highly hydrophobic membrane protein. The transport substrate range above comes from the reviewed reference annotation; “probably leucine” is less certain than isoleucine and valine.
