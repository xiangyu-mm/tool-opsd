## Functional summary

The 362-aa sequence is an **exact match** to reviewed yeast protein **YIM1** from *Vanderwaltozyma polyspora*. It contains Pfam **ADH_N** (residues 31–91) and **ADH_zinc_N_2** (residues 207–359) domains, consistent with an alcohol-dehydrogenase-like protein.

The reference annotation assigns **NAD(P)⁺-dependent alcohol dehydrogenase activity** and reports localization to **lipid droplets and mitochondria**. It is also associated with the **DNA damage response**. Close *Saccharomyces cerevisiae* YIM1 homologs have the same reported localizations.

These functional and localization annotations are computationally supported; the available results do not specify a substrate, reaction equation, or experimentally established role. Thus, the safest interpretation is that this is a YIM1-family alcohol-dehydrogenase-like protein, potentially involved in redox metabolism, with a possible connection to DNA-damage response.
