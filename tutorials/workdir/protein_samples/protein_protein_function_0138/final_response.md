### Functional prediction

This sequence is **PLASMODESMATA CALLOSE-BINDING PROTEIN 3 (PDCB3)** from *Arabidopsis thaliana* (UniProt **Q9FZ86**). The identification is highly confident because the sequence is an exact 100% match to the reviewed reference protein.

- **N-terminal signal peptide:**

  Directs the protein into the secretory pathway.

- **Extracellular cysteine-rich region:**  
  Residues approximately 20–91 contain multiple cysteines and a strongly supported **X8 domain** (Pfam PF07983). This domain is characteristic of carbohydrate- and polysaccharide-binding proteins.

- **Serine/threonine/proline-rich low-complexity region:**  
  The central region is rich in S, T, and P residues, consistent with an exposed, flexible, and likely glycosylated extracellular stalk.

- **C-terminal hydrophobic segment:**  
  The final approximately 20 residues form a hydrophobic tail characteristic of a **GPI-anchor signal**. Thus, the protein is expected to be attached to the outer leaflet of the plasma membrane rather than functioning as a typical cytosolic transmembrane protein.

#### Predicted biological role

PDCB3 is an **extracellular, GPI-anchored plant plasma-membrane protein localized to plasmodesmata**. It binds **callose**, a β-1,3-glucan deposited around plasmodesmata, and is therefore likely involved in organizing or sensing the callose-rich plasmodesmal cell wall environment.

**Summary:** a small secretory-pathway protein with an extracellular X8 carbohydrate-binding domain and a C-terminal GPI anchor, functioning as a **plasmodesmal callose/polysaccharide-binding protein**. The sequence supports callose binding and plasmodesmal localization directly through the exact reference match; a more specific role in regulating plasmodesmal permeability is plausible but not established solely from this sequence.
