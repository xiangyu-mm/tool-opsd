## Functional summary

This 244-amino-acid protein is **ribosomal RNA small-subunit methyltransferase G (GidB)**.

### Key evidence

- **Exact sequence match:** The sequence is identical to curated UniProt protein **C1D0A7** from *Deinococcus deserti*.
- **Molecular function:** Site-specific methylation of **guanine 527 in bacterial 16S rRNA** at the **N7 position**.
- **Reaction:**

  \[
  \text{16S rRNA-G527} + \text{SAM}
  \rightarrow
  \text{16S rRNA-m}^7\text{G527} + \text{SAH}

  Here, SAM is **S-adenosyl-L-methionine**, the methyl donor, and SAH is S-adenosyl-L-homocysteine.

### Overall interpretation

The protein is a **bacterial GidB rRNA methyltransferase involved in 30S ribosomal subunit maturation and function**. Its sequence, domain architecture, and exact curated homology assignment provide very strong evidence for this function.

The analysis does **not** establish a specific oligomeric state, detailed catalytic mechanism, or experimentally measured kinetic parameters. No transmembrane segment or secretion signal is apparent, supporting a cytosolic role.
