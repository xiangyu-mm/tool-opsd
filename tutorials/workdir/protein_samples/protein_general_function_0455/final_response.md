### Functional summary

The 89-residue sequence is an **exact match to human islet amyloid polypeptide precursor (IAPP/amylin; UniProt P10997)**.

- **Type:** Secreted peptide hormone precursor  
- **Length / mass:** 89 residues; approximately **9.81 kDa** unprocessed  
- **Localization:** Secretory pathway and extracellular space  
- **Family:** Calcitonin/CGRP/IAPP peptide family; Pfam **Calc_CGRP_IAPP** domain  
- **Function:** After processing, mature amylin regulates glucose and energy homeostasis, including inhibition of insulin-stimulated glucose utilization, and signals through **calcitonin receptor–RAMP amylin receptor complexes**.  
- **Sequence features:** N-terminal signal peptide; basic character with predicted pI ≈ **9.83**. The precursor is proteolytically processed to mature amylin, a disulfide-containing peptide hormone.

Thus, this is not a conventional globular enzyme but a **secreted endocrine signaling peptide precursor**.
