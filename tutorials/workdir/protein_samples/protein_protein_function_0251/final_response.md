## Functional summary

The 768-aa sequence is an exact match to the reviewed *Debaryomyces hansenii* Set1 protein (UniProt **Q6BKL7**): 100% sequence identity across 100% of the supplied sequence. The reference is annotated as a histone-lysine N-methyltransferase specific for histone H3 lysine 4 (H3K4).

**Likely function:** Set1 is the catalytic component of the Set1C/COMPASS complex. It methylates H3K4 to produce mono-, di-, and trimethylated forms (H3K4me1, H3K4me2, and H3K4me3). This chromatin modification places the protein in transcription- and chromatin-associated processes. The reference annotation also reports RNA binding, which may negatively affect its methyltransferase activity; treat this as an annotation rather than an experimentally verified property of the submitted protein.

**Localization and complex:** The matched reference is annotated to the **nucleus** and **chromosome**, and as a component of the **Set1C/COMPASS complex**. The reference describes COMPASS recognition of ubiquitinated H2B as stimulating H3 methylation. These are reference-derived annotations, not localization or interaction measurements made directly on the supplied sample.

**Sequence features and estimates:** The sequence contains Pfam matches to **SET1_RBD** (residues 251–338) and **SET_assoc** (residues 438–503), consistent with a Set1-family protein. Estimated molecular mass is about **87.2 kDa** and theoretical pI is **9.10**; these are sequence-based estimates.

**Confidence and limitation:** The identification is strongly supported by the exact match to a reviewed protein reference. Functional, localization, and complex annotations are transferred from that reference; the analysis itself does not experimentally test activity, binding, localization, or biological effects.
