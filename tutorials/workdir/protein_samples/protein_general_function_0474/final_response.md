#### Likely biochemical activities

The protein is expected to catalyze two reactions:

1. **Heptose-7-phosphate kinase activity**  
   D-glycero-β-D-manno-heptose-7-phosphate + ATP  
   → D-glycero-β-D-manno-heptose-1,7-bisphosphate + ADP  
   **EC 2.7.1.167; Rhea RHEA:27473**

2. **Heptose-1-phosphate adenylyltransferase activity**  
   D-glycero-β-D-manno-heptose-1-phosphate + ATP  
   → ADP-D-glycero-β-D-manno-heptose + pyrophosphate  
   **EC 2.7.7.70; Rhea RHEA:27465**

Thus, it likely performs two nonconsecutive steps in the biosynthesis of **ADP-L-glycero-β-D-manno-heptose**, a nucleotide sugar associated with bacterial lipopolysaccharide/core oligosaccharide biosynthesis.

- **Cofactor/substrate:** ATP is required; no additional metal or organic cofactor is firmly established from the available evidence

### Functional summary

This 482-residue acidic soluble enzyme is very likely a **bifunctional HldE protein**. Its N-terminal kinase-like region and C-terminal nucleotidyltransferase-like region support dual activity in **ADP-heptose biosynthesis**. The assignment is supported by strong full-length homology to reviewed HldE proteins and is more reliable than a domain-only prediction, although experimental confirmation would still be required to establish substrate specificity and kinetic properties for this exact sequence.
