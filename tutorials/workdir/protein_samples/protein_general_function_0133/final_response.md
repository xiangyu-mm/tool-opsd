## Functional identification

The sequence is **SepF, a bacterial cell-division protein**. This assignment is especially strong because it is an **exact sequence match** to reviewed UniProt protein **A0LTN7** from *Acidothermus cellulolyticus*, with 100% identity and complete alignment coverage. Strong homologs are also annotated as SepF in *Thermobifida fusca* and *Frankia casuarinae*.

SepF is a component of the **divisome**. It is recruited early to the **FtsZ Z-ring**, where it helps organize or stabilize the division machinery and may promote cross-linking of FtsZ protofilaments. The protein is reported to form a **homodimer** and interact with **FtsZ**.

### Biological roles

- **FtsZ-dependent cytokinesis**
- **Cell septum assembly**
- **Z-ring/divisome organization**
- Cytoplasmic localization with FtsZ-dependent recruitment to the division site

**Overall conclusion:** This is a small, soluble bacterial **SepF cell-division protein**, likely functioning as a homodimeric FtsZ-associated factor that promotes proper Z-ring organization and septum formation.
