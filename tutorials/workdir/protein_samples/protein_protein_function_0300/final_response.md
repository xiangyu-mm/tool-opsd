## Functional role

uL16m is a structural and RNA-binding component of the **39S mitochondrial large ribosomal subunit**. Its principal roles are likely to:

- Bind mitochondrial ribosomal RNA
- Stabilize and organize the mitochondrial large subunit
- Contribute to assembly and function of the mitochondrial ribosome
- Support translation of mitochondrially encoded proteins

It is therefore involved in **mitochondrial translation and mitochondrial gene expression**, rather than acting as a metabolic enzyme. No catalytic reaction or cofactor requirement was identified.
