### Functional summary

The protein is a **major structural component of the PM2 bacteriophage capsid**, where it likely forms homotrimeric capsid building blocks. It is not expected to be a membrane, secreted, or periplasmic protein. The sequence has no evident long hydrophobic transmembrane segment or classical N-terminal secretion signal.

Strictly speaking, “virion” is not a cellular compartment. During synthesis before virion assembly, the protein would be produced in the infected host cell, but the strongest evidence identifies its predominant final residence as the **phage particle/capsid**, rather than the host cytoplasm or a cellular membrane.
