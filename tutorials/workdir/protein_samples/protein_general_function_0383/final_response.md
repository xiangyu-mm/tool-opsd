## Functional overview

The 570-amino-acid sequence is an **exact, full-length match** to reviewed *Arabidopsis thaliana* protein **NRT1/PTR FAMILY 8.2 (UniProt Q9LFB8)**. This makes the functional assignment unusually strong.

- **Likely function:** High-affinity transporter of di- and tripeptides across membranes.
- **Biological context:** Annotated as involved in peptide uptake during pollen germination and pollen-tube growth.
- **Localization and architecture:** Multi-pass membrane protein, annotated at the cell/plasma membrane. The sequence scan detects a **PTR2 domain (PF00854)** at residues 97–526.
- **Sequence-derived properties:** Predicted molecular mass ~63.3 kDa; theoretical pI ~8.15. These are calculated estimates, not experimental measurements.

The protein-tool results also show related *Arabidopsis* peptide transporters with similar annotations, but the exact sequence match to Q9LFB8 is the most relevant evidence. These findings support the transporter assignment; they do not independently establish transport kinetics or substrate selectivity beyond the curated annotation.
