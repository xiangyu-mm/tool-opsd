## Molecular function

GcpE/IspG is an **iron–sulfur-dependent reductive enzyme** that catalyzes the conversion of:

\[
\text{2-C-methyl-D-erythritol 2,4-cyclodiphosphate (ME-cPP)}
\rightarrow
\text{HMBPP}
\]

where HMBPP is **1-hydroxy-2-methyl-2-(E)-butenyl 4-diphosphate**.

The enzyme is assigned:

- **EC number:** 1.17.7.3
- **Cofactor:** one **[4Fe–4S] cluster**
- **Electron donor system:** reduced flavodoxin or ferredoxin

The [4Fe–4S] cluster is essential for the reductive chemistry performed by IspG.

## Biological pathway

The protein functions in the **methylerythritol 4-phosphate (MEP/DOXP) pathway** of isoprenoid biosynthesis. It performs the **penultimate enzymatic step**, converting ME-cPP into HMBPP.

HMBPP is subsequently converted by **IspH/LytB** into the universal isoprenoid precursors:

- Isopentenyl diphosphate (IPP)
- Dimethylallyl diphosphate (DMAPP)

These precursors support synthesis of bacterial and plastidial terpenoids, including components of membranes, quinones, pigments, and other isoprenoid-derived metabolites.
