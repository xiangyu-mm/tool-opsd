## Functional summary

It is annotated as a **c-di-GMP synthase** that uses two GTP molecules to produce cyclic di-GMP:

\[
2\ \mathrm{GTP} \rightarrow 3',3'\text{-c-di-GMP} + 2\ \mathrm{diphosphate}

- **Reaction database:** Rhea RHEA:24898  
- **Biological role:** Produces a cyclic nucleotide second messenger during CBASS-mediated phage defense. The signal activates downstream effectors that can induce abortive infection and bacterial cell death.
