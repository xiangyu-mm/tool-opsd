## Functional summary

The 34-residue sequence is most consistent with **Photosystem II reaction-center protein Psb30**, also called **Ycf12**.

### Evidence

- **Length:** 34 amino acids  
- **Hydrophobicity:** strongly hydrophobic, with a likely single transmembrane helix  
- **Predicted location:** chloroplast thylakoid membrane  
- **Pfam match:** PSII_Ycf12 domain spanning the complete sequence  
- **Homology:** exact sequence match to reviewed Psb30 protein Q6B8L0; related proteins show ~73.5% identity across the full sequence  
- **Estimated mass:** ~3.65 kDa  

### Biological role

Psb30/Ycf12 is a **small structural subunit of Photosystem II**. It likely helps **stabilize the Photosystem II reaction center** within the thylakoid membrane rather than catalyzing an independent enzymatic reaction. Therefore, its likely biological-process involvement is in **oxygenic photosynthesis and photosynthetic electron transport**.

**Confidence: high**, because the sequence exactly matches an annotated Psb30 protein and independently matches the corresponding Pfam family.
