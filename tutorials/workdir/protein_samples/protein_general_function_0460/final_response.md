### Function

PsbJ is an accessory component of the **photosystem II reaction-center complex**. It is not an enzyme and is unlikely to perform catalysis independently. Its small hydrophobic structure allows it to be embedded within the photosystem II membrane complex, where it may contribute to complex organization or **photosystem II biogenesis and assembly**.

Photosystem II uses light energy to oxidize water, release oxygen, generate a proton gradient, and initiate photosynthetic electron transfer. The sequence itself supports membrane integration very strongly, while the exact role in photosystem II is established by the exact annotated sequence match and homologous protein evidence.

**Overall confidence: high.**
