### Functional assignment

The 154-residue protein is a **Cu/Zn superoxide dismutase (Cu/Zn SOD)**, EC **1.15.1.1**.

Evidence:

- Strong matches to reviewed fungal Cu/Zn SODs: **82.5–85.7% identity over the full sequence**.
- Pfam identifies the characteristic **Sod_Cu domain (PF00080)** across residues 14–150.
- Homologs bind one **copper ion** and one **zinc ion** per subunit.

### Catalyzed reaction

\[
\boxed{2\,\mathrm{O_2^{\bullet-}} + 2\,\mathrm{H^+}
\rightarrow \mathrm{H_2O_2} + \mathrm{O_2}}
\]

The enzyme removes toxic superoxide radicals by disproportionation. The copper center cycles between oxidation states during catalysis, while zinc primarily contributes structural stabilization.

**Conclusion:** This sequence encodes a compact, apparently complete **copper/zinc superoxide dismutase**, involved in cellular defense against oxidative stress.
