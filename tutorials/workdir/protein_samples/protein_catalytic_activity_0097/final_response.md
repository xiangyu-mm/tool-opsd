## Functional summary

The 297-amino-acid sequence is an **exact full-length match** to reviewed *Mycobacterium bovis* UniProt entries for **phosphoribosylaminoimidazole-succinocarboxamide synthase** (PurC; **EC 6.3.2.6**). The enzyme participates in **de novo IMP (purine) biosynthesis**.

It catalyzes an ATP-dependent ligation of L-aspartate to the purine intermediate carboxylate, forming SAICAR:

**5-amino-1-(5-phospho-D-ribosyl)imidazole-4-carboxylate + L-aspartate + ATP → (2S)-2-[5-amino-1-(5-phospho-β-D-ribosyl)imidazole-4-carboxamido]succinate + ADP + phosphate + 2 H⁺**

The product is **SAICAR** (succinylaminoimidazole carboxamide ribotide). The annotation supports this reaction and pathway assignment; no specific cofactor was reported in the retrieved results.
