The 445-residue sequence is an **exact sequence match** to the reviewed *Candida glabrata* protein **SVP1-like protein 2** (UniProt **Q6FXC1**; 100% identity and coverage). The annotation is therefore highly reliable.

### Likely molecular function

SVP1-like protein 2 is a **phosphoinositide-binding membrane adaptor/scaffold**. It is associated with:

- Vacuolar and endosomal membranes
- Cytoplasmic vesicle membranes
- Phagophore assembly sites
- Selective autophagy-related membrane trafficking

The protein is annotated to bind several phosphoinositides, including:

- PI3P
- PI(3,5)P₂
- PI4P
- PI5P

These lipid interactions likely help recruit the protein to specific endosomal or vacuolar membranes.

### Biological role

In *Saccharomyces cerevisiae*, the ortholog participates in **piecemeal microautophagy of the nucleus** and related selective autophagy processes, including mitochondrial, glycogen, and peroxisome-related autophagy. The *Candida glabrata* annotation additionally links the protein to mitochondrial/peroxisomal functions and amino-acid signaling.

### Enzymatic activity

No catalytic reaction, enzyme-class annotation, or cofactor requirement was retrieved. The sequence and domain evidence support a **nonenzymatic lipid-binding adaptor/scaffold**, not a metabolic enzyme. Consequently, no catalytic residues or reaction mechanism should be assigned from this sequence.

### Overall conclusion

This protein is best identified as a **yeast SVP1-like phosphoinositide-binding protein involved in endosomal/vacuolar membrane organization and selective autophagy**, especially piecemeal microautophagy of the nucleus. The functional assignment is strong because the sequence exactly matches a reviewed protein, although the precise biological roles may vary among fungal species.
