### Catalytic activity

The 201-aa sequence is an **exact match** to the reviewed *Mycobacterium ulcerans* protein A0PU27, annotated as a **probable molybdenum cofactor guanylyltransferase** (EC **2.7.7.77**).

It transfers a guanylyl (GMP) group from GTP to molybdopterin, producing the guanine dinucleotide form of the molybdenum cofactor:

**Mo-molybdopterin + GTP + H⁺ → Mo-molybdopterin guanine dinucleotide + diphosphate**

Thus, the enzyme helps form **Mo-MGD**, a molybdenum cofactor used by molybdoenzymes. The annotation is supported by an exact sequence match to the reviewed reference; the reference describes the enzyme as “probable,” so this sequence analysis alone does not establish direct experimental validation for the submitted protein.
