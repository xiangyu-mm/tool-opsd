## Functional identification

The 213-residue protein is **octanoyltransferase (LipB)**, **EC 2.3.1.181**. This assignment is strongly supported by an exact full-length sequence match to reviewed *E. coli* octanoyltransferases (100% identity and coverage; E-value \(7.3\times10^{-156}\)).

## Catalyzed reaction

LipB transfers an octanoyl group from octanoyl-acyl carrier protein to a specific lysine residue within the lipoyl domain of a lipoate-dependent enzyme:

\[
\text{octanoyl-ACP} + \text{apo-lipoyl-domain lysine}
\rightarrow
\text{N}^{6}\text{-octanoyl-lysyl-lipoyl-domain} + \text{ACP}
\]

This octanoylated lysine is subsequently converted to the mature **lipoyl-lysine** cofactor by LipA-dependent sulfur insertion. Thus, LipB catalyzes the first committed step of endogenous protein lipoylation.

- **Localization:** cytoplasm

- **Biological role:** protein lipoylation, particularly of enzymes such as pyruvate dehydrogenase and 2-oxoglutarate dehydrogenase complexes
