## Functional identification

This 329-residue protein is **glycerol-3-phosphate dehydrogenase [NAD(P)+]** (**EC 1.1.1.94**), also called **G3P dehydrogenase**.

The assignment is very strong: the sequence is an **exact match** to reviewed UniProt protein Q47IF9 and contains the characteristic **NAD_Gly3P_dh_N** and **NAD_Gly3P_dh_C** domains.

## Catalytic reaction

The enzyme reversibly interconverts dihydroxyacetone phosphate and glycerol 3-phosphate:

**Reductive direction:**

\[
\text{Dihydroxyacetone phosphate} + \text{NAD(P)H} + H^+
\rightarrow
\text{sn-glycerol 3-phosphate} + \text{NAD(P)}^+
\]

**Oxidative direction:**

\[
\text{sn-glycerol 3-phosphate} + \text{NAD(P)}^+
\rightarrow
\text{dihydroxyacetone phosphate} + \text{NAD(P)H} + H^+
\]

Chemically, it performs **NAD(P)H-dependent reduction of the ketone group of dihydroxyacetone phosphate to the alcohol group of sn-glycerol 3-phosphate**.

## Biological role

The reductive reaction produces **sn-glycerol 3-phosphate**, a central precursor for **glycerophospholipid and membrane-lipid biosynthesis**. The enzyme is annotated as **cytosolic** and can use **NADH and/or NADPH**; the exact cofactor preference may depend on the organism and experimental conditions.

The short hydrophobic segment near the N-terminus does not override the strong curated annotation and exact sequence match indicating a soluble cytosolic enzyme; it is therefore unlikely to represent a cleaved secretion signal in this protein.

**Overall confidence: very high.**
