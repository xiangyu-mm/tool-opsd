## Functional assignment

The 93-residue protein is most consistent with a **bacterial pyrimidine/purine nucleoside phosphorylase** of the **Ppnp family**.

Evidence:

- Strong Pfam match to the Ppnp domain: **PF06865**, residues 3–92, i-Evalue \(1.3 \times 10^{-35}\).
- Approximately **68.5% sequence identity** over nearly the full length to reviewed Swiss-Prot nucleoside phosphorylases.
- Closely matching homologs are annotated with **EC 2.4.2.1** and **EC 2.4.2.2** activities.
- The protein is predicted to be a small soluble/cytosolic enzyme of approximately **10 kDa**.

## Catalytic reaction

The enzyme catalyzes reversible phosphorolysis of nucleosides:

\[
\text{nucleoside} + \text{orthophosphate}
\rightleftharpoons
\text{free nucleobase} + \alpha\text{-D-ribose-1-phosphate}
\]

Representative reactions include:

\[
\text{guanosine} + P_i
\rightleftharpoons
\text{guanine} + \alpha\text{-D-ribose-1-phosphate}
\]

and analogous reactions involving adenosine, inosine, cytidine, uridine, thymidine, and related nucleosides.

Thus, the enzyme participates in **nucleoside salvage and nucleotide metabolism**, converting nucleosides into free bases and ribose-1-phosphate. The reverse reaction can synthesize nucleosides from a nucleobase and ribose-1-phosphate.
