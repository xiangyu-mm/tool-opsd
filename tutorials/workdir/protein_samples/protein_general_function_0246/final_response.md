## Functional summary

The sequence is **large ribosomal subunit protein bL25**. It is **204 amino acids** long and exactly matches the reviewed *Rhizobium meliloti* protein Q92N68 over its full length, strongly supporting this identification.

bL25 binds **5S rRNA** and contributes to the **central protuberance of the large ribosomal subunit**. Its annotations support a role as a ribosomal structural component involved in **translation**.

- Estimated molecular mass: **22.16 kDa**
- Theoretical pI: **5.10**
- Pfam domains: **Ribosomal_L25p** (residues 7–94) and **Ribosomal_TL5_C** (residues 103–186)
- GO annotations: 5S rRNA binding; structural constituent of ribosome; cytosolic large ribosomal subunit; translation

These physicochemical values are sequence-based estimates; they do not account for modifications or the protein’s assembled state in the ribosome.
