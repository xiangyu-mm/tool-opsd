## Catalytic activity

The enzyme hydrolytically removes the amino group from the cytosine base of **deoxycytidine triphosphate (dCTP)**, converting it into **deoxyuridine triphosphate (dUTP)** and releasing ammonia/ammonium.

\mathrm{dCTP + H_2O + H^+ \rightarrow dUTP + NH_4^+}
\]

## Biological role

The reaction supplies dUTP for the **dUTP route of dUMP biosynthesis**:

\[
\mathrm{dCTP \rightarrow dUTP \rightarrow dUMP}
\]

Thus, the protein helps regulate pyrimidine nucleotide composition and contributes to production of dUMP, a precursor for thymidylate metabolism.

**Overall conclusion:** this sequence encodes a small archaeal **dCTP deaminase**, not a general cytidine deaminase or canonical dUTPase.
