**Identified enzyme:** ADP-L-glycero-D-manno-heptose-6-epimerase (EC 5.1.3.20).

**Reaction it catalyzes:**

**ADP-D-glycero-β-D-manno-heptose ⇌ ADP-L-glycero-β-D-manno-heptose**

The enzyme interconverts these nucleotide sugars by changing the stereochemistry at carbon 6 of the heptose (a reversible epimerization). It is annotated as binding NADP⁺, one molecule per subunit. The enzyme participates in nucleotide-sugar biosynthesis.
