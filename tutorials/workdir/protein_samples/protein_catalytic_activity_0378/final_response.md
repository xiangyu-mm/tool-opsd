### Functional identification

The sequence corresponds to a **secreted plant acidic endochitinase**, matching the reviewed *Beta vulgaris* protein **UniProt P36910** with **100% sequence identity and coverage**.

- **Enzyme class:** Glycoside hydrolase family 18 (GH18; Pfam PF00704)
- **EC number:** **EC 3.2.1.14**
- **Localization:** Secreted/extracellular
- **Signal peptide:** Approximately residues 1–25
- **Mature enzyme:** Approximately residues 26–293
- **Likely biological role:** Defense against chitin-containing fungal pathogens

### Catalyzed reaction

The enzyme performs **endo-hydrolysis of β-(1→4)-linked N-acetyl-D-glucosamine residues in chitin and chitodextrins**:

\[
\text{Chitin}_{n} + H_2O
\rightarrow
\text{shorter chitin oligosaccharides}
\]

More explicitly:

\[
(\mathrm{GlcNAc})_n + H_2O
\rightarrow
(\mathrm{GlcNAc})_i + (\mathrm{GlcNAc})_{n-i}
\]

where **GlcNAc** is **N-acetyl-D-glucosamine**.

The cleavage is **endo-type**, meaning the enzyme cuts internal β-(1→4) glycosidic bonds rather than removing residues exclusively from a chain end. The curated annotation also reports some **exochitinase activity**, including hydrolysis of chito-oligosaccharides such as chitobiose.

**Conclusion:** This is a secreted GH18 chitinase that cleaves chitin into shorter N-acetylglucosamine-containing oligosaccharides, contributing to antifungal plant defense.
