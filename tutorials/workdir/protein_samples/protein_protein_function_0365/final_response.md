## Biological process

The protein functions in the **methylerythritol 4-phosphate (MEP) pathway**, also known as the **non-mevalonate pathway** of isoprenoid biosynthesis.

It catalyzes the **first committed step** of the six-step pathway leading to **isopentenyl diphosphate (IPP)** and related isoprenoid precursors. These precursors are used to synthesize bacterial quinones, cell-envelope components, carotenoids, and other isoprenoid metabolites.
