**Identity:** The sequence is an exact match to reviewed *Arabidopsis thaliana* protein Q9M7Z1, the mitochondrial E2 lipoamide acyltransferase of the branched-chain α-ketoacid dehydrogenase (BCKDH) complex (EC 2.3.1.168).

**Catalytic activity:** E2 transfers a branched-chain acyl group between its lipoyl arm and coenzyme A. In the productive direction of the complex, the reaction is:

**S-acyl-dihydrolipoyl-E2 + CoA-SH ⇌ dihydrolipoyl-E2 + acyl-CoA**

Examples of acyl groups include isobutyryl, isovaleryl, and 2-methylbutanoyl. The annotation’s reaction records also show the reversible transfer in the opposite direction. E2 performs this acyl transfer; the overall conversion of α-ketoacids to acyl-CoA and CO₂ involves the other BCKDH components as well.
