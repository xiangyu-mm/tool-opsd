## Functional identification

The 150-residue protein is **protein-arginine phosphatase**, corresponding to the reviewed *Bacillus subtilis* protein **P39155**. The sequence is an **exact 100% match** to that annotated protein and contains an **LMWPc low-molecular-weight protein phosphatase domain** spanning almost the entire sequence.

### Catalyzed reaction

\[
\text{N}^{\omega}\text{-phospho-L-arginyl-[protein]} + \mathrm{H_2O}
\rightarrow
\text{L-arginyl-[protein]} + \mathrm{P_i}
\]

In words, the enzyme hydrolytically removes the phosphate group from **protein phosphoarginine residues**, regenerating arginine residues and releasing inorganic phosphate.

- **EC:** 3.9.1.2  
- **Rhea:** RHEA:43380  
- **Primary activity:** protein arginine dephosphorylation  
- **Substrate specificity:** strong preference for phosphoarginine; little activity toward phosphoserine, phosphothreonine, or phosphotyrosine substrates.

### Biological role

This phosphatase counteracts the protein arginine kinase **McsB**. In *B. subtilis*, it can reverse McsB-mediated phosphorylation of substrates such as the stress-response regulator **CtsR**, thereby restoring their unphosphorylated functional state.

**Summary:** The protein is a small LMW protein phosphatase specialized for reversing **protein arginine phosphorylation**, rather than a general serine/threonine or tyrosine phosphatase.
