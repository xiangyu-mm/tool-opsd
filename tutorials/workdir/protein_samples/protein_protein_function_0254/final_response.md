## Functional prediction

This 310-aa protein is **YihG, a bacterial 1-acylglycerol-3-phosphate O-acyltransferase** involved in membrane phospholipid biosynthesis.

### Evidence

- **Exact sequence match:** 100% identity and coverage to reviewed *E. coli* YihG, UniProt **P32129**.
- **Membrane topology:** The N-terminal region is strongly hydrophobic and is annotated as a membrane anchor, consistent with an **inner-membrane-associated enzyme**.
- **Catalytic domain:** A Pfam **Acyltransferase domain (PF01553)** occurs at approximately residues **84–214**.
- **Enzyme classification:** **EC 2.3.1.51**; the reaction is mapped to **Rhea RHEA:19709**.

### Predicted reaction

\[
\text{1-acyl-sn-glycero-3-phosphate} + \text{acyl-CoA}
\rightarrow
\text{1,2-diacyl-sn-glycero-3-phosphate} + \text{CoA}
\]

Thus, YihG adds an acyl chain—reported for *E. coli* to include cis-vaccenoyl (18:1)—to the **sn-2 position** of a lysophosphatidic-acid-like substrate during phospholipid synthesis.

### Structural interpretation

The protein is not most consistent with a receptor or transporter. Rather, it has:

1. An N-terminal hydrophobic membrane-associated segment that positions it within the bacterial inner membrane.
2. A downstream acyltransferase catalytic region exposed to the relevant membrane-side substrate pool.
3. A C-terminal region compatible with a soluble catalytic portion rather than a multi-pass transport channel.

### Summary

**The sequence encodes YihG, an inner-membrane bacterial phospholipid acyltransferase that converts 1-acyl-glycerol-3-phosphate to phosphatidic acid using acyl-CoA.** Its likely biological role is to modify membrane fatty-acyl composition and support bacterial membrane biogenesis.
