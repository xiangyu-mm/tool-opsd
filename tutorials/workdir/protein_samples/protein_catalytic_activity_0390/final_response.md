### Functional assignment

The 103-residue protein is most consistent with a **pyrimidine/purine nucleoside phosphorylase** of the Ppnp family.

### Catalyzed reaction

\[
\text{nucleoside} + \text{phosphate}
\rightleftharpoons
\text{free nucleobase} + \alpha\text{-D-ribose-1-phosphate}
\]

Examples include:

- Adenosine → adenine + ribose-1-phosphate  
- Guanosine → guanine + ribose-1-phosphate  
- Inosine → hypoxanthine + ribose-1-phosphate  
- Cytidine, uridine, and thymidine can also be substrates in closely related enzymes.

### Evidence

- The sequence contains a strong **Ppnp domain** prediction spanning residues 12–101.
- It matches reviewed nucleoside phosphorylases over nearly the entire sequence:
  - **75% identity**, 100% coverage to UniProt Q31FE7
  - Approximately **64% identity** to additional reviewed homologs
- Homologs are annotated with **EC 2.4.2.1 and EC 2.4.2.2**, corresponding to purine- and pyrimidine-nucleoside phosphorylase activities.

Thus, the enzyme likely participates in **nucleoside salvage**, reversibly cleaving nucleosides using inorganic phosphate and releasing the corresponding base plus ribose-1-phosphate. Exact substrate preference for this particular sequence remains inferred rather than experimentally demonstrated.
