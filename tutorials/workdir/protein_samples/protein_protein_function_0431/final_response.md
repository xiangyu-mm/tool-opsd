### Functional summary

The 480-aa sequence is an **exact match** to reviewed UniProt **Q83HJ1**, glutamate–tRNA ligase from *Tropheryma whipplei* strain TW08/27. The independent matching reference Q83GP4 is also from *T. whipplei*.

This enzyme attaches glutamate to **tRNA(Glu)** in two steps: it uses ATP to form glutamyl-AMP, then transfers glutamate to the tRNA. The resulting glutamyl-tRNA is used in protein synthesis.
