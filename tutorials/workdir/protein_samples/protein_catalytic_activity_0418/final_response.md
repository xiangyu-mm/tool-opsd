## Functional identification

The protein is the **α subunit of glycine--tRNA ligase (GlyRS)**, also called **glycyl-tRNA synthetase α**.

- **EC:** 6.1.1.14  
- **Localization:** Cytosol  
- **Length:** 290 aa  
- **Calculated molecular mass:** ~33.45 kDa  
- **Pfam domain:** tRNA-synt_2e (PF02091), residues 3–280  
- **Evidence strength:** Very high—the sequence is an exact match to the reviewed Swiss-Prot protein **C0R0J6** from *Brachyspira hyodysenteriae*. Related reviewed homologs also show ~69–71% identity and the same annotation.

## Catalyzed reaction

\[
\text{tRNA}^{\mathrm{Gly}} + \text{glycine} + \text{ATP}
\rightarrow
\text{glycyl-tRNA}^{\mathrm{Gly}} + \text{AMP} + \text{pyrophosphate}
\]

In words, GlyRS charges glycine-specific tRNA with glycine, producing **glycyl-tRNA\(^\mathrm{Gly}\)** for incorporation into proteins.

The reaction occurs in two chemical stages:

1. **Activation of glycine:**  
   \[
   \text{glycine}+\text{ATP}\rightarrow\text{glycyl-AMP}+\text{PP}_{i}
   \]

2. **Transfer to tRNA:**  
   \[
   \text{glycyl-AMP}+\text{tRNA}^{\mathrm{Gly}}
   \rightarrow
   \text{glycyl-tRNA}^{\mathrm{Gly}}+\text{AMP}
   \]

Thus, the enzyme uses ATP to activate glycine as a glycinyl-adenylate intermediate and then transfers the glycine residue to the 3′ terminal adenosine of tRNA\(^\mathrm{Gly}\).

## Summary

This sequence encodes a **cytosolic glycyl-tRNA synthetase α subunit**, responsible for the ATP-dependent aminoacylation of tRNA\(^\mathrm{Gly}\). The assignment is supported by an exact curated sequence match, the characteristic tRNA-synthetase domain, ATP-binding annotation, and the conserved EC/Rhea reaction assignment.
