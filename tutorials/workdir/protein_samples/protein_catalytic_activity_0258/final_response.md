## Functional identification

The 382-residue protein is most consistent with **RlmN**, a **dual-specificity radical-SAM RNA methyltransferase** (**EC 2.1.1.192**).

This assignment is strongly supported by matches to reviewed RlmN proteins:

- **89.5% sequence identity**
- Approximately **100% sequence coverage**
- E-value approximately **10⁻²⁵¹**
- Conserved cysteine residues consistent with a radical-SAM **[4Fe–4S] cluster**

## Catalytic activity

RlmN methylates the **exocyclic N2 atom of adenine** in RNA:

1. **23S rRNA:** adenosine A2503 → **2-methyladenosine (m²A2503)**
2. **tRNA:** adenosine A37 → **2-methyladenosine (m²A37)**

The overall transformations can be represented as:

\[
\text{Adenosine in RNA} + 2\,\text{SAM}
\rightarrow
\text{N2-methyladenosine in RNA}
+ \text{5′-deoxyadenosine}
+ \text{S-adenosylhomocysteine}
\]

with reducing equivalents supplied through the radical-SAM electron-transfer system. The enzyme uses one **[4Fe–4S] cluster**, coordinated by three cysteines, to activate SAM and generate the radical required for methyl transfer.

## Functional summary

This protein is a **[4Fe–4S]-dependent radical-SAM RNA methylase that installs N2-methyladenosine in both 23S rRNA and tRNA**. Its rRNA modification at A2503 contributes to proper proofreading and fidelity at the ribosomal peptidyl-transferase center.
