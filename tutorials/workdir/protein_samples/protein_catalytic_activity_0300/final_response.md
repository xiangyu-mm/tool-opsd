### Catalytic reaction

\[
\text{octanoyl-ACP} + \text{apo-lipoyl domain}
\rightarrow
\text{N}^{6}\text{-octanoyl-lysyl-lipoyl domain} + \text{ACP}
\]

More explicitly, LipB transfers an octanoyl group from **octanoyl-acyl carrier protein (octanoyl-ACP)** to the ε-amino group of a specific lysine residue in the lipoyl domain of enzymes such as pyruvate dehydrogenase and 2-oxoglutarate dehydrogenase.

The resulting **octanoyl-lysine** is subsequently converted into mature **lipoyl-lysine** by sulfur insertion, typically catalyzed by LipA.
