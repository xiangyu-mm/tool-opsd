## Protein assessment

- **Length:** 424 amino acids

- **Theoretical pI:** 4.63

### Targeting and localization
- The N-terminus contains a clear **secretory-pathway signal peptide**.
- No additional long hydrophobic segments are present, supporting a **soluble luminal protein rather than a membrane protein**.
- The C-terminus ends in **HDEL**, an endoplasmic-reticulum retention signal.
- Therefore, this protein is expected to reside primarily in the **endoplasmic reticulum lumen**, rather than being constitutively secreted extracellularly.

### Domain architecture
- Pfam analysis identifies the **Calreticulin domain** across approximately:
  - residues **23–257**
  - residues **260–333**
- The remaining C-terminal region is acidic and enriched in charged residues, consistent with the calcium-binding/chaperone tail characteristic of calreticulins.

### Functional identification
The sequence is an **exact 100% sequence match** to reviewed **Dictyostelium discoideum calreticulin Q23858**. This provides much stronger evidence than a conventional similarity-based annotation.

**Likely function:**  
A soluble ER-resident **calcium-binding molecular chaperone** involved in:

- folding and quality control of newly synthesized glycoproteins;
- transient recognition of monoglucosylated glycoproteins;
- the **calreticulin/calnexin cycle**;
- ER-associated protein quality control and ERAD-related processes.

## Overall conclusion

This protein is **calreticulin**, an ER-lumen glycoprotein-folding chaperone. Its N-terminal signal peptide targets it to the secretory pathway, while the terminal **HDEL** motif retains it in the ER. The acidic composition and annotated calreticulin domains are consistent with calcium binding and chaperone activity. The functional assignment is highly confident because the sequence exactly matches the reviewed *D. discoideum* calreticulin protein.
