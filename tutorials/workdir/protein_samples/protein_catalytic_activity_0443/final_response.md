### Functional assignment

The 768-aa protein is the **β subunit of phenylalanine–tRNA ligase (PheRS)**, also called **phenylalanyl-tRNA synthetase β**.

This assignment is strongly supported by:

- 100% sequence identity to reviewed *Streptococcus thermophilus* PheRS β-subunit homologs
- 80.1% identity to a reviewed *Streptococcus mutans* homolog
- Conserved PheRS β-subunit domains, including **tRNA_bind**, **B3_4**, **B5**, and **tRNA_synthFbeta**
- Curated annotation as **EC 6.1.1.20**

### Catalytic reaction

The PheRS complex catalyzes the ATP-dependent attachment of L-phenylalanine to phenylalanine-specific tRNA:

\[
\mathrm{L\!-\!phenylalanine + tRNA^{Phe} + ATP}
\rightarrow
\mathrm{L\!-\!phenylalanyl\!-\!tRNA^{Phe} + AMP + PP_i + H^+}
\]

In words, the enzyme produces **phenylalanyl-tRNA\(^\mathrm{Phe}\)** for incorporation of phenylalanine during protein synthesis.

The reaction proceeds through an aminoacyl-adenylate intermediate:

1. \[
\mathrm{L\!-\!Phe + ATP \rightarrow Phe\!-\!AMP + PP_i}
\]

2. \[
\mathrm{Phe\!-\!AMP + tRNA^{Phe} \rightarrow Phe\!-\!tRNA^{Phe} + AMP}
\]

### Important subunit qualification

The queried protein is the **β subunit**, not the complete independent catalytic enzyme. Bacterial PheRS is generally an **α₂β₂ heterotetramer**:

- The **α subunits** contain the principal amino-acid activation/catalytic region.
- The **β subunits** contribute tRNA recognition, structural organization, and complex function.

Thus, the most accurate statement is that this protein is a component of the **phenylalanine-tRNA ligase complex**, which catalyzes phenylalanyl-tRNA formation.

The complex is annotated to bind **Mg²⁺**, with two Mg²⁺ ions reported per tetramer.
