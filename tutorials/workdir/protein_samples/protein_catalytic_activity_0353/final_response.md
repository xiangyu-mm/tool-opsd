The 187-residue protein is most likely an **inosine triphosphate pyrophosphatase (ITPase; EC 3.6.1.66)** of the **Ham1p-like family**.

### Catalytic activity

It hydrolyzes noncanonical purine nucleoside triphosphates:

- **ITP + H₂O → IMP + pyrophosphate (PPi) + H⁺**
- **dITP + H₂O → dIMP + PPi + H⁺**
- **XTP + H₂O → XMP + PPi + H⁺**

The enzyme therefore removes abnormal purine triphosphates from nucleotide pools, helping prevent their incorporation into DNA and RNA.

**Functional summary:** a metal-dependent Ham1p-like pyrophosphatase that sanitizes the purine nucleotide pool by converting ITP, dITP, and XTP into their corresponding monophosphates and inorganic pyrophosphate.
