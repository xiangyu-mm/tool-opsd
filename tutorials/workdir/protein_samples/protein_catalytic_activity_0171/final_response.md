## Functional identification

This is **serine hydroxymethyltransferase (SHMT; EC 2.1.2.1)**. The 417-amino-acid sequence is an **exact match** to reviewed UniProt protein **Q8K9P2** from *Buchnera aphidicola*, and it contains the expected SHMT domain (Pfam PF00464). This supports a high-confidence functional assignment.

## Catalytic reaction

SHMT catalyzes the reversible transfer of a one-carbon unit between serine and tetrahydrofolate:

**L-serine + tetrahydrofolate ⇌ glycine + 5,10-methylene-tetrahydrofolate + H₂O**

In the serine-to-glycine direction, the enzyme produces **5,10-methylene-THF**, a one-carbon donor used in biosynthetic metabolism. The opposite direction forms serine from glycine and 5,10-methylene-THF. The reaction is recorded as **Rhea RHEA:15481**.

The enzyme uses **pyridoxal 5′-phosphate (PLP)** as a cofactor; the matched reference annotates a PLP-linked lysine at position 229. Reference annotations also describe SHMT as a **cytosolic homodimer** and report additional THF-independent aldolase activity toward some β-hydroxyamino acids. The main supported role is the reversible serine–glycine interconversion that links amino-acid metabolism with folate-mediated one-carbon metabolism.
