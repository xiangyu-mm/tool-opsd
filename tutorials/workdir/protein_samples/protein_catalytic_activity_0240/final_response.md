## Functional assignment

The 107-residue protein is a **heme-degrading monooxygenase (heme oxygenase/decyclizing)**, **EC 1.14.14.18**. Its sequence is an exact full-length match to curated *Bacillus* heme oxygenase homologs, providing strong evidence for this assignment.

## Catalyzed reaction

The enzyme oxidatively cleaves the heme porphyrin ring:

\[
\text{heme b} + O_2 + \text{reducing equivalents}
\rightarrow \text{biliverdin IX}\alpha + CO + Fe^{2+} + H_2O
\]

A more explicit database-referenced form uses three equivalents of oxygen and reducing power supplied by an NADPH–hemoprotein reductase:

\[
\text{heme b} + 3O_2 + 3\,\text{NADPH-reductant}
\rightarrow \text{biliverdin IX}\alpha + CO + Fe^{2+}
+ 3H_2O + \text{oxidized reductant} + H^+
\]

Thus, the enzyme **opens and degrades heme to biliverdin, carbon monoxide, and free iron**, supporting bacterial acquisition of iron from host heme. It is annotated as a **heme- and iron-binding cytoplasmic protein**. The assignment is supported by exact sequence identity to reviewed UniProt proteins and Rhea reaction mappings **RHEA:11964, RHEA:11965, and RHEA:21764**.
