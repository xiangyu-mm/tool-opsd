## Domain and sequence characteristics

- The **PX domain** is strongly detected at residues **100–183** and is characteristic of sorting nexins.
- PX domains commonly recognize phosphoinositides, especially **phosphatidylinositol-3-phosphate**, enabling recruitment to endosomal membranes.
- The N-terminal region contains a relatively low-complexity, acidic/proline-rich segment.
- The remainder of the sequence is rich in charged residues and is consistent with a soluble interaction/scaffolding protein.
- There is no apparent long hydrophobic segment suggesting a transmembrane helix or secretory signal peptide; SNX4 is therefore consistent with a **peripheral membrane protein**.

## Functional interpretation

SNX4 is a **nonenzymatic endosomal trafficking adaptor**. Its principal functions include:

- Regulation of **endocytosis and intracellular membrane trafficking**.
- Promotion of **transferrin receptor recycling**, helping prevent receptor degradation.
- Regulation of **ATG9A trafficking and recycling** during autophagosome assembly.
- Binding to phosphoinositide-containing membranes through its PX domain.

## Interactions and complexes

Curated annotations indicate that SNX4:

- Forms heterodimers with **SNX7** or **SNX30**.
- Interacts with **SNX32**, **WWC1/KIBRA**, and **BIN1**.
- Has been detected in complexes involving dynein-associated components.
