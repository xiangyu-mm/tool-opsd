## Functional summary

This 234-amino-acid protein is almost certainly **7-carboxy-7-deazaguanine synthase**, also called **QueE**.

### Evidence

- The sequence is an **exact full-length match** to reviewed UniProt protein **Q9ZCV2**.
- It contains a **Radical SAM domain** spanning approximately residues 53–133.
- The motif `C...C..C` around residues 60–70 is characteristic of Radical SAM enzymes and coordinates a catalytic **[4Fe–4S] cluster**.
- Homologs are annotated with **EC 4.3.99.3** and the same biochemical activity.

### Likely reaction

The enzyme catalyzes the radical-mediated conversion:

**6-carboxy-5,6,7,8-tetrahydropterin (CPH4) → 7-carboxy-7-deazaguanine (CDG)**

It uses:

- a **[4Fe–4S] cluster**
- **S-adenosyl-L-methionine (SAM)**
- likely **Mg²⁺**

The protein is annotated as a **homodimer**.

### Biological process

This is an early enzyme in **7-deazapurine biosynthesis**, particularly the pathway that produces the precursor to **queuosine**, a modified nucleoside found at position 34 of certain tRNAs. Thus, it likely contributes to:

- **tRNA queuosine modification**
- **tRNA wobble-base biology**
- broader **purine/7-deazapurine metabolism**

**Confidence: very high**, because the sequence exactly matches a curated reference protein, rather than relying only on domain similarity. Cellular localization cannot be determined confidently from this sequence alone, although the compact, soluble enzyme architecture is consistent with a bacterial cytosolic protein.
