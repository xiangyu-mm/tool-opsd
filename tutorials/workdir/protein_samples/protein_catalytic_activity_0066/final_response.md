## Functional summary

The 280-aa sequence is an **exact full-length match** to reviewed UniProt entry **A1AT85**, annotated as a **pyruvate, phosphate dikinase (PPDK) regulatory protein**. It is a bifunctional **serine/threonine kinase and phosphorylase** that regulates PPDK. The matching Kinase-PPPase domain (Pfam **PF03618**) supports this assignment.

Its key chemistry is reversible phosphate transfer at PPDK’s regulatory threonine, while a phosphorylated histidine on PPDK remains phosphorylated:

- **Phosphorylation:**  
  phospho-His–PPDK–Thr + **ADP** → phospho-His–PPDK–phospho-Thr + **AMP** + H⁺

- **Dephosphorylation (phosphorolysis):**  
  phospho-His–PPDK–phospho-Thr + **inorganic phosphate** + H⁺ → phospho-His–PPDK–Thr + **diphosphate (pyrophosphate)**

Thus, this is not simply a conventional ATP-dependent kinase: in the annotated phosphorylation reaction, **ADP is the phosphate acceptor**, and the phosphate is transferred from PPDK’s phospho-histidine to its threonine. The reported EC numbers are **2.7.11.32** and **2.7.4.27**. No separate cofactor requirement was identified in the retrieved annotations.
