## Functional role

This protein is a **serine acetyltransferase (SAT)**, also called **L-serine O-acetyltransferase**. It catalyzes the first step in the plant cysteine-biosynthesis pathway:

\[
\text{L-serine} + \text{acetyl-CoA}
\rightarrow
\text{O-acetyl-L-serine} + \text{CoA}
\]

O-acetyl-L-serine is subsequently used for sulfur incorporation and cysteine formation. Thus, the protein connects **serine metabolism, acetyl-CoA utilization, sulfur assimilation, and cysteine biosynthesis**.
