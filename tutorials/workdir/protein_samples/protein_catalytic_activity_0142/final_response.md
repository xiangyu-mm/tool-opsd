## Catalytic reaction

The enzyme detoxifies nitric oxide under aerobic conditions by converting NO to nitrate:

\[
\mathbf{2\,NO + NAD(P)H + 2\,O_2 \rightarrow 2\,NO_3^- + NAD(P)^+ + H^+}
\]

The enzyme may use either NADH or NADPH:

\[
2\,NO + NADH + 2\,O_2 \rightarrow 2\,NO_3^- + NAD^+ + H^+
\]

This activity is annotated as **nitric oxide dioxygenase [NAD(P)H]**, **EC 1.14.12.17**, with Rhea references **RHEA:19465** and **RHEA:19469**.

## Cofactors and mechanism

- **Heme b:** Located in the N-terminal globin domain; binds oxygen and participates directly in NO dioxygenation.
- **FAD:** Located in the C-terminal reductase domain; accepts electrons from NADH or NADPH and transfers them toward the heme center.
- The reductase domain contains FAD-binding and NAD(P)-binding regions.

## Concise summary

This protein is a **flavohemoglobin nitric oxide dioxygenase** that uses molecular oxygen and reducing equivalents from NADH/NADPH to oxidize toxic nitric oxide into nitrate. It likely functions in **bacterial defense against nitrosative stress**.
