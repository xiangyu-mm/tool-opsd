### Functional overview

The 407-aa sequence is strongly supported as a **bifunctional RibBA protein** involved in **riboflavin (vitamin B₂) biosynthesis**. Its closest reviewed match is bacterial RibBA, with **74.7% identity across 97.3% of the sequence**.

- **Likely activities:**  
  - **3,4-dihydroxy-2-butanone-4-phosphate synthase** (EC 4.1.99.12), acting on D-ribulose 5-phosphate.  
  - **GTP cyclohydrolase II** (EC 3.5.4.25), initiating formation of a riboflavin pyrimidine precursor from GTP.
- **Domain evidence:** The sequence matches the expected DHBP synthase domain (residues 10–200) and GTP cyclohydrolase II domain (residues 209–375).
- **Sequence properties:** 407 residues; estimated molecular mass **45.6 kDa**; theoretical pI **5.95**.
- **Cofactors and location:** Reference homologs are annotated as binding divalent metal ions (Mg²⁺ or Mn²⁺) and Zn²⁺, and as cytosolic. These are **homology-based expectations**, not direct measurements for this sequence.

Overall, the sequence and domain architecture strongly support a role in two branches of bacterial riboflavin precursor synthesis; the specific activities are inferred from close homologs rather than experimentally demonstrated for this exact protein.
