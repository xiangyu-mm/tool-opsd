### Functional identification

The 588-residue protein is the **hemoprotein beta-component of NADPH-dependent sulfite reductase** (assimilatory sulfite reductase, **EC 1.8.1.2**).

This assignment is highly reliable because the sequence is an **exact full-length match** to reviewed UniProt entry **A6VPZ1**. It also contains characteristic **NIR_SIR** and **NIR_SIR_ferr** domains.

### Catalytic reaction

The sulfite reductase complex performs the six-electron reduction of sulfite to sulfide:

\[
\mathrm{SO_3^{2-} + 3\,NADPH + 5\,H^+ \rightarrow H_2S + 3\,NADP^+ + 3\,H_2O}
\]

The database reaction is also represented in the reverse direction as Rhea **RHEA:13801**.

### Cofactors and complex organization

- **Siroheme**: catalytic multi-electron redox cofactor  
- **[4Fe–4S] cluster**: electron-transfer cofactor  
- The beta-component forms an **α₈β₈ sulfite reductase complex**:
  - α-component: flavoprotein that accepts reducing equivalents from NADPH
  - β-component: siroheme/[4Fe–4S] hemoprotein where sulfite reduction occurs

### Biological role

This reaction produces sulfide for **assimilatory sulfate reduction**, ultimately supporting **cysteine biosynthesis** and sulfur metabolism.

**Summary:** The sequence encodes the siroheme- and iron–sulfur-containing catalytic beta subunit of sulfite reductase, which reduces sulfite to hydrogen sulfide using reducing equivalents supplied by NADPH through the flavoprotein alpha subunit.
