### Functional identification

The 413-aa protein is a **Hedgehog-family precursor**, most consistent with a Sonic hedgehog-like protein. The identification is strongly supported by:

- An N-terminal signal peptide, approximately residues 1–24.
- A Hedgehog signaling domain, **HH_signal** (residues ~25–185).
- A C-terminal **Hedgehog Hint domain** (residues ~187–398), characteristic of Hedgehog autoproteolysis.
- 75–77% identity across essentially the full sequence to curated mouse, rat, and human Sonic hedgehog proteins.

### Enzymatic activity

Its C-terminal Hint domain catalyzes an unusual **intramolecular autoproteolysis coupled to cholesterol transfer**. This is not a conventional enzyme acting on a freely diffusible substrate: the precursor itself is the substrate and enzyme-containing molecule.

The overall reaction can be represented as:

\[
\text{Gly-Cys-[Hedgehog precursor]} + \text{cholesterol} + H^+
\rightarrow
\text{[N-terminal Hedgehog]-Gly-cholesterol ester}
+
\text{Cys-[C-terminal fragment]}
\]

Mechanistically:

1. The precursor undergoes cleavage at the conserved internal **Gly–Cys junction**.
2. The newly generated C-terminal glycine of the N-terminal Hedgehog product is covalently esterified with cholesterol.
3. This produces:
   - an N-terminal, cholesterol-modified Hedgehog signaling product; and
   - a C-terminal protein remnant containing the Hint domain.

The N-terminal product is subsequently also lipid-modified, typically by N-terminal palmitoylation, enabling membrane association and Hedgehog morphogen signaling.

### Cofactors and catalytic residues

No external cofactor is required for the core reaction. The Hint domain provides the catalytic machinery for self-processing and cholesterol transfer. The sequence analysis supports the domain-level assignment, but the available tool output does not reliably map experimentally validated catalytic residues onto this particular sequence, so specific residue claims should be avoided.

### Functional summary

**This protein is a secreted Hedgehog-family morphogen precursor whose C-terminal Hint domain catalyzes self-cleavage and covalent transfer of cholesterol to the C terminus of its N-terminal signaling fragment.** The reaction generates the lipidated Hedgehog morphogen required for membrane tethering and subsequent developmental signaling through the Hedgehog pathway.
