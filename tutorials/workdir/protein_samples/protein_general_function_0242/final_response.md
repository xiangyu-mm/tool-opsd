### Functional summary

The 494-aa sequence is most consistent with a **bifunctional NAD(P)H-hydrate repair enzyme**. Its closest reviewed homologs are annotated as Nnr enzymes, with 40–45% sequence identity and nearly complete coverage. This supports a role in repairing hydrated NADH and NADPH (NAD(P)HX), which can form through enzymatic or heat-dependent damage.

The proposed activities are:
- **NAD(P)HX epimerase:** interconverts the R and S forms.
- **ADP-dependent NAD(P)HX dehydratase:** converts the S form back to NAD(P)H or NADPH, using ADP, which is converted to AMP and phosphate.

Sequence analysis predicts a molecular mass of about **50.9 kDa** and theoretical pI of **6.11**. Pfam matches identify **YjeF_N** (residues 35–192) and **Carb_kinase** (residues 251–488). Potassium binding is reported for homologs, but is not confirmed experimentally for this sequence.

**Evidence caveat:** this is a strong homology-based functional assignment, not a direct activity measurement of the supplied protein. Localization and organism-specific biological context cannot be determined from the available evidence.
