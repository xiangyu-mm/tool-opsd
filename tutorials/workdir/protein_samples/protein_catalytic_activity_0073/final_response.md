## Functional identification

The 275-aa sequence is an **exact match** to reviewed UniProt entry **Q609U9**, annotated as **malonyl-[acyl-carrier protein] O-methyltransferase** (EC **2.1.1.197**). The reference protein is from *Methylococcus capsulatus*.

It catalyzes transfer of a methyl group from **S-adenosyl-L-methionine (SAM)** to the free carboxyl group of ACP-bound malonyl substrate:

**malonyl-[ACP] + SAM → malonyl-[ACP] methyl ester + S-adenosyl-L-homocysteine (SAH)**

This methylation is associated with **pimeloyl-ACP formation** in the **biotin-biosynthesis pathway**. The identification and reaction are strongly supported by the exact sequence match and the reviewed annotation; the sequence analysis itself does not establish experimental activity for a separately tested sample.
