### Function

Prp3 is a structural/assembly factor of the **U4/U6·U5 tri-snRNP**, a major spliceosomal subcomplex. It contributes to:

- Assembly and stability of the tri-snRNP
- Formation of the precatalytic spliceosome
- **Pre-mRNA cis-splicing**
- Nuclear mRNA export, based on the *S. pombe* annotation

It is not identified as an enzyme; the sequence and annotations instead support a **spliceosomal scaffolding and RNA–protein complex assembly role**.
