## Catalyzed reaction

The primary reaction is:

\[
\text{L-kynurenine} + \mathrm{H_2O}
\rightarrow
\text{anthranilate} + \text{L-alanine} + \mathrm{H^+}
\]

In biochemical terms, kynureninase cleaves the kynurenine carbon skeleton, releasing:

- **Anthranilate** (anthranilic acid)
- **L-alanine**

The enzyme may also act on 3-hydroxy-L-kynurenine:

\[
\text{3-hydroxy-L-kynurenine} + \mathrm{H_2O}
\rightarrow
\text{3-hydroxyanthranilate} + \text{L-alanine} + \mathrm{H^+}
\]

The corresponding curated reaction mappings are **Rhea RHEA:16813** and **RHEA:25143**.

## Cofactor and pathway role

The enzyme is expected to use **pyridoxal 5′-phosphate (PLP)** as a catalytic cofactor. Kynureninase participates in:

- **L-kynurenine degradation**
- **L-tryptophan catabolism**
- In some organisms, **quinolinate/NAD⁺ biosynthesis**
