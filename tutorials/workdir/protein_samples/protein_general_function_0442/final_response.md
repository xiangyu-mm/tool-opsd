### Functional summary

This is a **hemoglobin alpha-D subunit**, with very high confidence. The 141-residue sequence is an **exact sequence match** to reviewed UniProt **Q9DF25** from *Chelonoidis carbonarius*.

- **Primary role:** Oxygen transport as part of hemoglobin.
- **Complex:** Forms a tetramer containing two alpha-D and two beta chains.
- **Cofactor/ligand:** Binds a **heme prosthetic group**, enabling reversible oxygen binding.
- **Domain:** Canonical **Globin domain** (Pfam **PF00042**) spanning approximately residues 26–136.
- **Likely localization:** Soluble erythrocyte/blood hemoglobin complex; no transmembrane segment or signal-peptide-like hydrophobic region is apparent.
- **Related activities:** The reference annotation includes oxygen-carrier activity, oxygen binding, heme binding, and haptoglobin binding. Peroxidase-related GO annotations are also present, but oxygen transport is the central biological function.

### Interpretation

The sequence is a compact, soluble globin protein rather than an enzyme. Its strong match to a curated hemoglobin alpha-D sequence and its highly significant globin-domain assignment make the functional identification robust. No independent catalytic reaction was retrieved; any peroxidase annotation should be treated as a secondary hemoglobin-associated activity rather than evidence that this protein is primarily a peroxidase.
