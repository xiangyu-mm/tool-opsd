### Functional evidence

The sequence is an **exact sequence match** to the reviewed *Caenorhabditis elegans* protein O16658, with 100% identity and full-length coverage. This provides very strong evidence that it is a **Golgi nucleotide-sugar transporter**, rather than a receptor or soluble enzyme.

The reference protein is annotated to transport:

- **UDP-N-acetylglucosamine (UDP-GlcNAc)**
- **UDP-N-acetylgalactosamine (UDP-GalNAc)**

These nucleotide sugars are transported from the cytosol into the Golgi lumen, where they supply substrates for glycosyltransferases. Related mammalian proteins, particularly **SLC35A3**, are annotated as UDP-GlcNAc transporters and may operate through nucleotide-sugar/UMP exchange.

### Overall conclusion

This is a **~37 kDa, multi-pass Golgi membrane nucleotide-sugar transporter**, most confidently identified as **NSTP-4/O16658**. Its principal role is likely supplying UDP-GlcNAc and UDP-GalNAc to the Golgi lumen for glycosylation. The exact transport mechanism and substrate balance should be regarded as experimentally determined for the reference protein rather than inferred solely from the sequence.
