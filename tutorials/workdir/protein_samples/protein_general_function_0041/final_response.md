### Likely function

This enzyme participates in **organosulfur and alkanesulfonate degradation**. It oxidatively processes sulfonate substrates using Fe²⁺, molecular oxygen, and 2-oxoglutarate. In the reference yeast protein, **taurine is a poor substrate**, while **isethionate and taurocholate** are reported as better natural substrates.

The assignment is unusually strong:

- The sequence is an **exact sequence match** to reviewed UniProt **Q12358**.
- A highly significant independent Pfam match supports the **TauD-like dioxygenase fold**.
- The reference protein is experimentally/curationally annotated as an Fe²⁺-dependent sulfonate dioxygenase.

Reference binding-site annotations occur around residues **218, 220, 245, 367, 379, and 383**, consistent with a compact catalytic domain. The N-terminal region before the TauD domain may contribute to structural organization or substrate recognition, but no separate high-confidence domain was reported.

### Bottom line

This is a **soluble fungal TauD-family Fe²⁺/2-oxoglutarate-dependent sulfonate dioxygenase**, most likely functioning in cytoplasmic sulfur-compound catabolism. Because the sequence exactly matches Q12358, the substrate and pathway assignment is much more reliable than a prediction based only on homology.
