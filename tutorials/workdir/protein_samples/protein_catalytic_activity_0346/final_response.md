## Functional summary

The 331-aa protein is **Sideroflexin-5 (SFXN5)**, a **multipass mitochondrial inner-membrane transporter**, not a soluble metabolic enzyme.

- **Sequence evidence:** Exact sequence match to reviewed *C. elegans* Sideroflexin-5 (UniProt **Q5FC79**).
- **Domain:** SFXNs domain, Pfam **PF03820**.
- **Localization:** Mitochondrial inner membrane.
- **Primary activity:** Transmembrane transport of small metabolites/amino acids.
- **Best-supported specific substrate:** Citrate, based on annotated human and mouse SFXN5 homologs; this substrate assignment is primarily homology-based for the query protein.

### Reaction

This protein does not catalyze a chemical transformation such as oxidation, hydrolysis, or group transfer. Its corresponding transport reaction is:

\[
\text{citrate}_{\text{in}} \rightleftharpoons \text{citrate}_{\text{out}}
\]

represented by **Rhea RHEA:33183**.

Thus, the protein facilitates **reversible citrate translocation across the mitochondrial inner membrane**. No catalytic cofactor is indicated. The strongest conclusion is therefore **mitochondrial metabolite transporter**, rather than enzyme.
