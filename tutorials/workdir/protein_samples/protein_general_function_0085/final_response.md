## Functional summary

The 171-amino-acid sequence is an **exact match** to reviewed UniProt protein **POP5 (Q54EP3)** from *Dictyostelium discoideum*. POP5 is a protein subunit of **ribonuclease P (RNase P)**, which processes the 5′ leaders of precursor tRNAs to produce mature tRNAs. The reference annotation also places POP5 in **RNase MRP**, though that role is annotated by similarity.

The RNase_P_Rpp14 Pfam domain (PF01900) is detected at residues 7–111. Reference annotations place the protein in the **nucleus and nucleolus**, including RNase P/MRP complexes. Related reviewed POP5 proteins from mammals provide additional, though less close, support for this family assignment.

**Sequence-derived properties:** length 171 aa; estimated molecular mass 19.9 kDa; theoretical pI 5.19; GRAVY −0.62. These are estimates for the unmodified sequence.

**Evidence caveat:** the exact sequence match strongly supports the POP5 identification. The RNase P/MRP roles and localization are reference-protein annotations; the RNase MRP association is specifically reported by similarity. POP5 is a complex subunit, so these data do not establish that it alone performs RNA cleavage.
