## Functional summary

The **637-aa sequence is an exact match to reviewed mouse PRMT5** (protein arginine N-methyltransferase 5; UniProt **Q8CIG8**). This is a strong sequence-based identification, not a distant-homology guess.

- **Main activity:** PRMT5 is an **S-adenosyl-L-methionine (SAM)-dependent arginine methyltransferase** (EC **2.1.1.320**). It produces monomethylarginine and symmetric dimethylarginine on proteins, with the curated annotation noting a preference for monomethylation.
- **Key biological role:** It symmetrically methylates Sm proteins such as **SNRPD1 and SNRPD3**, modifications important for snRNP assembly and biogenesis. It also methylates histones and numerous other protein substrates, linking it to RNA processing, chromatin regulation, and signaling.
- **Domains:** Pfam detects PRMT5-related regions at residues **37–290**, **297–464**, and **467–635**.
- **Localization and complexes:** Annotated in the **nucleus, cytoplasm, chromatin, and Golgi**. It forms homo-oligomers and is found in methylosome complexes, including complexes with **WDR77** and either **CLNS1A** or **RIOK1**.
