## Functional identification

The sequence encodes a **plant G-type lectin S-receptor-like serine/threonine-protein kinase (LECRK-like protein)**, highly similar to the curated rice protein **LECRK3 (UniProt Q0JEU6)**.

### Domain architecture

- **Residues 1–23:** N-terminal signal peptide  
- **Residues ~24–460:** extracellular domain containing a **B-type/G-type lectin domain** and cysteine-rich regions  
- **Residues ~465–485:** single transmembrane helix  
- **Residues ~486 onward:** cytoplasmic region  
- **Residues ~523–719:** canonical eukaryotic protein kinase domain, including Pfam Pkinase and PK_Tyr_Ser-Thr signatures  

Thus, it is a **single-pass type-I membrane receptor kinase**, with ligand/signal recognition outside the cell and catalytic signaling activity inside the cell.

## Catalytic activity

The cytoplasmic domain is an **ATP-dependent protein serine/threonine kinase**, EC **2.7.11.1**.

It accelerates transfer of the terminal, γ-phosphate of ATP to the hydroxyl group of a serine or threonine residue in a protein substrate:

\[
\text{Protein–Ser–OH} + \text{ATP}
\rightarrow
\text{Protein–Ser–O–PO}_3^{2-} + \text{ADP} + \text{H}^+
\]

and/or

\[
\text{Protein–Thr–OH} + \text{ATP}
\rightarrow
\text{Protein–Thr–O–PO}_3^{2-} + \text{ADP} + \text{H}^+
\]

In words, the enzyme **phosphorylates protein serine or threonine residues**, thereby converting extracellular recognition or stress signals into intracellular phosphorylation signals.

## Catalytic evidence

- Curated homologs are annotated with:
  - **ATP binding**
  - **protein serine kinase activity**
  - **protein serine/threonine kinase activity**
  - **EC 2.7.11.1**
- The sequence contains strong kinase-domain signatures and matches curated rice LECRK proteins with approximately **89–100% sequence identity** among the retrieved references.
- A catalytic proton-accepting residue is annotated in the homologous kinase domain, but its exact position should not be transferred directly to this shorter query sequence without an explicit sequence alignment.

## Functional context

The closest curated rice homolog, LECRK3, is associated with plant defense against the brown planthopper. That biological role is supported by homolog annotation, whereas the precise physiological substrate(s) of this sequence cannot be determined from sequence alone.

**Summary:** This is a membrane-anchored plant receptor-like **Ser/Thr kinase** that uses ATP to phosphorylate serine and/or threonine residues on protein substrates, producing ADP and a phosphorylated protein.
