## Functional annotation

The 59-residue protein is **large ribosomal subunit protein bL32**.

### Evidence
- **Exact sequence match** to reviewed UniProt entry **Q8D3B2**.
- Strong match to additional reviewed bacterial bL32 proteins.
- Contains the characteristic **Ribosomal_L32p domain (PF01783)** spanning nearly the entire sequence.
- It is extremely basic (**predicted pI ≈ 10.7; charge at pH 7 ≈ +13.8**), consistent with binding negatively charged ribosomal RNA.

### Functional role
bL32 is a small **structural protein of the bacterial large ribosomal subunit**. It helps stabilize ribosome architecture and contributes to the assembly and function of the translational machinery. It is not expected to act as an enzyme or independent signaling protein.

### Cellular processes
Its primary involvement is in:

- **Ribosome assembly and maintenance**
- **Protein translation**
- Structural organization of the **large ribosomal subunit**

### Subcellular location
The protein is expected to be located in the **cytosol as a component of the bacterial ribosome**, specifically the **large ribosomal subunit**. The sequence has no apparent signal peptide or transmembrane segment, providing no evidence for secretion or membrane localization.

**Overall:** This is a highly basic, very small bacterial ribosomal protein that associates with rRNA and supports translation as part of the large ribosomal subunit.
