## Functional assignment

The 425-aa protein is most consistent with a **pyridoxal-5′-phosphate (PLP)-dependent kynureninase**.

### Catalytic reaction

Primary reaction:

\[
\text{L-kynurenine} + \mathrm{H_2O}
\rightarrow
\text{anthranilate} + \text{L-alanine} + \mathrm{H^+}
\]

It likely also acts on 3-hydroxy-L-kynurenine:

\[
\text{3-hydroxy-L-kynurenine} + \mathrm{H_2O}
\rightarrow
\text{3-hydroxyanthranilate} + \text{L-alanine} + \mathrm{H^+}
\]

This activity corresponds to **kynureninase, EC 3.7.1.3**, with the reactions mapped to Rhea **RHEA:16813** and **RHEA:25143**, respectively.

### Evidence

- The sequence is 425 residues long and matches reviewed kynureninases over nearly its entire length.
- The strongest homolog, *Myxococcus xanthus* Q1DDU5, has **57.9% sequence identity**, approximately **98% coverage**, and an E-value of \(5.66\times10^{-187}\).
- It contains the expected **Aminotran_5** domain and a C-terminal **KYNU_C** domain.
- Homologous proteins are annotated as PLP-dependent enzymes with a conserved catalytic lysine forming the PLP internal aldimine.
- The enzyme is likely cytosolic and homodimeric, as reported for the reviewed homolog, although those properties are inferred rather than experimentally established for this exact sequence.

**Conclusion:** This sequence most likely encodes a **PLP-dependent kynureninase that cleaves kynurenine to anthranilate and alanine**, with probable activity toward 3-hydroxykynurenine as well.
