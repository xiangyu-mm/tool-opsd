The 449-residue protein is **adenylosuccinate synthetase** (**EC 6.3.4.4**), a cytosolic enzyme in the de novo purine biosynthesis pathway.

This assignment is very strong because:

- The sequence is an **exact full-length match** to reviewed UniProt protein **Q2JQ36**.
- It contains the characteristic **Adenylsucc_synt Pfam domain (PF00709)** across residues 5–421.
- Close reviewed homologs are annotated as **homodimeric, Mg²⁺-binding adenylosuccinate synthetases**.
- The enzyme is associated with **GTP binding** and **adenylosuccinate synthetase activity**.

## Catalyzed reaction

\[
\text{IMP} + \text{L-aspartate} + \text{GTP}
\rightarrow
\text{adenylosuccinate} + \text{GDP} + \text{P}_{i} + 2\text{H}^{+}
\]

More explicitly, adenylosuccinate is:

\[
N^6\text{-(1,2-dicarboxyethyl)-AMP}
\]

The reaction is recorded as **Rhea RHEA:15753**.

## Biochemical role

Adenylosuccinate synthetase catalyzes the **first committed step in AMP formation from IMP**. It couples:

1. GTP-dependent activation of IMP,
2. incorporation of the amino acid **aspartate**, and
3. formation of the succinylamino group on IMP,

yielding adenylosuccinate. Adenylosuccinate is subsequently cleaved by adenylosuccinate lyase to produce **AMP** and fumarate.
