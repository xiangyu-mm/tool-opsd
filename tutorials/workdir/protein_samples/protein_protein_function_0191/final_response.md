\[
\text{undecaprenyl diphosphate (UPP)} \rightarrow \text{undecaprenyl phosphate (UP)} + P_i

Undecaprenyl phosphate is a lipid carrier required for transporting peptidoglycan and other cell-envelope precursors across the bacterial membrane.
