### Functional summary

This 361-residue protein is most likely a **WD-repeat nucleolar scaffold involved in ribosomal small-subunit biogenesis**, particularly maturation or assembly of the eukaryotic 40S ribosomal subunit.

### Evidence

- The sequence is an **exact match** to *Schizosaccharomyces pombe* UniProt **O13856**, annotated as WD repeat-containing protein C1A6.02.
- It contains a **WDR55 WD-repeat domain** spanning approximately residues 12–311, with six annotated WD repeats.
- WD repeats typically form a **β-propeller protein–interaction scaffold**, rather than an enzymatic catalytic domain.
- The protein has a short, acidic and lysine-rich/disordered C-terminal region, consistent with regulatory or nucleolar interaction functions.
- The matching protein is curated as localized to the **nucleus and nucleolus** and associated with **GO:0042274, ribosomal small subunit biogenesis**.

### Likely biological process

**Nucleolar ribosome production—specifically assembly and maturation of the small ribosomal subunit.**

The protein probably functions by recruiting or organizing other ribosome-biogenesis factors rather than catalyzing a biochemical reaction itself.

### Confidence and caveat

- **Protein-family identification:** very high confidence, because the sequence exactly matches O13856.
