## Likely molecular function

Rad50 is an **ATP-dependent DNA-end-bridging and DNA-repair factor**. It forms a homodimer and associates with two Mre11 subunits to produce a Rad50–Mre11 complex.

Its ATPase cycle drives conformational changes that regulate whether DNA ends are:

- held together or bridged, or
- positioned for processing and resection.

The zinc-hook region coordinates **Zn²⁺**, typically one zinc ion per Rad50 homodimer, and helps connect the extended Rad50 coiled-coil arms.

## Biological process

The protein is expected to participate in:

- **DNA double-strand break repair**
- Recognition and organization of damaged DNA ends
- Coordination of DNA-end bridging and resection
- In archaea, likely cooperation with **Mre11**, and potentially recruitment or regulation of the **HerA–NurA** DNA-resection machinery

Its highly charged, helical composition supports formation of a stable nucleoprotein complex rather than an enzyme acting independently in the cytosol.
