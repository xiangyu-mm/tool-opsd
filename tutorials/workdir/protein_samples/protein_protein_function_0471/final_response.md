### Identification
The 227-amino-acid sequence is an **exact match** to reviewed *Caenorhabditis elegans* LIN-28 (UniProt **P92186**). This supports the species-specific annotation directly, rather than by distant-homology inference.

### Function and molecular features
LIN-28 is an RNA-binding **heterochronic regulator**: it helps control when developmental events occur and which stage-specific cell fates are adopted. Its annotations include binding to mRNA, including 3′ UTRs and coding regions, and binding to primary and precursor miRNAs. In *C. elegans*, it may delay the larval-to-adult transition by suppressing **let-7 miRNA** during larval development, including by negatively regulating primary-miRNA processing.

The sequence analysis detects a **cold-shock domain** (residues 55–118) and a **CCHC zinc-finger domain** (residues 167–183), consistent with its RNA-binding annotations. These are computationally detected sequence domains, not structural predictions.

### Localization
LIN-28 is annotated primarily in the **cytoplasm**, and also in the **nucleus**.

### Biological processes associated with LIN-28
Reported roles include regulation of larval developmental timing—especially L2 events in the hypodermis—and cell-fate specification. It is also implicated in nervous-system sexual maturation, sexual-fate determination, seam-cell number, male tail-tip morphogenesis, and vulval development.

### Summary
This sequence encodes *C. elegans* LIN-28, an RNA-binding developmental-timing regulator that influences miRNA biology and stage-specific cell fates. The protein is found in the cytoplasm and nucleus and contains predicted cold-shock and CCHC zinc-finger domains. The sequence-derived molecular-weight estimate is about **25.5 kDa**.
