### Functional identification

The sequence encodes a **secreted fungal endo-polygalacturonase** (**EC 3.2.1.15**), a glycoside hydrolase family 28 enzyme.

Evidence:

- **Exact full-length sequence match** to reviewed *Penicillium olsonii* polygalacturonase 2 (UniProt **Q9Y833**).
- N-terminal signal peptide, consistent with secretion.
- **Glyco_hydro_28 (PF00295)** domain across approximately residues 60–380.
- Homologs from *Penicillium* and *Aspergillus* are annotated as secreted polygalacturonases acting on plant pectin.

### Catalytic reaction

The enzyme hydrolyzes internal **1,4-α-glycosidic bonds** in de-esterified pectin (pectate/polygalacturonate):

\[
(\text{1,4-α-D-galacturonosyl})_{n+m} + H_2O
\rightarrow
(\text{1,4-α-D-galacturonosyl})_n
+
(\text{1,4-α-D-galacturonosyl})_m
\]

Thus, it cleaves the pectate backbone endolytically into shorter polygalacturonate fragments. This activity contributes to plant-cell-wall degradation and can promote tissue maceration or soft rot.
