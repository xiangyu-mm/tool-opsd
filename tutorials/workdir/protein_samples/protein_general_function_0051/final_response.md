### Functional assignment

The sequence is an **exact sequence match** to reviewed UniProt entry **O91253**, a **cell-cycle link protein from Faba bean necrotic yellows virus**. Closely related viral proteins from other isolates show 92.9% and 70.8% identity, supporting assignment to a conserved viral protein family.

This protein is not an enzyme. Its reported function is regulatory and protein–protein-interaction based:

- Interacts with host **retinoblastoma-related proteins (RBR)**.
- Disrupts RBR-mediated cell-cycle arrest.
- Promotes transcription of E2F-regulated S-phase and G2/M genes.
- This activity is proposed to facilitate viral DNA replication.
- It is also reported to interact with host **SKP1**.

### Interpretation

The best-supported description is a **small viral host-interaction protein that manipulates plant cell-cycle control**, rather than a metabolic enzyme or structural enzyme-domain protein. The functional assignment is high confidence because of the exact reviewed Swiss-Prot sequence match, although the interaction mechanisms and cellular effects are primarily annotation- and homology-supported rather than experimentally demonstrated for this exact isolate.

One annotation refers to an **LXCXE-mediated RBR interaction**; an exact canonical LXCXE sequence is not obvious on manual inspection of the supplied sequence, so that motif assignment should be treated cautiously or verified against the source annotation and sequence numbering.
