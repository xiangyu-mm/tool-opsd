## Molecular function

The protein forms the **carboxyl-transferase component of ACC**. ACC carries out two coordinated steps:

1. Biotin carboxylase carboxylates enzyme-bound biotin.
2. The carboxyl-transferase subunit transfers the carboxyl group from carboxylated biotin to **acetyl-CoA**, producing **malonyl-CoA**.

Thus, the protein contributes to:

- **Acetyl-CoA carboxylase activity**
- **Carboxyl- or carbamoyltransferase activity**
- Formation of the ACC complex
- **Zinc ion binding**, according to the curated annotation

The subunit itself is not the complete ACC enzyme; catalytic activity depends on cooperation with the biotin carboxylase and biotin-carrier components.
