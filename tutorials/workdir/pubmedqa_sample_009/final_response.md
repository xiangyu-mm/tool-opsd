The evidence supports **yes**.

- **Study:** PMID:25446283, using cultured discarded human saphenous-vein rings.
- **Intervention:** clustered ephrin-B2/Fc (2 μg/mL), an agonistic ligand that activates EphB4.
- **Duration:** 14 days of organ culture.
- **Outcome:** neointima/media ratio decreased from **0.726 ± 0.142** in untreated cultured veins to **0.630 ± 0.132** with ephrin-B2/Fc (**matched n=19, P=.017**).
- EphB4 phosphorylation was also demonstrated, supporting receptor activation.
- Caveat: mechanistic Akt/eNOS/nitric-oxide experiments were primarily performed in human umbilical-vein endothelial cells, not directly in saphenous-vein smooth-muscle cells. Thus, the direct human saphenous-vein evidence is for reduced neointimal hyperplasia, not necessarily a fully defined smooth-muscle mechanism.

FINAL DECISION: YES
