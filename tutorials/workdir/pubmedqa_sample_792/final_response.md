Yes—**in this finite-element study, recreating sinus space was associated with more normal modeled valve mechanics** than using a cylindrical graft.

Grande-Allen et al. compared three aortic-root graft geometries under simulated diastolic pressure. All altered leaflet stress patterns relative to the normal model, but the pseudosinus design had the smallest increases in leaflet stress (9–28%) and strain (2–31%), and leaflet coaptation closest to normal. The cylindrical graft produced the largest increases; a tailored graft was intermediate.

This supports sinus recreation as a biomechanical consideration when sparing the valve. It does **not** demonstrate that it improves long-term clinical valve survival: the evidence is from a finite-element model, not a patient-outcome comparison.

**Reference:** Grande-Allen KJ, et al. *J Thorac Cardiovasc Surg.* 2000;119(4 Pt 1):753–763. PMID: 10733765. DOI: 10.1016/S0022-5223(00)70011-0.

FINAL DECISION: YES
