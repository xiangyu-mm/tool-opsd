Yes—**at the group level**. In the 2018 controlled study of infant rhesus monkeys (7 iron-deficient and 7 iron-sufficient), CSF pyruvate/glutamine and phosphocreatine/creatine ratios were already lower at 4 months, before all iron-deficient infants met clinical anemia criteria at 6 months. The phosphocreatine/creatine ratio remained low at 8 months, even after hemoglobin and mean corpuscular volume had returned to normal (PMID: 27499134).

The timing is not absolute for every animal: iron deficiency was detectable at 4 months, and some infants already had low hemoglobin then. So the study supports CSF metabolic impairment preceding **group-level clinical anemia**, but not necessarily preceding every hematological abnormality in every infant.

FINAL DECISION: YES
