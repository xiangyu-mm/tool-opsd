The updated systematic review and meta-analysis supports **ART as an alternative** for restoring occlusoproximal cavities in primary molars.

- **Review:** *ART is an alternative for restoring occlusoproximal cavities in primary teeth—evidence from an updated systematic review and meta-analysis* (International Journal of Paediatric Dentistry, 2017; PMID: **27489205**).
- **Evidence base:** Four studies were included in both qualitative and quantitative analyses.
- **Intervention:** ART, generally involving hand excavation followed by high-viscosity glass-ionomer cement.
- **Comparator:** Conventional restorative treatment.
- **Main outcome:** Restoration survival.
- **Pooled result:** OR **0.887** (95% CI **0.574–1.371**), indicating no statistically significant difference in survival between ART and conventional treatment.

Thus, ART appears to provide **similar restoration survival** and may be particularly useful where conventional operative dentistry is difficult, costly, or less acceptable to children. However, the evidence base was small, and the accessible report did not provide sufficient detail on aggregate sample size, follow-up duration, heterogeneity, or certainty of evidence. Therefore, the result supports ART as a reasonable option, but not as definitively superior to conventional treatment.
