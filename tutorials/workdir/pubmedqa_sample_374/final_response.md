Yes—one human pre/post study supports restoration of **subcutaneous adipose PNPLA3 expression** after bariatric-surgery-induced weight loss. In 28 severely obese patients, expression measured by qRT-PCR increased significantly six months after laparoscopic gastric banding; hepatic PNPLA3 expression did not significantly increase. The authors concluded that weight loss restored adipose PNPLA3 expression, which TNF-α suppresses in adipocytes.

The evidence is limited to a small cohort and one adipose depot. The abstract does not establish that expression returned to a specific lean-control level or report the size of the increase, so “restored” is best understood as a significant postoperative increase rather than proven normalization.

Source: [PMID 27514759](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27514759/), *Weight loss induced by bariatric surgery restores adipose tissue PNPLA3 expression*.

FINAL DECISION: YES
