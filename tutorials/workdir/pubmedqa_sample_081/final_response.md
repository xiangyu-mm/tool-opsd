Yes. A direct study in WHO grade I meningioma cells found that **photon irradiation increased cell migration** (*p* < 0.02), while **carbon-ion irradiation did not measurably affect migration**. Photon irradiation also increased αvβ1 integrin expression by 14% (*p* = 0.0057), and blocking αvβ1 or αvβ5 inhibited photon-induced migration. The authors identified αvβ1 signaling as an important mechanism.

This supports the claim in the tested cells and conditions; it does not establish that the effect generalizes to all meningiomas or radiation protocols. A separate α3β1 radiation-migration paper was retracted and should not be treated as corroborating evidence.

**Source:** PMID 25445155; DOI: 10.1007/s00066-014-0778-y.

FINAL DECISION: YES
