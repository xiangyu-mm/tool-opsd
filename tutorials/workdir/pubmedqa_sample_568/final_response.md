**Yes.** In a three-year secondary analysis of 159 generally healthy older adults with T2D from the NuAge cohort (mean age 75), neither diet quality alone nor its combination with change in physical activity was associated with decline in functional capacity in adjusted analyses. Diet quality was assessed using the Canadian Healthy Eating Index; physical activity using PASE; and functional capacity using SMAF.

The finding is limited to this relatively healthy sample, which had good average diet quality and only minimal, clinically nonsignificant functional decline. The abstract reports no effect estimates or p-values, so the size and precision of the null association cannot be assessed. **Source: PMID 25092023** (search evidence: `call_qLnfftb8Gd1I6vgtex1zSFjO`, `call_SBr2sqzsMnbVXgQ1r4OJf3NX`).
