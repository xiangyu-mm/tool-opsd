**Yes—in an in-vitro model.** In primary hepatocytes made steatotic with oleic acid, sophocarpine reduced elevated TLR4 expression and pro-inflammatory cytokine synthesis. The study also reported reduced NF-κB, JNK, and ERK pathway activation; TLR4 and cytokine measures recovered after sophocarpine was removed. These results support the proposed effect in that experimental model, but do not establish that sophocarpine directly binds or blocks TLR4, nor do they demonstrate clinical benefit.

**Key evidence:** *Sophocarpine attenuates toll-like receptor 4 in steatotic hepatocytes to suppress pro-inflammatory cytokines synthesis* (PMID: [25089018](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25089018/)). A separate rat NASH study reported reduced inflammatory cytokines with sophocarpine, but did not by itself establish this TLR4 mechanism (PMID: [21054517](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21054517/)).
