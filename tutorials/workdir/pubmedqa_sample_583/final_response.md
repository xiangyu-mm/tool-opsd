Yes. A 2014 study of 88 men found all three factors associated with leukocyte **ABCA1 promoter DNA methylation** (PMID: 25093045):

- **Acetylsalicylic acid (ASA) therapy:** associated with 3.6% lower methylation (*P*=0.006), adjusted for age and coronary artery disease (CAD).
- **Age:** men aged ≥61 had higher mean methylation than men under 61 (38.0±1.2 vs 35.2±1.0; *P*=0.02).
- **CAD:** men with prior CAD had higher mean methylation than men without CAD (38.7±1.2 vs 36.0±1.0; *P*=0.04).

These are observational associations from one relatively small study; they do not establish that ASA, aging, or CAD caused the methylation differences.

FINAL DECISION: YES
