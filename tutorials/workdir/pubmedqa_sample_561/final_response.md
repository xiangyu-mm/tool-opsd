**Yes—callus formation is reported to be related to the ERα:ERβ expression ratio** in ovariectomy-induced osteoporotic fracture healing.

The directly relevant study, *“Callus formation is related to the expression ratios of estrogen receptors-alpha and -beta in ovariectomy-induced osteoporotic fracture healing”* (PMID: **25085540**), studied fracture healing in sham-operated and ovariectomized rats. It reported that the ERα:ERβ ratio correlated with BMP-2 expression (*r* = 0.601, *p* = 0.001), while BMP-2 correlated with callus width (*r* = 0.709, *p* < 0.001) and area (*r* = 0.588, *p* = 0.001). The authors concluded that a higher ERα:ERβ ratio favors callus formation.

**Qualification:** These findings support an association, but do not establish that experimentally changing the receptor ratio causes improved callus formation; the reported correlations link the ratio to BMP-2 and BMP-2 to callus dimensions.

FINAL DECISION: YES
