Available evidence does **not show that prophylactic plasma reduces bleeding complications** before interventional radiology procedures. The search identified a prospective case-control study of fresh frozen plasma before image-guided drainage whose title reports “futility” in correcting coagulopathy, but the retrieved record did not include bleeding rates or effect estimates. A randomized trial of TEG-guided transfusion before high-risk liver-related procedures was only adjacent evidence, not a direct plasma-versus-no-plasma comparison.

So the evidence retrieved does not support a reduction in bleeding; however, without the direct study’s full results, this should not be interpreted as proof that plasma can never help in a particular clinical circumstance.
