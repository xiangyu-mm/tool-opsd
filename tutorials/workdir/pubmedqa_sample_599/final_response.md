**Yes.** The study is titled *“Biomechanical comparison of 3 ankle braces with and without free rotation in the sagittal plane”* (2014; PMID: 25098661).

It was a crossover study of 17 healthy volunteers. Researchers compared **two hinged braces that permitted free sagittal-plane plantar flexion and dorsiflexion** with **one nonhinged brace**, plus a no-brace control. All three braces reduced dynamic and passive foot inversion compared with control. The hinged braces allowed greater passive plantar flexion; the study reported that differences in inversion stabilization did not appear clinically relevant.
