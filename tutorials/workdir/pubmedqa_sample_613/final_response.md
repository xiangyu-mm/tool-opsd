**Yes, with qualifications.** A reported SCN5A-P1332L long-QT model showed prolonged action-potential duration in iPSC-derived cardiomyocytes compared with a CRISPR/Cas9-corrected isogenic control: APD90 was **646 ± 7 ms** versus **482 ± 23 ms**. This supports using isogenic iPSC models to reproduce a cellular long-QT phenotype and test candidate compounds. The evidence should not be overgeneralized: the clearest quantified results retrieved were for this SCN5A model, and cardiomyocyte APD is a surrogate—not a direct clinical QT measurement.
