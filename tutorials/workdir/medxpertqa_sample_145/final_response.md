The presentation is most consistent with **polyarteritis nodosa (PAN)**:

- Medium-vessel vasculitis causing abdominal pain and gastrointestinal bleeding from mesenteric ischemia  
- Cutaneous ischemic ulcers  
- Renal ischemia causing new hypertension and renal dysfunction  
- Fever, weight loss, and elevated inflammatory markers  
- PAN is classically associated with **hepatitis B infection**
- Unlike microscopic polyangiitis, PAN typically does **not** cause glomerulonephritis and is usually ANCA-negative

Therefore, the most likely positive test is **hepatitis B surface antigen (HBsAg)**.
