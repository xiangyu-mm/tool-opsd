**Yes—in an in-vitro study.** The 2016 study *“Naringin protects against HIV-1 protease inhibitors-induced pancreatic β-cell dysfunction and apoptosis”* tested cultured RIN-5F β cells exposed to nelfinavir, saquinavir, or atazanavir. The authors reported that naringin protected against protease-inhibitor-associated oxidative damage and apoptosis: it reduced lipid peroxidation and caspase-3/9 activity and increased glutathione and ATP. The study also reported that the inhibitors impaired glucose-dependent insulin secretion.

This supports a protective effect in that cell model, but does **not** establish that naringin prevents pancreatic dysfunction or diabetes in people taking HIV protease inhibitors.  
**Source:** PMID 27496642; DOI: 10.1016/j.mce.2016.07.041
