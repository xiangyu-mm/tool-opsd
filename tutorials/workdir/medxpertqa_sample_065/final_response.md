The severe burning pain and marked sensitivity in both feet are characteristic of **burning feet syndrome**, classically associated with pantothenic acid (vitamin B5) deficiency. Thiamine deficiency can also cause neuropathy, but pantothenic acid is the classic answer for this specific presentation.
