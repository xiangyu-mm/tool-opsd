Yes. A 2016 mouse study (PMID:27511352) directly compared intrabone marrow injection (IBMI) with intravenous injection (IVI) of human placental mesenchymal stem cells (PMSCs).

In sublethally irradiated NOD/SCID mice receiving human umbilical cord-blood mononuclear cells:

- PMSC administration increased human hematopoietic-cell recovery in the tibiae compared with cord-blood cells alone.
- IBMI of PMSCs was more effective than IVI, indicating enhanced local support of hematopoietic engraftment/recovery.
- A separate murine bone-marrow MSC experiment in the same study also found significantly higher hematopoietic-cell numbers after IBMI than IVI, supporting a delivery-route effect.

The available abstract provides directional findings but not exact effect sizes or detailed blood-count outcomes.

FINAL DECISION: YES
