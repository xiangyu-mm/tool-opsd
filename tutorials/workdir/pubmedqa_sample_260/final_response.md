**Yes—deep subcutaneous adipose-tissue lipid unsaturation was inversely associated with intramyocellular lipid (IMCL) in the directly relevant human study.** In a 2016 MRS study, the association was observed in 16 healthy BMI-discordant monozygotic twin pairs (R = −0.462, P = 0.001) and replicated in an independent group of 12 healthy volunteers (R = −0.641, P = 0.025; PMID: 27506730).

The finding was specific to the deep depot in the reported results; superficial subcutaneous-fat unsaturation showed different relationships. The evidence is limited to small cohorts in one study, and does not establish causality or an association for particular fatty-acid species. A separate older study did not report a direct test of the deep-depot relationship (PMID: 10426379).
