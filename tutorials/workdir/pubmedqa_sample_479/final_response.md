Yes—assuming “gOLP3” refers to **GOLPH3** (Golgi phosphoprotein 3).

Primary human HCC studies report that **high GOLPH3 expression is associated with poorer survival**:

- In 167 HCC samples, high expression predicted worse overall survival (OS; HR **1.87**, 95% CI 1.19–2.94) and disease-free survival (DFS; HR **1.90**, 95% CI 1.21–2.98). Multivariable Cox analysis identified GOLPH3 as an independent prognostic factor (PMID: **24867097**).
- A separate study of 180 HCC samples reported high GOLPH3 as an independent predictor of poor OS (HR **2.01**, 95% CI 1.26–3.64; PMID: **25634514**).
- Additional translational evidence found correlations between GOLPH3 expression, clinical stage, and survival, although not all studies provide complete multivariable model details.

The evidence is therefore consistent with **GOLPH3 being an adverse prognostic biomarker and predictor of survival**, though the available reports are limited and some PubMed records may represent overlapping or duplicate cohorts.
