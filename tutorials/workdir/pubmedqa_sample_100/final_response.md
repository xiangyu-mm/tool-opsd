**Yes.** Two direct studies support that PKD1 deficiency promotes differentiation in epidermal keratinocytes:

- In mouse epidermal keratinocytes with conditional PKD1 deletion, differentiation markers loricrin, involucrin, and keratin 10 increased, as did transglutaminase expression/activity; proliferation decreased (**PMID: 25450094**).
- In normal human epidermal keratinocytes, PKD1 siRNA increased involucrin and keratin 10 mRNA and reduced PCNA mRNA (**PMID: 18259765**).

The finding is supported across these models, though the human study notes PKD1 effects can differ between primary and immortalized keratinocytes.
