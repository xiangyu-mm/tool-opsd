Yes. Strong GWAS evidence supports associations between polymorphisms near **GDF7** and **TBX5** and Barrett’s esophagus susceptibility:

- **Near GDF7:** **rs3072** at 2p24.1 was associated with increased risk: OR **1.14** (95% CI 1.09–1.18), **P=1.8×10⁻¹¹**.
- **Near TBX5:** **rs2701108** at 12q24.21 was associated with Barrett’s esophagus: reported OR **0.90** (95% CI 0.86–0.93), **P=7.5×10⁻⁹**. This OR refers to one allele; the alternate allele is therefore associated with increased risk.

The primary study included **10,158 Barrett’s esophagus cases and 21,062 controls**, with discovery and replication phases, including UK and Irish cohorts (**PMID:25447851**). A subsequent study also reported associations of both variants with esophageal adenocarcinoma (**PMID:26783083**). These are susceptibility associations near the genes; they do not establish that TBX5 or GDF7 themselves are the causal genes.

FINAL DECISION: YES
