In the directly relevant study, sural-nerve-evoked cutaneous A-beta reflexes recorded in the tibialis anterior and biceps femoris were not significantly different in five patients with acute lower-limb reflex sympathetic dystrophy (RSD) compared with five healthy volunteers. The normal phase-dependent change—from facilitation in early swing to suppression near the end of swing—was also preserved.

This supports normal reflexes during gait in the studied patients, though the small sample means the finding should be interpreted cautiously. **“Leg muscle reflexes mediated by cutaneous A-beta fibres are normal during gait in reflex sympathetic dystrophy.”** PMID: 10727919.
