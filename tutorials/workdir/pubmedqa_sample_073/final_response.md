Yes. The exact study (PMID: 25425321, BMC Cardiovascular Disorders, 2014) followed 739 middle-aged Finnish patients with type 2 diabetes for 18 years.

- Prolonged P-wave duration: **≥114 ms**
- Population: patients with prevalent non-major macrovascular disease
- Outcome: stroke mortality
- Association: **2.45-fold higher stroke mortality** (95% CI **1.11–5.37**)
- Stroke deaths: **59**
- In patients without prevalent non-major macrovascular disease, no association was observed.

Thus, prolonged P-wave duration was associated with and predicted higher stroke mortality in the specified subgroup, although the evidence comes from a single observational cohort and should not be interpreted as proof of causation.

FINAL DECISION: YES
