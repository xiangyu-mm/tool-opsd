A directly relevant human study included 12 people with Parkinson’s disease and found that **chronic subthalamic nucleus deep brain stimulation restored physiological rate-dependent attenuation of auditory P1/N1 responses**, consistent with improved auditory sensory gating (PMID: **25085452**).

However, the evidence has important limitations:

- The study assessed **P1/N1 attenuation during rhythmic auditory stimulation**, not the classic **P50 paired-click suppression** paradigm.
- Turning DBS on or off acutely did **not** produce an immediate change in auditory evoked potentials.
- The evidence comes from a small primary study, so it supports improvement in a specific neurophysiological measure rather than establishing a broad or clinically proven correction of all auditory sensory-gating deficits.
