**Yes.** In a 2016 analysis of Mexico’s nationally representative ENSANUT 2012 survey (10,096 participants), sugar-sweetened beverages contributed **69% of added sugar intake**, compared with **25%** from foods high in saturated fat and/or added sugar. The study therefore identified SSBs as the largest source of added sugars in the surveyed population.

**Source:** *Sugar-Sweetened Beverages Are the Main Sources of Added Sugar Intake in the Mexican Population.* *The Journal of Nutrition* (2016), PMID: 27511931. The estimate reflects the 2012 survey and its dietary recall method.
