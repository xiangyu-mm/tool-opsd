Yes. The primary study by Ibernon et al. found that **low pre-transplant serum MBL was associated with more inflammation and tubular-cell apoptosis** in early kidney-allograft surveillance biopsies, obtained within six months after transplantation.

Among recipients with adequate biopsy tissue (41 of 126), the low-MBL group had a higher acute Banff index than the high-MBL group (3.14 ± 1.96 vs 1.88 ± 1.56; *p* = 0.044), more tubular apoptosis (4.3 ± 3.6 vs 0.2 ± 0.9; *p* = 0.012), and more interstitial macrophages (109 ± 118 vs 32 ± 46 cells/mm²; *p* = 0.04).

This was an observational association, not evidence that low MBL causes these biopsy changes; the biopsy subset was also small. **Source:** Ibernon M, et al. *Transplant Immunology*. 2014. PMID: 25073029; DOI: 10.1016/j.trim.2014.07.001.

FINAL DECISION: YES
