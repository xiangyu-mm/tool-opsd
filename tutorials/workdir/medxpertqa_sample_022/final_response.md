This is a **hypertensive emergency** with acute target-organ injury, including worsening renal function and probable hypertensive encephalopathy/retinopathy.

**Fenoldopam** is particularly appropriate because it is an IV, titratable dopamine-1 agonist that lowers blood pressure while promoting renal vasodilation and natriuresis. It avoids the cyanide/thiocyanate accumulation risk of **nitroprusside** in renal dysfunction.

Nicardipine or clevidipine would also be reasonable alternatives, especially for neurologic hypertensive emergencies, but the renal dysfunction makes fenoldopam the best targeted choice among these options.
