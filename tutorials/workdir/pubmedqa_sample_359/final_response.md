The primary study (Yang et al., *Molecular Vision*, 2016; PMID: 27499609) directly investigated this mechanism in CoCl₂-treated ARPE-19 cells. It found that:

- Tissue factor (F3) overexpression increased VEGF expression.
- Tissue factor silencing reduced VEGF expression.
- Tissue factor overexpression was associated with activation-related changes in Wnt/β-catenin signaling, including phosphorylated GSK3β and β-catenin.
- IWR-1-endo, a Wnt-pathway inhibitor, attenuated the tissue-factor-associated increase in VEGF.

Thus, the study supports the conclusion that tissue factor induces VEGF expression through Wnt/β-catenin signaling in ARPE-19 cells. However, because the pathway evidence relied primarily on pharmacological inhibition rather than definitive genetic epistasis or rescue experiments, it establishes strong support rather than absolute proof of direct pathway mediation.
