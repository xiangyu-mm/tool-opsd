Yes. In a randomized crossover study of 12 patients with type 1 diabetes (PMID: 10754365), induced hyperglycemia significantly slowed erythromycin-treated hypertonic liquid gastric emptying compared with euglycemia:

- Half-meal emptying time: **24.9 ± 5.5 vs 16.6 ± 6.0 minutes**
- Lag phase: **7.8 ± 4.5 vs 3.7 ± 2.5 minutes**
- Gastric retention at 60 and 100 minutes: significantly higher during hyperglycemia (**p < 0.001**)

Thus, hyperglycemia **attenuated but did not abolish** erythromycin’s prokinetic effect.

FINAL DECISION: YES
