The evidence supports **yes**, particularly in **premenopausal women**.

- A directly relevant HR-pQCT study of 146 Chinese-American and Caucasian women found that women with a plate-like tibial trabecular phenotype were **25.7 times more likely** to be Chinese-American than rod-like women (95% CI 7.3–90.1). After adjustment for constitutional and lifestyle factors, the odds were **85.0-fold** (95% CI 12.7–568.0) (PMID: 25069706).
- Supporting studies found substantially higher plate BV/TV and plate-to-rod ratios in premenopausal Chinese-American women, including approximately **twofold higher plate-to-rod ratios** and 80–95% higher plate BV/TV at measured sites (PMID: 21351150).
- The difference is less consistent after menopause: the plate-to-rod ratio may remain higher, but absolute plate BV/TV differences are often attenuated or nonsignificant (PMIDs: 23299863, 31437567).

Thus, the trabecular plate-like phenotype is overrepresented in Chinese-American versus Caucasian women, with the clearest evidence before menopause. This describes an observed group difference and does not by itself establish a genetic cause.

FINAL DECISION: YES
