Yes—classic spinally elicited peripheral-nerve responses, often termed neurogenic motor evoked potentials (NMEPs), are predominantly **sensory and antidromic**, not motor. Human collision studies and dorsal-rhizotomy experiments support activation of sensory afferent pathways.

However, spinal stimulation can also recruit motor roots or tracts at higher intensities or with different electrode positions, producing mixed responses. Thus, “sensory rather than motor” is correct for the classic peripheral-nerve response, but not for every spinal-evoked response.

FINAL DECISION: YES
