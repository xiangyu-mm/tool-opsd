**Yes.** FIT has low sensitivity for sessile serrated lesions, including larger lesions. In a colonoscopy-controlled study of 9,989 average-risk adults, FIT detected only **5.1% of sessile serrated polyps ≥1 cm** at the study’s positivity threshold of **more than 100 ng hemoglobin/mL of buffer**. By comparison, multitarget stool DNA detected 42.4% of these lesions (Imperiale et al., *New England Journal of Medicine*, 2014; PMID: 24645800).

This result applies to that study’s assay threshold and population; estimates can vary by test and cutoff. Still, it shows that a negative FIT should not be taken as reliable evidence that a sessile serrated lesion is absent. FIT remains useful for colorectal cancer screening, but it is substantially less sensitive for these precursor polyps.
