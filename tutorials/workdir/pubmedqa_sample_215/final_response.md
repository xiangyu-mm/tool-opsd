Yes. A phase II study was conducted in 20 men with recurrent prostate cancer, using sulforaphane-rich broccoli sprout extract at 200 μmol/day for up to 20 weeks (PMID: 25431127).

The study’s primary endpoint—a PSA decline of at least 50%—was met by 1 of 20 participants. PSA doubling time increased from 6.1 to 9.6 months, but that finding does not establish clinical efficacy. No grade 3 adverse events were reported. A separate broccoli sprout extract study in men undergoing prostate biopsy addressed biomarkers, not treatment of recurrent disease.

FINAL DECISION: YES
