**Biliary atresia** is the leading concern: conjugated hyperbilirubinemia with dark urine, pale stools, and elevated GGT is characteristic of obstructive neonatal cholestasis. **Cholangiography—typically intraoperative cholangiography—is the definitive test to confirm biliary atresia**, by showing whether the extrahepatic bile ducts are patent.

Ultrasound, hepatobiliary scintigraphy, and liver biopsy can help evaluate suspected biliary atresia, but they do not definitively confirm it.
