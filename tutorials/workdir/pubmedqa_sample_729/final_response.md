The evidence supports **similar effects on the hippocampal synaptic-plasticity measures tested**. In a rat study, 15 days of repeated electroconvulsive stimulation (rECS) and daily fluoxetine both increased dentate-gyrus field excitatory postsynaptic potentials and attenuated long-term potentiation (LTP) induced by perforant-path stimulation. The authors described these effects as equivalent (Stewart & Reid, *Psychopharmacology*, 2000; PMID: 10755734).

That conclusion is limited to those electrophysiological outcomes and this treatment protocol: the abstract does not report a formal statistical equivalence test. The treatments also differed in a separate outcome—rECS impaired Morris watermaze spatial learning, whereas fluoxetine did not.

FINAL DECISION: YES
