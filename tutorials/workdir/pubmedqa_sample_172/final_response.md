The evidence supports **yes**. In a longitudinal study of 835 low-income African-American and Latina mother–child pairs, children whose mothers did not regulate television content watched significantly more television about 16 months later than children whose mothers strongly regulated content:

Because the confidence interval excluded zero, the study found a statistically significant association between maternal regulation and less subsequent television viewing. This demonstrates association, not definitive causation.
