## Evidence summary

The claim is supported by two clinical studies:

- **Direct comparison (PMID: 27493883):** In a 3-month randomized study of 100 patients with hypothyroidism, levothyroxine increased circulating EPC counts, including **CD133+ and CD34+ populations**. Selenium supplementation at **83, 166, or 249 μg/day** did **not** increase EPC counts.
- **Independent supporting study (PMID: 19897678):** In 20 patients with subclinical hypothyroidism, EPC markers (**CD133+/VEGFR-2+** and **CD34+/VEGFR-2+**) were reduced at baseline and increased after T4 replacement, approaching healthy-control levels. This study did not include selenium.

Thus, the evidence directly supports the statement that **levothyroxine increases EPC counts in hypothyroid patients, whereas selenium did not show this effect in the identified direct comparative trial**. The conclusion is limited by the small number of studies and the use of circulating EPC marker populations rather than a single universally standardized EPC definition.

FINAL DECISION: YES
