Yes. Optic nerve head (optic disc) torsion is a prominent morphological feature of normal-tension glaucoma, particularly compared with primary open-angle glaucoma and in patients with myopia or tilted optic discs.

Key evidence:

- In a study of 78 NTG and 78 matched POAG eyes, NTG eyes had significantly greater optic nerve head torsion. Superior torsion was present in **33.3% of NTG eyes versus 10.3% of POAG eyes** (PMID: **25425302**).
- Among 225 NTG eyes, disc torsion was found in **75.9% of myopic eyes**, and torsion direction was associated with the location of visual-field damage (PMID: **22595297**).
- In NTG eyes with tilted discs, greater torsion was associated with focal lamina cribrosa defects, although torsion itself was not independently associated with visual-field progression (PMID: **27755347**).

Thus, torsion is an important and relatively characteristic structural feature of NTG, but it is **not universal** and is especially prominent in myopic or anatomically tilted discs.

FINAL DECISION: YES
