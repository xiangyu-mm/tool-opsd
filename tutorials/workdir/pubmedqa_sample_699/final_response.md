**Conclusion:** Yes—evidence supports a joint role for ACS2 and ACS6 in cadmium-induced ethylene production and responses. In the Arabidopsis study by Tian et al. (PMID: 25082369; DOI: 10.1186/s12870-014-0212-6), cadmium increased ACC and ethylene, and ACS2/ACS6 transcripts rose strongly. This supports joint contribution, but does not establish that ACS2 and ACS6 are each individually essential. **FINAL DECISION: YES**
