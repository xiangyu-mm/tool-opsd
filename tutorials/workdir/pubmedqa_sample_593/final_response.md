Yes—in a mouse study, **p8/NUPR1-deficient mice had splenic siderosis**, and during experimentally induced acute pancreatitis they showed **increased splenic apoptosis and greater loss of CD3+ and CD3+CD4+ lymphocytes** compared with wild-type mice. The siderosis was reported in mature knockout mice; the increased lymphocyte apoptosis was observed during pancreatitis. This is preclinical evidence, not proof of the same effect in humans.

Source: *“p8 Deficiency causes siderosis in spleens and lymphocyte apoptosis in acute pancreatitis”* (2014), PMID: 25098696.

FINAL DECISION: YES
