Evidence indicates that **physical activity is associated with arterial stiffness in patients with lumbar disk herniation**, but the evidence is limited.

- One cross-sectional study (PMID:25075988) included **490 patients with lumbar disk herniation** and 490 controls.
- Arterial stiffness was assessed using **brachial-ankle pulse-wave velocity (baPWV)**.
- Patients with lumbar disk herniation had higher baPWV than controls (**P < 0.001**).
- Within the study, **lower physical-activity frequency was associated with higher baPWV**; physical activity remained a contributor to increased baPWV in multivariable analysis alongside BMI, LDL cholesterol, and systolic blood pressure.
- However, the study does not establish causality because it was cross-sectional. Reduced activity could result from pain or disability, and residual confounding is possible. No corroborating LDH-specific studies using augmentation index or CAVI were identified.

FINAL DECISION: YES
